# 🎄 24 Days of Xmas — Character Guide

Who's who in the December series. Every drawn character has an ALL-CAPS prompt token; the copy-paste designs and ID phrases live in one place, [`tools/cast.py`](tools/cast.py). Change a design there, rebuild, and every story updates.

The designs for Yeh Yeh, Lina, Gabby, Kyle, and SpicySquid are first drafts written for this series. They are easy to change.

## New family and friends

### Yeh Yeh — Clay Teapot of Old Oolong (爺爺 yéye = grandpa) · `YEHYEH`
- **Catchphrase:** "**慢慢來** (màn man lái) = take it slow. Slow tea is the best tea."
- **Personality:** Rosie and Tori's grandpa. Unhurried, twinkly, notices what fast people miss. His birthday is on Thanksgiving (he eats one very long long-life noodle without stopping). Stays home at first and arrives in Frostpeak by snow train on the 20th, in time to teach everyone to roll tangyuan for the winter solstice.
- **Appearance:** A small round reddish-brown clay teapot of aged oolong tea, a short curved spout, a looped handle, a wisp of white steam from his lid like a tuft of hair, bushy white eyebrows, round wire spectacles, tiny arms and legs, a little bamboo walking stick. In cold air his steam freezes into tiny clouds.
- **Voice:** Slow, warm, chuckling. Tap, tap, tap goes his stick.
- **Poses:** teapot poses: stands, leans on his stick, lifts a tiny teacup, puffs steam.

### Lina — Snowflake Crisp (雪花酥 xuěhuā sū) · `LINA`
- **Catchphrase:** "Poof!" (and "No two alike!")
- **Personality:** Crafty, bright, a born decorator. Leaves a little puff of sugar snow wherever she hops. Leads the paper-snowflake day (the 7th).
- **Appearance:** A small square snowflake crisp cookie bar, soft white and powdery like fresh snow, studded with ruby-red cranberries and green pistachio bits (Christmas colors, built in), big bright eyes, rosy cheeks, tiny arms and legs, a tiny red ribbon bow on one corner.
- **Voice:** Quick, cheerful, pleased with herself in a way nobody minds.

### Gabby — the Black Star Shark · `GABBY`
- **Catchphrase:** "Don't worry. I only bite pie."
- **Personality:** The gentlest friend with the toothiest smile. Swims through the air a little above the ground, as star sharks do, and through snow like water. A little shy about her teeth and her spots until the 22nd, when the night sky turns out to match her back. Leads the under-the-ice day (the 14th) and the sky-full-of-stars night (the 22nd).
- **Appearance:** A small chubby black shark pup with tiny white star-shaped spots sprinkled across her back, a pale grey tummy, big shiny dark eyes, a wide gentle smile with tiny rounded teeth, short rounded fins, and a red-and-white striped knit scarf.
- **Voice:** Soft, dry, funny.
- **Prompt note:** "star" in a still flips it to the night look. By day, stills say only `GABBY` plus her pose; the reference view carries the spots.

### Kyle — Formosan Black Bear Cub (台灣黑熊 Táiwān hēixióng) · `KYLE`
- **Catchphrase:** "I was just taste-testing."
- **Personality:** Strong, hungry, huge-hearted. Carries anything, hugs everyone, tastes everything. The polar bears adopt him as an honorary cub. Says sorry and helps fix it when a taste-test goes too far (the 10th).
- **Appearance:** A small round Formosan black bear cub with fluffy black fur, a creamy white V on his chest, round ears, a tan muzzle, and a red knit beanie with a white pom-pom.
- **Voice:** Earnest, cheerful, usually chewing.

### SpicySquid — Spicy Grilled Squid (烤魷魚 kǎo yóuyú) · `SPICYSQUID`
- **Catchphrase:** "Spice is nice! A little heat keeps the cold away."
- **Personality:** Runs the Squid Grill, the warmest stall on Lantern Lane. Feeds everybody, never sits down, never mentions that his birthday is on the 24th. The whole month is a surprise for him: one lantern a day until the lane is full of light. Penguins keep telling him he smells amazing; he keeps handing them corn.
- **Appearance:** A cheerful grilled squid on a bamboo skewer, glossy red-orange with a sweet chili glaze, sesame seeds and chili flakes, big friendly eyes, a wide grin, curly tentacles, a tiny white chef's headband.
- **Voice:** Big, warm, a little gruff, laughs first.
- **Note:** His relationship to the family is left open in the stories ("everybody's favorite"). Nobody ever eats SpicySquid.

### Noa — Sufganiyah (jelly doughnut) · `NOA`
- **Catchphrase:** "One more light!"
- **Personality:** Herschel's young friend who helps host Hanukkah at the lodge. Proud to explain the helper candle and the dreidel; generous with chocolate coins.
- **Appearance:** A small round golden jelly doughnut dusted with powdered sugar, a ruby-red jelly dot on top, big warm eyes, tiny arms and legs, a little blue knit scarf.
- **Note:** No story has anyone eating jelly doughnuts. The Hanukkah food on the table is latkes.

## Montreal and Quebec City (the Market Train friends)

Met on the four day trips to the Christmas markets (days 9, 12, 17, 18); all four ride in for the finale.

### Sésame — Montreal Bagel · `SESAME`
- **Catchphrase:** "**Bonjour-hi!**" (the Montreal hello: both languages at once)
- **Personality:** Wood-fired, honey-boiled, covered in sesame seeds, smaller and sweeter than other bagels and proud of it. Noa's cousin. Warm, quick, bilingual, a born tour guide. Being a bagel, he can roll himself like a wheel in a hurry.
- **Appearance:** A small golden-brown bagel with a big round hole, a honey glaze, a thick coat of sesame seeds, bright eyes, tiny arms and legs, a red knit tuque with a white pom-pom.
- **Stories:** 9 (the Montreal Christmas market), 17 (carols at the Old Port).

### Couic — Cheese Curd · `COUIC`
- **Catchphrase:** "Couic!" (KWEEK: the squeak a fresh curd makes)
- **Personality:** A very tiny fresh cheese curd from a poutine stand. One squeak is his whole voice, and he is shy about it until the carol on the 17th, when his squeak is the last note and the one everybody cheers.
- **Appearance:** A very tiny lumpy white cheese curd, huge round eyes, a shy smile, a tiny blue-and-white striped scarf.

### Margot — Maple Taffy on a Stick (tire d'érable) · `MARGOT`
- **Catchphrase:** "Stick with me!"
- **Personality:** Hot maple syrup poured on clean snow and rolled up on a stick. Stretchy, easygoing, hops along on her stick, runs the taffy stand at the bottom of the toboggan slide.
- **Appearance:** A golden-amber swirl of maple taffy around a wooden stick, honey-colored eyes, tiny stretchy arms, snow crystals sparkling on her swirl.
- **Stories:** 12 (the toboggan slide in Old Quebec).

### Harfang — Snowy Owl · `HARFANG`
- **Catchphrase:** "Hoo-là-là."
- **Personality:** The snowy owl is Quebec's official bird, and Harfang is the calm, dignified guide of Old Quebec. Turns his head all the way around to check on everyone. Starts the toboggan race with one slow blink.
- **Appearance:** A big fluffy snowy owl, white feathers flecked with grey, round golden eyes, a red woven arrow-pattern sash around his middle.
- **Stories:** 12 (the toboggan slide), 18 (the Christmas market and the wood-carver's chalet).

## The world of the series

- **Frostpeak Lodge** — where the family stays all December. In the great hall stands **the Door Wall**: twenty-four little carved doors, one opened each morning. Behind each is a small object or carved picture (never writing) that hints at the day.
- **The Market Train** — the snow train that brought the family north. When a door holds a tiny golden train ticket, it runs to the Christmas markets of **Montreal** (the big downtown market on the 9th, the Old Port with its skating rink and Ferris wheel on the 17th) or **Quebec City** (the toboggan slide on Dufferin Terrace on the 12th, the Christmas market in the old town on the 18th) and is home by nightfall. French words use the same format as the Mandarin: `**Bonjour-hi** (bohn-ZHOOR hi) = the Montreal hello`.
- **Lantern Lane** — the lane through the Frostpeak winter night market. One paper lantern is earned each day and hung on the long string at dusk. The ritual line is **"One more light."** On the 24th the whole lane is lit for SpicySquid's birthday.
- **The bunk room** — every story ends here at night, with two small things from the day on the nightstand.

## Getting the holidays right

- **Hanukkah** (in 2026: first candle on Friday, December 4; eighth candle on Friday, December 11; candles are lit as the sun goes down, and on a Friday just before sunset). The hanukkiah has nine holders: eight nights plus the **shamash**, the helper candle that lights the others. One more candle is added each night; a grown-up lights them. Foods fried in oil (latkes), the dreidel game with chocolate coins (gelt), and the story of a little oil that lasted eight nights. The family are welcome guests; Herschel and Noa do the explaining, in one or two warm lines.
- **St. Nicholas Eve** (December 5): shoes or boots are left out and found filled with small treats in the morning.
- **St. Lucia Day** (December 13): the eldest girl wears a crown of lights and carries buns to the household before sunrise.
- **Las Posadas** (begins December 16): friends go door to door asking for room, and the last door says yes.
- **Dōngzhì 冬至**, the winter solstice (December 21 in North America in 2026; December 22 on the calendar in Taiwan): families roll and eat **湯圓** (tāngyuán), round rice balls that stand for togetherness, **團圓** (tuányuán). Eating them makes you one year older.
- **Christmas Eve** (December 24) is also SpicySquid's birthday.
