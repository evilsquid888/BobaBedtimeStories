# Master Plan — Four Bedtime Story Collections

Cozy, funny, food-themed bedtime stories for a 9-year-old (ages 9–12). Warm Roald-Dahl-meets-a-night-market tone, sentient food characters, light local-language vocabulary, soft Goodnight Blessings. Nothing genuinely scary, sad, or dark — gentle "cute" spookiness only.

## Highlights
- **Taiwan** expands beyond desserts into savory street food (beef noodle, xiao long bao, gua bao, popcorn chicken…) plus festivals, a regional road trip, gentle folklore, seasons, games, and a grand finale at #100.
- **Hawaii** stars dole whip, shave ice, malasadas, spam musubi, poke, kalua pig, haupia & friends — luau & ʻohana (family) warmth, a **surfing** theme, gentle lava/volcanoes, Waikiki, and island-hopping to Maui, Kauaʻi, the Big Island, and beyond.
- **Costa Rica** ("Pura Vida") features gallo pinto, casado, tres leches, granizados, coffee & coconut friends, plus sloths, toucans, sea turtles, rainforests, and volcanoes.
- **Japan** covers Tokyo Tower & Skytree, onsen & snow monkeys, anime/otaku & gachapon, torii gates & festivals, hanami & hanabi, street eats (sushi, ramen, Pepper Lunch, takoyaki), kimono & Kyoto, the shinkansen across regions, kawaii folklore — **and a cozy "Import Night" car-culture arc** (#88–92: garage tuning, neon car meets, a *touge* sunrise drive, a friendly time-attack, and a sushi food-truck crew).

## Pipeline
1. ✅ Planning workflow — roadmaps generated & deduped.
2. ▶️ Writing workflows — one per collection, fanning out to write every story file.
3. ⏳ QA workflow — final duplicate + bedtime age-appropriateness sweep across all collections.
