# Life Lessons — Character Guide

## The Hero

### Rosie — Rose Milk Tea (玫瑰奶茶 méiguī nǎichá)
- **Catchphrase:** "One step at a time, one sip at a time."
- **Personality:** Sweet, curious, a little nervous about new people and places — and very brave once she knows the steps. Loves lists, likes to practice first, and feels proud when she finishes something.
- **Appearance:** A small, friendly cup of pale-pink rose milk tea with a clear rounded dome lid. Her tea is a soft blush pink with creamy swirls, and three tiny dark tapioca pearls float at the bottom like happy freckles. A single pink rose petal rests on top of her lid like a hair clip. Big warm brown eyes, rosy cheeks, tiny arms and legs, and a pink-striped straw that wiggles when she's excited and droops when she's nervous. Smells faintly of roses and honey.
- **Voice:** Gentle, honest, short sentences. Asks "What's the next step?" a lot.
- **Self-calming tool:** "Rose breath" — smell the rose (breathe in for 4), blow out the candle (breathe out for 4). She does it whenever her straw starts to droop.

### Tori — Rose Lychee Oolong (玫瑰荔枝烏龍 méiguī lìzhī wūlóng), Rosie's big sister
- **Catchphrase:** "You've got this, little sip."
- **Personality:** Rosie's big sister. Patient, funny, always has time for Rosie. Gives the best hugs and the best "you did it!" cheers.
- **Appearance:** A taller cup of golden-amber rose-lychee oolong tea with pearly white lychee jelly cubes, a darker rose petal on her lid, and a tall green straw. Wears a tiny phone clipped to her cup sleeve.
- **Voice:** Warm, encouraging, a little silly.
