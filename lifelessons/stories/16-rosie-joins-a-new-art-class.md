# 16 — Rosie Joins a New Art Class

**Life skill:** The first day in a new class or club — walking in, finding the teacher, introducing yourself and asking where to sit, saying hi to the kid next to you, raising your hand when you need something, helping clean up, and saying goodbye.
**Magic sentence:** "Hi, I'm Rosie. I'm new. Where should I sit?"
**Coaches:** Momo Mochi (Japan) + Aiyu (Taiwan) — with big sister Tori as the pretend teacher
**Cat’s role:** art-supply helper
**Setting:** Tori's sunny attic bedroom with an easel (practice), then a bright community art studio with rainbow brush jars, a garden window and a paper fish lantern — where Cat the penguin helps lay newspapers beneath the easels

---

## The Story

Rosie the Rose Milk Tea was starting art class on Saturday. A real class, with a real **老師** (lǎoshī) = teacher.

"But I don't know anyone," she said. "Or where to sit." Her pink-striped straw drooped.

Up the attic stairs bounced Momo Mochi, peachy cheeks glowing. "Squish together, stay together!"

Behind him wobbled Aiyu, the golden jelly. "I was nervous on my first day too."

"You can see right through me. That's how you know I'm honest."

Cat the penguin, Rosie's houseguest, sorted brushes beside Tori's beanbag. One looked like a penguin moustache.

Big sister Tori put on paint-splattered glasses. "Today, I am the teacher. A new class has steps."

**Rosie's Steps**
1. Walk in and find the teacher.
2. Say, **"Hi, I'm Rosie. I'm new. Where should I sit?"**
3. Sit where the teacher shows you. Say "Hi!" to the kid next to you.
4. If you need something, raise your hand and wait.
5. When class ends, help clean up.
6. Say, "Thank you! See you next week!"

"In Japan we say ***hajimemashite*** = nice to meet you," said Momo. He bowed and squished flat.

Aiyu went first. "Hi, I'm Aiyu. I'm new." Her voice was tiny.

But she did it. She glowed a little brighter.

Then Rosie practiced:
1. Walk in and find the teacher.
2. Say, **"Hi, I'm Rosie. I'm new. Where should I sit?"**
3. Sit where the teacher shows you. Say "Hi!" to the kid next to you.
4. If you need something, raise your hand and wait.
5. When class ends, help clean up.
6. Say, "Thank you! See you next week!"

"Excellent work, class," said Teacher Tori. "You may **畫畫** (huàhuà) = paint."

Momo painted a very round peach. It was a circle.

On Saturday, the art studio smelled like paint and lemons. Jars of brushes stood in rainbow water.

Cat came too. She spread spare newspapers beneath the easels.

Step one. Rosie found the teacher. She had a silver braid and a splattered apron.

Step two. "**Hi, I'm Rosie. I'm new. Where should I sit?**"

"Welcome, Rosie! Right here, next to Tang."

Step three. Rosie sat. Beside her stood Tang the Tanghulu, five candied strawberries on a stick.

"Hi!" said Rosie. "Hi!" said Tang. "I'm the tall one. Everyone sits behind me."

Rosie painted a boat. Then — bump! Tang's elbow tipped a jar.

**藍色** (lánsè) = blue water flooded across the table, straight toward Rosie's paper.

That was not like practice. Rosie felt flustered. Her straw drooped all the way down. That's okay.

Rosie did a Rose breath. Smell the rose — in, two, three, four. Blow out the candle — out, two, three, four.

Step four. Rosie raised her hand. She waited. "Excuse me, can I have a paper towel?"

"Of course!" The teacher brought a whole roll. Tang mopped with both arms.

"Sorry! My elbows are very far from my brain."

One drip landed on Cat's tummy. "Already blue," said Cat, fetching a cloth.

Rosie looked at her paper. The puddle had dried around her boat. "It's the sea!" she whispered.

Step five. When class ended, Rosie rinsed the brushes. Tang carried the jars, far from his elbows.

Step six. "Thank you! See you next week!" "See you next week, Rosie!"

Tori waited at the gate, still in her splattered glasses.

"You found your seat AND made a sea. You've got this, little sip!"

"One step at a time," Rosie said. "One sip at a time."

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)
1. Walk in and find the teacher.
2. Say, **"Hi, I'm Rosie. I'm new. Where should I sit?"**
3. Sit where the teacher shows you. Say "Hi!" to the kid next to you.
4. If you need something, raise your hand and wait.
5. When class ends, help clean up.
6. Say, "Thank you! See you next week!"

**If something surprising happens:** Rose breath, then raise your hand, wait, and say: "Excuse me, can I have a paper towel?" (or whatever you need). A spill is not a problem — it is a step four.

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)
- Set up a "class" at home with a grown-up or big sibling as the teacher (paint-splattered glasses optional). Let your child walk in from the hallway and run all six steps out loud, then swap: your child plays the teacher and shows *you* where to sit.
- Practice the hand-raise on purpose: knock over a cup of water or "run out" of paper, and praise the wait. "You raised your hand and kept it up until I came — that was step four!" Practice the exact words: "Excuse me, can I have ___?"
- Let a shy toy go first, like Aiyu. Hearing someone else say "Hi, I'm new" in a tiny voice makes it easier to say it in a medium voice.
- For the real first day, arrive five minutes early so the teacher is free, and agree ahead of time where you will wait (the gate, a bench). Afterward ask, "Who sat next to you?" and "What did you make?" and peg the picture up somewhere it can be seen from bed.

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing
Goodnight, little painter, with blue on your sleeve.
Goodnight, brushes, resting in rainbow water.
Goodnight, Cat, already blue, already dreaming.
A new seat, a new friend, a new sea. Sleep tight.
