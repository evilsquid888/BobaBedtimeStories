# 02 — Rosie Asks for Help at the Grocery Store

**Life skill:** Asking a store worker for help finding an item.
**Magic sentence:** "Excuse me, can you help me find the honey?"
**Coaches:** Taro 🇹🇼 (wise, patient purple taro) + Gallo & Pinto 🇨🇷 (cheerful rice-and-bean duo who LOVE grocery shopping)
**Setting:** Rosie's cozy kitchen, then a bright, friendly neighborhood grocery store — with Cat the penguin napping in the cart

---

## The Story

Rosie the Rose Milk Tea had an important job.

Big sister Tori had a scratchy throat. Mom wanted to make her honey tea, but the jar was empty.

"We'll go to the **超市** (chāoshì) = supermarket after lunch," said Mom. "Rosie, will you find the honey?"

Rosie's straw drooped. The store was so big.

DING-DONG! In rolled Taro, the wise purple taro. Behind him bounced Gallo the rice grain and Pinto the black bean.

"Rise and pinto!" sang Gallo. "The free cheese samples!"

"He once waved at a watermelon for ten minutes," said Pinto. "He thought it was his cousin."

Rosie giggled. "But what if I can't find the honey?"

"Then you ask for help," said Taro. "Store workers know where everything lives. Good things take time to steam."

**Rosie's Steps**
1. Find a worker with a name tag and a store apron or vest.
2. Walk up and wait until they look at you.
3. Say, **"Excuse me, can you help me find the honey?"**
4. Look where they point.
5. Say, "Thank you!"
6. Go find it, staying where your grown-up can see you.

"**蜂蜜** (fēngmì) = honey," said Taro. "Bees work hard, like store workers. Only fuzzier."

Gallo put on a name tag and a napkin apron. "Now he is a store worker," said Pinto. "A very small one."

Rosie did each step:
1. Find a worker with a name tag and a store apron or vest.
2. Walk up and wait until they look at you.
3. Say, **"Excuse me, can you help me find the honey?"**
4. Look where they point.
5. Say, "Thank you!"
6. Go find it, staying where your grown-up can see you.

"Aisle five!" said Gallo, pointing so hard he fell into the grape bowl. "I'm fine! Grapes are soft!"

After lunch, Mom pushed the cart into the store. Taro, Gallo, and Pinto rode along. In the cart, Cat the penguin napped on a bag of marshmallows. Cat was staying with Rosie's family.

"Five more minutes," she mumbled. She had not been awake for ANY minutes.

Step one. Rosie looked for a worker. She found one in a green apron with a name tag. But he was up on a ladder, arms full of boxes.

This was not like practice. Rosie felt nervous. Her straw drooped. That's okay.

Rosie did a Rose breath. Smell the rose — in, two, three, four. Blow out the candle — out, two, three, four.

"That's okay," she said. "I can find a different worker with a name tag."

"Wise choice," said Taro. Near the apples stood Maya, a worker in a green vest and a name tag.

Step two. Rosie walked up and waited. Maya looked down and smiled.

Step three. "**Excuse me, can you help me find the honey?**"

"Sure! Aisle five, with the peanut butter." Maya pointed.

Step four. Rosie looked where Maya pointed. Step five. Rosie said, "Thank you!"

Step six. Rosie went to aisle five, with Mom behind her. The honey jars glowed like tiny golden suns. Rosie picked one shaped like a bear.

Gallo tried to hug the bear. "Not a cousin," said Pinto gently.

At home, Tori sipped her tea. "Mmm! You found this?"

"The first worker was busy," said Rosie. "So I did a Rose breath and asked another."

Tori hugged her tight. "You've got this, little sip!" On the marshmallows, Cat yawned. "Five more minutes."

"One step at a time," said Rosie. "One sip at a time."

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)
1. Find a worker with a name tag and a store apron or vest.
2. Walk up and wait until they look at you.
3. Say, **"Excuse me, can you help me find the honey?"**
4. Look where they point.
5. Say, "Thank you!"
6. Go find it, staying where your grown-up can see you.

**If something surprising happens:** Rose breath, then say: "That's okay. I can find a different worker with a name tag."

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)
- **Play store at home.** Put a sticky-note name tag and a kitchen apron on a grown-up or a stuffed animal. Your child walks up, waits for eye contact, and says **"Excuse me, can you help me find the honey?"** Point to a "shelf" (a cupboard) and say "Aisle five." Swap in bread, bananas, or crayons once the sentence feels easy.
- **Practice the busy worker.** Sometimes be "busy" — on the phone, or stacking pretend boxes (a stuffed penguin napping in the toy cart makes a fun extra). Coach a Rose breath and the backup sentence, and praise choosing another worker.
- **Praise the exact step:** "You waited until she looked at you. That was step two!" Specific praise helps the steps stick.
- **Real-world tip:** Pick a quiet time at a familiar store. Stand where your child can see you and let them do the talking. Before you go in, remind them: workers wear name tags and store aprons or vests, and we stay where we can see each other.
