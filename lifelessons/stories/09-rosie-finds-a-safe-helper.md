# 09 — Rosie Finds a Safe Helper

**Life skill:** What to do if you can't find your grown-up in a store or mall — stay inside, find a safe helper, and say your name and your grown-up's name and phone number.
**Magic sentence:** "I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"
**Coaches:** Caǎo the Grass Jelly (Taiwan), Estrella the Sea Turtle (Costa Rica), Flan the Coconut Flan (Costa Rica)
**Setting:** A bright morning beach beside an old wooden pier (practice at a driftwood-log counter, with Cat the penguin napping in the beach bag), then a grand glass-domed department store with escalators, a koi pond fountain and rainbow towers of towels — where Cat naps in Mom's basket

---

## The Story

"Steady, steady... ooh, wobbly!" Flan the Coconut Flan wobbled across the sand. "What if the store is TOO big?"

"Chill," said Caǎo, the grass jelly in the tiny black beanie.

In the beach bag napped Cat the penguin, who was staying with Rosie's family. She napped like a cat — that's how she got her name.

Estrella the Sea Turtle glided in from the surf. "We sea turtles follow the moon home," she said. "In a store, your moon is a safe helper."

Caǎo counted on his fingers.

**Rosie's Steps**
1. Stop. Stay inside the store.
2. Do a Rose breath.
3. Find a safe helper: a worker with a name tag, or a mom with kids.
4. Say, **"I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"**
5. Say your name, your grown-up's name, and their phone number.
6. Stay with the helper. Never go outside or to a car with anyone.

"Phone numbers are so WIGGLY," said Flan.

"So we make it a song," said Caǎo.

Rosie sang her **媽媽** (māma) = Mom's number to "Twinkle, Twinkle." Flan sang too — very off-key. Cat opened one eye. "Five more minutes," she mumbled.

Caǎo wore a paper name tag. "I'm the helper. Very official." Rosie practiced:
1. Stop. Stay inside the store.
2. Do a Rose breath.
3. Find a safe helper: a worker with a name tag, or a mom with kids.
4. Say, **"I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"**
5. Say your name, your grown-up's name, and their phone number.
6. Stay with the helper. Never go outside or to a car with anyone.

"My name is Rosie. My mom's name is Mei," she told Caǎo, and sang her song. Flan played Mom. He hugged her so hard his caramel dripped.

On Saturday, Rosie, Mom, and Cat went to the big department store. Cat napped in Mom's basket, right on the softest towel. "She's testing it," said Mom.

Rosie sniffed a rose-shaped soap. When she looked up, Mom was not there. Just towels, towels, towels.

Rosie felt worried. Her straw drooped. That's okay.

Step one. Rosie stopped. She stayed inside the store.

Step two. She did a Rose breath. Smell the rose — in, two, three, four. Blow out the candle — out, two, three, four.

Step three. Rosie looked for a safe helper. A man nearby had no name tag. That was different from practice. Her straw drooped more.

Another Rose breath. "I need a helper with a name tag."

At a counter stood Pat, in a red vest with a name tag.

Step four. Rosie said, "**I can't find my mom. Can you help me?**"

"I sure can," said Pat kindly. "Thank you for telling me."

Step five. Rosie said, "My name is Rosie. My mom's name is Mei." She sang the number.

Pat picked up the store phone. Her voice came from the ceiling: "Mei, please come to the counter by the towels."

Step six. Rosie stayed right next to Pat. She counted the rose soaps. Seven.

Then Mom hurried up with the basket. "Rosie!" The biggest hug.

In the basket, Cat had napped through the whole thing.

"You stayed inside and found a helper," said Mom. "**好棒** (hǎo bàng) = great job!"

That night, Tori called. "You've got this, little sip — no, you HAD it!"

Flan wobbled proudly. "I didn't wobble ONCE!" He paused. "Okay. One tiny wobble."

Rosie smiled sleepily. "One step at a time, one sip at a time."

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)
1. Stop. Stay inside the store.
2. Do a Rose breath.
3. Find a safe helper: a worker with a name tag, or a mom with kids.
4. Say, **"I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"**
5. Say your name, your grown-up's name, and their phone number.
6. Stay with the helper. Never go outside or to a car with anyone.

**If something surprising happens:** Rose breath, then say: "I need a helper with a name tag." Name tags live at the counter and the register.

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)
- **Make a phone-number song.** Put your number to a tune your child already knows ("Twinkle, Twinkle" works well). Sing it in the car, in the bath, and at bedtime until they can sing it alone. Practice your full first and last name too.
- **Role-play anywhere.** Build a "counter" from a cereal box at home, or a log or picnic bench at the beach or park like Caǎo did; wear a paper name tag, and let your child walk through all six steps out loud while you play the helper; then have another grown-up play Mom arriving. Add one surprise: someone nearby with no name tag, so they practice a Rose breath and choosing the name tag instead.
- **Point out real helpers.** On your next store trip, play "Spot the Name Tag." Show where the registers and service counters are, and agree ahead of time: "If we get apart, stay inside and go to the counter."
- **Praise the staying.** The most important step is staying inside with the helper. Praise it loudly: "You stayed put — that's how I found you so fast!"

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing
The towels are folded, soft and still.
The rose soaps sleep upon the shelf.
You know your steps, you know your song.
Follow the moon home, little sip. Goodnight.
