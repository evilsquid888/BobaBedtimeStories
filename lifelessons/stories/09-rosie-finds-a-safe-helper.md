# 09 — Rosie Finds a Safe Helper

**Life skill:** What to do if you can't find your grown-up in a store or mall — stay inside, find a safe helper, and say your name and your grown-up's name and phone number.
**Magic sentence:** "I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"
**Coaches:** Caǎo the Grass Jelly (Taiwan), Estrella the Sea Turtle (Costa Rica), Flan the Coconut Flan (Costa Rica)
**Setting:** Rosie's cozy living room (practice), then a big, friendly department store at the mall

---

## The Story

"Steady, steady... ooh, wobbly!"

Flan the Coconut Flan was wobbling all over the living room rug. His caramel drizzle slid down one side.

"What if the store is TOO big?" he fussed. "What if it has two floors? AND an escalator?"

"Chill," said Caǎo, the cool little grass jelly in the tiny black beanie. "I've got this. We're only practicing."

Estrella the Sea Turtle glided in slowly, calm as a warm ocean tide.

"Little one," she said to Rosie, "sea turtles swim far, far away. But we always find our way back. We follow the moon home."

"I don't have a moon," said Rosie.

"In a store, your moon is a safe helper," said Estrella. "Helpers help you get back to your grown-up."

Rosie's pink-striped straw wiggled. She loved a plan. "What are the steps?"

Caǎo held up one cool, dark finger at a time.

**Rosie's Steps**
1. Stop. Stay inside the store. Do not go outside or to the parking lot.
2. Do a Rose breath.
3. Look for a safe helper: a store worker with a name tag at the counter, or a mom with kids.
4. Say the magic sentence: **"I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"**
5. Say your name, your grown-up's name, and their phone number if you know it.
6. Stay with the helper at the counter until your grown-up comes. Never go outside or to a car with anyone.

"But phone numbers are so WIGGLY," said Flan.

"So we make it a song," said Caǎo.

Rosie sang the phone number for her **媽媽** (māma) = Mom, to the tune of "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star." Flan sang it too. Three times. Very loudly. A little off-key. Everybody giggled.

Then they practiced. Caǎo made a pretend counter out of a cereal box. He stuck a paper name tag on his beanie. It said HELPER in big letters.

"Very official," said Caǎo.

Rosie did every step.

1. Stop. Stay inside the store. Do not go outside or to the parking lot.
2. Do a Rose breath.
3. Look for a safe helper: a store worker with a name tag at the counter, or a mom with kids.
4. Say the magic sentence: **"I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"**
5. Say your name, your grown-up's name, and their phone number if you know it.
6. Stay with the helper at the counter until your grown-up comes. Never go outside or to a car with anyone.

"My name is Rosie," she told Caǎo. "My mom's name is Mei." Then she sang the number song.

Flan played Mom. He wobbled in from the kitchen. "My sweet sip! You stayed put!" He hugged her so hard his caramel dripped.

"Now you know the way home," said Estrella.

On Saturday, Rosie and Mom went to the big, friendly department store at the mall. Mom was looking at fluffy towels. Rosie found a shelf of soaps shaped like roses. She sniffed one. Mmm.

When she looked up, Mom was not there. Just towels. Towels, towels, towels.

Rosie felt worried. Her straw drooped. That's okay. She knew the steps.

Step one. Rosie stopped. She stayed inside the store. She did not go looking outside.

Step two. She did a Rose breath. Smell the rose... one, two, three, four. Blow out the candle... one, two, three, four.

Step three. She looked around. A tall man stood nearby, talking on his phone. Rosie took a step toward him.

Then she remembered Caǎo's calm voice. *"Chill. Look for the name tag. Name tags live at the register."*

The man had no name tag. That was different from practice. Rosie's straw drooped a little more.

She did another Rose breath. Then she said to herself, "I need a helper with a name tag."

She looked past the towels. At the end of the aisle was a counter with a register. Behind it stood a lady in a red vest. Her name tag said PAT.

Step four. Rosie walked to the counter. "**I can't find my mom. Can you help me?**"

Pat smiled a big, kind smile. "I sure can. Thank you for telling me."

Step five. "My name is Rosie. My mom's name is Mei." Rosie hummed her song, then said Mom's phone number.

"You did exactly the right thing," said Pat. "Let's stay right here together."

Pat picked up the store phone. Her friendly voice came from the ceiling: "Mei, please come to the register by the towels. Rosie is waiting for you."

Step six. Rosie stayed right next to Pat. She did not wander. She counted the rose soaps. Seven.

Before she could count them again, Mom hurried around the corner, still holding a fluffy towel.

"Rosie!"

Big hug. The biggest hug. Rosie's straw wiggled like crazy.

Mom had only been three aisles away. "You stayed inside and found a helper with a name tag," Mom said. "**好棒** (hǎo bàng) = great job!"

That night, Tori burst into Rosie's room. "You've got this, little sip — no, you HAD it!"

Flan wobbled proudly. "I didn't wobble ONCE when I heard!" He paused. "Okay. One tiny wobble."

Caǎo tipped his beanie. "Chill work, Rosie."

"Tranquila, little one — that means calm," said Estrella softly. "You followed the moon home."

Rosie smiled a sleepy smile. "One step at a time, one sip at a time."

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)
1. Stop. Stay inside the store. Do not go outside or to the parking lot.
2. Do a Rose breath.
3. Look for a safe helper: a store worker with a name tag at the counter, or a mom with kids.
4. Say the magic sentence: **"I can't find my mom. Can you help me?"**
5. Say your name, your grown-up's name, and their phone number if you know it.
6. Stay with the helper at the counter until your grown-up comes. Never go outside or to a car with anyone.

**If something surprising happens:** Rose breath, then say: "I need a helper with a name tag."

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)
- **Make a phone-number song.** Put your number to a tune your child already knows ("Twinkle, Twinkle" works well). Sing it in the car, in the bath, and at bedtime until she can sing it alone. Also practice saying your full first and last name.
- **Role-play at home.** Build a "counter" from a box, wear a paper name tag, and let her walk through all six steps. Then switch: have one person be a "busy grown-up on a phone with no name tag" so she practices choosing the name tag instead.
- **Point out real helpers.** On your next store trip, play "Spot the Name Tag." Show her where the registers and service counters are, and agree ahead of time: "If we get apart, stay inside and go to the counter."
- **Praise the staying.** The most important step is staying inside with the helper. Praise that loudly: "You stayed put — that's how I found you so fast!"

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing
The towels are folded, soft and still.
The rose soaps sleep upon the shelf.
You know your steps, you know your song,
and every helper helps you home.
Follow the moon, little sip.
Goodnight, and sweet dreams.
