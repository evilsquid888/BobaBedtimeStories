# 11 — Rosie Says Goodbye at the Party

**Life skill:** Leaving a party the right way — gathering your things, finding the hosts, saying thank you, and leaving together.
**Magic sentence:** "Thank you for having us. We had fun!"
**Coaches:** Marcus (teen Inclusive Navigator) + Feng (Taiwan) + Mochi (Taiwan)
**Setting:** Rosie's cozy kitchen (practice), then Lulu the Almond Jelly's birthday party at her house — balloons, a bouncy castle, a cake, and one sleepy penguin named Cat

---

## The Story

Rosie the Rose Milk Tea was going to a party. Her friend Lulu the Almond Jelly was turning eight.

Rosie was excited, but worried about the end. "How do I leave?" she asked. "Do I just... go?" Her pink-striped straw drooped.

DING-DONG. In walked Marcus, the teen next door. He was an Inclusive Navigator — a helper who shows kids the steps for tricky places.

"Leaving a party has steps," said Marcus. "I've left about a hundred parties. Mostly on purpose."

Feng the Pineapple Cake bowed so low his gold wrapper crinkled. Stretchy Mochi got stuck in the door. "Stick together!" he said. "Also, unstick together."

Rosie giggled. On the windowsill, Cat the penguin opened one eye. Cat was staying with Rosie's family. She napped like a cat, which is how she got her name. "Five more minutes," she mumbled.

"Here are the steps," said Marcus.

**Rosie's Steps**
1. When a grown-up says "Five more minutes," stop playing.
2. Gather your things: shoes, jacket, bag, and your little sister.
3. Find the host kid and their mom and dad.
4. Look at their faces, smile, and say, **"Thank you for having us. We had fun!"**
5. Wave, say "Bye!", and leave with your family.

"Little sister means Bud," said Marcus. Bud was under the table, chewing a sock.

"Practice time!" said Marcus. "I'm Lulu's dad. Feng is Lulu. Mochi is Lulu's mom." Mochi put a napkin on his head. "I am a very beautiful mom."

Rosie practiced:
1. When a grown-up says "Five more minutes," stop playing.
2. Gather your things: shoes, jacket, bag, and your little sister.
3. Find the host kid and their mom and dad.
4. Look at their faces, smile, and say, **"Thank you for having us. We had fun!"**
5. Wave, say "Bye!", and leave with your family.

"Beautifully done," sniffed Feng. "I'm not crying. My wrapper is."

On Saturday, Cat came to the party too. Rosie bounced in Lulu's bouncy castle. There was a big **蛋糕** (dàngāo) = cake with eight candles.

Dad called, "Five more minutes, Rosie!" Step one. Rosie climbed out. Step two. Shoes. Bag. Bud. Jacket —

The jacket was on the couch. On the jacket was Cat, snoring like a tiny motor.

This was not like practice. Rosie felt stuck. Her straw drooped all the way down. That's okay.

Rosie did a Rose breath. Smell the rose — in, two, three, four. Blow out the candle — out, two, three, four.

Marcus tapped his compass pin. "When something is in the way, you can ask for help. That's a step too."

Rosie found Lulu's mom. "Excuse me. Cat is on my jacket. Can you help me?" Lulu's mom laughed and lifted the penguin. "Five more minutes," sighed Cat.

Step three. Rosie found Lulu and her mom and dad by the cake. Step four. Rosie looked at their faces. She smiled. "**Thank you for having us. We had fun!**"

"**再見** (zàijiàn) = goodbye!" said Lulu.

Step five. Rosie waved. "Bye!" Out went the whole family, plus one sleepy penguin. Nobody left behind. Not even the sock.

That night, Tori called. "You thanked the hosts AND rescued your jacket from a penguin! **加油** (jiāyóu) = you can do it — and you DID."

"One step at a time," Rosie said sleepily. "One sip at a time." Cat was already snoring on her pillow.

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)
1. When a grown-up says "Five more minutes," stop playing.
2. Gather your things: shoes, jacket, bag, and your little sister.
3. Find the host kid and their mom and dad.
4. Look at their faces, smile, and say, **"Thank you for having us. We had fun!"**
5. Wave, say "Bye!", and leave with your family.

**If something surprising happens:** Rose breath, then say: "Excuse me. Can you help me?" — a host is always glad to help you find your things or find the right person.

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)
- Play "party" at home: scatter shoes, a jacket, and a bag around the room, hide a stuffed animal as the "little sister," and have one grown-up be the host kid and another be the host mom and dad. Call "Five more minutes!" and let your child run all five steps, saying each one out loud.
- Add one small surprise each time — a toy penguin napping on the jacket, a host who is in the backyard, a balloon that floats away — so your child can practice a Rose breath and "Excuse me. Can you help me?"
- Praise the gathering as much as the thank-you: "You found your bag AND your sister before anyone reminded you — that was step two!" Leaving without a jacket is very common, so celebrate every full set.
- At a real party, give the five-minute warning early and quietly, stand where your child can see you, and let them find the hosts on their own. On the drive home, ask, "Who did you thank?" and name everyone they remembered.

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing
Goodnight, little sip, with your five small steps.
Goodnight, balloons, bobbing slow by the door.
Goodnight, Cat, dreaming on somebody's jacket.
Everybody together. Everybody home. Sleep tight.
