# 36 — Rosie Leaves the Picnic Place Ready

**Life skill:** Care for a shared space by sorting and clearing personal belongings.
**Magic sentence:** "What needs tidying before we go?"
**Coaches:** Niko Nigiri 🇯🇵 + Slowy the Sloth 🇨🇷
**Cat’s role:** helper
**Setting:** the carport camping shelf (practice), then the orchard picnic clearing (real outing).

---

## The Story

Rosie was going to an orchard picnic, where a shared table waited beneath leafy apple trees.

At the carport shelf, she practiced spotting her napkin among the picnic things before anybody packed them.

Slowy lifted a cloth very slowly. "Puuura... viiida... This napkin is enjoying a remarkably leisurely departure."

Niko was a remarkably tidy salmon nigiri.

Slowy was a sloth taking his time.

Cat the penguin practiced sorting picnic things. She solemnly returned a pretend banana to its pretend owner.

Niko checked the pretend picnic place. **"What needs tidying before we go?"** He sorted and returned their things.

"A shared space needs care for the next visitors," Slowy said. "Start with what belongs to you."

**Rosie's Steps**

1. Check your own belongings.
2. Put rubbish in the correct place.
3. Return shared things where they belong.

4. Ask before moving someone else's things.
5. Check the space with your grown-up.

**整理** (zhěnglǐ) = tidying. Rosie tried **"What needs tidying before we go?"** Slowy the Sloth pretended.

1. Check your own belongings.
2. Put rubbish in the correct place.
3. Return shared things where they belong.

4. Ask before moving someone else's things.
5. Check the space with your grown-up.

Rosie practiced gathering her lunch box and placing pretend rubbish into baskets with simple sorting pictures.

Niko aligned the empty boxes. "A tidy picnic procession. Every lid has found its own very rectangular home."

Mom and Rosie finished lunch in the orchard clearing, where a shade cloth fluttered above wooden picnic benches.

Cat carried the empty fruit crate to the packing spot, keeping their picnic blanket clear.

Rosie noticed her cloth napkin, an empty paper wrapper, and a serving tray borrowed from the picnic shelter.

Step one: she checked her belongings. **"What needs tidying before we go?"** Rosie asked Mom beside the bench.

Step two: Mom helped her match the wrapper to the correct local sorting bin's picture and instructions.

Step three: Rosie carried the borrowed tray with Mom to its proper return place inside the shelter.

A little scarf remained on the table. Rosie did not recognize it and wondered whether to pack it.

She felt unsure. Her straw drooped. That's okay; tidying did not mean taking somebody else's belongings.

Rosie took a Rose breath.

Smell the rose: in, two, three, four.

Blow out the candle: out, two, three, four.

"Who does this belong to?" Rosie asked Mom, leaving the scarf visible on the table where it was.

Mom checked with the nearby picnic family. They claimed their scarf and thanked Rosie for noticing it.

Step four: Rosie had asked before moving the scarf. She packed only her own cloth napkin and lunch box.

Step five: she checked the space with Mom, who handled anything sharp or unfamiliar and inspected the bench.

Niko admired the tabletop. Slowy finally folded his napkin. "The cloth has completed its farewell."

Cat collected their folded cloths. "My job is cloth patrol. This napkin was attempting to become a kite."

Rosie smiled. The picnic place was ready for its next visitors.

Tori met Rosie beneath the orchard's little apple-weighing canopy, where their packed picnic bag stood ready.

"You left it ready for others. You've got this, little sip," she said, checking the little folded napkin.

Rosie felt proud of caring for the shared place, and looked forward to taking their apples home.

**加油** (jiāyóu) = go for it.

"One step at a time, one sip at a time," said Rosie.

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)

1. Check your own belongings.
2. Put rubbish in the correct place.
3. Return shared things where they belong.
4. Ask before moving someone else's things.
5. Check the space with your grown-up.

**If something surprising happens:** Take a Rose breath or a comfortable pause, then say: "Who does this belong to?"

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)

- Role-play the situation using a cloth picnic napkin or a safe pretend substitute; read the five steps together.
- Swap roles so your child can see you model **"What needs tidying before we go?"** and hear the other person's answer.
- Introduce this story's small surprise; practice "Who does this belong to?" and allow a comfortable breath or pause.
- On a real outing, stay nearby, offer choices, and praise the specific step your child tried.

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing

Goodnight, picnic table, ready for new visitors.
Goodnight, napkins, folded safely away.
Goodnight, Cat, cloth patrol finished.
May your care leave kindness behind.
