# 01 — Rosie Makes a Friend on the Train

**Life skill:** Starting a friendly conversation with another kid on a train (Taipei MRT).
**Magic sentence:** "Hi! I'm Rosie. What's your name?"
**Coaches:** Boba (Taiwan) + Momo Mochi (Japan)
**Setting:** Rosie's kitchen, then a Taipei MRT train on a sunny Saturday morning

---

## The Story

Rosie wanted to ride the **捷運** (jiéyùn) = MRT train with her big sister, Tori. She also wanted to do something new. She wanted to make a friend on the train.

"But how?" asked Rosie. Her pink-striped straw drooped. "What's the first step?"

BOING! Boba bounced onto the kitchen table and landed right in the sugar bowl.

"Let's bounce!" he said. He was covered in sugar. "Oops. Now I'm a sugar pearl."

Momo Mochi squished in behind him. "I ride trains in Tokyo every day," she said. "In Japanese, a train is a *densha*. I know all the train manners!"

She counted on her stretchy fingers. "Use a quiet voice. Let people get off first. Don't stand in the doors. Offer your seat to someone who needs it more."

"And no eating or drinking on the Taipei MRT," said Boba.

Rosie gasped. "But I'm a drink!"

"You're a passenger," said Tori, smiling. "Passengers are allowed."

Boba climbed out of the sugar bowl. "Making a friend has steps," he said. "Here they are."

**Rosie's Steps:**
1. Find a kid who is not busy. (No headphones. Not sleeping.)
2. Smile and give a small wave.
3. Say the magic sentence in an inside voice: **"Hi! I'm Rosie. What's your name?"**
4. Listen to their name. Say it back: "Nice to meet you, ___!"
5. Ask one easy question or say one nice thing: "Where are you going?" or "I like your backpack!"
6. At your stop, say: "Bye! Have a nice day!"

"Six steps," said Rosie. "One step at a time, one sip at a time."

"Step one is important," said Momo. "If a kid has headphones on, they are listening to something. If a kid is sleeping, they are resting. That means they are busy. Let them be."

Boba put a teacup on his head like giant headphones. Then he snored. Loudly. "Am I busy?"

Rosie giggled. "Yes. Very busy."

Then it was time to practice. Momo sat on a kitchen chair and pretended to be a kid on a train. She swung her feet.

Rosie did her steps, one by one:
1. Find a kid who is not busy. (No headphones. Not sleeping.)
2. Smile and give a small wave.
3. Say the magic sentence in an inside voice: **"Hi! I'm Rosie. What's your name?"**
4. Listen to their name. Say it back: "Nice to meet you, ___!"
5. Ask one easy question or say one nice thing: "Where are you going?" or "I like your backpack!"
6. At your stop, say: "Bye! Have a nice day!"

"I'm Momo!" said Momo.

"Nice to meet you, Momo!" said Rosie.

They practiced three times. By the third time, Rosie's straw was wiggling.

On Saturday morning, Rosie and Tori went to the MRT station. Boba and Momo came too. They stood behind the yellow line. When the train came, they let people get off first. Then they stepped inside. Ding-dong went the doors.

Rosie sat down next to Tori. She looked around.

A big boy had headphones on. Busy. A grandma was sleeping. Busy. Boba pretended to snore. Momo gently squished his mouth shut.

Then Rosie saw a small round kid. She was a Sweet Potato Ball, golden and crispy, with a yellow backpack with a star on it. She was swinging her feet and looking out the window. Not busy.

Rosie smiled and gave a small wave. Then she said it in her inside voice.

**"Hi! I'm Rosie. What's your name?"**

The Sweet Potato Ball looked down at her shoes. She did not say anything.

Rosie felt nervous. Her straw drooped. That's okay.

Rosie did a Rose breath. Smell the rose: in, two, three, four. Blow out the candle: out, two, three, four.

She waited a few seconds. Some kids need more time. Then she tried one easy, nice thing.

"I like your backpack!"

The Sweet Potato Ball peeked up. "It has a star," she whispered. "I'm Guagua."

"Nice to meet you, Guagua!" said Rosie.

"Where are you going?"

Guagua smiled a little bigger. "To the **動物園** (dòngwùyuán) = zoo. I want to see the pandas."

"Pandas are my favorite!" said Rosie.

Behind them, Boba whispered, "SHE'S DOING IT!" It was not a quiet whisper. Momo squished him again.

Soon the train slowed down. Ding-dong. It was Rosie's stop.

"Bye! Have a nice day!" said Rosie.

"Bye, Rosie!" said Guagua. She waved a big wave.

Rosie stepped off the train with Tori. Her straw was wiggling so hard it went boing-boing-boing.

Tori gave her a big hug. "You've got this, little sip," she said. "And you did it!"

"I made a **朋友** (péngyǒu) = friend," said Rosie.

"Group squish!" said Momo. Everyone squished together. Boba got stuck in the middle and didn't mind one bit.

That night, Rosie snuggled into bed. She thought about Guagua and her star backpack. She thought about the pandas.

Next time, she would know the steps.

"One step at a time," Rosie whispered, "one sip at a time."

And she fell fast asleep.

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)
1. Find a kid who is not busy. (No headphones. Not sleeping.)
2. Smile and give a small wave.
3. Say the magic sentence in an inside voice: **"Hi! I'm Rosie. What's your name?"**
4. Listen to their name. Say it back: "Nice to meet you, ___!"
5. Ask one easy question or say one nice thing: "Where are you going?" or "I like your backpack!"
6. At your stop, say: "Bye! Have a nice day!"

**If something surprising happens:** Rose breath, then wait a few seconds and say: "I like your ___!"

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)
- Role-play on two chairs side by side like train seats. Take turns being Rosie and being "the kid." Swap in the child's own name for "Rosie."
- Practice the hiccup on purpose: as "the kid," look down and stay quiet. Praise the Rose breath, the waiting, and the backup "I like your ___!" — not just the happy ending.
- Play "Busy or Not Busy?": act out headphones, sleeping, reading, or looking out the window, and let your child say which kids to leave alone.
- On a real ride, your child stays right next to you, chats only on the train, and does not share where they live or go anywhere with the other kid. Praise the try, even if the other kid stays quiet.

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing
The train is sleeping in its station now.
The doors are closed. Ding-dong, goodnight.
Somewhere, a little friend is dreaming of pandas.
You found a kind hello today, one step at a time.
Smell the rose. Blow out the candle.
Goodnight, little sip.
