# 27 — Rosie Saves for the Little Kite

**Life skill:** Choose a purchase within an agreed budget and save toward a goal.
**Magic sentence:** "Is this within my spending money?"
**Coaches:** Feng 🇹🇼 + Don Café 🇨🇷
**Setting:** the spare room suitcase shelf (practice), then the hilltop kite shop (real outing).

---

## The Story

Rosie wanted a little kite. Mom helped her choose spending money for their outing.

At the suitcase shelf, she sorted toy coins beside a kite picture, keeping the real pouch with Mom.

Don Café studied the picture. "A coffee bean in a kite shop. Finally, a truly uplifting afternoon."

Feng arrived, pineapple cake in fancy wrapping.

Don Café arrived, a wise coffee bean.

Cat the penguin napped on a cushioned open suitcase.

Feng checked a pretend price with Don Café. **"Is this within my spending money?"** He kept his savings aside.

"You can save instead of buying today," Don Café explained. "Both are choices."

**Rosie's Steps**

1. Check your money with your grown-up.
2. Remember what you want to save for.
3. Ask the price before choosing.

4. Choose only what you can afford.
5. Keep the remaining money safely.

**練習** (liànxí) = practice. Rosie tried **"Is this within my spending money?"** Don Café pretended.

2. Remember what you want to save for.
3. Ask the price before choosing.

Rosie checked a ribbon's pretend price, then compared buying it with saving for her kite.

Feng wrapped one toy coin in tissue. "Beautifully dressed savings. Unfortunately, the ribbon still costs a ribbon's price."

Mom brought Rosie to the hilltop kite shop, where bamboo frames hung above spools of brightly colored string.

Cat settled onto a folded carry-bag, appearing to test whether a kite's luggage could carry a penguin nap.

Rosie spotted a little kite, then a much bigger dragon kite with a wonderfully long scarlet tail.

Step one: she checked her money with Mom. Step two: she remembered her little-kite saving goal.

Step three: Rosie asked the price before choosing. **"Is this within my spending money?"** she asked Mom.

Mom helped compare the prices. The little kite fit her plan; the large dragon cost more than her money.

Rosie liked the dragon's tail very much. Her straw drooped, and she felt disappointed about its price.

That's okay. She could enjoy looking at the dragon without turning her wish into an immediate purchase.

Rosie took a Rose breath.

Smell the rose: in, two, three, four.

Blow out the candle: out, two, three, four.

"Can we make a saving plan for that?" Rosie asked, leaving the dragon hanging safely on its display hook.

Mom helped her think about saving longer. Rosie decided she still wanted the little kite for today's outing.

Step four: Rosie chose the little kite within her budget. Mom helped her pay and check the change.

Step five: she tucked the remaining money safely into her pouch and gave it to Mom to carry.

Don Café admired the kite. Feng examined its wrapping. "A small purchase can still travel in excellent style."

Cat peeked from the carry-bag. "I am saving energy for later. So far, my budget is doing beautifully."

Rosie giggled, holding the kite spool close. Her little kite had plenty of sky ahead of it.

Tori met them beside the shop's wind spinners, admiring Rosie's kite and its brightly fluttering tail.

"You checked before choosing. You've got this, little sip," she said, looking at the neatly closed coin pouch.

Rosie felt proud. The dragon could stay a future wish while her little kite flew today.

**加油** (jiāyóu) = go for it.

"One step at a time, one sip at a time," said Rosie.

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)

1. Check your money with your grown-up.
2. Remember what you want to save for.
3. Ask the price before choosing.
4. Choose only what you can afford.
5. Keep the remaining money safely.

**If something surprising happens:** Take a Rose breath or a comfortable pause, then say: "Can we make a saving plan for that?"

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)

- Role-play the situation using a little coin pouch or a safe pretend substitute; read the five steps together.
- Swap roles so your child can see you model **"Is this within my spending money?"** and hear the other person's answer.
- Introduce this story's small surprise; practice "Can we make a saving plan for that?" and allow a comfortable breath or pause.
- On a real outing, stay nearby, offer choices, and praise the specific step your child tried.

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing

Goodnight, little kite and safely tucked coins.
Goodnight, wishes with room to wait.
Goodnight, Cat, saving your energy.
May careful choices leave room for joy.
