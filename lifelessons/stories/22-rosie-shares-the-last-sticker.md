# 22 — Rosie Shares the Last Sticker

**Life skill:** Share a limited resource through an agreed fair choice.
**Magic sentence:** "How can we share this fairly?"
**Coaches:** Feng 🇹🇼 + Neko-Pan 🇯🇵
**Setting:** the mailroom sorting table (practice), then the postcard kiosk at the ferry terminal (real outing).

---

## The Story

Rosie and Lulu were making postcards, and both wanted a shiny shell sticker for the corner.

At the mailroom table, Rosie imagined a whole ocean of stickers. The sample tray contained exactly one.

Feng inspected an envelope. "Beautiful wrapping," he announced, then realized he was complimenting somebody else's recycling.

Feng arrived, pineapple cake in fancy wrapping.

Neko-Pan was a cream bun waving cheerfully.

Cat the penguin napped on a padded parcel basket.

Neko asked Feng, **"How can we share this fairly?"** They checked the tray and chose different decorations.

"Fair does not always mean identical," Feng explained. "Both people need a real chance to have a say."

**Rosie's Steps**

1. Check what is available.
2. Ask what each person wants.
3. Suggest a fair choice.

4. Agree before taking anything.
5. Follow the agreement together.

**分享** (fēnxiǎng) = sharing. Rosie tried **"How can we share this fairly?"** Neko-Pan pretended.

1. Check what is available.
2. Ask what each person wants.
3. Suggest a fair choice.

4. Agree before taking anything.
5. Follow the agreement together.

Rosie practiced asking what Lulu wanted, then choosing between a sticker and a handmade drawing for each card.

Neko drew a shell shaped suspiciously like a bun. "Art can have filling," he said, waving his paw.

Mom brought Rosie to the postcard kiosk, where shell stamps sat beside blue postcards and coils of twine.

Cat nestled into a spare packing-cloth cushion, apparently awaiting delivery to the nearest comfortable sofa.

Lulu held her postcard beneath a bright shell sticker. Rosie reached toward the tray, then paused.

Step one: Rosie checked. One shiny shell remained, but several fish stickers and drawing pencils were available.

Step two: she asked Lulu what she wanted. "The shell," said Lulu. Rosie wanted that one too.

Step three: **"How can we share this fairly?"** Rosie suggested a draw, or choosing different decorations together.

Lulu did not want a draw. "I might feel upset if I lose," she explained quietly.

Rosie felt stuck, and her straw drooped. That's okay; their first suggestion was still only a suggestion.

Rosie took a Rose breath.

Smell the rose: in, two, three, four.

Blow out the candle: out, two, three, four.

"What other idea would work for both of us?" Rosie asked, looking at the pencils beside the tray.

Lulu suggested putting the shell on a shared postcard for Tori, then drawing shells on their own cards.

Step four: Rosie agreed. They checked that both really wanted the shared card before peeling the sticker.

Step five: Lulu held the postcard flat, Rosie placed the shell, and they signed it with Mom's help.

Feng admired their decorated corners. Neko's drawn shell acquired whiskers. "A rare sea bun," he explained proudly.

Cat opened an eye when the counter bell chimed. "Please mark my parcel fragile. My nap is delicate."

Lulu giggled, and Rosie held up their shared card. One little shell had found a home they both liked.

Tori met them at the terminal's model ferry display, where Rosie and Lulu presented the shared postcard.

"You made room for both voices. You've got this, little sip," Tori said, keeping the shell in view.

Rosie felt proud. They had shared the deciding as carefully as they had shared their last shiny sticker.

**加油** (jiāyóu) = go for it.

"One step at a time, one sip at a time," said Rosie.

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)

1. Check what is available.
2. Ask what each person wants.
3. Suggest a fair choice.
4. Agree before taking anything.
5. Follow the agreement together.

**If something surprising happens:** Take a Rose breath or a comfortable pause, then say: "What other idea would work for both of us?"

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)

- Role-play the situation using a shiny shell sticker or a safe pretend substitute; read the five steps together.
- Swap roles so your child can see you model **"How can we share this fairly?"** and hear the other person's answer.
- Introduce this story's small surprise; practice "What other idea would work for both of us?" and allow a comfortable breath or pause.
- On a real outing, stay nearby, offer choices, and praise the specific step your child tried.

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing

Goodnight, shiny shell and generous little voices.
Goodnight, postcards, carrying care across the water.
Goodnight, Cat, our delicate parcel.
May sharing leave room for everyone.
