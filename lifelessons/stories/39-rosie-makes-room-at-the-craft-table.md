# 39 — Rosie Makes Room at the Craft Table

**Life skill:** Include a newcomer by offering an accessible space and a real choice.
**Magic sentence:** "Would you like a place beside us?"
**Coaches:** Mochi 🇹🇼 + Flan 🇨🇷
**Setting:** the spare dining alcove puzzle table (practice), then the community paper-flower fair (real outing).

---

## The Story

Rosie was making paper flowers at a fair and wanted their table to feel welcoming.

At the puzzle table, Flan tried sitting between two stools, wobbling as if considering both at once.

"Room matters," Mochi said, stretching gently. "A warm invitation also needs somewhere comfortable for its guest."

Mochi was a white mochi stretching gently.

Flan arrived, coconut custard wobbling hopefully.

Cat the penguin napped on a spare stool cushion.

Mochi noticed Flan standing nearby. **"Would you like a place beside us?"** He checked the spare stool.

"Invite without deciding for somebody," Flan explained. "A person may want to join, watch, or choose something else."

**Rosie's Steps**

1. Notice who is looking for a place.
2. Ask if they want to join.
3. Check that there is room.

4. Offer an available way to take part.
5. Respect their answer.

**歡迎** (huānyíng) = welcome. Rosie tried **"Would you like a place beside us?"** Flan pretended.

1. Notice who is looking for a place.
2. Ask if they want to join.
3. Check that there is room.

4. Offer an available way to take part.

Rosie practiced offering a clear space, then letting her pretend guest choose between placing petals and watching.

Mochi folded his arms. "My elbows have booked one seat today. An improvement for everybody."

Mom brought Rosie to the paper-flower fair, where petals waited in trays beside low tables and spare cushions.

Cat napped beside the supplies on a soft cushion, resembling the fair's only flower that preferred horizontal petals.

Lulu looked toward the table. Rosie put her supplies close to her cup, leaving a clear space.

Step one: Rosie noticed Lulu looking for a place. Step two: **"Would you like a place beside us?"**

"Maybe," said Lulu. Step three: Rosie checked the space, moving her own tray back to its proper spot.

Step four: she offered a spare cushion and a choice between gluing petals, arranging colors, or watching.

Lulu wanted to watch first. Rosie had imagined both of them making flowers straight away at the table.

Rosie felt uncertain. Her straw drooped. That's okay; joining could begin with looking rather than making.

Rosie took a Rose breath.

Smell the rose: in, two, three, four.

Blow out the candle: out, two, three, four.

"You can watch until you're ready," Rosie said, keeping the spare space available without pushing the glue toward her.

Lulu sat comfortably and watched Rosie fold one petal. Mom stayed nearby while they explored the little craft choices.

Step five: Rosie respected Lulu's answer, continuing her own flower while Lulu watched the folds and colors.

After a while, Lulu chose to arrange blue petals. Rosie offered the tray and let Lulu decide her pattern.

Flan wobbled happily. Mochi folded his elbows. "Enough room for friendship and remarkably well-behaved mochi arms."

Cat opened an eye beside the supplies. "I have joined the flower fair as an expert in soft centers."

Lulu smiled, arranging her blue petals. Rosie felt pleased that their table could welcome different ways of taking part.

Tori met Rosie beneath the fair's arch of giant paper leaves, admiring the flower Rosie had made.

"You offered room and a choice. You've got this, little sip," she said, looking at Lulu's blue petals.

Rosie felt proud. A welcome could offer space without making everyone begin the same way.

**加油** (jiāyóu) = go for it.

"One step at a time, one sip at a time," said Rosie.

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)

1. Notice who is looking for a place.
2. Ask if they want to join.
3. Check that there is room.
4. Offer an available way to take part.
5. Respect their answer.

**If something surprising happens:** Take a Rose breath or a comfortable pause, then say: "You can watch until you're ready."

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)

- Role-play the situation using a folded paper flower or a safe pretend substitute; read the five steps together.
- Swap roles so your child can see you model **"Would you like a place beside us?"** and hear the other person's answer.
- Introduce this story's small surprise; practice "You can watch until you're ready." and allow a comfortable breath or pause.
- On a real outing, stay nearby, offer choices, and praise the specific step your child tried.

## 🌙 Goodnight Blessing

Goodnight, welcoming table and folded paper flowers.
Goodnight, choices with room to grow.
Goodnight, Cat, our soft center.
May you find a place that welcomes you.
