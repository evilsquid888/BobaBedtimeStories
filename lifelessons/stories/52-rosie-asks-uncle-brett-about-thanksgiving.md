# 52 — Rosie Asks Uncle Brett About Thanksgiving

**Life skill:** Ask a grown-up for a favor, then ask for help to make it happen.
**Magic sentence:** "Uncle Brett, I have a favor to ask. Could Armin come to Thanksgiving?"
**Coaches:** Dai Daruma 🇯🇵 + Hóng Dòu 🇹🇼
**Cat’s role:** practical helper (snowboard laid flat as a dog ramp, carries the four-bowl stack)
**Friends:** Uncle Brett (cider thermos) and Aunty Ethel (pumpkin pie), Armin and Miso (Shiba Inus), Zeke (sea lion), Cole (cable car)
**Setting:** the hallway phone nook under the stairs (practice), then Uncle Brett's leaf-strewn front porch and yard (real outing).

---

## The Story

Rosie had a November wish. She wanted Uncle Brett's dog Armin to come to **感恩节** (Gǎn'ēn jié) = Thanksgiving.

Asking Uncle Brett felt enormous. Rosie's straw drooped. That's okay.

Dai Daruma rocked in. "Fall down seven, bounce up eight! A wobbly ask still counts."

Hóng Dòu shuffled in with her ladle. "Everyone needs a little sweetness. I have fed forty cousins."

Cat the penguin laid her snowboard flat, a practice ramp for a practice dog.

"A favor has steps," Dai said. "Grown-ups like to know what, and why."

**Rosie's Steps**

1. Pick a calm moment.
2. Say their name and look at their face.
3. Say, "I have a favor to ask."
4. Say what you want and why, in one breath.
5. Offer to help with it.
6. Wait for the answer and say thank you either way.

"Sometimes the answer is no," Hóng Dòu said. "That is about the day, not you."

Dai modeled it. **"Uncle Brett, I have a favor to ask. Could Armin come to Thanksgiving?"**

Hóng Dòu held the block phone and played Uncle Brett.

Her voice went very deep. Her ladle did not.

Rosie practiced, **"Uncle Brett, I have a favor to ask. Could Armin come to Thanksgiving?"**

1. Pick a calm moment.
2. Say their name and look at their face.
3. Say, "I have a favor to ask."
4. Say what you want and why, in one breath.
5. Offer to help with it.
6. Wait for the answer and say thank you either way.

At Uncle Brett's porch, red leaves drifted over the pumpkins.

Cat laid her snowboard flat on the steps. "Dog ramp. Also penguin ramp. Mostly penguin ramp."

Uncle Brett was raking. Armin's curled tail waved by the dog bowl.

Step one: Rosie waited until Uncle Brett set down the rake.

Step two: she hopped onto the step and looked up at his face.

Step three and four: **"Uncle Brett, I have a favor to ask. Could Armin come to Thanksgiving?"**

"He makes everyone laugh." Step five: "I can fill his bowl and hold his leash."

Uncle Brett chuckled. "Yes! And Aunty Ethel is bringing Miso, and Miso's friends Zeke and Cole."

Zeke was a sea lion. Cole was a cable car. Rosie had planned for one **狗** (gǒu) = dog.

Four guests! Her straw drooped all the way down. Rosie felt worried. That's okay.

Rosie took a Rose breath.

Smell the rose: in, two, three, four.

Blow out the candle: out, two, three, four.

"Can you help me get ready for four?" Asking for **帮忙** (bāngmáng) = help is a step too.

"Of course," said Uncle Brett. They planned four bowls, a leash hook, a wide spot for Cole.

Aunty Ethel arrived. Miso trotted, Zeke flopped, and Cole rang his bell up the path.

Step six: "Thank you, Uncle Brett. Thank you, Aunty Ethel."

Cat carried a wobbling stack of four bowls. "Four bowls, one penguin. Dramatic balance."

Armin sniffed her scarf. Miso sniffed her feet. Zeke clapped. Cole rang his bell.

Hóng Dòu measured the leash hook. "One ladle high. Grandmother approved."

Dai rocked by the pumpkins. "Four guests, zero falls. A personal record."

Tori popped up from a pile of red leaves on the porch swing.

"You asked for a favor and for help. You've got this, little sip!"

Rosie's straw wiggled high. Four guests, and she had asked for every one.

"One step at a time, one sip at a time," said Rosie.

## 🌹 Rosie's Steps (Recap)

1. Pick a calm moment.
2. Say their name and look at their face.
3. Say, "I have a favor to ask."
4. Say what you want and why, in one breath.
5. Offer to help with it.
6. Wait for the answer and say thank you either way.

**If something surprising happens:** Rose breath, then say: "Can you help me get ready for four?"

## 💬 Practice Together (for grown-ups)

- Role-play the ask with a pretend phone or a kitchen chair as the grown-up; read the six steps together, and let your child pick the calm moment.
- Swap roles so your child can see you model **"Uncle Brett, I have a favor to ask. Could Armin come to Thanksgiving?"** and hear a warm yes, and once, a kind no.
- Add this story's surprise (more than you planned for); practice "Can you help me get ready for four?" and make a short plan together.
- Before a real ask, praise the specific step your child tried, especially saying thank you either way.

May every favor you ask be met with kindness.
