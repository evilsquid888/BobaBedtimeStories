# 🎄 24 Days of Xmas — Roadmap

One five-minute video story for each day of December 1–24, plus a Thanksgiving prologue. The family (Rosie, Cat, Tori, Mom) spends December at Frostpeak Lodge, Cat's home valley, with the Penguinsnowboard cast. Each morning one door on the Door Wall opens; each dusk one more paper lantern is hung on Lantern Lane. On the 24th the lane is full of light for SpicySquid's birthday.

Series rules are in [../xmas/README.md](../xmas/README.md); characters in [../xmas/CHARACTER_GUIDE.md](../xmas/CHARACTER_GUIDE.md). Dates are for December 2026.

**The Market Train.** Four days (9, 12, 17, 18) are day trips to the Christmas markets of Montreal and Quebec City. Behind those doors is a tiny golden train ticket; the snow train runs south for the day and is home by nightfall, so the lantern still goes up on Lantern Lane.

**Style.** Fun holiday happenings and warmth. These are not lesson stories: no morals said out loud, no steps, no Rose breath, no named-feeling formula. When something goes wrong, it goes wrong funnily.

Each brief gives: the door (what is behind it), the new place, the guests, the shape of the story, the warm note (what the day feels like at heart; shown, never said), and the lantern.

---

## 00 — The Thanksgiving Double Birthday ✅
Thanksgiving, Yeh Yeh's birthday, and Cat's birthday at one table. The one long long-life noodle is carried down the hall by everybody; it snags; Rosie freezes the line, breathes, and they step together. Cat wishes her family could see Frostpeak. Piper slides in with the invitation and one folded paper lantern.

## 01 — The First Door · Tuesday, December 1
- **File:** `01-the-first-door`
- **Door:** a tiny brass lantern hook. **Place:** Frostpeak Station platform, then the lodge great hall (the Door Wall, first seen), then Lantern Lane at night.
- **Guests:** Herschel, Piper, SpicySquid. **Friends:** Kyle.
- **Story:** The snow train puffs north past the lights of Montreal and the castle roofs of Quebec City, and arrives. Everything is big, white, and new; Rosie's straw droops. Cat belly-slides off the train: home. Herschel shows the Door Wall and its rule: one door a day. (Some doors hold a tiny golden train ticket; on those days the Market Train goes to the city markets.) Rosie opens door one. At dusk they hang lantern one beside the Squid Grill, the warmest stall on the lane. Herschel whispers the plan: a lane full of light by the 24th, a surprise for SpicySquid's birthday. SpicySquid hands Kyle a grilled corn and suspects nothing.
- **Warm note:** A new place feels smaller once you find one warm light.
- **Lantern:** red, with a plain little door on it.

## 02 — Rosie's First Snowboard Lesson · Wednesday, December 2
- **File:** `02-rosies-first-snowboard-lesson`
- **Door:** a tiny purple snowboard. **Place:** the Bunny Bump, the beginner slope.
- **Guests:** Willa, Finnegan. **Friends:** Kyle (becomes a snowball).
- **Story:** Cat teaches: bend, look where you want to go, arms out. Finnegan demonstrates how not to stop. Rosie plops down in soft snow three times. Willa: the smallest step is still a step. Fourth try: three whole seconds of gliding. Tori cheers from the chairlift.
- **Warm note:** Falling down is part of learning. Get up one more time. **Lantern:** purple.

## 03 — The Wonky Tree · Thursday, December 3
- **File:** `03-the-wonky-tree`
- **Door:** a tiny pinecone. **Place:** the Whispering Pines tree grove, then the great hall.
- **Guests:** Bjorn, Magnus. **Friends:** Kyle, Lina.
- **Story:** Everyone wants the tallest, straightest tree. Rosie finds a small crooked one nobody picked and likes it at once. Magnus grumbles and carries it: his one noble act for the week. Bjorn descends with style into a snowbank. Decorated, the tree's bent top turns out to be the perfect hook for the lantern. (Day 23 calls back to this bent top.)
- **Warm note:** Nobody has to be perfect to be chosen. **Lantern:** green.

## 04 — The Helper Candle · Friday, December 4 · Hanukkah, first night
- **File:** `04-the-helper-candle`
- **Door:** a tiny candle. **Place:** the window nook of Herschel's cocoa kitchen at nightfall.
- **Guests:** Herschel, Noa. **Friends:** Gabby.
- **Story:** Herschel and Noa welcome the family to the first night of **光明節** (Guāngmíng jié) = Hanukkah. Nine holders: eight nights and the shamash, the helper candle. Rosie worries a helper who gives its light away will run out. Herschel lights the helper; the helper lights the first candle; both burn just as bright. Gabby's smile gleams in the candlelight and startles Noa, then they are friends. The day's lantern is lit from another lantern the same way.
- **Warm note:** A light that is shared is never spent. **Lantern:** blue.

## 05 — Boots by the Door · Saturday, December 5 · St. Nicholas Eve
- **File:** `05-boots-by-the-door`
- **Door:** a tiny boot. **Place:** the lodge boot room and its snowy porch.
- **Guests:** Teddy, Tilda, Nora. **Friends:** Gabby.
- **Story:** Tonight everyone leaves a boot by the door. Rosie has no boots; she sets out her tiny knitted cup cozy. Gabby has no feet at all, and goes quiet. Rosie notices and makes her a boot from a spare mitten. The twins plan to stay up and peek, and end up tiptoeing around filling everyone else's boots with oranges and walnuts instead. "We didn't mean to— / —but it was worth it."
- **Warm note:** The best surprises are the ones you give. **Lantern:** orange.

## 06 — Latkes and Scallion Pancakes · Sunday, December 6 · Hanukkah, third night
- **File:** `06-latkes-and-scallion-pancakes`
- **Door:** a tiny frying pan. **Place:** two griddles side by side at the Squid Grill on Lantern Lane.
- **Guests:** Noa, Barnaby, SpicySquid. **Friends:** Kyle.
- **Story:** (One line: the boots were full this morning.) Noa is frying latkes; Mom is frying **蔥油餅** (cōngyóubǐng) = scallion pancakes. Both crispy, both fried in oil, both better shared. The line of hungry penguins gets long; each batter runs low. Rosie's idea: mix what is left. The scallion latke is born. Barnaby: always enough for one more. Kyle taste-tests responsibly (one).
- **Warm note:** Two recipes at one table make a bigger feast. **Lantern:** gold.

## 07 — No Two Snowflakes · Monday, December 7
- **File:** `07-no-two-snowflakes`
- **Door:** a folded paper snowflake. **Place:** the lodge craft loft with its tall frosted windows.
- **Guests:** Cleo Coldwater. **Friends:** Lina (leads), Gabby.
- **Story:** Paper-snowflake day. Rosie tries to copy Tori's perfect one; hers comes out lopsided, and she hides it. Lina: "I am a snowflake crisp. No two alike. That is the whole point." Cleo holds Rosie's up to the window, where it throws the best shadow of all. The window fills with one of everybody's.
- **Warm note:** Yours is supposed to be different. **Lantern:** white, cut like a snowflake.

## 08 — The Great Dreidel Spin · Tuesday, December 8 · Hanukkah, fifth night
- **File:** `08-the-great-dreidel-spin`
- **Door:** a tiny wooden spinning top. **Place:** the frozen pond, with a low table on the ice.
- **Guests:** Noa, Finnegan, Nibbles, Splash.
- **Story:** Noa teaches the dreidel: four sides, four carved shapes (take nothing, take half, take all, put one in), and a pot of chocolate coins. Finnegan spins himself across the pond. Rosie wins the whole pot, and the game stops: nobody else has coins. She shares them out so the game can go on. The seals clap.
- **Warm note:** Winning is more fun when the game keeps going. **Lantern:** silver.

## 09 — A Picture for Yeh Yeh · Wednesday, December 9 · Market Train to Montreal
- **File:** `09-a-picture-for-yeh-yeh`
- **Door:** a tiny golden train ticket, the first one. **Place:** the big Christmas market in downtown Montreal: wooden chalet stalls, a giant fir, a wood-fired bagel oven, a bright red mailbox.
- **Guests:** Sésame the Montreal bagel (new), Whistlewick (the train's mail fox). **Friends:** Lina.
- **Story:** Rosie misses Yeh Yeh. In Montreal, Sésame bounces out of the bagel-oven stall: "Bonjour-hi!" Rosie draws Yeh Yeh a picture postcard: the market, the giant tree, a bagel in a hat. The mail goes at noon; the card slips away under the chalets; Rosie asks for help. One sharp whistle, one fast fox, one bagel rolling like a wheel, and the card makes the red mailbox. Tori draws one too: a potato swan.
- **Warm note:** When you miss someone, send it. **Lantern:** pink, carried home on the train.

## 10 — The Gingerbread Night Market · Thursday, December 10
- **File:** `10-the-gingerbread-night-market`
- **Door:** a tiny gingerbread roof tile. **Place:** the long floury table of the lodge bakery.
- **Guests:** Barnaby, Teddy, Tilda. **Friends:** Kyle, Lina.
- **Story:** They build Lantern Lane in gingerbread, with Lina's sugar snow on every roof. Kyle taste-tests one wall too many and the whole lane falls down. He is very sorry, and very full. Everybody pitches in and they rebuild it smaller and sturdier, with a gumdrop for every lantern so far.
- **Warm note:** Sorry, plus help, fixes most things. **Lantern:** brown with white icing lines.

## 11 — Eight Lights · Friday, December 11 · Hanukkah, eighth night
- **File:** `11-eight-lights`
- **Door:** eight tiny brass dots. **Place:** the great window of the lodge at night, looking over the whole valley.
- **Guests:** Herschel, Noa, Magnus.
- **Story:** The last night: all eight candles and the helper. Herschel tells it in three lines: a little oil, enough for one night, that lasted eight. Magnus says the dark never bothered him. Then, one by one, every window in the valley puts out a light to answer the lodge, and the last one to appear is Magnus's cabin. Rosie counts: a little can last a long time.
- **Warm note:** A little can last a long time when you tend it. **Lantern:** deep blue with eight dots.

## 12 — The Cocoa Cup · Saturday, December 12 · Market Train to Quebec City
- **File:** `12-the-cocoa-cup`
- **Door:** a golden train ticket with a tiny tin trophy cup. **Place:** the old wooden toboggan slide on Dufferin Terrace in Old Quebec, under the copper-roofed castle hotel, above the frozen St. Lawrence.
- **Guests:** Aurora, Harfang the snowy owl (new), Margot the maple taffy (new). **Friends:** Gabby, Kyle.
- **Story:** The big toboggan race: last one down buys cocoa. Harfang starts it with one slow blink. Gabby snow-swims her lane and is winning by a mile. Rosie's toboggan tips at the halfway bump. Gabby turns around. They cross the line last, together, and buy the cocoa with great ceremony. Margot rolls everyone a maple taffy on the snow; Kyle taste-tests the snow.
- **Warm note:** Finishing together beats finishing first. **Lantern:** yellow.

## 13 — The Crown of Lights · Sunday, December 13 · St. Lucia Day
- **File:** `13-the-crown-of-lights`
- **Door:** a tiny crown. **Place:** the long dark staircase of the lodge before sunrise, then the breakfast hall.
- **Guests:** Nora, Willa. **Friends:** Lina.
- **Story:** A big-sister story. Tori, the eldest, wears the crown of little lamp-candles and leads the morning walk with buns for everyone. Rosie carries the tray and is afraid of the dark stairs. Tori: "Walk in my light." Halfway down, Tori's crown slips; Rosie steadies it without spilling a bun. By the bottom Rosie is carrying a little lamp of her own.
- **Warm note:** In the dark, walk in someone's light until you have your own. **Lantern:** cream with a gold crown.

## 14 — Under the Ice · Monday, December 14
- **File:** `14-under-the-ice`
- **Door:** a tiny spiral shell. **Place:** the ice-fishing huts on Glacier Lake, and the blue world under the ice.
- **Guests:** Captain Prism, Barnaby, Nibbles, Splash. **Friends:** Gabby (leads).
- **Story:** Barnaby's snack satchel has gone down the fishing hole. Nobody can fetch it but Gabby. Under the ice she meets Captain Prism, who delivers a monologue; she is shy about her teeth; he says his tusk is one very long tooth and he has never hidden it once. She brings up the satchel and something better: she smiles all the way. Up top, everyone waits around the hole, holding the rope and their breath.
- **Warm note:** Everyone has a place where they shine. Cheer when it is a friend's turn. **Lantern:** sea green.

## 15 — Snowed In · Tuesday, December 15
- **File:** `15-snowed-in`
- **Door:** a tiny folded blanket. **Place:** the lodge library by the fire, in a blizzard.
- **Guests:** Nora, Magnus. **Friends:** Kyle, Lina.
- **Story:** The sleigh ride is cancelled. Rosie had it on her list and her straw droops. Nora: "Sit. Cocoa first." Then the best indoor day ever: they build a blanket fort; Magnus is recruited as a tent pole and tells shadow-puppet stories against his will. The lantern is hung indoors tonight, in the fort.
- **Warm note:** A changed plan can still be a good day. **Lantern:** soft grey with a white swirl.

## 16 — Is There Room? · Wednesday, December 16 · Las Posadas begins
- **File:** `16-is-there-room`
- **Door:** a tiny door with a smaller door in it. **Place:** Cabin Row at dusk, door after glowing door.
- **Guests:** Tico, Gallo, Pinto (just off the mail-boat, and freezing), Herschel.
- **Story:** The Costa Rica friends teach the posada: go door to door and ask, "Is there room?" and the last door says yes. It is a game until their own guest cabin turns out to be buried in a snowdrift. Rosie does not wait to be asked. "There's room with us." One bunk room, eight friends, one toucan's beak sticking out of the quilt.
- **Warm note:** Make room. **Lantern:** turquoise.

## 17 — Every Size of Voice · Thursday, December 17 · Market Train to Montreal
- **File:** `17-every-size-of-voice`
- **Door:** a golden train ticket with a tiny bell. **Place:** the little bandstand at the Old Port of Montreal, beside the skating rink and the giant Ferris wheel.
- **Guests:** Cleo Coldwater (conducting), Finnegan (too loud), Sésame, Couic the cheese curd (new; one squeak is his whole voice). **Friends:** Kyle (a bass growl).
- **Story:** Carol practice for the market sing-along: "Vive le vent", which is Jingle Bells in French. Rosie thinks her voice is too small to count and decides to hold the bell instead. Then she finds Couic hiding behind the poutine stand for the same reason. Cleo gives every voice a part: the rumble, the trumpet, the bounce, the bell. The song has two small gaps in it. Rosie fills one. Couic squeaks the very last note, and that is the one everybody cheers.
- **Warm note:** A choir needs every size of voice. **Lantern:** bell-shaped, brass yellow.

## 18 — The Made-by-Me Workshop · Friday, December 18 · Market Train to Quebec City
- **File:** `18-the-made-by-me-workshop`
- **Door:** a golden train ticket with a tiny spool of ribbon. **Place:** the wood-carver's chalet at the German Christmas market in Old Quebec.
- **Guests:** Magnus (grumbling, knows wood), Harfang.
- **Story:** Rosie wants to give Tori a present. Every beautiful thing at the market costs coins and she has none left. Harfang leads her to the wood-carver's chalet, where you make instead of buy; Magnus shows her how to sand, his one noble act for the week. Rosie sands a little pine straw-rest. Blue paint tips over; she turns the spill into a wave pattern. Tori nearly walks in; Cat blocks the door with a hip-hop routine. The present is wrapped and hidden until the 24th.
- **Warm note:** Made-by-me is worth more than bought. **Lantern:** pine green with a ribbon.

## 19 — Boba on Ice · Saturday, December 19
- **File:** `19-boba-on-ice`
- **Door:** a tiny glossy pearl. **Place:** the *Boba on Ice* stand on Lantern Lane.
- **Guests:** Boba, Bīng, Hóng Dòu, SpicySquid.
- **Story:** The Taiwan crew arrives to open their winter stand. Now Rosie is the one who knows the way: she gives the tour (the Door Wall, the Bunny Bump, the lane) with a list, of course. Bīng is thrilled to finally be somewhere he does not melt: "More is more!" and builds a shaved-ice mountain taller than the stall. Hóng Dòu ladles hot **紅豆湯** (hóngdòu tāng) = red bean soup. SpicySquid nearly spots the lantern count; Boba bounces a distraction.
- **Warm note:** Once you know a place, you can welcome someone else. **Lantern:** milk-tea tan with dark dots.

## 20 — Yeh Yeh on the Snow Train · Sunday, December 20
- **File:** `20-yeh-yeh-on-the-snow-train`
- **Door:** a tiny teapot. **Place:** the snowy path from Frostpeak Station to the lodge.
- **Guests:** Piper, Willa. **Friends:** Yeh Yeh arrives.
- **Story:** Rosie's postcard from Montreal reached him. Yeh Yeh steps off the snow train, tap, tap, tap. Piper offers to rocket him to the lodge by sled. He prefers to walk: 慢慢來. Everyone fidgets; Rosie matches his pace. At that speed they see what the fast ones miss: fox tracks, a frozen waterfall, their own breath. His steam freezes into tiny clouds that Cat collects.
- **Warm note:** Go at the pace of the slowest, and you will see more. **Lantern:** teapot brown.

## 21 — The Longest Night · Monday, December 21 · Dōngzhì 冬至, the winter solstice
- **File:** `21-the-longest-night`
- **Door:** a tiny bowl with round white dots. **Place:** the hearth kitchen of the lodge.
- **Guests:** Herschel, Nora, Barnaby. **Friends:** Yeh Yeh, Kyle.
- **Story:** The longest night of the year. Yeh Yeh teaches everyone to roll **湯圓** (tāngyuán), round for **團圓** (tuányuán) = being together. The penguins roll theirs the size of snowballs. Rosie's come out as eggs; Yeh Yeh says his first hundred did too. Eat a bowl and you are one year older; Kyle asks how many bowls make him a grown-up. Yeh Yeh shares the good news hidden in the longest night: tomorrow is a little brighter.
- **Warm note:** The longest night is the turning point. **Lantern:** round and white.

## 22 — Gabby and the Sky Full of Stars · Tuesday, December 22
- **File:** `22-gabby-and-the-sky-full-of-stars`
- **Door:** a tiny silver star. **Place:** Aurora Ridge lookout at night.
- **Guests:** Aurora, Cleo Coldwater. **Friends:** Gabby (leads), Lina.
- **Story:** Why is Gabby called a star shark? She has always thought her spots were odd. On the ridge at night the northern lights come out, and Cleo notices that the stars overhead match the spots on Gabby's back, one for one. Everyone picks a star to be theirs; Rosie picks a small pink one. Gabby stops tucking her fins.
- **Warm note:** The thing that makes you different is the thing that shines. **Lantern:** midnight black with white stars.

## 23 — The Lantern That Flew Away · Wednesday, December 23
- **File:** `23-the-lantern-that-flew-away`
- **Door:** a tiny paper kite. **Place:** the rooftops of Lantern Lane and Windy Pass.
- **Guests:** Piper, Willa, Whistlewick, Bjorn. **Friends:** Kyle.
- **Story:** One day to go. A gust lifts lantern twenty-three off its hook and away over the roofs. Rosie is sure the surprise is ruined. Then the rescue run: Whistlewick scouts, Piper leaps, Willa takes the smallest step along the narrowest ledge, Kyle is the anchor. They find it caught on the bent top of the wonky tree from Day 3.
- **Warm note:** When something goes wrong at the last minute, ask for help and go together. **Lantern:** sky blue, a little crumpled.

## 24 — SpicySquid's Birthday Lights · Thursday, December 24 · Christmas Eve
- **File:** `24-spicysquids-birthday-lights`
- **Door:** the biggest door: one birthday candle. **Place:** Lantern Lane at night, all twenty-four lanterns.
- **Guests:** SpicySquid, Herschel, Piper. **Friends:** Yeh Yeh, Lina, Gabby, Kyle. **Off the Market Train:** Sésame, Couic, Harfang, Margot (one group still).
- **Story:** SpicySquid is grilling for the whole valley, as always, and has not sat down since the first of December. At dusk Rosie gives the signal and all twenty-four lanterns light at once. Everyone has cooked for him: latkes, scallion pancakes, tangyuan, a pie with one slice missing, and one very long long-life noodle that nobody bites, Kyle. Rosie gives Tori the pine straw-rest she made in Quebec. He sits. He is speechless for the first time in his life, then: "Spice is nice. But this is nicer." Snow falls on Christmas Eve. Rosie's list has one line left: thank you.
- **Warm note:** The one who feeds everyone deserves a feast too. **Lantern:** the twenty-fourth, chili red.
