# Story Roadmap — Japan — Stories 1–100

*Mochi Bedtime Stories: Japan — 100 Cozy Tales from the Land of the Rising Sun*

**Language vocab:** Japanese (kana/kanji written form + romaji + English gloss on first use). Sprinkled naturally: konnichiwa, arigatou, oishii (delicious), kawaii (cute), ganbatte (do your best), itadakimasu, ohayou, oyasumi (goodnight), sugoi (amazing), genki (lively/well), daijoubu (it's okay), yokatta (thank goodness). 3-5 Japanese terms per story; the Spotlight term is the headline vocabulary word.


**100 stories · 22 notable new characters**
