# Story Roadmap — Costa Rica (Pura Vida) — Stories 1–50

*Costa Rica — Pura Vida Bedtime Stories*

**Language vocab:** Costa Rican "Tico" Spanish + English. Spanish term + simple pronunciation + English on first use. Signature phrase "Pura Vida" (pure life). Recurring vocab: gracias, hola, mae (buddy), tuanis (cool), familia, playa (beach), selva (jungle).


**50 stories · 12 notable new characters**


## New / Notable Characters

| Character | Food | Catchphrase |
|---|---|---|
| **Gallo** | A sentient grain of fried rice from gallo pinto (Costa Rica's national rice-and-beans breakfast) | "Rise and pinto!" |
| **Pinto** | A sentient black bean from gallo pinto, Gallo's inseparable partner | "Better together, mae." |
| **Lala the Tres Leches** | A square of tres leches cake (sponge soaked in three milks) | "Soak it all in!" |
| **Coco the Granizado** | A creamy Costa Rican granizado (shaved ice with syrup and powdered milk) | "Stay frosty, stay sweet!" |
| **Tato the Patacon** | A patacon — a twice-fried, smashed green plantain disc | "Smash now, worry never!" |
| **Don Café** | A roasted Costa Rican coffee bean from the highlands | "Good things brew slowly." |
| **Tamalita** | A banana-leaf Christmas tamale (masa with filling steamed in banana leaf) | "Wrapped with love!" |
| **Flan the Coconut Flan** | A coconut flan (custard with caramel and coconut) | "Steady, steady... ooh, wobbly!" |
| **Slowy the Sloth** | Not a food — a friendly three-toed sloth of the rainforest (animal friend) | "Puuura... viiida..." |
| **Tico the Toucan** | Not a food — a keel-billed toucan of the jungle (animal friend) | "Beak the news!" |
| **Estrella the Sea Turtle** | Not a food — a gentle green sea turtle of Tortuguero (animal friend) | "Follow the moon home." |
| **Cajeta the Coconut Fudge** | A cajeta de coco — a coconut-fudge ball | "Sweet and round, watch me bound!" |
