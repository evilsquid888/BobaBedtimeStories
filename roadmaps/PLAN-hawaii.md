# Story Roadmap — Hawaii — Stories 1–50

*Hawaii (Boba Bedtime Stories sibling collection)*

**Language vocab:** Hawaiian words woven into English narration. On first use: bold Hawaiian term + simple pronunciation + English meaning (e.g., aloha (ah-LOH-hah) = hello/love, mahalo (mah-HAH-loh) = thank you, ohana (oh-HAH-nah) = family, keiki (KAY-kee) = child, honu (HOH-noo) = sea turtle, ono (OH-noh) = delicious, wikiwiki (wee-kee-wee-kee) = quickly, pau (POW) = finished, mauka (MOW-kah) = toward the mountains, makai (mah-KYE) = toward the sea, luau (LOO-ow) = feast, mahimahi, lei, hula, aloha aina = love of the land).


**50 stories · 15 notable new characters**
