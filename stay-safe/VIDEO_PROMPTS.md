# Stay Safe — Video Prompts

Three cinematic prompts per story (**Opening / Challenge / Close**) for generating bedtime highlight clips (e.g., with NotebookLM or a text-to-video tool). Style baseline for the whole arc: *whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm lantern and firelight against deep-blue snow, gentle snowfall, no gore, no weapon close-ups, wonder over threat; the darkest beats are carried by stillness and sound design, not by what is shown.*

Character look references: see [CHARACTER_GUIDE.md](CHARACTER_GUIDE.md). Story text: [README.md](README.md).

## Story 1: Head Up, Phone Down

1. **Opening:** Slow push-in on a snowy night market as paper lanterns flicker on one by one; a rockhopper penguin bursts through a paper GRAND OPENING banner and topples a towering shaved-ice sculpture in slow motion, toppings raining, while a small black tapioca pearl bounces up shouting. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm lantern light, gentle snowfall.
2. **Challenge:** Aiyu's point-of-view — the crowd rendered semi-transparent like golden jelly, camera drifting across dozens of glowing faces all turned toward fire-dancers, then locking onto one figure whose gaze tracks bags instead; a soft "orange" color wash rises at the frame edges; cut to a calm polar bear sergeant setting down her cocoa and walking slowly through the crowd. Cinematic, quiet, no shouting.
3. **Close:** The market asleep, camera slowly pulling back from the tea-egg pot where a jelly cube, a tiny penguin, and a tea egg sit in a row watching the last embers on the frozen lake; the final lantern clicks off; snow, stars, a narwhal's fin briefly breaking the ice far away. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 2: The Smiling Stranger

1. **Opening:** Montage of a white mochi blob hugging everything on a bright snow hill — penguins, a fox, two seals, a snowman twice — while a round penguin at the top marks tallies on a napkin; playful music, quick cuts, snow sparkle. Whimsical 3D storybook animation.
2. **Challenge:** Slow, quiet: a tall friendly figure crouches to talk to Mochi, his face never quite in focus, an empty red leash swinging; the camera drifts to a chinstrap penguin whose eyes lock on the leash; he takes Mochi's long arm and walks her uphill without looking back. Tension through stillness, no scary music — just the wind.
3. **Close:** Golden hour to night: two yellow-skied cubs rocket downhill in a blur to a walrus's side; a white polar bear sergeant walks slowly up the hill; dissolve to a dim bunk room where a mochi arm stretches across for one last hug. Gentle lullaby pacing.

## Story 3: Secrets Are Not Surprises

1. **Opening:** Bright lodge morning — a fun grown-up (face softly unfocused) juggles snowballs and a ski pole for cheering cubs, remembers everyone's cocoa order in rapid cuts; then the camera finds a tiny blue penguin at the back of the line, lit for the first time as he crouches to talk to her. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm, upbeat — with one held beat of quiet at the end.
2. **Challenge:** A week compressed into a slow visual pattern — marshmallows, purple mittens, a whispered "our secret," a hand on a small back — each beat shown from a translucent jelly cube's point of view, the scene tinting faintly orange with each one; then a gear-room door drifting toward shut, and a tiny penguin planting both feet. Quiet, deliberate, no music until she speaks.
3. **Close:** Willa walking — not running — down a long warm hallway past friends and firelight to a door marked NORA; the door closes gently; dissolve to a snow-covered iceberg cottage at night, a lamp in the window, a pair of new mittens left folded on an empty bench outside as snow slowly covers them. Slow, restful, lullaby pacing.

## Story 4: Never Get in the Van

1. **Opening:** A snowy one-way plow lane at golden dusk behind a lodge; an emperor penguin on a glittery blue snowboard carves clean turns while a shiny black tapioca pearl pings wildly off snowbanks, a shed, and a startled seal, briefly taking the lead before skidding on glare ice toward a parked white van. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, playful racing music, snow spray and sparkle.
2. **Challenge:** Silence. A friendly grey-parka'd figure (face never in focus) holds out a small purple glove; a fist closes; the world tilts toward a cracked van door. Then: the pearl goes soft and slippery, the frame fills with shouted words rendered as bold text — HELP! THIS IS NOT MY DAD! CALL 911! — and a glittery blue snowboard slams into a knee in a burst of snow. Cut to two small figures sprinting the WRONG way up the arrows of a one-way lane toward lantern light as a van lurches, stalls, and cannot follow. Cinematic, urgent, triumphant.
3. **Close:** A night market of a hundred faces turning as one toward two shouting kids; a huge polar bear rising and walking calmly through the parting crowd; a teal-boarded penguin on a shed roof sketching a license plate in the last light. Dissolve to a tea egg sitting on cocoa-hut steps between a trembling penguin and a sticky pearl, handing each a warm egg; then a dim bunk room, two friends asleep, snow falling past the window. Slow, warm, lullaby pacing.

## Story 5: The Uh-Oh Feeling

1. **Opening:** A dreamy five-pointed starfruit with glowing freckles drifts along a snowy path making wishes — and walks straight into a lamppost, twice, in gentle slow motion, while a macaroni penguin on a snowbank sketches the whole thing and flips to a page titled *Starfruit Meets Pole, Chapter Three.* Whimsical 3D storybook animation, purple dusk, lamps flickering on one by one.
2. **Challenge:** Long tracking shot down a frozen river path — two small friends walking, an ordinary dark-coated figure keeping pace far behind, stopping when they stop to tie a boot; the frame edges tint faintly orange; the penguin slides her sketchbook away, both lift their heads and straighten; the starfruit turns and looks directly back, says "Good evening" loud and clear, and the figure's step hitches. Quiet, deliberate, only wind and footsteps — no scary music.
3. **Close:** Warm lantern-lit fish market bursting with noise and color; the two friends step into the light and the tension dissolves; dissolve to the sketchbook page — *We Went Inside* with a tiny star drawn beside it — then to a bunk room where the starfruit sleeps, her freckles dimming softly one by one like lamps going out. Lullaby pacing, gold and deep blue.

## Story 6: Sludge and the Words That Melt

1. **Opening:** A caramel pudding clomps proudly into a busy lodge cocoa line in rental snowboard boots that are clearly on the wrong feet, leaving tiny caramel footprints; a seal in the background squints at him like he's a lamp; then the camera tilts up to reveal an enormous melting blue-raspberry slush puppy looming behind him, puddle spreading across the floorboards. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, grey sleety window light, playful-then-uh-oh music.
2. **Challenge:** Slow push-in on the pudding as laughter ripples down the line — a rockhopper penguin's honking "HA!" freezes mid-frame as his own flipper flies to his beak; a single caramel tear falls in slow motion onto a backwards boot; the slush puppy's sneer widens as if being fed. Then, later: the pudding plants both boots, eyes lift, and a shout so big it rattles a ski pole off its hook; the penguin steps in shoulder to shoulder; the slush puppy waits for a reaction that never comes and shrinks in his own puddle. Quiet tension, one boom, then silence.
3. **Close:** Two small figures — pudding and penguin — walk side by side down a long firelit hallway to a door marked NORA; dissolve to a dark rental room lit by the orange glow of a boot dryer, where a big white polar bear sits down beside a hunched blue slush puppy whose puddle slowly stops spreading. Camera pulls back through the window into falling snow. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and amber.

## Story 7: When Sludge Grabbed

1. **Opening:** A golden sesame ball rockets off lodge steps shouting "We roll NOW!", ricochets through a raked garden of shaved-ice toppings, emerges wearing a mango slice as a hat, bounces off two snow rails and lands spinning in a blue puddle that turns out to have a face. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, grey blustery light, fast playful cuts.
2. **Challenge:** Slow motion — a drippy blue fist lifts the sesame ball off the ground; a quick twist toward the thumb, a stomp that splashes slush, a shout that echoes up the mountain; the ball drops free and begins to roll forward — then a vast polar-bear shadow slides across the snow and the ball stops one inch short, seeds rattling. Held silence, then a slush cup sits down in a snowbank by himself.
3. **Close:** A polar bear with snow in both ears skis slowly uphill with a tiny penguin on his shoulder and a sesame ball rolling beside him; dissolve to a quilted office chair, cocoa steam, a nodding white bear; dissolve again to a dim bunk where the sesame ball's seeds go perfectly still. Lullaby pacing, amber and blue.

## Story 8: The Cold Thing in the Snow

1. **Opening:** Bright crisp snow, a wrinkly purple passion fruit grinning "Bet you won't—" at two fluffy polar bear cubs eyeing a frost-shimmering ski pole; quick cut to both cubs stuck tongue-first to the pole, swinging together like a wind chime while the fruit rolls in the snow laughing; then all three tumbling off a creaky lift chair into deep powder in slow motion. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, playful music, snow sparkle.
2. **Challenge:** Sudden silence on the empty back ridge — only wind and the slow creak of the lift; a cub's paw brushes snow from a half-buried dark shape (kept indistinct); the camera holds on the passion fruit's face as her grin drains away and she looks from the object to two trusting cub faces; a single word — STOP — and all three back away, ten slow steps, footprints trailing behind them. No music, just breath and wind.
3. **Close:** Three small shapes tumbling and sliding down a grey hillside toward a glowing yellow cocoa hut; a huge walrus sets down a mug and comes around the counter; dissolve to night — snow drifting past the hut windows, a tea egg and a passion fruit racing cocoa mugs at the counter, then a dim bunk where the passion fruit smiles in her sleep. Slow, warm, lullaby pacing.

## Story 9: The Knock at the Cottage Door

1. **Opening:** Cozy iceberg-cottage kitchen at dusk — camera follows an impossibly long handwritten list of rules down a refrigerator, across the floor, and up the side of a stove where the last lines are gently toasting; a small burgundy red bean with a tiny ladle reads rules aloud while a little blue penguin protests from the couch about the roof. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm amber light, soup steam.
2. **Challenge:** Three firm knocks; the camera holds on a plain closed wooden door as a cheerful shadow shifts behind the frosted glass; cut between the door, the tiny penguin's wide eyes, and the red bean lifting the phone like a shield and her ladle like a stop sign; the voice outside changes from "Package!" to "pipe company" to "is your mom home?" — quiet, tense, no scary music, just the tick of the cooling pot — until the bean bellows about her napping husband and footsteps crunch away.
3. **Close:** Slow exterior push-in through falling snow as footprints fill and disappear; through the glowing window a penguin mom, a tiny penguin on a stool checking the lock a third time, and a red bean ladling three bowls of soup; the last shot is a pen writing *Rule 41: You did everything right* at the bottom of the list. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 10: The Friendly Screen

1. **Opening:** A golden egg-waffle in a tiny top hat slides a snowy rail at dusk in a spray of powder, steam puffing from his grid as he bows to a phone on a tripod; a rockhopper penguin cameraman zooms in, skis straight through the frame, and vanishes into a snowbank while fire emojis float up the screen. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, upbeat, purple-and-gold twilight.
2. **Challenge:** A dark bunk room; a phone screen is the only light. A friendly chat window flips — the same photo returns twisted and wrong (never shown clearly, only its cold glow on Dàn's face) and a red countdown clock appears and begins to tick; the waffle's steam dies, his thumb drifts toward the keyboard typing "I'm sorry—", and a rockhopper flipper slams down flat over the send button. Tension through silence and one ticking sound.
3. **Close:** Firelit Cocoa Hut, a walrus, a polar bear sergeant, and a small bear with a laptop gathered around a phone as the clock reads 0:00 — and nothing happens; slow pull-back through the window to a narwhal's spin on moonlit ice, dissolve to the waffle asleep with his top hat over his eyes as a real wall clock ticks softly. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and amber.

## Story 11: Run. Hide. Fight.

1. **Opening:** A snow day so thick the mountain disappears; slow push through white flakes to a glowing lodge window, then inside — a great room crammed with penguins on cushions, polar bears on couches, dessert characters lined up on the hearth, a projector showing a surfing dog; a round chinstrap penguin ceremonially sets one extra popcorn bucket in the middle of the floor "for the room." Whimsical 3D storybook animation, firelight, cozy and funny.
2. **Challenge:** Sound design over image: a wrong loud noise from off-screen, every head turning at once, a walrus in the doorway with whiskers flat, then a calm voice over the lodge speakers; split into three quiet vignettes — a penguin leading a dozen figures out a kitchen door into white and *not stopping*; a polar bear rolling a heavy cabinet in front of a bolted door as the lights go out and nine phone screens dim; a small tea egg lifting an iron ladle in a kitchen while a bear braces a fire extinguisher. Slow, restrained, no music — wind and breath only. No weapon shown.
3. **Close:** Red and blue light strobing softly off snow; a wooden door opening onto a huge white polar bear's gentle face, nine small pairs of hands rising; dissolve to the great room hours later — fire to embers, a heap of blankets and friends, a headcount whispered name by name, a tiny blue penguin's eyes finally closing against a polar bear's side. Long slow pull-back, lullaby pacing, ember-gold and indigo.

## Story 12: Bīng and the Fight That Never Happened

1. **Opening:** A steamy, packed cocoa hut after a downhill race; slow reveal of an absurd eleven-layer shaved-ice topping tower on the counter, condensed milk spiraling like a staircase, a tiny MORE IS MORE flag on top; a glittering shaved-ice character in sunglasses poses beside it while a rockhopper penguin sneezes and mochi balls tumble in slow motion. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm firelight, playful music.
2. **Challenge:** The room quiets; a large loud bear in an expensive jacket waves his mug and laughs alone; drips run down the shaved-ice character's side as the frame slowly warms toward red; a huge grey polar bear rises from his chair and his shadow falls across the table — then the camera cuts to a tiny tea egg on the counter, calmly peeling an egg, and the shadow stops. Tension held in stillness; no fight music, just the crackle of the fire.
3. **Close:** Bīng walks out the door with the tower on his shoulder like a trophy, firelight catching the flag, applause swelling behind him; outside, under a slow green aurora, he and the old bear sit on the porch steps with cocoa while, through the steamed window, a walrus gently guides the loud guest out into the snow. Pull back to stars; lullaby pacing.

## Story 13: What Bjorn Did After

1. **Opening:** A huge polar bear on forest-green skis crosses a race finish line in a glorious slow-motion wipeout, snow spray arcing into the sky as a narwhal in a lake hole applauds; a tiny blue penguin on a dinner-plate-sized purple board slides in fourth and skids happily into his lap. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, bright afternoon light, cheering crowd.
2. **Challenge:** The rink falls silent; a red-jacketed adult shoves the little penguin down; the bear rises and steps between them, a white wall; a fist swings, is caught like a closing door, the man sits down hard — then the camera holds on the bear's paw lifting off, him stepping back one, two, three steps, and NOT following as the man skates away; he raises a phone with visibly shaking paws. Quiet, deliberate, no fight music — only wind and breath.
3. **Close:** Golden sunset on a bench outside the lodge: the bear, the penguin between, and a kumquat with a thimble at the far end; a police sergeant polar bear walks away with her notebook; the camera pulls back slowly over the empty glowing rink as snow begins to fall. Lullaby pacing, warm amber to deep blue.

## Story 14: Dìguā Gets Pulled Over

1. **Opening:** A rickety charcoal cart-truck with one headlight rattles down a snowy pine-lined highway at golden hour, trailing a wisp of campfire smoke; inside, two yellow polar bear cubs and a white mochi bounce in the back seat singing a made-up "tapioca" song while a sweet potato in a tiny yellow hardhat drives; the horn blares like a startled goose and everyone shrieks with laughter. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm and bouncy.
2. **Challenge:** Dusk turns to blue; the cab suddenly floods with alternating red and blue light. Slow, deliberate close-ups: a turn signal clicking, a key turning off, a dome light clicking on, two rough brown hands settling wide on top of the steering wheel; in the back, cubs freezing like garden gnomes. Big slow footsteps crunch closer; a flashlight beam sweeps the cab and a huge white polar bear officer leans to the window. Quiet, calm, tension through stillness — no scary music, just snow and breathing.
3. **Close:** The cruiser's tail lights pull away and vanish around a bend; the truck rolls slowly under rising moonlight into a market lot glowing with paper lanterns; camera drifts through the cab to find everyone asleep in seatbelts among tapioca sacks, a cub's paw on her buckle, the driver's hardhat tipped over his eyes; a tiny sleepy "hwaaank." Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 15: Say Less, Call Kumquat

1. **Opening:** Morning light on a snowy night-market souvenir hut, its window in a thousand glittering pieces; slow zoom onto a small lodge-camera screen replaying a marble-black tapioca pearl bouncing past, leaving a faint sticky trail; cut to Boba's enormous worried eyes and a calm white polar bear sergeant writing slowly in a notebook. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, quiet comedic tension.
2. **Challenge:** A marble-sized orange kumquat with tiny glasses climbs onto a matchbox briefcase and points at Boba mid-sentence — freeze-frame on his open mouth; then a montage as she counts rules on tiny fingers, a clock face flickering "9:00 / 9:15 / 8:50"; finally a second rooftop camera angle: a canoe-sized icicle lets go in slow motion and spears through the window two seconds after Boba bounces out of frame. Cinematic, crisp, satisfying.
3. **Close:** Night — a bustling Safety Fair of booths on a snow hill (a giant cardboard clock, cubs shouting for a code word, a refrozen slush-puppy awkwardly nodding at a fox), then silence as a tiny tea egg pulls a cord and three warm words light up one by one: ATTITUDE. SKILLS. PLAN.; a tiny blue penguin speaks to the hushed valley; dissolve to midnight, Boba alone on a cold pot rim under the glowing words as friends' footprints lead away into the snow. Slow lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.
