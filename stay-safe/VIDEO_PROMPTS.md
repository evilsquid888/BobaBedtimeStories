# Stay Safe — Video Prompts

Three cinematic prompts per story (**Opening / Challenge / Close**) for generating bedtime highlight clips (e.g., with NotebookLM or a text-to-video tool). Style baseline for the whole arc: *whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm lantern and firelight against deep-blue snow, gentle snowfall, no gore, no weapon close-ups, wonder over threat; the darkest beats are carried by stillness and sound design, not by what is shown.*

Character look references: see [CHARACTER_GUIDE.md](CHARACTER_GUIDE.md). Story text: [README.md](README.md).

## Story 1: Head Up, Phone Down

1. **Opening:** Slow push-in on a snowy night market as paper lanterns flicker on one by one; a rockhopper penguin bursts through a paper GRAND OPENING banner and topples a towering shaved-ice sculpture in slow motion, toppings raining, while a small black tapioca pearl bounces up shouting. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm lantern light, gentle snowfall.
2. **Challenge:** Aiyu's point-of-view — the crowd rendered semi-transparent like golden jelly, camera drifting across dozens of glowing faces all turned toward fire-dancers, then locking onto one figure whose gaze tracks bags instead; a soft "orange" color wash rises at the frame edges; cut to a calm polar bear sergeant setting down her cocoa and walking slowly through the crowd. Cinematic, quiet, no shouting.
3. **Close:** The market asleep, camera slowly pulling back from the tea-egg pot where a jelly cube, a tiny penguin, and a tea egg sit in a row watching the last embers on the frozen lake; the final lantern clicks off; snow, stars, a narwhal's fin briefly breaking the ice far away. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 2: Coach Rosa and the Pink Belt Academy

1. **Opening:** A weathered mail-boat crunches straight through frozen lake ice toward a snowy dock while a rainbow-beaked toucan on the bow announces passengers like a boxing card; a rice grain in a palm hat tumbles off the rail and a black bean hooks him back with a tiny spoon; camera pushes past them all to a seven-foot pink flamingo standing on one leg at the stern, sound asleep, snow gathering on her headband. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, bright and bouncy, gentle snowfall.
2. **Challenge:** Inside the warm boot-drying room under a PINK BELT ACADEMY sign: a short golden plantain disc stands friendly and relaxed as an oven-mitted toucan grabs his shoulders — then, on a whistle, transforms in one cut into a roaring wall of motion, pulling down and driving two knee strikes into the padded vest, shoving, and freezing instantly on the second whistle, hands dropping, face returning to friendly. Slow-motion on the switch ON, hard cut to stillness on the switch OFF; a sombrero drifts down off a heavy bag in the background.
3. **Close:** Eleven students turn their heads left, right, left in unison like meerkats; a rockhopper penguin over-rotates and tumbles into the heavy bag; dissolve to the lodge porch at night — a pink flamingo asleep on one leg beside a small tea egg on the railing, stars over the frozen lake, the mail-boat rocking gently in its hole in the ice. Slow pull-back, lullaby pacing, deep blue and warm window gold.

## Story 3: The Smiling Stranger

1. **Opening:** Montage of a white mochi blob hugging everything on a bright snow hill — penguins, a fox, two seals, a snowman twice — while a round penguin at the top marks tallies on a napkin; playful music, quick cuts, snow sparkle. Whimsical 3D storybook animation.
2. **Challenge:** Slow, quiet: a tall friendly figure crouches to talk to Mochi, his face never quite in focus, an empty red leash swinging; the camera drifts to a chinstrap penguin whose eyes lock on the leash; he takes Mochi's long arm and walks her uphill without looking back. Tension through stillness, no scary music — just the wind.
3. **Close:** Golden hour to night: two yellow-skied cubs rocket downhill in a blur to a walrus's side; a white polar bear sergeant walks slowly up the hill; dissolve to a dim bunk room where a mochi arm stretches across for one last hug. Gentle lullaby pacing.

## Story 4: Hands Off!

1. **Opening:** A seven-foot pink flamingo on one leg reaches out and grabs a soft white mochi's arm — and the arm stretches like taffy across an entire steamy boot-drying room, thinner and thinner, until the flamingo holds a string and the mochi waves cheerfully from six feet away; a toucan in the rafters shrieks an announcement. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm amber light, rows of damp mittens on pipes, comic timing.
2. **Challenge:** Slow, clear, almost instructional: a tiny blue penguin's flipper caught in a gentle mochi grip — camera pushes in on the hand, the thumb glowing softly as the "weak side," the flipper rotating in a big slow circle toward it, a step in, and *pop* — free. Then cut wider: the same penguin lifted in a bear hug from behind, going suddenly heavy, stomp, elbows, duck, out — and a whip-pan head turn as she scans the room. Calm music, satisfying sound cues.
3. **Close:** A dim bunk room lit by fairy lights; a long thin mochi arm stretches across the dark to the edge of a small penguin's bunk, rests a moment, then deliberately lets go and drifts back, snapping softly home; the mochi tucks both arms in and closes her eyes. Snow at the window. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 5: Secrets Are Not Surprises

1. **Opening:** Bright lodge morning — a fun grown-up (face softly unfocused) juggles snowballs and a ski pole for cheering cubs, remembers everyone's cocoa order in rapid cuts; then the camera finds a tiny blue penguin at the back of the line, lit for the first time as he crouches to talk to her. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm, upbeat — with one held beat of quiet at the end.
2. **Challenge:** A week compressed into a slow visual pattern — marshmallows, purple mittens, a whispered "our secret," a hand on a small back — each beat shown from a translucent jelly cube's point of view, the scene tinting faintly orange with each one; then a gear-room door drifting toward shut, and a tiny penguin planting both feet. Quiet, deliberate, no music until she speaks.
3. **Close:** Willa walking — not running — down a long warm hallway past friends and firelight to a door marked NORA; the door closes gently; dissolve to a snow-covered iceberg cottage at night, a lamp in the window, a pair of new mittens left folded on an empty bench outside as snow slowly covers them. Slow, restful, lullaby pacing.

## Story 6: The Turtle: Protect Your Head

1. **Opening:** A jiggly coconut flan and a caramel pudding meet in a lodge doorway and wobble at each other in slow-motion mutual awe; then a snowball sails through the window and the flan's dome snaps around like a bobblehead while a walrus outside shouts "FOR SCIENCE!" beside a stovepipe cannon. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm cocoa-hut light, playful.
2. **Challenge:** A green sea turtle rises from an ice-hole, a snowball flies at her face, and her head draws calmly into her shell as the snowball bursts to powder — cut to the flan raising his arms into the same shape, elbows in, chin down, eyes peeking, and walking steadily across the deck toward the door as snowballs *pomf* harmlessly off his forearms; at the door he stops, scans left and right, steps through. Steady, rhythmic, no fear — just the sound of snow hitting arms.
3. **Close:** Golden hour fades to night; the flan drops his arms after his last run, grins an enormous caramel grin, and a pink flamingo on one leg says "…and then, shrimp"; dissolve to a dim bunk room where the flan and pudding wobble gently asleep, a pillow tucked around the flan's head like a soft shell. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and amber.

## Story 7: Never Get in the Van

1. **Opening:** A snowy one-way plow lane at golden dusk behind a lodge; an emperor penguin on a glittery blue snowboard carves clean turns while a shiny black tapioca pearl pings wildly off snowbanks, a shed, and a startled seal, briefly taking the lead before skidding on glare ice toward a parked white van. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, playful racing music, snow spray and sparkle.
2. **Challenge:** Silence. A friendly grey-parka'd figure (face never in focus) holds out a small purple glove; a fist closes; the world tilts toward a cracked van door. Then: the pearl goes soft and slippery, the frame fills with shouted words rendered as bold text — HELP! THIS IS NOT MY DAD! CALL 911! — and a glittery blue snowboard slams into a knee in a burst of snow. Cut to two small figures sprinting the WRONG way up the arrows of a one-way lane toward lantern light as a van lurches, stalls, and cannot follow. Cinematic, urgent, triumphant.
3. **Close:** A night market of a hundred faces turning as one toward two shouting kids; a huge polar bear rising and walking calmly through the parting crowd; a teal-boarded penguin on a shed roof sketching a license plate in the last light. Dissolve to a tea egg sitting on cocoa-hut steps between a trembling penguin and a sticky pearl, handing each a warm egg; then a dim bunk room, two friends asleep, snow falling past the window. Slow, warm, lullaby pacing.

## Story 8: The Rifle on the Rack

1. **Opening:** An ancient rust-red pickup truck coughs up a snowy back-ridge road at golden hour; inside, two polar bear cubs share a single seatbelt, a tiny rice grain in a palm hat bounces on the dashboard beside a calm black bean, and a huge grey-muzzled polar bear grips the wheel; the camera drifts once past a long dark shape resting on a rack behind the seat and the bear says "Don't." Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm light, engine rattle, one held beat of quiet.
2. **Challenge:** Inside the one-room cabin, stove light flickering; a cub pulls open a third drawer and the room goes still — camera stays on faces, never on the drawer's contents; a tiny bean raises his wooden spoon; the cubs back away step by step and push out the door into blue evening snow toward a big bear at a water pump, who sets his kettle down very carefully. Quiet, deliberate, no music until the bear says "You did that right."
3. **Close:** Night. The bear kneels and turns a key on a dusty iron lockbox beneath a giant hammock; dissolve to the hammock sagging to the floor under five sleeping shapes, a wide window full of stars over the ridge, a tiny rose teacup on the sill catching the last of the stove glow. Slow pull back, lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 9: The Uh-Oh Feeling

1. **Opening:** A dreamy five-pointed starfruit with glowing freckles drifts along a snowy path making wishes — and walks straight into a lamppost, twice, in gentle slow motion, while a macaroni penguin on a snowbank sketches the whole thing and flips to a page titled *Starfruit Meets Pole, Chapter Three.* Whimsical 3D storybook animation, purple dusk, lamps flickering on one by one.
2. **Challenge:** Long tracking shot down a frozen river path — two small friends walking, an ordinary dark-coated figure keeping pace far behind, stopping when they stop to tie a boot; the frame edges tint faintly orange; the penguin slides her sketchbook away, both lift their heads and straighten; the starfruit turns and looks directly back, says "Good evening" loud and clear, and the figure's step hitches. Quiet, deliberate, only wind and footsteps — no scary music.
3. **Close:** Warm lantern-lit fish market bursting with noise and color; the two friends step into the light and the tension dissolves; dissolve to the sketchbook page — *We Went Inside* with a tiny star drawn beside it — then to a bunk room where the starfruit sleeps, her freckles dimming softly one by one like lamps going out. Lullaby pacing, gold and deep blue.

## Story 10: The Bat and the Superman Burst

1. **Opening:** A glittering shaved-ice mountain in sunglasses strides into a cozy wooden boot-drying room trailing a soaked striped towel as a cape, announcing himself; a seven-foot pink flamingo on one leg atop a boot bin nods off mid-sentence and snaps awake; a rainbow-beaked toucan on a coat hook bellows an introduction as a floppy blue pool noodle "bows." Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm indoor light, steam from boot dryers, playful music.
2. **Challenge:** Slow-motion lesson: the flamingo swings the pool noodle in a long glowing arc while a fuzzy green sloth backs up straight into it — soft bonk; then a cut to a golden sesame ball planting both feet, watching the shoulder, and bursting forward arms-first like Superman the instant the noodle goes back — forearms meet the wing, wrap, knee, noodle sails across the room into a boot, three steps, head whips left-right-back in a SCAN. Camera pushes in on the burst; sound drops to a single whoosh.
3. **Close:** The sloth performs the entire burst at glorious slow-motion speed — arms drifting forward, gentle wrap, majestic knee, a four-foot throw onto a rockhopper penguin — while the room erupts: toucan falls off his hook, the shaved-ice mountain weeps into his towel, the flamingo turns away with one pink tear; dissolve to a dim bunk room where the sesame ball whispers "chōng" to the ceiling and closes his eyes. Lullaby pacing, warm amber to deep blue.

## Story 11: Sludge and the Words That Melt

1. **Opening:** A caramel pudding clomps proudly into a busy lodge cocoa line in rental snowboard boots that are clearly on the wrong feet, leaving tiny caramel footprints; a seal in the background squints at him like he's a lamp; then the camera tilts up to reveal an enormous melting blue-raspberry slush puppy looming behind him, puddle spreading across the floorboards. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, grey sleety window light, playful-then-uh-oh music.
2. **Challenge:** Slow push-in on the pudding as laughter ripples down the line — a rockhopper penguin's honking "HA!" freezes mid-frame as his own flipper flies to his beak; a single caramel tear falls in slow motion onto a backwards boot; the slush puppy's sneer widens as if being fed. Then, later: the pudding plants both boots, eyes lift, and a shout so big it rattles a ski pole off its hook; the penguin steps in shoulder to shoulder; the slush puppy waits for a reaction that never comes and shrinks in his own puddle. Quiet tension, one boom, then silence.
3. **Close:** Two small figures — pudding and penguin — walk side by side down a long firelit hallway to a door marked NORA; dissolve to a dark rental room lit by the orange glow of a boot dryer, where a big white polar bear sits down beside a hunched blue slush puppy whose puddle slowly stops spreading. Camera pulls back through the window into falling snow. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and amber.

## Story 12: Knife = Distance

1. **Opening:** A toucan with a rainbow beak sweeps across a cozy boot-drying room hung with pink banners and steaming mittens, announcing like a boxing ring-master; a seven-foot pink flamingo on one leg holds up a floppy grey rubber knife with a smiley face that flops over sideways to total silence. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm lamplight, quick comic cuts, one held beat of quiet as the flamingo's face turns serious.
2. **Challenge:** Slow and deliberate — the flamingo drags a wire mitten rack between herself and a rainbow shaved-ice cone; an emperor penguin raises a glittery blue snowboard like a shield and peers over it; a crispy plantain disc blocks a slow rubber-knife swing *arm to arm* and steps in; a single drop of blue syrup slides down the cone's side and lands on the floor. No music, just breath and the creak of the rack.
3. **Close:** Wide shot of a vast frozen lake under a pink-to-blue dusk — the shaved-ice cone streaks across the ice toward a warm cluster of friends at the shore, stops, turns his head left, right, behind; cut to the flamingo on one leg at the lake's center closing her eyes as snow begins to fall; dissolve to a dim bunk room, sunglasses folded on a sleeping cone's chest, the empty white lake glowing through the window. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 13: When Sludge Grabbed

1. **Opening:** A golden sesame ball rockets off lodge steps shouting "We roll NOW!", ricochets through a raked garden of shaved-ice toppings, emerges wearing a mango slice as a hat, bounces off two snow rails and lands spinning in a blue puddle that turns out to have a face. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, grey blustery light, fast playful cuts.
2. **Challenge:** Slow motion — a drippy blue fist lifts the sesame ball off the ground; a quick twist toward the thumb, a stomp that splashes slush, a shout that echoes up the mountain; the ball drops free and begins to roll forward — then a vast polar-bear shadow slides across the snow and the ball stops one inch short, seeds rattling. Held silence, then a slush cup sits down in a snowbank by himself.
3. **Close:** A polar bear with snow in both ears skis slowly uphill with a tiny penguin on his shoulder and a sesame ball rolling beside him; dissolve to a quilted office chair, cocoa steam, a nodding white bear; dissolve again to a dim bunk where the sesame ball's seeds go perfectly still. Lullaby pacing, amber and blue.

## Story 14: The Street Fight That Ended in a Doorway

1. **Opening:** A sleek orange mango with a tiny sweatband skids into a wood-paneled boot-drying room in a spray of golden juice, shouting a personal best; a seven-foot pink flamingo on one leg with a crooked felt mustache blocks the frame; a rainbow-beaked toucan on a coat rack bellows "WELCOME TO THE STREET" to a room of boot benches. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm amber light, playful sports-broadcast energy.
2. **Challenge:** Slow, tense: the flamingo stalks forward — shoulders up, one wing hidden behind her back, staring — while the toucan drifts clumsily around behind the mango; the mango's fists come up like a boxer, then drop and open into a calm palms-out "fence" at chest height, feet turning, one slow step back toward a glowing doorway; the mustache slides sideways. No music, just breathing and the creak of the floor — until a wing grabs the fence and the mango turns and bolts.
3. **Close:** The mango sprints through the golden doorway past a huge walrus polishing a mug, skids to a stop, whips his head left-right-behind ("SCAN!"); a rice grain and a black bean carry him out laughing on a second run; dissolve to a bunk room at night, the mango asleep facing the door with one hand open on the pillow, and far off a pink flamingo asleep on one leg with a shrimp in her beak. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 15: The Cold Thing in the Snow

1. **Opening:** Bright crisp snow, a wrinkly purple passion fruit grinning "Bet you won't—" at two fluffy polar bear cubs eyeing a frost-shimmering ski pole; quick cut to both cubs stuck tongue-first to the pole, swinging together like a wind chime while the fruit rolls in the snow laughing; then all three tumbling off a creaky lift chair into deep powder in slow motion. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, playful music, snow sparkle.
2. **Challenge:** Sudden silence on the empty back ridge — only wind and the slow creak of the lift; a cub's paw brushes snow from a half-buried dark shape (kept indistinct); the camera holds on the passion fruit's face as her grin drains away and she looks from the object to two trusting cub faces; a single word — STOP — and all three back away, ten slow steps, footprints trailing behind them. No music, just breath and wind.
3. **Close:** Three small shapes tumbling and sliding down a grey hillside toward a glowing yellow cocoa hut; a huge walrus sets down a mug and comes around the counter; dissolve to night — snow drifting past the hut windows, a tea egg and a passion fruit racing cocoa mugs at the counter, then a dim bunk where the passion fruit smiles in her sleep. Slow, warm, lullaby pacing.

## Story 16: Road Rage and the Goose Horn

1. **Opening:** Golden-hour highway in a snowy valley; inside a rattling charcoal truck, a coffee bean sips from a thimble in the passenger seat, a black tapioca pearl bounces on the dashboard, two yellow polar-bear cubs in the back yell for the horn — the sweet-potato driver honks and a cartoon goose-shaped sound-wave rolls out over the pines. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm light, bouncing camera on the ruts.
2. **Challenge:** Dusk turning blue; headlights swell in the rearview mirror until they fill it, a dark car swerves past in a spray of slush and its brake lights flare red across the cab; the driver's rough brown hands never leave the wheel; a coffee bean slowly presses the door-lock — *click* — and rolls the window up. Then a red light, a truck-length of empty snow, and a coat-wrapped figure (never a face) stepping out into the slush toward the mirror. Quiet, tense, only engine hum — until the horn.
3. **Close:** Six porch lights blink on down a snowy street as one continuous goose-honk rings out and the truck calmly pulls around the corner; dissolve to a glowing stone police station where a polar-bear sergeant and a one-legged pink flamingo wait on the steps; then the slow drive home under one headlight, cubs asleep and belted, lanterns of the night market appearing gold in the dark. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and amber.

## Story 17: The Knock at the Cottage Door

1. **Opening:** Cozy iceberg-cottage kitchen at dusk — camera follows an impossibly long handwritten list of rules down a refrigerator, across the floor, and up the side of a stove where the last lines are gently toasting; a small burgundy red bean with a tiny ladle reads rules aloud while a little blue penguin protests from the couch about the roof. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm amber light, soup steam.
2. **Challenge:** Three firm knocks; the camera holds on a plain closed wooden door as a cheerful shadow shifts behind the frosted glass; cut between the door, the tiny penguin's wide eyes, and the red bean lifting the phone like a shield and her ladle like a stop sign; the voice outside changes from "Package!" to "pipe company" to "is your mom home?" — quiet, tense, no scary music, just the tick of the cooling pot — until the bean bellows about her napping husband and footsteps crunch away.
3. **Close:** Slow exterior push-in through falling snow as footprints fill and disappear; through the glowing window a penguin mom, a tiny penguin on a stool checking the lock a third time, and a red bean ladling three bowls of soup; the last shot is a pen writing *Rule 41: You did everything right* at the bottom of the list. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 18: Cover vs. Concealment

1. **Opening:** Bright morning on a frozen lake; a huge walrus proudly pats a homemade cannon built from a cocoa drum, bicycle pump, and stovepipe; it fires with a *THOOMP* and a snowball rockets across the lake, knocking the head off one snowman, which sails on and knocks the head off a second. A pink flamingo on one leg looks prouder than the walrus. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, crisp blue snow, golden light, playful slapstick timing.
2. **Challenge:** Two figures stand still in the open — a dense golden plantain disc and a soft milk-soaked cake square; slow-motion: the first snowball hits the patacón and simply stops in a puff of powder; the second passes gently *through* the cake and out the other side, leaving her wetter and blinking. Cut to a rainbow shaved-ice cone frozen behind a cocoa drum as a snowball bangs against it — then the patacón slides in front of him like a door closing, points to the next sled, and they go together, low and fast. Bright, tense-but-safe, sound design of pump *sssk* and *THOOMP*.
3. **Close:** Pink sunset over the ridge; snowmen with new heads dotting the lake; a rainbow ice cone leaning against a golden patacón at the water's edge; behind them a tea egg screwing the lid on a thermos and a flamingo asleep on one leg murmuring in her sleep. Very slow pull back, warm amber fading to lavender, lullaby pacing.

## Story 19: The Friendly Screen

1. **Opening:** A golden egg-waffle in a tiny top hat slides a snowy rail at dusk in a spray of powder, steam puffing from his grid as he bows to a phone on a tripod; a rockhopper penguin cameraman zooms in, skis straight through the frame, and vanishes into a snowbank while fire emojis float up the screen. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, upbeat, purple-and-gold twilight.
2. **Challenge:** A dark bunk room; a phone screen is the only light. A friendly chat window flips — the same photo returns twisted and wrong (never shown clearly, only its cold glow on Dàn's face) and a red countdown clock appears and begins to tick; the waffle's steam dies, his thumb drifts toward the keyboard typing "I'm sorry—", and a rockhopper flipper slams down flat over the send button. Tension through silence and one ticking sound.
3. **Close:** Firelit Cocoa Hut, a walrus, a polar bear sergeant, and a small bear with a laptop gathered around a phone as the clock reads 0:00 — and nothing happens; slow pull-back through the window to a narwhal's spin on moonlit ice, dissolve to the waffle asleep with his top hat over his eyes as a real wall clock ticks softly. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and amber.

## Story 20: Down But Not Out

1. **Opening:** A translucent white coconut-jelly cube in flip-flops lies belly-down on a coconut-oiled snowboard, paddling across a perfectly flat frozen lake at walking speed, then pops up in one smooth surfer motion, rides four feet, wipes out in a slow spin with flip-flops in the air, and pops up again — on repeat, grinning — while on the shore a seven-foot pink flamingo on one leg narrows her eyes. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, bright glittering ice, upbeat.
2. **Challenge:** Inside a warm boot-drying room turned dojo, the camera lies low on the mat: the jelly shells his head with his forearms, spins his feet toward a small crispy plantain disc, bicycle-kicks the air, the plantain steps back — and in a slow-motion beat the jelly plants a hand and a foot, lifts his hips as if the floor were lava, swings his leg through, and stands; cut to a mossy sloth doing the identical sequence at one-tenth speed while penguins drink cocoa and a toucan flies a bored lap. Cozy, funny, then quietly awed.
3. **Close:** Golden hour to dusk on a wooden dock over a frozen lake; the jelly lies on his back arms wide, the sloth's claws dangle beside him, the lake shifts pink to deep blue, stars come out; dissolve to a dark boot room where a small sesame ball practices pop-ups in silhouette, then finally lies down and stays down. Lullaby pacing, soft snowfall.

## Story 21: Run. Hide. Fight.

1. **Opening:** A snow day so thick the mountain disappears; slow push through white flakes to a glowing lodge window, then inside — a great room crammed with penguins on cushions, polar bears on couches, dessert characters lined up on the hearth, a projector showing a surfing dog; a round chinstrap penguin ceremonially sets one extra popcorn bucket in the middle of the floor "for the room." Whimsical 3D storybook animation, firelight, cozy and funny.
2. **Challenge:** Sound design over image: a wrong loud noise from off-screen, every head turning at once, a walrus in the doorway with whiskers flat, then a calm voice over the lodge speakers; split into three quiet vignettes — a penguin leading a dozen figures out a kitchen door into white and *not stopping*; a polar bear rolling a heavy cabinet in front of a bolted door as the lights go out and nine phone screens dim; a small tea egg lifting an iron ladle in a kitchen while a bear braces a fire extinguisher. Slow, restrained, no music — wind and breath only. No weapon shown.
3. **Close:** Red and blue light strobing softly off snow; a wooden door opening onto a huge white polar bear's gentle face, nine small pairs of hands rising; dissolve to the great room hours later — fire to embers, a heap of blankets and friends, a headcount whispered name by name, a tiny blue penguin's eyes finally closing against a polar bear's side. Long slow pull-back, lullaby pacing, ember-gold and indigo.

## Story 22: The Trunk

1. **Opening:** A cluttered boot-drying room lit by warm lamps, rows of ski boots steaming; a seven-foot pink flamingo balances on one leg atop a battered wooden chest labeled SEÑOR BAÚL while a toucan announcer flaps his rainbow beak like a ring MC and a small wrinkly purple passion fruit points and grins, "Bet you won't." Quick, bouncy cuts; whimsical 3D storybook animation, cocoa-brown and pink palette.
2. **Challenge:** Cut to total darkness — only the sound of fast breathing and the dull *tap tap* of a flamingo toe on wood above; slowly the breathing steadies to a four-count, and a faint green glow resolves into a small T-handle inches from two wide eyes; then a kick, a crack of red plastic, and a thin blade of grey light with a tiny purple hand waving through it. Intimate, near-silent, camera locked inside the chest; muffled cheering from outside.
3. **Close:** The lid flies open and the passion fruit rolls out like a cork, scans left-right-left, catches a thrown shrimp; dissolve to night — a tea egg with a flashlight peering into the open trunk of a rusty truck under snowfall, then a dim bunk where the passion fruit sleeps with both arms up under the blanket and a stripe of moonlight from a taillight-shaped hole crosses the floor. Slow, lullaby pacing, deep blue and amber.

## Story 23: Bīng and the Fight That Never Happened

1. **Opening:** A steamy, packed cocoa hut after a downhill race; slow reveal of an absurd eleven-layer shaved-ice topping tower on the counter, condensed milk spiraling like a staircase, a tiny MORE IS MORE flag on top; a glittering shaved-ice character in sunglasses poses beside it while a rockhopper penguin sneezes and mochi balls tumble in slow motion. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm firelight, playful music.
2. **Challenge:** The room quiets; a large loud bear in an expensive jacket waves his mug and laughs alone; drips run down the shaved-ice character's side as the frame slowly warms toward red; a huge grey polar bear rises from his chair and his shadow falls across the table — then the camera cuts to a tiny tea egg on the counter, calmly peeling an egg, and the shadow stops. Tension held in stillness; no fight music, just the crackle of the fire.
3. **Close:** Bīng walks out the door with the tower on his shoulder like a trophy, firelight catching the flag, applause swelling behind him; outside, under a slow green aurora, he and the old bear sit on the porch steps with cocoa while, through the steamed window, a walrus gently guides the loud guest out into the snow. Pull back to stars; lullaby pacing.

## Story 24: Tape, Ties, and Rope

1. **Opening:** Slow reveal in a steamy boot-drying room: a rockhopper penguin wrapped neck-to-knee in silver duct tape stands perfectly still like a burrito with a beak; a macaroni penguin sketches him; a toucan looks guiltily at the ceiling; a seven-foot pink flamingo asleep on one leg opens one eye. Then a soft milk-soaked cake hugs the burrito and the tape peels away in a glistening *shlorp*. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm amber light, comic timing.

Playful, kinetic, one held beat of surprise.
3. **Close:** Night in the boot room, one lamp; the camera drifts past an eleven-panel comic taped to the wall, a snoring toucan, the flamingo asleep on one leg, and settles on the cake asleep on a folded blanket, hands loose on her chest, slowly turning her wrists in her sleep; snow at the window. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold, fade to black.

## Story 25: What Bjorn Did After

1. **Opening:** A huge polar bear on forest-green skis crosses a race finish line in a glorious slow-motion wipeout, snow spray arcing into the sky as a narwhal in a lake hole applauds; a tiny blue penguin on a dinner-plate-sized purple board slides in fourth and skids happily into his lap. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, bright afternoon light, cheering crowd.
2. **Challenge:** The rink falls silent; a red-jacketed adult shoves the little penguin down; the bear rises and steps between them, a white wall; a fist swings, is caught like a closing door, the man sits down hard — then the camera holds on the bear's paw lifting off, him stepping back one, two, three steps, and NOT following as the man skates away; he raises a phone with visibly shaking paws. Quiet, deliberate, no fight music — only wind and breath.
3. **Close:** Golden sunset on a bench outside the lodge: the bear, the penguin between, and a kumquat with a thimble at the far end; a police sergeant polar bear walks away with her notebook; the camera pulls back slowly over the empty glowing rink as snow begins to fall. Lullaby pacing, warm amber to deep blue.

## Story 26: The Gun Up Close

1. **Opening:** A toucan mid-announcement in a cozy boot-drying-room dojo, beak wide — then he stops as a tall pink flamingo on one leg sets a small blue rubber training gun on a bench and steps back; a cartwheeling rockhopper penguin finishes his cartwheel and quietly sits. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm lamp light; the sound design drops from fanfare to near-silence.
2. **Challenge:** Slow-motion, no music: a huge white polar bear takes one clean step sideways as the blue muzzle swings to point at an empty wall; his paw pushes it toward a corner; he bursts in, both paws wrapping the flamingo's wing; then three big steps back, the toy pointed at the floor, his eyes sweeping the room. Cut to a rockhopper penguin getting bonked between the eyes by a squeaky pink foam gun — *squeak* — three times, ending in a hug. Gentle comedy after tension.
3. **Close:** Dusk to night on a frozen lake under paper lanterns; the flamingo stands utterly still on one leg like a pink lamppost, eyes closed, snow falling; a tiny black tapioca pearl bounces softly toward a glowing boba stand and climbs into a bunk; the lanterns dim one by one. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and rose.

## Story 27: Dìguā Gets Pulled Over

1. **Opening:** A rickety charcoal cart-truck with one headlight rattles down a snowy pine-lined highway at golden hour, trailing a wisp of campfire smoke; inside, two yellow polar bear cubs and a white mochi bounce in the back seat singing a made-up "tapioca" song while a sweet potato in a tiny yellow hardhat drives; the horn blares like a startled goose and everyone shrieks with laughter. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm and bouncy.
2. **Challenge:** Dusk turns to blue; the cab suddenly floods with alternating red and blue light. Slow, deliberate close-ups: a turn signal clicking, a key turning off, a dome light clicking on, two rough brown hands settling wide on top of the steering wheel; in the back, cubs freezing like garden gnomes. Big slow footsteps crunch closer; a flashlight beam sweeps the cab and a huge white polar bear officer leans to the window. Quiet, calm, tension through stillness — no scary music, just snow and breathing.
3. **Close:** The cruiser's tail lights pull away and vanish around a bend; the truck rolls slowly under rising moonlight into a market lot glowing with paper lanterns; camera drifts through the cab to find everyone asleep in seatbelts among tapioca sacks, a cub's paw on her buckle, the driver's hardhat tipped over his eyes; a tiny sleepy "hwaaank." Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 28: The Great Cocoa Heist

1. **Opening:** A cocoa hut door bursts open and a keel-billed toucan in a comically tiny black knit cap strides in holding a paper bag marked LOOT and a fan of rating cards, announcing himself with a flourish; cut to a seven-foot pink flamingo by the fireplace closing her eyes in exasperation while a room of penguins, bears, and dessert characters watch from their tables. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, warm firelight, gentle snowfall at the windows, playful music.
2. **Challenge:** Slow, quiet: from a translucent jelly cube's point of view the room goes soft and see-through — glowing outlines highlight two doors, a thick oak counter, and a stone fireplace wall as the camera drifts without ever locking onto the toucan; the jelly slides low behind a purple taro and a sea turtle; a big purple hand settles calmly on the table beside her as the word 等 fades in and out. Almost no music — fire crackle and slow breathing.
3. **Close:** A huge white polar bear sergeant fills the doorway; everyone drops flat with open hands in a gentle synchronized wave — a rockhopper penguin bouncing slightly on landing — leaving only the toucan standing, wings raised, still holding a rating card; dissolve to night snow, a jelly cube and a taro walking a lantern path toward a glowing boba stand. Slow lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 29: Say Less, Call Kumquat

Whimsical 3D storybook animation, quiet comedic tension.
2. **Challenge:** A marble-sized orange kumquat with tiny glasses climbs onto a matchbox briefcase and points at Boba mid-sentence — freeze-frame on his open mouth; then a montage as she counts rules on tiny fingers, a clock face flickering "9:00 / 9:15 / 8:50"; finally a second rooftop camera angle: a canoe-sized icicle lets go in slow motion and spears through the window two seconds after Boba bounces out of frame. SKILLS. PLAN.; a tiny blue penguin speaks to the hushed valley; dissolve to midnight, Boba alone on a cold pot rim under the glowing words as friends' footprints lead away into the snow. Slow lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.

## Story 30: Pink Belt Graduation

1. **Opening:** Slow aerial glide down a frozen lake at dusk — sixteen little snow-and-lantern stations in a line from a lodge to a dock, each with a hand-painted sign, boxed-up boba stand in the foreground, two boats waiting; a toucan swoops through frame announcing at full volume as a seven-foot pink flamingo asleep on one leg jolts awake mid-sentence. Whimsical 3D storybook animation, lavender sky, lantern gold on blue snow, gentle snowfall.
2. **Challenge:** Fast joyful montage cut to a rising drumline — hands up like *whoa*, a mochi apologizing while grabbing wrists, a snowball cannon bouncing off shelled heads, a sesame ball bursting at a pool noodle, cubs running past an empty gun rack while a grumpy bear glowers, a goose-horn *HWAAANK*, soaked sponge cake and bone-dry patacón, a sloth popping up in four minutes as everyone waits, a foam gun lowered into a pocket — then all sound drops out as every character stops at the last lantern and turns their heads, together, all the way around. Whimsical, warm, no violence shown, comic timing.
3. **Close:** The flamingo stands on two legs at the dock's end, hands up, and bends nearly in half to trade a pink shrimp for a warm tea egg with a tiny cracked egg in a vest; nobody speaks. Dissolve: a mail-boat crunches through lake ice under a hundred swaying lanterns as a crew of tropical desserts and animals sing at the rail; the flamingo at the bow tucks up one leg and falls asleep mid-wave. Dissolve: a ferry deck, a heap of sleeping desserts under blankets, one small black pearl lifting his head to look all the way around — then closing his eyes as the valley's lights shrink to a glow behind. Slow pull-back to stars. Lullaby pacing, deep blue and gold.
