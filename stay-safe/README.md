# 🛡️ Stay Safe — Boba × Frostpeak Bedtime Stories

15 whimsical-but-serious bedtime stories for ages 9–12 that teach real self-protection skills. The Taiwan dessert crew spends a winter running **Boba on Ice** at the Frostpeak Winter Night Market, alongside the snowboarding penguins and skiing polar bears of [Penguinsnowboard](https://github.com/evilsquid888/Penguinsnowboard). Inspired by the principles taught on *Active Self Protection* — **attitude, skills, plan** — scaled down to kid size. Part of the *Boba Bedtime Stories* family.

The narrator stays warm and silly. The danger is **named plainly** — a van, a stranger, a gun, a police officer, sexual abuse, a blackmail AI — because a lesson wrapped in allegory doesn't transfer. Bad actors are never graphic and never win. Every story ends soft.

See also: [Character Guide](CHARACTER_GUIDE.md) · [Outline / Roadmap](../roadmaps/PLAN-stay-safe.md) · [Video Prompts](VIDEO_PROMPTS.md)

> **For grown-ups:** these are meant to be read *with* a kid. Each story ends with **🛡️ What We Learned** (the takeaway) and **💬 Talk About It** (three questions). Stories **3, 4, 10, and 11** are the heaviest — read them earlier in the evening the first time, not as the last thing before sleep. Laws are described as "the rules here in the valley, and in most of America — ask a grown-up about where *you* live."

## Arc Structure

- **1–5** Awareness, tricky people, body safety, abduction, gut feelings
- **6–7** Bullies (words, then hands)
- **8–10** Hazards on the slope, at home, on a screen
- **11** The darkest story — Run. Hide. Fight.
- **12–13** Fights and the law
- **14–15** Police, lawyers, and the warm finale
