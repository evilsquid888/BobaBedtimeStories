# 🛡️ Stay Safe — Boba × Frostpeak Bedtime Stories

30 whimsical-but-serious bedtime stories for ages 9–12 that teach real self-protection skills. The Taiwan dessert crew spends a winter running **Boba on Ice** at the Frostpeak Winter Night Market, alongside the snowboarding penguins and skiing polar bears of [Penguinsnowboard](https://github.com/evilsquid888/Penguinsnowboard). Two interleaved tracks: the **odd-numbered** stories follow the principles taught on *Active Self Protection* — **attitude, skills, plan** — and the **even-numbered** stories are the **Krav Maga track**, where **Coach Rosa the Flamingo** and the visiting Costa Rica crew run the Pink Belt Academy: fun, a little silly, one real practical lesson each. Part of the *Boba Bedtime Stories* family.

The narrator stays warm and silly. The danger is **named plainly** — a van, a stranger, a gun, a police officer, sexual abuse, a blackmail AI — because a lesson wrapped in allegory doesn't transfer. Bad actors are never graphic and never win. Every story ends soft.

See also: [Character Guide](CHARACTER_GUIDE.md) · [Outline / Roadmap](../roadmaps/PLAN-stay-safe.md) · [Video Prompts](VIDEO_PROMPTS.md) · [CapCut Guide + Storyboards](capcut/CAPCUT_GUIDE.md)

> **For grown-ups:** these are meant to be read *with* a kid. The Krav Maga stories teach *principles* with one memorable cue each — they are stories, not a training manual; a real class with a real coach is where the moves get learned. Each story ends with **🛡️ What We Learned** (the takeaway) and **💬 Talk About It** (three questions). Stories **5, 7, 19, 21, and 26** are the heaviest — read them earlier in the evening the first time, not as the last thing before sleep. Laws are described as "the rules here in the valley, and in most of America — ask a grown-up about where *you* live."

## Arc Structure

**Odd (ASP track):** 1–9 awareness, tricky people, body safety, abduction, gut feelings · 11–13 bullies · 15–19 slope, home, screen · 21 Run. Hide. Fight. · 23–25 fights and the law · 27–29 police, lawyers, Safety Fair.

**Even (Krav Maga track):** 2 the academy opens · 4–10 grabs, head, guns at a friend's house, sticks · 12–14 knives and street fights · 16–18 road rage, cover vs. concealment · 20–24 ground, trunk, restraints · 26–28 guns up close, hostage · 30 graduation and farewell.

Read in order — the two tracks share one winter and reference each other.
