# Stay Safe — Character Guide

Who's who in the *Stay Safe* arc, where the Taiwan dessert crew spends a winter running **Boba on Ice** at the Frostpeak Winter Night Market alongside the Penguinsnowboard cast.

---

## New Characters (introduced in this arc)

### Auntie Hù (護 hù = protect) — a Tea Egg
- **Role:** The self-defense mentor of the arc. A retired night-market security guard who now runs the tea-egg pot beside Boba on Ice.
- **Catchphrase:** "Head up. Hands free. Have a plan."
- **Appearance:** A brown tea egg with a cracked, marbled shell — the dark lines wander over her like a map of every market she ever patrolled. Small, dense, impossible to knock over. Wears a padded vest with too many pockets and a whistle she has never once needed to blow.
- **Voice:** Calm, dry, unhurried. Never lectures; asks questions until you answer yourself. Says "Mm." when she's proud of you. Her three words — **Attitude. Skills. Plan.** — hang on a sign over her pot.

### Sergeant Solveig Snowmane — Polar Bear
- **Role:** Frostpeak Valley police sergeant; Nora Snowmane's older sister. Appears whenever the law shows up, and shows kids what a *good* officer looks like — while the stories still teach what to do regardless of who's walking up.
- **Catchphrase:** "Slow hands, honest words."
- **Appearance:** Broad, snow-white, a navy uniform jacket with a silver snowflake badge. Moves slowly on purpose. Radio on the shoulder, notebook in the paw, a scar through one eyebrow she says is from a sled and Nora says is from a door.
- **Voice:** Level and plain. Explains what she's doing before she does it. Tells kids to ask for a parent and a lawyer, because that's what she'd tell her own cubs.

### Counselor Kumquat (金橘 jīnjú) — a Kumquat
- **Role:** Public defender. The lawyer of the arc.
- **Catchphrase:** "Say less. Call me."
- **Appearance:** A glossy orange kumquat the size of a large marble, carrying a black briefcase the size of a matchbox. Tiny round glasses. The peel is sweet and the middle is *tart*, and she is exactly that way around.
- **Voice:** Clipped, fast, fierce, funny. Interrupts anyone about to over-explain. Believes talking is a privilege you should spend carefully.

### The Smiling Stranger
- **Role:** The tricky person. Appears in stories 2, 4, and (as a machine imitating a kid) 10.
- **Appearance:** Deliberately ordinary — tall, warm smile, nice coat, a face the narrator never quite pins down. Not a monster. Not scary-looking. That is the whole point.
- **Voice:** Friendly, helpful, a little too interested. Uses the red-flag asks: *help me · come with me · keep this quiet · don't tell your parents · your mom sent me.*

### Sludge — a Slush Puppy
- **Role:** The lodge bully (stories 6, 7). Redeemed at the Safety Fair (story 15).
- **Catchphrase:** "What are you gonna do about it?" (later: "You can say stop. I'll listen.")
- **Appearance:** An oversized, half-melted cup of blue-raspberry slush with drippy fists and a permanent sneer. Leaves puddles. Mean because he is melting inside and nobody ever told him he's allowed to refreeze.
- **Voice:** Loud, sloshy, looking for a reaction to feed on.

## The Arc's Shared Vocabulary

| Phrase | Who says it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Attitude. Skills. Plan.** | Auntie Hù | Decide ahead of time that you're worth protecting; learn a few things well; know what you'll do. |
| **The Four Coats** | Aiyu | White = asleep to the world · Yellow = looking around, relaxed (live here) · Orange = something's off, make a plan · Red = do the plan. |
| **Judge the ask, not the smile** | Barnaby | Grown-ups don't ask kids for help. Red-flag asks: help me · come with me · keep this quiet · don't tell your parents · your mom sent me. |
| **Polite is not the same as obedient** | Auntie Hù | You may say NO to any adult, loudly, and leave. In danger: be rude, be loud, be bold. Rude is cheap. |
| **Surprises end in telling. Secrets are a red flag.** | Nora | No grown-up ever needs a kid to keep a secret from their parents. |
| **The Swimsuit Rule** | Nora | The parts a swimsuit covers are private. Nobody touches, looks, or asks — doctors only, with a parent there. |
| **Fight at the grab** | Auntie Hù | The moment someone tries to take you is your best moment. Never get in the car. Scream *words*. |
| **You can't lose a fight you're not in** | Auntie Hù | Leaving is winning. |
| **Enough, then stop** | Bjorn | Force only to get safe; stop when the threat stops; never chase. |
| **Call 911 first** | Solveig | The first caller is the victim in the report. |
| **Slow hands, honest words** | Solveig | How to move and speak around police. |
| **Say less. Call me.** | Counselor Kumquat | "I want my parent." "I'd like a lawyer." Complete sentences. |
| **Run. Hide. Fight.** | Everyone | Two exits everywhere. Lock–lights–silence. Fight last, together, loud. |
| **Don't touch. Leave. Tell.** | The twins | A found gun. |
| **A clock only ticks** | Herschel | A blackmailer's countdown has power only while it's a secret. |
