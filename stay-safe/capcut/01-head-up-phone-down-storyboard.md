# Storyboard: Stay Safe Story 01 — Head Up, Phone Down

**Platform:** CapCut (AI video / Seedance + editor) — see [CAPCUT_GUIDE.md](CAPCUT_GUIDE.md)
**Source story:** [../stories/01-head-up-phone-down.md](../stories/01-head-up-phone-down.md)
**Total Shots:** 12
**Estimated Runtime:** ~3:20 + 16 s of end cards
**Art Style (paste on ALL prompts):** `whimsical 3D storybook animation, soft rounded shapes, warm paper-lantern light against deep-blue snowy night, gentle snowfall, cozy wonder, no text, no watermark`
**Reference sheets needed:** Aiyu · Auntie Hù · Willa Wobble · Finnegan Flipsworth · Sergeant Solveig (see guide, Phase 1)

> **Tip:** Aiyu is *translucent*. Add `you can faintly see the lanterns through her body` to every shot she's in — it's her whole visual identity and it pays off in Shot 8.

---

## Shot 1 — GRAND OPENING (0:00–0:15)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A brand-new boba stand called BOBA ON ICE at a snowy night market, a four-foot shaved-ice tower topped with a tiny paper flag reading MORE IS MORE, a rockhopper penguin bursting through a paper GRAND OPENING banner and colliding face-first with the tower, toppings flying in slow motion, a small black tapioca pearl bouncing up on the counter.`
**Motion:** slow-motion topping explosion, banner paper tearing, gentle push-in.
**SFX:** paper rip, soft *splat*, one cheerful crowd gasp.

**Narration:**
> "The first thing that happened on opening night of the Frostpeak Winter Night Market was that Bīng's topping tower fell on a penguin. 'MY MASTERPIECE!' wailed Bīng. 'MY EVERYTHING!' wailed Finnegan, from under the red bean. 'That's the bad luck used up!' said Boba. 'Let's BOUNCE!'"

---

## Shot 2 — THE MARKET (0:15–0:30)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Wide establishing shot of a winter night market in a snowy mountain valley — strings of paper lanterns between a wooden lodge and a frozen lake, penguins and polar bears in scarves, steam from cocoa, a distant narwhal spinning through a hole in the ice, fire-dancers on the lake.`
**Motion:** slow drift across the crowd toward the lake.
**SFX:** soft crowd murmur, lantern creak, distant fire whoosh.

**Narration:**
> "The Taiwan dessert crew had come across the ocean to run a boba stand for one winter in Frostpeak Valley — where penguins snowboard, polar bears ski, and the snow squeaks like a small happy mouse. Almost nobody was watching the crowd. That, as it turned out, was the whole story."

---

## Shot 3 — THE TIP JAR (0:30–0:45)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Ground-level view: a small translucent golden jelly cube with lemon-seed freckles clutches a glass tip jar to her chest as a crowd of tall penguins and bears pushes past, she jiggles nervously, lantern light visible through her body, the crowd closing over her like water.`
**Motion:** handheld wobble, bodies passing close to camera.
**SFX:** coins rattling, muffled crowd.

**Narration:**
> "Aiyu was in charge of the tip jar. Somebody bumped her. Somebody else bumped her. And Aiyu discovered she had no idea who had bumped her, or where they'd gone, or which way was the stand. 'Mm,' said a voice by her knee."

---

## Shot 4 — AUNTIE HÙ (0:45–1:00)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A small brown tea egg with a cracked marbled shell sits on the rim of a bathtub-sized steaming iron pot, arms folded, padded vest with many pockets, watching the crowd; above her a hand-painted wooden sign reads ATTITUDE. SKILLS. PLAN.`
**Motion:** steam curling, slow tilt up from egg to sign.
**SFX:** pot bubbling, soft wood creak.

**Narration:**
> "'I'm Auntie Hù. I sell eggs and I mind the crowd.' 'Nobody minds the crowd,' said Aiyu. 'The crowd minds itself.' 'Mm. Tell me — when they bumped you, which coat were you wearing?' Aiyu was a jelly. She had never worn a coat in her life."

---

## Shot 5 — THE WHITE COAT (1:00–1:15)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A tiny Little Blue Penguin with enormous fluffy white ear-muffs stares down at a glowing phone, humming, while a seal walks directly between her and the camera carrying a fish the size of a sled; she does not notice any of it. Soft white color wash on the edges of frame.`
**Motion:** seal crosses frame slowly; penguin never looks up.
**SFX:** tinny phone music, a fish *flop*.

**Narration:**
> "'Willa,' said Auntie Hù. 'What color is Bīng's flag?' Willa didn't hear. A seal walked past carrying a fish the size of a sled, and Willa did not notice the seal, or the fish, or the sled. 'That,' said Auntie Hù, 'is a white coat. Asleep to the world.'"

---

## Shot 6 — THE FOUR COATS (1:15–1:35)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Four gentle vignettes side by side of the same small jelly cube wearing a tiny coat in each: white coat with eyes closed and headphones; yellow coat head up looking around calmly; orange coat with a raised eyebrow and a small thought-bubble map of exits; red coat mid-step, moving with purpose. Each panel tinted its color.`
**Motion:** panels light up one at a time, left to right.
**SFX:** four soft chimes, rising.

**Narration:**
> "'White — asleep. Yellow — head up, eyes moving, relaxed. That's where you live. Orange — something's off. Make a plan. Red — do the plan.' Auntie Hù shrugged. 'Most nights you wear yellow all evening and nothing happens, and that's a good night. Zhùyì. Pay attention. It's a game you get to win every day.'"

---

## Shot 7 — THREE EXITS (1:35–1:50)

**Visual Prompt:**
`The jelly cube stands at the boba stand and looks around; soft glowing arrows appear over the lodge doors, a gap between two huts, a lake path, and — very small — a slot between two stacked crates of tapioca just her size. A polar bear with twin cubs, a fox, and a walrus with a tray are faintly outlined behind her.`
**Motion:** arrows fade in one by one; slow orbit around Aiyu.
**SFX:** four soft *pings*.

**Narration:**
> "'Three exits. Go.' 'The lodge doors. The gap by the cocoa hut. The lake path.' 'Four.' Behind the stand was a slot between two crates, just wide enough for a small jelly. 'Small people have small exits. Learn yours. Now the hard one — who's watching the crowd instead of the show?'"

---

## Shot 8 — SEEING THROUGH (1:50–2:10)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Aiyu's point of view: the entire crowd rendered semi-transparent like golden jelly, every glowing face turned toward orange fire-dancers on the frozen lake — except one figure in a grey coat two rows back whose eyes point at handbags and an open backpack zipper. The frame edges tint faintly orange.`
**Motion:** slow drift across faces, then lock and hold on the grey coat.
**SFX:** crowd sound drops away to a low hum; one soft heartbeat.

**Narration:**
> "Aiyu had spent her whole life being the see-through one. Now she let her eyes go soft and discovered she could see through a crowd. Every face pointed at the fire — except one. A grey coat, two rows back, looking at pockets. Not playing a game. Shopping. 'Orange,' she whispered. 'Mm. What's your plan?'"

---

## Shot 9 — THE PLAN (2:10–2:30)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Aiyu leans toward the tea egg and whispers; the tea egg turns her head one inch toward a very large snow-white polar bear in a navy jacket with a silver snowflake badge who sets down her cocoa and walks slowly, unhurried, into the crowd. In the background the fire-dancers finish and the crowd cheers, oblivious.`
**Motion:** slow, calm walk; nothing fast.
**SFX:** cocoa mug set down, crowd *ooooh*.

**Narration:**
> "'My plan is not to go over there. My plan is to tell you.' 'Sergeant Solveig. Grey coat. Shopping.' 'Saw him.' What happened next was the least dramatic thing Aiyu had ever seen, and it was the best thing. Nobody shouted. Nobody chased. 'That is what winning looks like,' said Auntie Hù. 'It looks like nothing.'"

---

## Shot 10 — EAR-MUFFS OFF (2:30–2:45)

**Visual Prompt:**
`The tiny blue penguin slowly pushes both fluffy ear-muffs up off her ears and, for the first time, looks around the glowing market with wide eyes — lanterns, crowd, lake, and in the corner a seal quietly stealing a pretzel.`
**Motion:** slow reveal as the muffs lift; her eyes widen.
**SFX:** the world's sound fades *in* — crowd, lanterns, laughter.

**Narration:**
> "'I like the white coat,' Willa admitted. 'It's quiet in there.' 'Yellow's quiet too,' said Auntie Hù. 'It's just quiet with the lights on.' Willa pushed the other ear-muff up and looked — really looked. 'Oh,' she said softly. 'There's a lot out here.'"

---

## Shot 11 — FINNEGAN TRIES YELLOW (2:45–3:00)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A rockhopper penguin with a mochi ball stuck to his crest whips his head left, right, up, down, spotting everything — then lunges for a pretzel and tumbles straight into a giant steaming vat of cocoa; only his feet and the mochi ball stick out. A tea egg reaches in with a ladle.`
**Motion:** fast head turns, comic lunge, slow-motion splash.
**SFX:** *whip whip whip*, big soft *sploosh*.

**Narration:**
> "'YELLOW COAT!' announced Finnegan. 'Lantern! Bear! Snow! PRETZEL—' Splash. 'I see,' said Finnegan, from inside the cocoa, 'that I have made a mistake.' 'Yellow coat,' said Auntie Hù, fishing him out with a ladle, 'also means watching where your own feet are going. We'll work on it.'"

---

## Shot 12 — GOODNIGHT (3:00–3:20)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Late night, lanterns dimmed to embers, snow falling softly over the frozen lake; a small black tapioca pearl asleep hugging a full tip jar, a tiny blue penguin counting exits on her flippers, a translucent jelly cube yawning on the rim of the tea-egg pot, and a tea egg reaching up to turn off the last lantern. Deep blue and gold.`
**Motion:** very slow pull back; the last lantern clicks off; hold on stars.
**SFX:** snow hush, one soft lantern click, then near-silence.

**Narration:**
> "Head up all day, little one — and now, head down on the pillow. You looked at the world tonight, so the world can look after itself for a while. Four exits, five friends, one warm egg. Put on the white coat — the good kind. Sleep."

---

## End Cards (3:20–3:36)

**Card 1 — 🛡️ What We Learned (5 s):** The Four Coats: White = asleep to the world · Yellow = head up, relaxed · Orange = something's off, make a plan · Red = do the plan.
**Card 2 — 🛡️ What We Learned (5 s):** Live in Yellow · Know two ways out of every room · Notice, then tell the right grown-up · Phone down, head up in crowds.
**Card 3 — 💬 Talk About It (6 s):** Which coat do you wear at the store? · Name two ways out of this room. · Who's the right grown-up to tell?

## Production Notes

- **Character consistency:** Aiyu = translucent golden cube, lemon-seed freckles, jiggly. Auntie Hù = small brown marbled tea egg, vest, arms folded. Willa = tiny blue penguin, huge white ear-muffs, purple board. Finnegan = spiky yellow crest, red sticker board. Solveig = broad white polar bear, navy jacket, snowflake badge, eyebrow scar. Grey Coat = ordinary adult, face never clear.
- **Color language:** tint the frame edges faintly for each "coat" — white (5), yellow (7), orange (8). Shot 6 is the legend.
- **Audio:** one warm narrator. Optional dry, lower voice for Auntie Hù's "Mm" and one-liners. Never raise the volume for the gags — bedtime.
- **Music:** music box / soft piano under everything at -20 dB. Drop to a single held note during Shot 8. Fade to -30 dB across Shot 12.
- **Pacing:** Shot 8 is the heart — hold the lock on the grey coat a full 2 seconds before the narration says "Shopping." Shot 9 must feel *boring*; that's the lesson.
- **Stills are fine** for Shots 4, 6, 7, and 12 with a slow Ken Burns zoom.
