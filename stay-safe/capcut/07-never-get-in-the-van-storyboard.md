# Storyboard: Stay Safe Story 07 — Never Get in the Van

**Platform:** CapCut (AI video / Seedance + editor) — see [CAPCUT_GUIDE.md](CAPCUT_GUIDE.md)
**Source story:** [../stories/07-never-get-in-the-van.md](../stories/07-never-get-in-the-van.md)
**Total Shots:** 12
**Estimated Runtime:** ~3:20 + 16 s of end cards
**Art Style (paste on ALL prompts):** `whimsical 3D storybook animation, soft rounded shapes, dusk light — lavender sky, deep-blue snow, warm lantern glow from the market in the distance, gentle snowfall, cozy wonder, no text, no watermark`
**Reference sheets needed:** Boba · Piper Paddlefoot · Auntie Hù · Cleo Coldwater · Bjorn Bigpaws · Sergeant Solveig · The Smiling Stranger (back/blurred only)

> **This is a serious story.** Read the safety rules in the guide (Phase 2) before generating Shots 3–6. The camera never shows the man's face, never shows a hand closing on Boba in close-up, and never shows the inside of the van. Fear lives in the *sound* and in Piper's reaction. If a generated clip looks frightening rather than tense, regenerate it wider and softer.

## Shot 1 — THE RACE (0:00–0:15)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Top of a long one-way plow lane behind a wooden ski lodge at dusk, gear sheds on one side, distant market lanterns glowing at the far end; a small glossy black tapioca pearl with tiny legs stands beside an emperor penguin strapping into a glittery blue snowboard, both grinning competitively.`
**Motion:** playful back-and-forth between the two; penguin pushes off.
**SFX:** snowboard strap *click*, snow squeak.

**Narration:**
> "'I am,' said Boba, 'the fastest thing in this valley.' 'You are a marble,' said Piper Paddlefoot. 'A fast marble.' 'Top of the lane to the market lanterns. Loser refills the tapioca vat.' 'Slide into it!' 'Let's BOUNCE!'"

## Shot 2 — OUT OF CONTROL (0:15–0:28)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A black tapioca pearl pinging wildly off a snowbank, a shed wall, and a startled seal, leaving a shiny sticky trail, careening down an icy lane straight toward a plain white van parked with its back doors open a crack, a puddle of melt under its engine.`
**Motion:** fast comic bounces, then a hard stop two inches from the bumper.
**SFX:** *ping ping ping*, seal *honk*, skid.

**Narration:**
> "Boba pinged off a snowbank, off a shed, off a startled seal — and hit glare ice. He stopped himself two inches from a white van's bumper, arms out, spinning. 'HA! Stopped! Piper, did you see—' 'Hey,' said a friendly voice. 'You dropped this.'"

## Shot 3 — THE SMILING STRANGER (0:28–0:43)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Low angle from a tapioca pearl's height: a tall adult in a puffy grey parka seen only from the chest down, holding out a small purple child's glove in a gloved hand; his face is above the frame, out of focus. The van looms behind him. The lane is empty. Distant market lanterns are very far away.`
**Motion:** almost still; the glove extends slowly toward camera.
**SFX:** wind. Market sounds fade to nothing.

**Narration:**
> "A man stepped around the van. Tall. A puffy grey parka — not the coat from the sled hill last week, but the same warm, easy dad-smile. He held out a purple glove. 'That's not mine,' said Boba. And a small cold part of his brain said: alone. One-way lane. Nobody near. That's orange."

---

## Shot 4 — THE GRAB (0:43–0:55)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Wide shot from far up the lane: the tall figure's shadow falls across the snow and swallows a tiny black dot beside the van; the lane tilts slightly, the crack in the van's back doors glows dark. No faces, no hands in close-up — only the long shadow and the small shape inside it.`
**Motion:** the shadow lengthens; slow tilt of the whole frame toward the van doors.
**SFX:** a single low note; everything else silent.

**Narration:**
> "He never got to red. The man's hand closed around him. A tapioca pearl is small. He fits, with terrible ease, inside a grown-up's fist. And every polite, cheerful atom in him said: don't make a fuss. It's a misunderstanding. Go quietly and it'll be fine."

---

## Shot 5 — AUNTIE HÙ'S VOICE (0:55–1:12)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Warm flashback, golden-tinted: the tea-egg pot at the night market, a small brown marbled tea egg on the rim holding up one finger, a black tapioca pearl sitting cross-legged on a crate listening; the sign ATTITUDE. SKILLS. PLAN. above them. Cozy, safe, lantern-lit.`
**Motion:** gentle, steady; steam curls.
**SFX:** pot bubbling, market murmur returns softly.

**Narration:**
> "And then he heard Auntie Hù. 'Which moment is your best moment? Right now. The grab. You will never have better odds than right now — outside, near people, before the doors close. The car is the worst place in the world. So you don't go along. You make yourself the biggest, loudest, most impossible problem he has ever tried to pick up. Be rude. Be SO rude.'"

---

## Shot 6 — LOOSE AND LOUD (1:12–1:27)

**Visual Prompt:**
`The tapioca pearl transforms from a hard glossy marble into a heavy, slippery, stretching blob of warm sticky goo that squirms and slides, mouth open wide in a shout that sends visible sound-rings rippling down the lane toward the far-off market lanterns. The man is only a grey blur at the frame edge.`
**Motion:** goo-stretch, shout rings expanding outward.
**SFX:** squelch, then Boba's shout — loud, words clear.

**Narration:**
> "So Boba was. First: he went loose — soft and heavy and slippery, a fistful of warm goo that squirmed. Second: he used words. 'JIÙMÌNG! HELP! THIS IS NOT MY DAD! I DON'T KNOW HIM! CALL 911!' Screams sound like playing. Words make people turn around."

## Shot 7 — SLIDE INTO IT (1:27–1:40)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A glittery blue snowboard with silver snowflakes, an emperor penguin crouched on it, rockets down the lane in a spray of snow and strikes the side of a grey-parka knee at the bottom of frame; the black pearl pops free like a cork and arcs into the air. Motion blur, snow spray, no faces.`
**Motion:** speed lines, impact snow-burst, pearl arcing upward.
**SFX:** board *swoosh*, soft *thump*, a *pop*.

**Narration:**
> "The third thing was not Boba's doing. It was a glittery blue snowboard, traveling at a speed Piper would later call 'not a lot of planning, honestly,' hitting the man squarely in the knee. 'Slide into it!' The grip went slack. Boba popped free like a cork and bounced. 'BOBA! Which way?!'"

## Shot 8 — UP THE ONE-WAY LANE (1:40–1:55)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A tapioca pearl and an emperor penguin hauling a snowboard run UPHILL against big painted one-way arrows on the snow, toward the glowing market lanterns; far behind and below them a white van lurches, stalls, and cannot follow. Their mouths are open, shouting.`
**Motion:** tracking shot running with them; van jerks and stops in the background.
**SFX:** panting, boots on snow, engine roar then stall, their shout echoing.

**Narration:**
> "'UP! Up the lane! Toward the market!' 'Boba, that's the wrong way—' 'THAT'S THE POINT!' You run against traffic. He can't follow in a car if you're going the wrong way. You run toward people. You run toward lights. Behind them an engine roared — and could not follow."

## Shot 9 — THE MARKET TURNS (1:55–2:15)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A crowded night market where a hundred heads — penguins, bears, seals, a fox mid-bite — turn at once toward the camera; a seven-foot polar bear rises from a bench and the crowd parts around him; on a shed roof a small macaroni penguin with a yellow crest sketches furiously in a teal notebook: a van, a dent, a license plate.`
**Motion:** the crowd turns as one; slow rise of the bear; cut to the pencil on paper.
**SFX:** crowd noise stops dead, then a rising murmur; pencil scratching.

**Narration:**
> "And the market turned. A hundred heads at once — because a hundred people had heard words. Bjorn Bigpaws simply rose and walked, and the crowd parted like snow off a plow. And Cleo, on the shed roof, had done the one thing she was best at. She had drawn it. The van. The dent. The license plate."

## Shot 10 — THE WHY (2:15–2:40)

**Visual Prompt:**
`The steps of a cocoa hut at night: a shaking emperor penguin staring at her own snowboard, a slightly gooey tapioca pearl beside her, and a small brown tea egg sitting between them setting down two warm tea eggs. Lantern light, snow, very still and warm.`
**Motion:** almost none; steam rising from the eggs.
**SFX:** wind hush, soft ceramic clink.

**Narration:**
> "'Auntie Hù — why does someone do that? What do they want?' She didn't make it smaller than it was. 'A grown-up who takes a child away from where they're safe wants to hurt them. Sometimes their bodies, in the ways no grown-up is ever allowed to touch a kid. That is why the answer is never go quietly and it'll be okay. It won't. So you fight there. Outside. Where people can hear you. And you never, ever get in the car.'"

## Shot 11 — RARE (2:40–2:58)

**Visual Prompt:**
`Wide, warm shot: the whole crew on the cocoa-hut steps — a huge polar bear, a walrus with enormous cocoa mugs, a macaroni penguin with her sketchbook, the emperor penguin, the tapioca pearl, and the tea egg — under swaying lanterns, the market glowing gold behind them, snow falling. Safe.`
**Motion:** slow push-in on the group; lanterns sway.
**SFX:** cocoa pour, a small laugh, lantern creak.

**Narration:**
> "'That's the scariest thing anyone's ever told me.' 'Mm. Now tell me the other thing. It's rare. You'll walk ten thousand roads and nothing will happen on nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine. Being ready isn't being afraid. Being ready is what lets you not be afraid.' 'For the record,' said Boba, 'I was winning.' 'You have to refill the vat.' 'We'll both do the vat.'"

## Shot 12 — GOODNIGHT (2:58–3:20)

**Visual Prompt:**
`A tiny bunk room behind a boba stand at night, fairy lights glowing; a black tapioca pearl asleep with his tiny arms wrapped around himself, an emperor penguin asleep in the next bunk with a glittery blue snowboard leaning beside her, snow drifting past a small window. Deep blue and warm amber.`
**Motion:** very slow pull back; fairy lights dim.
**SFX:** two slow breaths, snow hush, silence.

**Narration:**
> "'Piper? You know how you launch before you plan? Don't ever fix that.' A long pause. 'Slide into it.' 'Let's bounce,' Boba whispered back. Little one — your legs know the way home. Your voice knows the words. Tonight there's nothing to run from. Only the pillow. Sleep."

## End Cards (3:20–3:36)

**Card 1 — 🛡️ What We Learned (5 s):** The grab is your best moment — fight *there*, in the open · Never, ever get in the car · Go heavy and loose, not stiff.
**Card 2 — 🛡️ What We Learned (5 s):** Scream *words*: "HELP! THIS IS NOT MY DAD! CALL 911!" · Run against traffic, toward people and lights · Be rude, be loud, be a problem.
**Card 3 — 💬 Talk About It (6 s):** What would you shout? · Which way would you run from our street? · Why is the car the worst place?

## Production Notes

- **Character consistency:** Boba = glossy black-brown marble, huge eyes, tiny limbs, sticky trail; goes to translucent goo in Shot 6 only. Piper = emperor penguin, goggles on forehead, glittery blue board with silver snowflakes. Auntie Hù = small brown marbled tea egg, vest. Cleo = macaroni penguin, yellow crest, teal sketchbook. Bjorn = huge white polar bear, forest-green skis. Smiling Stranger = grey parka, **chest-down or blurred only, every time.**
- **The dark beats (Shots 3–4):** wide, still, silent. The frame tilt and the long shadow carry it. If the generator gives you a clear face or a close-up hand, throw it out.
- **Audio arc:** market sound *drains away* across Shots 2→3→4 to near silence, returns in the golden flashback (5), explodes in the shout (6–8), and swells to full warmth by Shot 9. The sound design *is* the story.
- **Music:** none in Shots 3–4 (one low held note only). Warm strings from Shot 9. Music box for 11–12, fading to -30 dB.
- **Pacing:** Shot 10 is long on purpose — do not rush Auntie Hù's answer. Let the narration breathe with 1-second pauses at the periods.
- **Stills are fine** for Shots 3, 4, 5, 10, and 12 with a slow Ken Burns move; the action shots (2, 6, 7, 8) want real motion.
- **Parent note for the video description:** *"This story names why abductions happen, in one plain sentence, without detail. Watch it with your kid the first time."*
