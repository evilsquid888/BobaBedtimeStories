# BobaBedtimeStories

A growing family of cozy, funny, food-themed **bedtime stories for ages 9–12**. Each story stars a sentient food, dessert, or fruit character on a warm nighttime adventure, weaves in light local-language vocabulary, and ends with a soft **Goodnight Blessing**. Tone: Roald Dahl meets a vibrant night market — silly but never mean, warm but never saccharine, and always bedtime-safe.

## Features
- ~1,800–2,000 words per story; fun starts in the first 150 words
- Vivid food characters with catchphrases and consistent voices
- Local-language vocabulary woven in naturally (term + pronunciation + English on first use)
- Joyful, never-scary "becoming a treat" moments; gentle cute folklore only
- 3 AI image prompts per story for illustration
- Cross-over guests from sibling universes (Penguinsnowboard, Uncle Piggle Wiggle)
- A **Stay Safe** arc: 15 whimsical-but-serious stories teaching real self-protection skills (awareness, tricky people, body safety, abduction, bullies, Run–Hide–Fight, fights and the law, police stops, lawyers) with the Frostpeak penguins and bears
