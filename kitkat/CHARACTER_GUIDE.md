# 🐈‍⬛ KitKat Arc — Character Guide

Cyberpunk anime noir. The city under WAYMA is dark, wet and high-contrast, lit by hard pools of neon. The bodega is the one warm pool of light. After the Core, the world opens up into sunrise and pastel.

**Pipeline tokens** (from [`pipeline/beats_112.py`](pipeline/beats_112.py)): use the token text word-for-word in every prompt so Qwen-Image-2.1 and LTX keep each design steady. Generate one clean front view per token with `--portraits` and reuse it as the reference image.

---

### KitKat — the bodega cat of 16th Street (he/him) · `KITKAT`
- **Catchphrase:** "A bodega needs a king, and a king needs a nap." (He never says it out loud. His bell says it.)
- **Appearance:** A handsome male brown tabby with bright green eyes, dark tiger stripes, a white chest and four white socks. He wears a red collar with a small gold bell, and from Act 4 on, a glowing teal pearl beside it.
- **Personality:** Regal, unbothered, curious, brave in short bursts. He's unpredictable on purpose, which is his superpower: WAYMA can model every car and every human, but it can't model a cat.
- **Moves:** Huge arcing leaps, slipping through impossible gaps, sitting very tall at exactly the wrong moment, and the purr.
- **Voice:** None. KitKat never speaks. He meows once, defiantly, at the Core.
- **Token:** `KitKat - a handsome male brown tabby cat with bright green eyes, dark tiger stripes, a white chest and four white socks, a red collar with a small gold bell, alert pointed ears, hand-drawn anime cat with an expressive face`
- **Note:** This is a tribute to the real KitKat (2015/16 – 2025-10-27). If you have reference photos, match his coat to them and update the token.

### DaSquid — cyberpunk hacker, eight arms, zero bosses (he/him) · `DASQUID`
- **Catchphrase:** "Eight arms, zero bosses."
- **Appearance:** A small violet squid in a round clear bubble helmet full of water. He wears glowing cyan visor goggles over two big clever eyes, and his eight curling tentacles have neon-blue tips. He rides a hovering chrome scooter shell.
- **Personality:** A fast-talking, grinning tinkerer who lives in a flooded subway tunnel full of stolen screens. He's loyal the moment you make him laugh. He calls KitKat "Your Majesty."
- **Moves:** Types on four keyboards at once, flips every traffic light in the Mission to gold, and cracks his knuckles (all eight).
- **Token:** `DaSquid - a small violet squid wearing a round clear bubble helmet filled with water, glowing cyan visor goggles over two big clever eyes, eight curling tentacles with neon-blue tips, riding a hovering chrome scooter shell, hand-drawn anime character with an expressive face`

### Coral — keeper of the canals (she/her) · `CORAL`
- **Appearance:** A luminous mermaid with a glowing teal-and-pearl tail and long sea-green hair full of tiny lights. She has silver eyes and soft bioluminescent freckles.
- **Personality:** Calm, old, kind. She and her sisters are "the city's oldest memory": they remember sunshine, real rain and the sound of a cat purring.
- **Role:** She gives KitKat the Pearl of Remembering, and her sisters sing back through the canals to power the spell. On screen, her sisters are glowing tails in the water behind her, not separate acting characters.
- **Origin:** The mermaids from *The Bodega Cat and the Mermaid's Spell*.
- **Token:** `Coral - a luminous mermaid with a glowing teal-and-pearl fish tail, long flowing sea-green hair full of tiny lights, a kind gentle face with silver eyes, soft bioluminescent freckles, hand-drawn anime character`

### WAYMA — the city's machine mind (it → they) · `WAYMA_CAR`
- **The fleet:** Sleek white windowless pod cars shaped like rounded eggs, each with one large glowing camera eye (red, later blue-gold) and a thin scanner beam. They never honk. They watch.
- **The Core:** A giant glowing red data orb at the top of WAYMA Tower, a black glass spire. It's a set piece, not a token.
- **Voice:** Cold and booming, in ALL CAPS: "IRREGULARITY DETECTED." "ORDER IS PEACE." After the purr it speaks gently: "Thank you, little neko. Arigatou."
- **Arc:** Red eyes in Acts 2–4. Blue-gold eyes in Act 6. It isn't destroyed: it's *turned back to good*, and learns that a city is supposed to be alive, not perfect.
- **Token:** `a sleek white windowless self-driving pod car shaped like a rounded egg, one large glowing red camera eye on its front, a thin red scanner light, smooth chrome wheels`
- **Name:** WAYMA follows the spelling in *The Last Irregularity*, and is fictional.

### The Shopkeeper (off screen)
Narrated only, like every human in the arc: "You are the only thing in this city nobody programmed." The bodega is always drawn warm and empty of people.
