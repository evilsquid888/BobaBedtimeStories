# How-To — Character Guide

New characters introduced in the How-To collection (most stories also star the existing Taiwan & Japan casts as recipe experts).

### Bao Bao bread/bao baker — bao and bread
- **Catchphrase:** "Good things rise with time."
- **Appearance:** Pale-gold pillowy bun, floury apron.
- **Voice:** Cozy baker; patience and rising.

### Hong Hong candied-fruit friend — candied fruit on a stick
- **Catchphrase:** "Shine outside, sweet inside."
- **Appearance:** Ruby fruits in crackly glass sugar.
- **Voice:** Bright, crackly, sparkly.

### Xia Xia shrimp-fishing guide — prawn
- **Catchphrase:** "Patience reels in the good stuff."
- **Appearance:** Blue-grey prawn, whiskers, fishing vest.
- **Voice:** Patient, playful fishing wisdom.

### Mala Malasada doughnut friend — sugar doughnut
- **Catchphrase:** "Roll me in sugar."
- **Appearance:** Golden hole-less doughnut, poofy sugar.
- **Voice:** Bouncy, sunshiny, messy-happy.

### Maccha-chan matcha whisk friend — matcha
- **Catchphrase:** "Whisk slow, froth grows."
- **Appearance:** Jade tea-spirit, whisk body, foam cap.
- **Voice:** Serene; stillness and froth.

### Gan Zhe sugarcane-juice friend — sugarcane juice
- **Catchphrase:** "Squeeze the day."
- **Appearance:** Tall green-gold cane, leaf hair.
- **Voice:** Easygoing, sun-warmed, slow-sweet.

### Deng Deng paper-lantern friend — paper lantern
- **Catchphrase:** "A little light glows warmest."
- **Appearance:** Red lantern, soft glow, gold tassels.
- **Voice:** Soft, glowy, shy but warm.

### Sa Sa spam-musubi friend — spam musubi
- **Catchphrase:** "Pack it neat, share it sweet."
- **Appearance:** Rice block, nori belt, Spam slice.
- **Voice:** Practical, picnic-cheerful.

## Pronoun Reference (canonical, used in every story)
- **She/her:** Boba, Bao Bao, Hong Dou
- **He/him:** Sa Sa, Tama, Niko, Tai, Mango, Coconut Jelly, Tang Yuan, Gan Zhe, Xia Xia, Hong Hong, Dou-Da, Deng Deng, Dan, Feng, Cong Cong, Digua, Zhima, Cao, Xiao Long
- **Note:** The recurring human grown-up across the collection is **Auntie Mei** (stories 8, 10, 15); hot, sharp, and frying jobs always go to a real grown-up.
