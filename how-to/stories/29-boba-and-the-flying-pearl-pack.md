# Story 29: Boba and the Flying Pearls

**Main Character:** Boba (tapioca pearl)
**Guest Characters:** Piper Paddlefoot
**Setting:** A boba workshop
**Theme:** Teamwork
**Word Spotlight:** **boba** (BOH-bah) — big tapioca pearls

---

Boba was the bounciest little pearl in all of Taiwan, and today she was so excited she very nearly bounced clean out of her own glossy skin. She was small and round and the deep shiny brown of a polished chestnut, and when she got *truly* thrilled she boinged straight up off the counter like a hiccup made of jelly. *Boing.* "Today," she announced to the empty workshop, "is the GREATEST day."

The boba workshop was warm and bright, full of fruit baskets and tall clear cups and a friendly humming blender that looked like a small robot waiting to dance. And down the lane, *flap-flap-flap-WHUMP*, came her guest of honor.

A tubby little penguin tumbled through the door and skidded across the floor on her belly. She had big webbed feet, sleepy eyes, and a determined wobble.

"I am Piper," she huffed, scrambling upright, "Piper Paddlefoot. I flew all the way from the cold place. Well — I *fell* most of the way. Penguins don't really fly." She blinked. "Are we making the chewy bubble drink? I heard there were bubbles."

"Not just any bubbles," said Boba, beaming. "**Boba** (BOH-bah) — big tapioca pearls. The biggest, chewiest, slurpiest pearls in the world — and *I'm* one of them! Today, Piper, you and me are making a fruit boba slushie. Together. As a TEAM."

Piper saluted with one flipper and immediately fell over.

---

"A team," Boba explained, helping Piper back up, "means nobody does the whole thing alone. One pearl can't shake a cup. One penguin can't blend the ice. But together?" She bounced. "Together we're unstoppable."

"Unstoppable," Piper repeated solemnly, then sneezed.

"Now," said Boba, "every great team needs a plan. So here's ours." She hopped onto a recipe card and read it out like a tiny bouncing general. "First: cook and sweeten the pearls. Second: blend the fruit and the ice into slush. Third: build the cup — pearls, then slush, then milk. Fourth: cap it and shake it and SLURP it. Four steps. Easy as falling over."

"I am *excellent* at falling over," said Piper, beaming. "Finally, a job I was born for."

First things first: the pearls. Boba waved cheerfully at a big pot bubbling away on the stove with a happy *gloop, gloop, gloop*. "I've got cousins in that pot," she said proudly. A few boba bobbed up and waved back. "Hi, Auntie Glug! Hi, Cousin Plop!"

But the pot was hot — *very* hot, the kind of hot that makes your whiskers wilt and your flippers say *no thank you*. And the stove was no place for one small pearl and one wobbly penguin.

"Piper," said Boba importantly, "this calls for the most powerful teammate of all. We must summon... a **Grown-Up**."

"OoooOOOoh," said Piper, deeply impressed.

A Grown-Up appeared, the way Grown-Ups magically do whenever there's a snack nearby. This one had a flowery apron and warm crinkly eyes. "Pearls, please!" said Boba. "Cooked soft and chewy, then a nice splash of sweet syrup to wake up their flavor!"

The Grown-Up stirred the bubbling pot so the big pearls swirled like little brown planets, soft and shiny. While they cooked, Boba and Piper did the very best thing a team can do while waiting: they cheered. "Go, pearls, go!" chanted Boba. "Swim, swim, swim!" Piper waved two tiny pom-poms she'd made out of bits of fluff, even though penguins aren't generally known for their pom-pom skills, and dropped them roughly nine times.

"Why do they take so long?" Piper whispered.

"Because good chewy things can't be rushed," said Boba wisely. "A pearl that's hurried is a pearl that's crunchy. And nobody wants a crunchy pearl. That's just a pebble with dreams."

When the pearls were perfectly tender, the Grown-Up scooped them into a bowl — carefully, away from little friends — and stirred in a glossy spoonful of sweet syrup until every pearl wore a shimmering coat.

"They look like they're going to a *party*," whispered Piper.

"They are," said Boba. "It's their party. The whole cup is invited."

Piper leaned over the warm pearls, sniffed, and her tummy made a sound like a foghorn. *BWAAARP.* She turned the color of a strawberry. "That," she said with great dignity, "was the pearls. Not me."

"Of course," said Boba kindly. "Definitely the pearls."

Now came Piper's big job. "TEAMMATE," Boba declared, "to the fruit station!" On the counter waited a hill of bright frozen fruit — strawberries and mango and a heap of glittering ice. "We blend the fruit with the ice," said Boba, "and it turns into a frosty, fruity slush!"

Piper's sleepy eyes went wide. "Cold things?" she gasped. "Cold things are my *specialty.* I am a penguin. This is my moment." She marched up to the blender and hugged it. "Hello, friend. We're going to do something beautiful."

The Grown-Up dropped the frozen fruit and the ice inside, snapped the lid on tight (because a blender is sharp and spinny, and *that* is a grown-up's job), and pressed the button.

*VRRRRRRRR-RRRRR-RRRR!*

The blender roared. The fruit tumbled. The ice crackled and crunched and swirled into a thick, frosty pink-and-orange slush, cold as a snow day and smelling like the sweetest summer.

"BLIZZARD!" hollered Piper joyfully, throwing both flippers in the air. "It's a tiny FRUIT BLIZZARD! This is the best day of my whole entire life!" She did a little waddle-dance, slipped on absolutely nothing at all, and skated halfway across the floor on her belly, shouting "*Wheeeee!*" the whole way.

She slid to a gentle stop against a sack of flour and lay there, blinking up at the ceiling, deeply content. "I have decided," she announced, "that the cold place sent me here for a reason. And the reason is slush." A little puff of flour drifted down and landed on her beak like snow. "See? Even the workshop agrees."

Boba laughed so hard she boinged off the counter and landed in a fruit basket. "See?" she giggled, climbing out wearing a tiny blueberry hat. "*Teamwork.* You handle the cold. I handle the chewy. Now let's put it all together!"

The grand assembly — Boba's very favorite part. She lined up a tall, clear cup like a stage waiting for a show.

"Step one," she said, "the pearls go in FIRST. Right at the bottom, like treasure in a chest." The Grown-Up spooned the glossy boba into the cup, and they slid down and painted gorgeous dark stripes up the sides.

"Treasure," Piper breathed, pressing her beak to the cup. "I would guard this with my life."

"Step two," said Boba, "pour the fruit slush right over the top!" Piper waddled up proudly and *helped* — well, she helped *watch*, which is also a job — while the Grown-Up poured the frosty slush in. It tumbled over the pearls in a glittering pink-orange avalanche, and the whole cup looked like a sunset that had decided to take a nap on a snowbank.

"Step three," said Boba, "a splash of creamy milk!" In swirled the milk, soft and cloudy, marbling through the slush in dreamy ribbons.

"And step FOUR," said Boba, eyes sparkling, "the part that makes it *magic.* We cap it..." The Grown-Up popped a lid on tight. "...and we SHAKE it!"

Now, here is the thing about teamwork: sometimes it gets a little *bouncy*. Boba grabbed one side of the cup. Piper grabbed the other with both flippers. "Ready?" said Boba. "Ready!" said Piper. "SHAKE!"

*Shaka-shaka-shaka-SHAKA!*

They shook with their whole bodies. Boba boinged. Piper flapped. The cup wiggled and the slush sloshed and the pearls did happy somersaults inside — and then Piper, who shook a *touch* too enthusiastically, let go at exactly the wrong moment. The capped cup floated up, up into the air, turning slow circles like a frosty comet.

"The pearls are flying," gasped Piper, delighted.

Boba launched herself off the counter — *BOING!* — flippers and pearl both stretching high — and *together*, exactly together, they caught it. *Plop!* Safe in four little hands and one round pearl belly. The cup was still sealed. Not a single drop spilled.

They stared at each other. Then they burst out laughing so hard the Grown-Up had to lean on the counter.

"We," wheezed Piper, "are the greatest team in the history of slushies."

"Told you," said Boba. "Unstoppable."

When they popped the lid at last, the fruit boba slushie was *perfect:* frosty pink-orange slush, dreamy milk ribbons, and the big dark boba waiting at the bottom like a chewy treasure hunt. The Grown-Up slid in two fat straws — one for a pearl, one for a penguin.

"Teammates," said Boba softly, holding up the cup, "share the very first sip."

So they did. *Bloop!* went a fat pearl up Piper's straw, and her sleepy eyes flew wide as moons — cold and sweet and fruity and chewy all at once. "It's a BLIZZARD," she sighed happily, "with PRIZES inside."

And the two of them sat together as the workshop glowed warm and golden, passing the cup back and forth, slurping and giggling, a penguin and a pearl who had learned that the best treats — and the best days — are the ones you make with a friend. Slowly the giggles softened into yawns, and the blender hummed itself quiet, and the last slurp went down slow and sweet. Outside, the night settled over the lane like a cozy lid on a cup, and inside, two sleepy teammates leaned together, warm and full and still.

## How to Make a fruit boba slushie 🍽️
*Ask a grown-up to help with anything hot or sharp!*
1. Have a grown-up cook the tapioca pearls in boiling water until soft and chewy, then stir in a little sweet syrup until coated and glossy.
2. Have a grown-up blend frozen fruit and ice together (lid on tight!) until it becomes a thick, frosty slush.
3. Spoon the pearls into the bottom of a tall cup.
4. Pour the fruit slush over the pearls, then add a splash of milk.
5. Put the lid on, shake it up well, add a wide straw, and share the first sip with a friend!
