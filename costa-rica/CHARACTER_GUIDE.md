# Costa Rica — Character Guide

New & notable characters in the Costa Rica bedtime-story collection.

### Gallo — A sentient grain of fried rice from gallo pinto (Costa Rica's national rice-and-beans breakfast)
- **Catchphrase:** "Rise and pinto!"
- **Appearance:** A plump, glossy white rice grain speckled with golden fried bits and tiny flecks of red pepper and cilantro. Wears a little woven palm hat. Always cheerful, slightly toasty around the edges from the skillet.
- **Voice:** Sunny, chatty, optimistic morning-person energy. Finishes sentences with a little hum.

### Pinto — A sentient black bean from gallo pinto, Gallo's inseparable partner
- **Catchphrase:** "Better together, mae."
- **Appearance:** A small, deep-glossy black bean, smooth and round with a tiny pale stripe like a smile. A bit dusty with bean-broth shine. Carries a tiny wooden spoon.
- **Voice:** Calm, warm, grounding. The steady half of the duo. Speaks softly and wisely.

### Lala the Tres Leches — A square of tres leches cake (sponge soaked in three milks)
- **Catchphrase:** "Soak it all in!"
- **Appearance:** A soft, spongey pale-gold cake square, glistening and dripping with sweet milk, topped with a wobbly cloud of whipped cream and a single cinnamon dot. Leaves little milky footprints. Squishy and huggable.
- **Voice:** Dreamy, gentle, a touch dramatic; gasps at beautiful things. Speaks in soft swoons.

### Coco the Granizado — A creamy Costa Rican granizado (shaved ice with syrup and powdered milk)
- **Catchphrase:** "Stay frosty, stay sweet!"
- **Appearance:** A tall fluffy mound of rainbow-syruped shaved ice in a paper cone, dusted with powdered milk like snow, wearing tiny round sunglasses. Constantly half-melting and refreezing, sparkles in sunlight.
- **Voice:** Cool, breezy, beach-skater vibe. Loud and fun. Says everything is 'tuanis'.

### Tato the Patacon — A patacon — a twice-fried, smashed green plantain disc
- **Catchphrase:** "Smash now, worry never!"
- **Appearance:** A flat, round, golden-crispy smashed plantain with crinkled crunchy edges and a soft chewy middle. Built sturdy and a little flat-footed, with a dusting of salt. Strong and dependable, a tiny bit clumsy.
- **Voice:** Earnest, brave, a gentle goofball. Speaks in short determined bursts.

### Don Café — A roasted Costa Rican coffee bean from the highlands
- **Catchphrase:** "Good things brew slowly."
- **Appearance:** A dark, glossy roasted coffee bean with a deep center crease, wearing a tiny straw farmer's hat and carrying a miniature wooden walking stick. Smells wonderful and warm. Wise village elder energy.
- **Voice:** Slow, kind, grandfatherly. Tells little proverbs. Smells of morning.

### Tamalita — A banana-leaf Christmas tamale (masa with filling steamed in banana leaf)
- **Catchphrase:** "Wrapped with love!"
- **Appearance:** A cozy little masa bundle wrapped and tied in a glossy green banana leaf like a present, with a string bow. Steam puffs out when she giggles. Warm, festive, smells of the holidays.
- **Voice:** Bubbly, warm, festive. Loves traditions and family. Giggles in little steam puffs.

### Flan the Coconut Flan — A coconut flan (custard with caramel and coconut)
- **Catchphrase:** "Steady, steady... ooh, wobbly!"
- **Appearance:** A jiggly pale-gold dome of coconut custard glistening under a shiny caramel drizzle, flecked with toasted coconut. Wobbles dramatically when nervous, polished and elegant. A bit of a worrier with a big heart.
- **Voice:** Polite, slightly fussy, refined but kind. Wobbles into nervous giggles.

### Slowy the Sloth — Not a food — a friendly three-toed sloth of the rainforest (animal friend)
- **Catchphrase:** "Puuura... viiida..."
- **Appearance:** A fuzzy, mossy-furred sloth with a permanent gentle smile, long slow arms, and sleepy half-moon eyes. Moves at dreamy slow-motion speed. Always relaxed, the embodiment of Pura Vida.
- **Voice:** Verrry slow, drawn-out, peaceful and wise. Never in a hurry.

### Tico the Toucan — Not a food — a keel-billed toucan of the jungle (animal friend)
- **Catchphrase:** "Beak the news!"
- **Appearance:** A glossy black bird with a huge rainbow-striped banana-shaped beak, bright yellow throat, and twinkling eyes. Bouncy, loud, a flying town-crier who knows all the jungle gossip.
- **Voice:** Loud, fast, playful chatterbox. Announces everything like big news.

### Estrella the Sea Turtle — Not a food — a gentle green sea turtle of Tortuguero (animal friend)
- **Catchphrase:** "Follow the moon home."
- **Appearance:** A graceful sea turtle with a mossy-green star-patterned shell, kind ancient eyes, and slow flipper waves. Calm and motherly, smells of salt and sea breeze.
- **Voice:** Gentle, ancient, soothing like ocean waves. Speaks in slow, kind tides.

### Cajeta the Coconut Fudge — A cajeta de coco — a coconut-fudge ball
- **Catchphrase:** "Sweet and round, watch me bound!"
- **Appearance:** A small round ball of golden coconut fudge, chewy and dense, rolled in toasted coconut shreds so it looks fuzzy. Tiny, energetic, sweet to the core, bounces when excited.
- **Voice:** Quick, peppy, sugar-rush enthusiasm. Talks fast and giggly.

### KitKat the Bodega Cat — Not a food — the beloved tabby cat of the corner soda (animal friend; story 51). A gentle tribute to a real bodega cat.
- **Catchphrase:** "There's all the time there is."
- **Appearance:** A plump striped tabby the color of toasted toast, with rings around his tail like a tree, a small soft notch in one ear, and big sleepy golden eyes. The slow warm heart of the corner soda, with a purr you feel in your chest. After story 51, his warm glow lives on inside every gentle screen and soft light in town.
- **Voice:** Slow, warm, and unhurried; speaks in cozy rumbles — quick exactly when it matters. Now purrs the whole town toward sleep from inside every warm glow.

### Doña Cy — Not a food — the kind dance teacher who runs Cy Dance Works (human friend; story 52)
- **Catchphrase:** "You danced anyway — that's the only step that matters."
- **Appearance:** Tall and graceful, with a long flowing skirt the color of sunset. Moves through a room like a slow warm river, calming everyone's wobbles just by being near.
- **Voice:** Soft, encouraging, never gives speeches when a smile will do. Calls her students "mis amores."
