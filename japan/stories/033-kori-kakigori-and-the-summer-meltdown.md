# Story 33: Kori Kakigori and the Summer Meltdown

**Main Character:** Kori Kakigori (kakigori shaved ice with strawberry syrup)
**Guest Characters:** Momo Mochi, Maru Matcha, Tako & Yaki
**Setting:** A blazing-hot summer beach boardwalk
**Theme:** Friends help you keep your cool
**Japanese Spotlight:** **atsui** (AH-tsoo-ee) — hot
**Cameo:** Godzilla (Toho) — wades ashore wanting shaved ice, sniffs Kori with hair-dryer breath, then fans him with his tail and blows the whole umbrella fort down the boardwalk; walk-on: Squirtle (Pokémon)

---

On the hottest morning of the summer, Kori Kakigori wobbled onto the boardwalk and screamed. A diva scream.

"It's **atsui** (AH-tsoo-ee) — hot!" he wailed, flinging a frosty arm across his syrup-ruby forehead.

"I am made of ICE. Today is made of FIRE. Do the math, feathers!" he told a seagull.

The seagull skipped the math, stole a fry, and left.

"I need shade," Kori announced, sunglasses sliding down his slope. "Shade, or I become a strawberry-flavored memory."

---

He wobbled to a bench. The bench was hot. He sizzled right off it.

He wobbled to a tree. The tree was cardboard. It fell over flat. Fwap.

He wobbled to an umbrella. A small angry crab was wearing it as a hat.

"Excuse me, small angry sir," said Kori. The crab pinched the air. Kori retreated, dripping pink.

Drip, drip, drip. "Goodbye, cruel boardwalk," he whispered. "Remember me as the COOLEST."

---

"KORI!" Momo Mochi came bouncing up, snow-white and pillowy. "You're getting THIN!"

"Squish together, stay together!" She hugged him. "OOP. BRR. Okay. Hugs later."

Maru Matcha studied the pink puddle with her calm half-moon eyes, tapping her whisk. Fwip.

"Kori is melting because he is in the sun," said Maru. "The answer to in-the-sun is not-in-the-sun."

"That's the smartest thing—" said Tako.

"—I've ever heard a tea say," finished Yaki. "SHADE!" They high-fived so hard they bounced.

---

Momo dragged four striped umbrellas, arms stretched so long she looked like a marshmallow octopus.

Foomp, foomp, foomp. The twins bounced up the poles, "CLIMBING-falling!", and strung a stripy blanket across the top.

Then the sea rose. Not a wave. A mountain of dark charcoal with fins glowing soft blue.

Godzilla waded ashore, dripping, small kind eyes fixed on the one thing he wanted. Shaved ice.

He leaned down and sniffed Kori. His breath was a hair dryer. Kori lost an entire inch.

"SIR," squeaked Kori. "I am a DESSERT, not a SAUNA guest."

Godzilla, sorry, fanned Kori with his tail. WHOOMP. Umbrellas, blanket and twins sailed into the fry stand.

Godzilla rumbled an apology, sat down in the shallows, and sulked, steam curling off his fins.

---

"Bigger," said Maru calmly. "Heavier. And we need that crab's umbrella."

Maru walked to the crab. She held out a popsicle. The crab considered. The crab accepted.

Umbrella number five, with a tiny crab hat on top, pinned the fort down for good.

Momo squished her whole self across the roof as a weight. "I'm a blanket now. Hello."

Snow-cone ice piled inside, wet seaweed over the roof, and gently they helped Kori wobble in.

---

Inside it was dim and blue-cool, like underwater without the wet part.

"Oh," said Kori. Then, louder, "Ohhhh." His puddle stopped spreading. His milk swirl perked up.

"I am MAJESTIC again," declared Kori, arms wide. "A frosty QUEEN of the boardwalk! I am eternal!"

"You're dripping a little on the left," said Maru.

"That," said Kori smoothly, "is called flair."

---

"I thought I'd melt all by myself," said Kori softly. "But you can't keep your cool alone."

"That's what friends are FOR," said Momo, squishing against his cold side. "Brr."

By sunset, a long droopy line of hot treat-folk had gathered outside the fort, fanning themselves.

First in line: a small blue turtle in round black sunglasses, flat on the warm sand. "Squirtle."

"Finally," said Kori, lowering his own sunglasses. "Someone who understands eyewear. Front of the line, darling."

One ruby-pink spoonful at a time, Kori cooled the whole boardwalk, laughing the entire time.

He melted into joy, which is a completely different and much sweeter thing from melting away.

The sea went melty orange and pink, Kori's two favorite flavors. "Stay cool, sweeties. The together kind."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
Whatever felt too hot today, too loud, too fast, too much, you don't have to keep your cool all by yourself. Friends will build you shade. Snuggle down where it's just-right, and let your worries firm up like frost. Goodnight.
