# Story 70: The Dango Three and the Doll Festival

**Main Character:** The Dango Three (Pinku, Shiro, Cha)
**Guest Characters:** A tiny forgotten doll, dusty and faded, found under the bottom step
**Setting:** A cozy home decorated for Hinamatsuri, the Doll Festival, in spring
**Theme:** Everyone deserves a place on the shelf
**Japanese Spotlight:** **hina-matsuri** (hee-nah-mah-tsoo-ree) — doll festival
**Cameo:** Hello Kitty (Sanrio) — sits among the court dolls posing as one, waves whenever only Shiro is looking, and hops a step higher every time the others turn around; walk-on: Kuromi (Sanrio)

---

On a foggy spring morning, three little dumplings on one stick hopped in and tipped to a stop.

"WHOA," said Pinku, the bold pink one on top. "Look at ALL these dolls!"

"Too many dolls," whispered Shiro, the shy white middle, hiding behind nobody, as nobody was there.

"Dolls can't blink," said Cha, the clever green bottom one. "Which is a very good blinking record."

It was the morning before **hina-matsuri** (hee-nah-mah-tsoo-ree) — the doll festival, and the seven-step shelf stood empty.

"Whole shelf before the rice is steamed," said Pinku. "Top to bottom. Let's HOP."

---

A picture-chart leaned on the shelf: a drawing of where every doll goes. "I love order," sighed Shiro.

"Bottom first, so nothing topples," said Cha. Hop, tip, hop: teeny tables, teeny chests, a teeny ox-cart.

"**Kawaii** (ka-wa-ii) — cute," sighed Shiro. "A little cart for little errands." He forgot to be scared.

Three tiny musicians went on the middle step. The tiny drum slipped and bounced down, boing, boing.

Pinku caught it in her teeth. "Mmf," said Pinku. "Nobody saw that."

---

Last came the Emperor and Empress, silk robes flowing, balanced on the dango like hats.

Step five. Step six. The stick wobbled. "Don't look down," said Cha. "Specifically you, Shiro."

Step seven. Dolls under the golden panel. Perfect. Then the stick tipped, and three dango bounced down.

They landed in the cloth. It flipped up. Beneath the bottom step sat one more doll, dusty, cobwebbed.

"Oh," said Shiro softly. "She's been down here all alone."

---

"Not on the chart," said Cha, checking twice. "Not an Empress, not a musician. Nobody official."

"I know that feeling," said Shiro, very small. "Everybody has their spot, and I'm the extra one."

"You're NOT extra," said Pinku, so loudly the stick jumped. "We'll FIND you a step."

---

Top step: the Emperor and Empress leaned together, happy. "No squeezing between THEM," said Pinku.

Step three: court ladies, and a white cat with a red bow. "Not on the chart," said Cha.

The dango hopped to step four. Behind them, one small white paw lifted and waved. Shiro turned. Stillness.

"She WAVED," squeaked Shiro. Pinku looked. Hello Kitty sat still as a teacup, now holding the tiny drum.

"Dolls can't wave," said Cha. "Excellent not-waving record." Now the ox-cart sat in Hello Kitty's lap.

Shiro stared hard. Hello Kitty hopped one step up and froze mid-hop, one paw out, bow perfectly straight.

When Pinku turned, Hello Kitty sat on the top step beside the Empress, waving at Shiro only.

Middle step: drumless. Bottom step: furniture tipped like dominoes. "Everything's MOVED," wailed Pinku.

---

Then Cha, orderly Cha, hopped onto the chart and sat on it.

"A chart is just an idea," said Cha. "No good idea leaves a friend in the dust."

"What if she doesn't squeeze onto somebody's step?" said Shiro slowly. "What if she gets her own?"

Shiro dabbed the dust away with a corner of cloth, gently, until her soft colors woke up.

They found a little wooden box and set it out front, where the light pooled warmest.

---

They lined it with cloth, a tiny lantern beside it. "Out front," said Cha. "She's the welcome."

"The one who says come in, there's room for everyone," said Shiro, and his voice held steady.

Pinku hauled the ox-cart back to the bottom step, the drum to the musicians. "Order," sighed Shiro.

---

That evening the house filled with music, and trays of treats floated in on little arms.

Diamond rice cakes in pink, white and green. "They made SNACKS of us," gasped Pinku. "We're extremely seen."

On the bottom step sat a rabbit-eared creature in a black jester hood, arms crossed. "Hmph," huffed Kuromi.

Every guest through the door met the tiny doll first, glowing in her own lantern light.

The dango settled beside her on their stick. A shelf, like a heart, has room for one more.

---
