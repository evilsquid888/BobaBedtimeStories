# Story 63: Tai Taiyaki and the Okinawa Coral Reef

**Main Character:** Tai Taiyaki
**Guest Characters:** Bumpu, a lumpy grinning stonefish; Fugu, a startled pufferfish
**Setting:** A glittering coral reef aquarium in Okinawa
**Theme:** There's a whole bright world for the brave to explore
**Japanese Spotlight:** **sakana** (sah-kah-nah) — fish
**Cameo:** Lapras (Pokémon) — surfaces outside the Great Tank's sea-window, sings one note, and leads every fish on a tour from outside, leaving Tai with nobody at his glass; walk-on: Gyarados (Pokémon)

---

Tai Taiyaki, a golden fish-shaped waffle-cake, had swum all his life. Through the air.

"Swim toward the sweet stuff!" he sang, looping over Okinawa harbor, and bonked a seagull.

Water was off-limits. A soggy taiyaki is a sad sandwich. He looped around every splash.

Then he saw a doorway painted blue, bubbles everywhere, ten thousand tiny painted fish.

"Real **sakana** (sah-kah-nah) — fish," whispered Tai, braking so hard his tail squeaked. "I'm making fish friends TODAY."

---

Inside: the Great Tank, glass taller than three houses, the whole **umi** (oo-mee) — sea — folded up indoors.

Silver fish flashed like tossed coins. A lazy ray flapped past like a flying pancake. Tai felt kinship.

"Hello, fish friends!" shouted Tai, squashing his crispy cheek against the glass. "I'm a fish too!"

---

A sleek silver fish glided up, looked Tai up and down with one eye, and zipped away.

"Was that a frown?" said Tai. "Hard to tell, with fish."

Three stripey fish hovered, whispering bubbles. "Stripey club?" said Tai. They shot off, giggling bubbles.

Tai tried his best fish impression. Blub. Blub. A passing crab clicked its claws and looked away.

He smiled at Fugu the pufferfish. Fugu puffed up like a startled balloon and bobbed off at speed.

---

The Great Tank's far wall looked straight out into the real sea. The sea bulged. Then ROSE.

Up surfaced a gentle blue sea creature with a long neck and a grey knobbly shell. Lapras.

It pressed its nose to the glass and sang one low musical note. The whole hall hummed.

Every fish in the Great Tank forgot Tai and rushed to the far glass to stare.

Lapras nodded left, and a thousand fish swam left. Nodded right. A thousand fish swam right.

Then Lapras sank slowly back under, until only its kind eyes showed above the waves, blinking.

---

"Even the SEA has more fish friends than me," sighed Tai, tail sagging. "I'm a cake. Beans inside."

"Pssst. Up here." Behind a curl of orange coral sat the lumpiest, wartiest, most wonderful fish ever.

"Name's Bumpu," said the lumpy fish. "Stonefish. Mostly I look like a rock that learned to smile."

"Funniest-looking sakana in the reef," said Bumpu. "Best job there is. Scared little fish hide behind me."

"But everyone swims AWAY from me," said Tai, perking up. "Unless I try something BIGGER."

---

Up at the tank's open top, Tai announced: "Behold! The loop-the-loop!" One loop. The fish drifted off.

"The DOUBLE loop! The TRIPLE, which nobody has ever done!" Loop, loop, loop. The hall went spinny.

Dizzy as a dropped doughnut, Tai wobbled, flipped, and plunged straight toward the open water.

"SOGGY!" he wailed. "I'm going to be a SAD SANDWICH!"

Up rose Fugu, puffing bigger and bigger, until she bobbed on top like a beach ball.

BOING. Tai bounced off Fugu's belly and landed on the walkway, dry and crispy. Fugu winked.

---

"Look down, crunchy friend," called Bumpu from his coral. "Look DOWN."

At the bottom waited every fish Tai had greeted. Silver, stripey, crab, ray. Hundreds, eyes shining up.

Even a long blue Gyarados at the back uncurled its fierce face into an enormous grin.

"They never saw a fish like you," said Bumpu. "Golden. Warm. They swam off to fetch EVERYBODY."

Tai's heart went whoomp. "SWIM TOWARD THE SWEET STUFF!" He zoomed along the glass, sakana chasing his glow.

"See?" hollered Bumpu. "There's a whole bright world. Plenty of room for the brave and the different."

---

The aquarium lights dimmed to a sleepy blue. The fish blew slow goodnight bubbles at the glass.

"Come back tomorrow, crunchy friend," yawned Bumpu, settling into his coral.

Tai flap-floated out into the warm Okinawa night, looping slowly under the stars.

"Ten thousand fish," he whispered, "and not one like me. That's the best part."
