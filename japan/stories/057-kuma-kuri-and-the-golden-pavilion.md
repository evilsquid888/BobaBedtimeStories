# Story 57: Kuma Kuri and the Golden Pavilion

**Main Character:** Kuma the Kuri
**Guest Characters:** Pepper-kun, Sora the Soft-Cream, Maru Matcha
**Setting:** Kinkaku-ji, the shimmering Golden Pavilion reflected in its pond
**Theme:** True shine comes from within
**Japanese Spotlight:** **kin** (keen) — gold
**Cameo:** Clevatess carrying baby Luna with Alicia (Clevatess) — the beast king pads in at nap time, baby Luna giggles at the flying gold robe, and one doting tail-wag sends Kuma twice as high; walk-on: Zenitsu (Demon Slayer)

---

The morning the dessert friends reached Kinkaku-ji, the whole world was showing off.

Kuma the Kuri stopped so suddenly that three friends bumped into his back. Traffic jam.

There stood the Golden Pavilion, wrapped head to toe in **kin** (keen) — gold — blazing over its pond.

"It SIZZLES without even being hot!" boomed Pepper-kun, fogging up his own bandana.

"It's so gold," sighed Sora, "it makes my swirl look beige."

Kuma tugged his scarf and felt, for the first time, plain. Walk-right-past brown.

"I want to shine like that," he said quietly. "Just once. Gold all over."

---

"Easy!" said Pepper-kun. "Shine is my whole business. Hold still."

He buffed Kuma with his butter swirl until the chestnut gleamed like a doorknob.

Kuma took one proud step. His buttery bottom shot out from under him.

He skidded down the path, bounced off a stone lantern, and plopped into the shallows.

"I'm fine," said Kuma from the water. "The fish seem impressed."

---

By the gate, a stall lent out **kimono** (kee-moh-noh) — silk robes.

Kuma chose the goldest one. It buzzed. It practically shouted.

Sora wrapped him up, fold by fold, and tied the sash in a floppy bow.

They turned him to the mirror. "You look," said Pepper-kun, "like a baked potato that won a lottery."

Kuma's glow dimmed. In all that gold, his face had vanished. A tiny shouting lamp.

---

Then the autumn wind came down the mountain and filled the robe like a sail.

Kuma lifted clean off the ground, sash flapping, and sailed out over the pond.

"I'm FLYING!" he squeaked. "I don't LIKE flying!"

"Hold on to something!" sizzled Pepper-kun. "Not the ROBE, something ELSE!"

---

Thump, softly. A towering black beast king padded into the garden, a baby snug on his chest.

"Softly," rumbled Clevatess, ducking his horns under the maples. "Luna is dozing."

Alicia marched behind him, arms folded. "It is NAP TIME. You promised one short walk."

Luna woke, spotted the gold robe over the pond, and giggled. The beast king's eyes melted.

"She likes the shiny one," rumbled Clevatess, and one wag of his tail sent Kuma twice as high.

"CLEVATESS. Your tail," said Alicia. Luna yawned, and the beast king froze mid-wag, a horned statue.

---

"Whisk slow," called Maru from the bank. "Kuma. Let go of the shine."

Kuma let go of the sash. The wind sighed out of the robe like a tired balloon.

He dropped, plip, onto a floating maple leaf, which wobbled, and held.

The soggy robe hung pond-brown around him. Wet gold, it turns out, is mud.

Then the maples let go too. Leaves came down by the hundred, red, amber, toasty brown.

A cloud slid over the sun. The pavilion's gold went flat and grey.

In the middle of the pond, one small chestnut kept glowing. Roasty. Warm. Himself.

---

"Kuma," whispered Sora, "look at the water."

The leaves had tucked the dull pavilion into a brown blanket. It looked, suddenly, cozy.

Maru hooked his leaf with her whisk and towed him to the bank.

"Gold borrows its light from the sun," said Maru. "You make your own."

"When the campfire's low, who do we huddle around?" said Pepper-kun. "Not a ROOF."

A maple leaf landed on his cap like a crown. "Small and roasty," said Kuma, "ready and toasty."

---

At dusk the lanterns came on around the pond, and the pavilion glowed again.

Nobody scooted toward it. Everybody scooted toward the chestnut, toes out, warm as toast.

Across the pond, Zenitsu in his yellow haori shrieked at a sparrow. The sparrow stayed put.

Sora leaned her swirl on Kuma's cap and yawned. "Still beige," she murmured. "Still happy."

Kuma glowed his own soft gold, the best kind of shining there is.

## Goodnight Blessing
