# Story 68: Dai Daruma and the New-Year First Shrine Visit

**Main Character:** Dai Daruma
**Guest Characters:** Maru Matcha; Coro the Korokke; Mero Melonpan
**Setting:** A bustling shrine on New Year's morning for the first visit (hatsumode)
**Theme:** A fresh year is a fresh chance to try
**Japanese Spotlight:** **akemashite omedetou** (ah-keh-mah-shee-teh oh-meh-deh-toh) — Happy New Year
**Cameo:** Shenron (Dragon Ball) — rises from behind the shrine roof when Dai practices his clap, grants one wish, and Dai blurts out a snack for starving Coro; walk-on: Lucario (Pokémon)

---

The first sunrise of **hatsumode** (hah-tsoo-moh-deh) — the year's first shrine visit, peeked over the gate.

A box lid popped open. Out hopped Dai Daruma. "WOBBLE-WOBBLE, WHEE!" Into a snowdrift. Only eyebrows showing.

"**Akemashite omedetou** (ah-keh-mah-shee-teh oh-meh-deh-toh) — Happy New Year!" hollered Dai from the top of a stone lantern.

"Every wobbly friend makes one brave wish this year," said Dai. "I'm the wish-coach!"

"Do wishes need a coach?" asked Maru Matcha, calmly sipping her own frothy hair.

"EVERYTHING needs a coach," said Dai. A crow said "caw," which is crow for "ooh, awkward."

---

Coro the Korokke shuffled up, steam rising off his head, tummy growling like a drum.

"I skipped breakfast," said Coro. "Nerves. What if I pick the WRONG wish?" Phoooosh.

"A wish isn't a promise it goes perfectly," said Dai. "It's pointing at what you'll try."

Behind a stone fox dozed Mero Melonpan. "I wished to stop dozing," he yawned. "Dozed off mid-wish."

"Practice clap!" said Dai, hopping to the offering box. "Bow, clap, clap, wish. Like THIS."

---

CLAP. CLAP. The sky went dark as ink, and from behind the roof rose an enormous green dragon.

"I am Shenron," rumbled the dragon, coiling into the sky, whiskers streaming. "Speak your one wish."

Dai opened his mouth, coaching speech ready. Beside him, Coro's tummy went GRRRROWL.

"A snack for Coro!" blurted Dai. Shenron blinked, very slowly.

"A... snack," rumbled Shenron. "It is done." A single rice cracker drifted down onto Coro's nose.

Shenron sighed a long, patient sigh and sank behind the roof, red eyes glowing like lanterns.

The sky went pink again. "I had a WHOLE speech," said Dai. Crunch. Cracker gone.

---

Then the great drum creaked, tipped off its icy stand, and rolled off down the steps.

"THE DRUM!" cried Dai, and hurled himself in front of it, eyebrows first.

The drum bounced him clean over its top. BOING. "I'm fine," said Dai, upside down.

It gathered snow as it rolled, growing from drum to boulder to avalanche.

Coro planted himself in the path and steamed as hard as he could. "PHOOOOSH!"

The steam melted the path to slick ice. The drum went FASTER. "Oops," said Coro.

It thundered past the stone fox and scooped Mero, dozing mid-snore, onto its front.

---

Mero's eyes popped open. The shrine whooshed past. "I'm AWAKE," he yelled. "So AWAKE!"

The snow-drum shot across the frozen pond and wobbled to a stop in the middle.

Mero clung on top, crumbs sparkling. "Most awake I've ever been," said Mero, amazed.

Dai bounced onto the ice and skidded past. Coro slid into Dai. "Fall down seven..." panted Dai.

---

Maru stepped onto the ice. "Whisk slow. Feel calm," said Maru, and crossed like a walking pond.

She slid her whisk under the drum's edge and leaned. The drum tipped upright.

Maru climbed up beside Mero, lifted the big mallet, and closed her half-moon eyes.

"I have always wanted to," she said quietly, "and always sat down instead. Not this year."

BOOOOM. The ice hummed. The roof shed all its snow, WHUMP, and the red gate blazed.

"That," said Maru calmly, "was extremely loud." Then she giggled. Nobody had ever seen that.

---

"I wish to make soup for everybody," said Coro. "Soup that feels like a hug."

"I wish to stay awake for one more story than last year," said Mero. "Then one more."

"Trying counts, even wobbly," said Dai. "A new year gives you a clean page."

They bowed, clapped twice, very gently, and rang the bell. "Akemashite omedetou!" they shouted.

---

The bell's hum faded into the frost. Sun lay warm on the red gate.

By the gate, a blue-and-black jackal sat cross-legged, eyes closed, snow on his nose. Lucario breathed out.

Mero yawned, dozed, and woke. "Still awake," he murmured. "Mostly."

Dai tipped sideways and bounced back up. A brand-new year. A brand-new bounce.

---
