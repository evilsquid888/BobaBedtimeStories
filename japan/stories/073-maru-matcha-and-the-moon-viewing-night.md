# Story 73: Maru Matcha and the Moon-Viewing Night

**Main Character:** Maru Matcha
**Guest Characters:** Momo Mochi, Pock Ramune, Kori Kakigori
**Setting:** A grassy hill on the autumn moon-viewing night (Tsukimi)
**Theme:** Quiet beauty is worth staying up a little for
**Japanese Spotlight:** **tsuki** (tsoo-kee) — moon
**Cameo:** Sailor Moon (Sailor Moon) — lands in a shower of sparkles mid-speech, spots the runaway dango and eats six of them, scolds the cloud for being late, trips over Kori-slush and ends up munching on the stone lantern; walk-on: Clefairy (Pokémon)

---
On a cool autumn night, Maru Matcha bonked up a grassy hill behind an enormous basket.

"Whisk slow. Feel calm," she told the basket. The basket tipped and fired a dumpling into her swirl-hair.

"There," said Maru, perfectly calm, with a dumpling on her head. "Now I am dressed for a party."

It was **tsukimi** (tsoo-kee-mee) — moon-viewing night, when friends climb somewhere high to look at the **tsuki** (tsoo-kee) — moon.

Maru wanted one thing: all her friends on one blanket, looking up at one moon.

---

Momo Mochi bounced up first, snow-white and pillowy, and hugged the entire hill.

"Squish together, stay together!" cried Momo, squishing a shrub.

Pock Ramune zipped in next, trailing bubbles. "Is it moon time, pop? Where's the moon, pop?"

Kori Kakigori swept up last, a shaved-ice mountain in sunglasses. "The moon is bright, darling," said Kori.

Maru stacked fifteen **dango** (dahn-go) — round rice dumplings into a neat white tower, round as the moon.

But where the moon should rise lay one fat gray cloud, stretched out like a sleeping cat.

---

"Did somebody EAT the moon?" fizzed Pock. "It's very round. I would eat it."

"A moon-viewing with NO MOON?" wailed Kori, sunglasses sliding. "This is the biggest meltdown of my LIFE."

"I'll blow it away, pop!" Pock rocketed up, bonked off the cloud, and plopped into the dango tower.

The tower wobbled. Fifteen dango bounced off the blanket and down the hill, boing, boing.

"The MOON SNACKS!" cried Momo, and stretched both taffy arms after them.

---

Down the dewy hill they went, Momo bounding, Pock zipping, Kori sliding on syrup.

Momo stretched so far she caught three dango and one very surprised beetle.

Kori skidded through a puddle and arrived as a strawberry slush with eyebrows. "I am a SMOOTHIE, sweeties."

---

Pink sparkles streaked down from the cloud. A heroine in a sailor uniform landed by the stone lantern.

"In the name of the moon, I'll..." declared Sailor Moon, twin-tails flying. Six dango bounced past her boots.

"...have one. Or six," said Sailor Moon, and scooped them up before anyone could squeak.

She pointed a dango at the cloud. "MOON! You are LATE!" The cloud yawned and grew fatter.

She tripped over Kori-slush, sat in the puddle, and wailed, "Worst moon-viewing EVER," still chewing.

She climbed onto the stone lantern, hugged her dango, and sat there munching, legs swinging.

Pock chased the last dango around the lantern eleven times. It bounced off into the dark.

---

Three muddy friends flopped onto the blanket, where Maru sat pouring tea, the fifteenth dango on her head.

"It came home," said Maru. "Things do, when you sit still long enough."

"Whisk slow," said Maru. "Feel calm. Now listen." Far below, crickets tuned up, a whole orchestra.

Kori took off his sunglasses. His eyes underneath were big and shiny and wondering.

Pock went miraculously still. "Maru," he whispered. "I think I'm being patient. Pop."

---

That was exactly when the fat gray cloud yawned, stretched, and drifted aside.

Up came the tsuki, huge and buttery-gold, pouring silver light over four round friends with open mouths.

"It's so beautiful I could MELT," whispered Kori. "But I want to keep looking."

On the next rock, a round pink creature with a head-curl and tiny wings danced at the moon.

Pock pointed one trembling bubble-arm. "Look look look. There's a RABBIT."

---

In the moon's gray markings sat a round **usagi** (oo-sah-gee) — rabbit, pounding rice in a bowl.

"He's making dango!" gasped Momo. "Up on the moon! Just like us!"

"He waited behind the cloud the whole time," said Maru. "The waiting was the party."

---

They ate the dango slowly, the way you eat when nobody is hurrying.

Momo finally got her group squish, soft and sleepy, mud on everybody.

Pock dozed off mid-pop, bubbles rising, and Maru's whole jade-green heart went warm and round.

---
