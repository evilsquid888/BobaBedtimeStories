# Story 39: Rin Ramen and the Secret Broth Recipe

**Main Character:** Rin Ramen
**Guest Characters:** Old Bowl, an ancient crackle-glazed ramen master; Mame, a small lost rice ball
**Setting:** A steamy back-street ramen counter run by a kindly old bowl
**Theme:** The secret ingredient is always care
**Japanese Spotlight:** **raamen** (RAH-mehn) — ramen
**Cameo:** Naruto Uzumaki and his shadow clones (Naruto) — bursts through the curtain demanding ramen right now, multiplies into eleven Narutos who fill every stool chanting "is it ready", then poofs away begging for a slow bowl; walk-on: Choji (Naruto)

---

Down the twistiest back-street in town, under lanterns like warm orange eggs, something steamed.

It was Rin Ramen, a bowl on stubby legs, bubbling so hard his noodle hair had frizzed.

"Tonight," he announced to the empty alley, "I learn the most LEGENDARY secret in the world!"

He skipped. His broth sloshed. A whole noodle slapped him in the eye.

At the alley's end lived Old Bowl, master of **raamen** (RAH-mehn) — ramen, keeper of a Secret Recipe.

"A SECRET," whispered Rin, steam puffing from both ears. "I'll learn it. Then I'll be famous."

---

Behind the indigo curtain: six stools, a hundred ladles, and a cat-shaped clock ticking extremely slowly.

There sat Old Bowl, enormous and ancient, his chipped rim like a smile missing one tooth.

Rin hopped onto a stool, overshot, and landed on the next one, legs swinging.

"Please," he breathed, leaning until his name-tag dipped. "Teach me the SECRET."

Old Bowl chuckled like a soup pot. "I'll show you. But you must watch the slow parts."

"I love slow parts," said Rin, who had never met one.

---

Old Bowl crooned to each bone before lowering it in. "You're talking to the bones?" said Rin.

"How rude not to," said Old Bowl. "Now we wait. **Yukkuri** (yook-KOO-ree) — slowly."

Rin sat very still. He lasted nine seconds. "Is it ready?" Bloop, said the pot. "NOW?" Bloop.

---

The curtain burst open. In skidded a spiky-haired ninja in orange. "ONE RAMEN! Believe it!" shouted Naruto.

Naruto eyed the slow pot. POOF. Ten more Narutos filled every stool, the bar, the ceiling beams.

"Is it ready?" "Is it READY?" "IS IT READY?" chanted eleven Narutos, drumming the counter.

Rin, squashed between two Narutos, felt his own impatience times eleven. His steam drooped.

Old Bowl hummed one slow note. POOF. Ten clones vanished, leaving one dizzy Naruto.

"Save me a SLOW one!" called Naruto, frozen in a grin at the curtain, thumb up.

---

Rin's eyes drifted to a high shelf, where one small jar hid under a cloth. The secret!

While Old Bowl hummed, Rin stacked three stools and climbed, wobbling, broth sloshing.

He stretched. He tipped. He grabbed the cloth, and a shelf of ladles clattered down.

Ladles bounced off Old Bowl's rim like a xylophone. Old Bowl said, "Mm."

Rin landed bottom-first on the stove knob. The flame roared tall.

The pot bubbled. The pot burped. It sneezed a noodle onto the cat-clock.

The broth rose like a golden tide. "I'LL CATCH IT!" cried Rin, and got a hot golden hat.

---

Broth flooded the floor. Bones bobbed past like boats. Rin splashed after them, scooping, slipping, scooping.

Old Bowl lowered the flame with one slow nudge. The tide sighed and stopped.

The secret jar lay on the floor, unbroken. Rin lifted the lid with shaking arms. Empty.

"NOTHING?" said Rin. His steam sagged flat. "Where's the secret?"

"That jar is full of time," said Old Bowl. "You just poured it all over my floor."

---

Rin's broth went quiet. He set the bones, one by one, gently into the pot.

"You waited all week to be warm," he told the first bone. "Sorry about the swim."

He lit the littlest flame. He sat. He listened to the bubbles. He let the clock tick.

Old Bowl's glaze glowed a shade warmer. "There," he said. "The secret ingredient is always care."

---

Later the curtain rustled. A small lost **onigiri** (oh-nee-GHEE-ree) — rice ball — stood there, shivering.

"Sit, little one," said Rin, patting a stool. "Everything's better warm and bubbly."

He ladled the slow bowl. Mame took one sip. His eyes went enormous and shiny.

A round ninja with swirl cheeks slurped on the last stool. "Mmf," said Choji. "Seconds?"

"What's your secret?" whispered Mame.

Rin grinned, steam curling softly. "You'll have to watch the slow parts."
