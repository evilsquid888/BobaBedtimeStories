# Story 67: Momo Mochi and the New-Year Mochi Pounding

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi
**Guest Characters:** Azu, a shy little adzuki bean; Mame, a sturdy roasted soybean
**Setting:** A village square at New Year, where mochi is pounded in a big wooden mortar
**Theme:** New beginnings are best started together
**Japanese Spotlight:** **omochi** (oh-moh-chee) — pounded rice cake
**Cameo:** Goku on Nimbus (Dragon Ball) — zooms in on his golden cloud and eats the mochi as fast as it is pounded, while Nimbus drops anyone else who tries to ride; walk-on: Majin Buu (Dragon Ball)

---

On the first morning of the New Year, the village square wore a hat of snow.

"It's TODAY!" squealed Momo Mochi, stretching one taffy arm across the square to ring the bell.

A steaming wooden mortar, a mallet taller than Momo: today they would pound **omochi** (oh-moh-chee) — pounded rice cake.

"I want a whole mountain of squishy mochi," said Momo, "before this rice goes cold!"

---

Treat-folk huddled around the mortar. "It's so heavy," whispered Azu, a tiny adzuki bean.

Mame, a sturdy roasted soybean, poked the steam and yelped. "And HOT," said Mame.

"What if I make a lumpy New Year for everybody?" said Azu. The snow held its breath.

Momo stretched both arms around the whole worried huddle and squished. Gently. Starchily.

"Nobody pounds the New Year alone," said Momo. "Except me. Watch."

---

Momo grabbed the mallet, lifted it four inches, and tipped over backwards. POMF. "See? Easy!" said Momo.

Try two. Momo wound both arms around the handle, stretched like a slingshot, and let go.

The mallet whizzed up, up, and hooked itself on the festival bell. BONG.

She stretched. Her arms went up like noodles, snapped back, and flung her into the mortar. SPLAT.

Hot, sticky, glossy rice. Momo sank in to her cheeks. "Oh," she said. "Oh, it's warm in here."

---

"Momo," said Mame, peering in, "you're a rice cake. Aren't you, technically, made of this?"

Momo went still. Her cheeks turned plum. "I AM," she whispered. "This is where I come from."

"Friends pounding together," said Momo, wonderstruck. "That's where mochi comes from. I'm made of teamwork!"

She tried to climb out. The rice held her like loving glue. "Family is clingy," said Momo.

Mame and Azu grabbed an arm each. "**Yoisho!** (yoy-shoh) — heave-ho!" they chanted, and PLOP, out she popped.

---

Mame yanked the bell rope until the mallet dropped. BONG. "I'll POUND!" boomed Mame.

"I'll turn," said Azu quietly, and darted in. BAM. Fold. "Yoisho!" chanted the square. The steam turned gold.

---

The first mochi plopped onto the board. WHOOSH. A small golden cloud skidded to a stop above it.

"Oh wow, mochi!" said Goku, a spiky-haired fighter in an orange gi riding the cloud. One bite. Gone.

BAM. Fold. Plop. Gulp. BAM. Fold. Plop. Gulp. The faster Mame pounded, the faster Goku ate.

Mame leaped onto the cloud to shoo him. Nimbus went soft as fog. Mame dropped into the snow.

Momo stretched one arm to snatch a cake back. Goku ate it off her arm. "Thanks!" said Goku.

He patted his belly and floated up. "Save me some next year!" called Goku, waving from the sky.

The board was empty. The mortar had one scoop of rice left. Azu's lip wobbled.

---

Momo looped her arms around the mallet, Mame, and Azu. "Last scoop," said Momo. "All of us."

"Yoisho!" Three paws on one handle. BAM. Fold. BAM. Fold. The rice went glossy as a snowball.

Momo grabbed an end and pulled. It stretched past the mortar, past the bell, past the square.

SNAP. It sprang back into a blob. Mame plopped a tangerine on top: **kagamimochi** (kah-gah-mee-moh-chee) — mirror mochi!

---

Azu held up the last pinch, shaped like a sneeze. "Mine came out wrong," whispered Azu.

Momo pressed it between their paws. "A new beginning isn't about perfect," said Momo. "It's about starting together."

"Squish together," said Azu shyly, "stay together?"

"SQUISH TOGETHER, STAY TOGETHER!" roared the square, and Momo laughed herself backwards into the snow.

---

That evening, under lanterns, they wrapped Momo in blankets, the softest thing to lean on.

Under the next lantern sat a round pink creature in a purple cape. "Buu likes squishy," giggled Buu.

"Even though you couldn't lift the mallet?" teased Mame, yawning.

"Especially because," murmured Momo, arms around two sleepy friends. "Best things take more than one."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
