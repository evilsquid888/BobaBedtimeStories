# Story 49: Sora Soft-Cream and the Hokkaido Milk Farm

**Main Character:** Sora the Soft-Cream
**Guest Characters:** Ushiko, a gentle dairy cow; Daikon, the laziest snow-rabbit in Hokkaido; Chibi, a brand-new baby swirl
**Setting:** A wide-open dairy farm in snowy Hokkaido
**Theme:** Home is wherever your gentle heart feels full
**Japanese Spotlight:** **gyuunyuu** (gyoo-nyoo) — milk
**Cameo:** The KonoSuba party: Kazuma, Aqua, Megumin and Darkness (KonoSuba) — four adventurers tumble out of a portal into the cow's path, Darkness begs to be charged and is ambled around, Aqua blesses the milk and is ignored, Megumin explodes an empty hill into harmless glitter that speeds the runaway sled, Kazuma sighs; walk-on: Slowpoke (Pokémon)

---

The train doors whooshed open onto a platform made entirely of snow.

Out stepped Sora the Soft-Cream, a tall pale-gold swirl in a cone, blinking.

A frosty wind puffed past. Sora braced to melt. Instead her drip-curl stood straight up, proud.

"Ohhh," she breathed. "I get MORE swirly here. So THIS is where I come from."

Sora was born somewhere in these fields. She wanted one thing: the exact spot.

Down the snowy lane she wobbled, leaving tiny swirl-prints, until the whole world opened WIDE.

---

Snow rolled to the sky in soft scoops. A red barn sat in the middle like a strawberry.

"MOOOO." A big gentle black-and-white cow stood chewing, with the kindest, sleepiest eyes.

"I am Ushiko," said the cow, finishing her mouthful first. "You smell like home."

"I'm made of **gyuunyuu** (gyoo-nyoo) — milk," said Sora. "Maybe yours. Where did I begin?"

"Follow me," said Ushiko, swishing her tail slowly. "Nobody rushes here."

A fat snow-rabbit named Daikon blinked once. That was his whole morning. Ushiko was very proud.

---

In the barn, a silver pail of fresh gyuunyuu sat frothing on a wooden sled.

A pink dopey Slowpoke floated in it, mouth open. "He got in last spring," said Ushiko. "Still deciding."

"The pail goes up to the churn," said Ushiko. "Slowly. Pails are shy."

Sora leaned in to sniff. Her cone nudged the sled. It slid one inch. Then two.

Then the sled shot out the door and down the long white hill, Sora on top.

"Soft swirls!" wailed Sora, hugging the pail. "SLOW SMIIIILES!"

---

Milk sloshed over a drift. The pink passenger floated calmly, enjoying this enormously, or not at all.

---

A glowing blue portal popped open on the hill. Out tumbled four adventurers, right in Ushiko's path.

A knight in silver armor planted herself before the cow. "Charge me, cow!" begged Darkness. Ushiko ambled around.

A blue-haired goddess flung both arms wide. "I BLESS this milk!" wailed Aqua. Ushiko chewed past.

A tiny witch in a huge pointed hat aimed her staff at an empty hill. "EXPLOSION!" chanted Megumin.

The hill burst into harmless glitter, a sparkle-wave shoved the sled faster, and Megumin flopped flat, dozing.

A tracksuited teen sat down among the weeping goddess, dozing witch and thrilled knight. "Every time," sighed Kazuma.

---

Far below, the sled bounced toward Daikon, sitting on the snow. Blinking.

"DAIKON!" cried Sora. "MOVE!"

Daikon considered this. Daikon had never moved for anyone. Daikon blinked twice, which was unheard of.

Then, with one enormous lazy hop, he landed square on the sled's front.

The sled plowed into a drift and stopped, soft as a pillow. Every drop stayed put.

Daikon yawned, exhausted. "The laziest rabbit in Hokkaido," said Ushiko, arriving at last. "We're very proud."

---

On the hilltop, Ushiko lifted the Slowpoke out by the tail and nudged the pail into the churn.

"Slow...," said the Slowpoke, about the sled ride, and lay down.

The milk swirled, slow and steady, growing colder and curlier. "I'm watching myself be born," whispered Sora.

"Spookiest thing I've ever seen," said Ushiko, looking fonder than ever.

Swoosh! Out came a brand-new baby swirl, pale gold, with a teeny drip-curl. It blinked.

"Hello," said the little swirl. "Soft swirls?"

"Slow smiles," said Sora, and her heart filled to the very tip of her curl.

---

Sora gave Ushiko the gentlest hug a soft-serve can give, cool swirl against warm cow.

"Home isn't one field," said Ushiko. "Home is wherever your gentle heart feels full."

"That's the nicest thing a cow has ever told me," said Sora.

"It's the only thing a cow has ever told you," said Ushiko.

---

On the train, a snowflake landed on Sora's tip and stayed.

"Home," she said softly to the snowflake. "And also home. Both. How lucky."

---
