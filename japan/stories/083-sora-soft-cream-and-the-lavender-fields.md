# Story 83: Sora Soft-Cream and the Lavender Fields

**Main Character:** Sora the Soft-Cream
**Guest Characters:** Pock Ramune, Momo Mochi, the Dango Three (Pinku, Shiro, Cha)
**Setting:** The rolling purple lavender fields of Furano, Hokkaido
**Theme:** Slow days are full of quiet color
**Japanese Spotlight:** **hana** (hah-nah) — flower
**Cameo:** Vash the Stampede (Trigun) — crashes through the maze hedge hopelessly lost and asking for donuts, sneezes a purple pollen cloud over everyone, flashes a peace sign and trips over his own coat; walk-on: Sylveon (Pokémon)

---

On the sunniest afternoon of summer, Sora the Soft-Cream stood on a hill doing nothing. Glorious.

Below her the lavender rolled on forever, row after purple row, like a blanket ironed with sunshine.

"SORA!" fizzed Pock Ramune, zipping uphill so fast he overshot her and bonked a pine.

"You've been standing there an HOUR!" He bounced back. "We planned SEVEN things! Maze, train, hill, home!"

"Has it?" said Sora dreamily, her drip-curl wobbling. "It felt like one very nice breath."

Sora gazed at nine million flowers. "Has anybody actually looked at one flower yet?" she asked.

---

Sora flowed down the hill the way soft-serve flows: a slow, smiling slide.

At the crooked fence waited Momo Mochi, bouncing, and the Dango Three, already stained purple to the stick.

"FINALLY!" said Pinku. "Maze first! Go go go!"

"One flower," said Sora, with the tiniest dreamy smile, "would take exactly the right amount of time."

Nobody was listening. They tumbled into the lavender maze in a giggling clump.

---

The maze was tall purple hedges, twistier than a noodle. Three minutes later: hopelessly lost.

"Left!" said Pinku, and the stick lurched left. Shiro pointed right, and it lurched right.

"We came from THERE," said Cha, pointing at a solid wall of flowers.

Pock fizzed straight up to look over the hedge and got stuck on top like a flag.

Momo stretched both arms up to pull him down and tangled herself into a bow.

"Squish together," said Momo, upside down, "stay... stuck together."

---

CRASH. A stranger in a long red coat burst through the hedge, spiky blond hair full of petals.

"Donuts?" asked Vash hopefully, round yellow glasses crooked. "I've been in here three days. Any donuts?"

His nose twitched. "Ah. Ah. AH—" and he sneezed a purple cloud of pollen over everyone.

Now the whole gang sneezed, purple head to toe, and the stick bounced three ways at once.

"Love and peace!" announced Vash, flashing a V for peace, and tripped over his own coat.

He landed flat in the lavender with the V still up, happy as a flower.

Then around the corner drifted Sora, who had only just wandered in. "Wrong way," she said.

---

"Follow the bees," said Sora, as a fat honeybee bumbled past. "Home is always toward the open sky."

The gang tiptoed after it, slower and slower, sneezing less, until they stopped feeling lost.

The bee floated over the last hedge and out. Everyone spilled into the gold afternoon, blinking and purple.

---

Next, the flower train. Everyone crowded the front, watching the flowers smear into one grape smudge.

Sora sat at the very back, facing the other way, watching the flowers drift slowly off.

"Mm," said Sora, patting the bench beside her. "I think you're missing it. Come sit by me."

Momo squished down beside her and saw it: one perfect **hana** (hah-nah) — flower, deep velvet purple.

Inside it, a tiny bee lay dozing like a fuzzy bean in a tiny bed.

"You can't rush past a thing and see it," said Sora. "Slow down, and it catches up."

---

One by one the others turned around, until the whole train rode forward looking back.

On the postcard hill, a bee landed on Pinku's nose, and all three Dango giggled off the stick.

Cha looked up. "The windmill will still be a windmill tomorrow. The light is only doing this now."

Pock lay still for almost four seconds, then fizzed one tiny pop. "It's hard. But it's nice."

---

The gold light poured sideways through the lavender, turning every flower into a tiny purple lantern.

Pale pink ribbons rose out of the rows: a little fox creature with ribbon feelers, trilling softly. Sylveon.

Sora dripped a little at the edges. "Melting means you stayed somewhere lovely long enough," she said.

"Slow swirls," whispered Sora, as the sun melted like soft-cream into the hills. "Slow smiles."

---
