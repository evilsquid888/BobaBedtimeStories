# Story 54: Maru Matcha and the Tea Ceremony

**Main Character:** Maru Matcha
**Guest Characters:** Kori Kakigori, Pock Ramune, Mero Melonpan
**Setting:** A serene Kyoto tea house with tatami mats and a tiny garden
**Theme:** Doing one thing slowly and well is a gift
**Japanese Spotlight:** **ocha** (oh-chah) — tea
**Cameo:** No-Face (Spirited Away) — drifts in through the paper door, sits perfectly still at the tea mat, heaps gold instead of taking the sweet until the table tips, and bows from the doorway when gently declined; walk-on: Mew (Pokémon)

---

On a quiet Kyoto hill, behind a wooden gate and polite little stones, sat a tea house.

Outside stood Maru Matcha, jade-green, matcha-cream swirl on top, bamboo whisk held like a wand.

The whisk was trembling. Not from wind. Maru was nervous, and the whisk told everybody.

"Today," Maru told the whisk, "I host my first tea ceremony. One perfect bowl of **ocha** (oh-chah) — tea."

"Whisk slow," she breathed, like fog leaving a window. "Feel calm." Then the gate bell jingled.

Chaos arrived wearing sunglasses.

---

Kori Kakigori swept in, a mountain of strawberry shaved ice, melting one dramatic degree at a time.

"Maru, darling, this place is SO peaceful I might cry," said Kori. "Which would also melt me."

Pock Ramune zipped in next, trailing bubbles. "Pop! Where do I sit? Can I touch everything?"

"No popping the teaware," said Maru gently.

"Aww. Pop," said Pock, and sat on his fizzy paws.

Last came Mero Melonpan, already yawning. "Is this the napping room? It smells like green dreams."

---

The room glowed like a lantern's inside. Through a round window: one stone, one moss, one maple.

"Only ONE tree?" said Kori. "Sweetie, where's the rest of the forest?"

"One tree you truly look at," said Maru, "beats a forest you walk past."

Kori blinked behind his sunglasses. "Okay, that was beautiful. Annoying. But beautiful."

Maru knelt on the **tatami** (tah-tah-mee) — woven straw mats and set out the wide tea bowl.

Mew, a small pink floating cat-like creature, peeked in the round window, giggled, and vanished.

"Help by watching," Maru told Pock's raised paw. "Watching IS helping." Pock sat still four seconds. A record.

---

Green powder. Warm water. Then the whisk, shoo-shoo-shoo, and foam rose like a tiny green cloud.

The paper door slid open by itself, and a tall shadow with a pale calm mask drifted in.

No-Face folded onto the tatami, stiller than the stone outside. "Ah... ah," he said.

Maru offered the little sweet. No-Face held out cupped shadow palms instead: gold nuggets, glittering.

"Darling, TAKE the gold," hissed Kori. Pock bounced around the shadow going pop, pop, pop.

More gold poured out, clinking, heaping, until the low table tipped like a seesaw.

"Tea first," said Maru softly. "Gold never." No-Face bowed low and held the bow in the doorway.

---

Gold dust glittered in the air. Pock's nose twitched. "Ah. Ah. AH."

"POP-CHOO!" Bubbles shot everywhere. One landed on the maple. One on Kori's sunglasses.

The sneeze launched Pock backward like a loose balloon, off the paper walls, pop, pop, POP.

Kori shrieked and melted a whole dramatic degree, and a strawberry puddle spread across the tatami.

The tilted table slid on the puddle. The bowl tipped, green and frothy and perfect.

Everyone lunged. Everyone shrieked. Maru breathed out.

She whisked her wand once, shoo, and turned two soft paws under the bowl. Slow. Sure.

The bowl settled into her palms. Every drop stayed put. The room gasped.

---

"HOW," said Kori, upside down in his own puddle, "did you do that SLOWLY?"

"Rushing shakes your paws," said Maru. "Slow is not late. Slow is sure."

Pock peeled off the wall, pale blue. "Sorry, Maru. Pop. I ruined the quiet part."

"You gave us something to fix calmly," said Maru. "That's part of the ceremony. Bless you."

Mero sipped first. A crumb-sparkle drifted off his crust. "Oh. It tastes like a nap feels."

Kori sipped and dabbed his eyes with a sunglass. "The warmest cool I've ever felt."

Pock held the bowl like a soap bubble and took the slowest sip in fizz history.

---

"One thing, slowly, with love," said Maru. "That's the gift. Also, Pock, sneeze outside next time."

"Pop," yawned Pock. "Deal."

Outside the round window, Mew floated past upside down, dozing. The whisk finally went perfectly still.
