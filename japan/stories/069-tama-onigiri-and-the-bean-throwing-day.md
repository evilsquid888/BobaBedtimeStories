# Story 69: Tama Onigiri and the Bean-Throwing Day

**Main Character:** Tama Onigiri
**Guest Characters:** Aoni, a tiny blueberry daifuku in a paper oni mask; Rin Ramen
**Setting:** A cozy home celebrating Setsubun with friendly bean-throwing
**Theme:** You can chase away worries with a giggle
**Japanese Spotlight:** **oni** (oh-nee) — ogre (friendly!)
**Cameo:** Bowser (Mario Kart) — bursts through the back door in his kart to volunteer as the oni, adores being pelted with beans and roars for seconds until every bean is gone; walk-on: Inosuke (Demon Slayer)

---

Have you ever seen a rice ball wrap a bean box in a polka-dot napkin? Meet Tama Onigiri.

It was **Setsubun** (seh-tsoo-boon) — bean-throwing day, when you fling soybeans at the **oni** (oh-nee) — ogre.

Tama packed extra everything. "And a spare box," she told the kitchen, "in case these beans get nervous."

"By lunch," said Tama, straightening her sash, "I want every friend inside, warm, and giggling."

Hic. From under the table. Tama froze. "Beans?" she whispered. "Nervous already?"

---

She lifted the tablecloth. Under it: the smallest oni ever, a teapot-sized blueberry daifuku in a mask.

"AH!" said Tama.

"AH!" said the little oni, and bonked a paper horn on the table. "Owie."

"I'm Aoni," he sniffled. "Every year, everyone throws beans at me and shuts the door."

"I sit outside and hear the laughing," said Aoni, shorts slipping. "I wanted the fun from inside."

Tama's plum blush went pink. She knew that feeling: the wrong side of a happy door.

---

Aoni tiptoed for the door. Tama plopped down in the doorway, spare beans and all.

"You just got here," said Tama. "And besides, I packed a little extra. For you."

"Beans are for fun," said Tama, "not for scaring. Watch." She bonked a soybean off her forehead.

Aoni snorted. Tama did it again, cross-eyed. The snort became a giggle. The hiccups stopped.

Aoni threw a bean into his own open mouth. "It's DELICIOUS," gasped Aoni. "I always ran away before!"

---

The door burst open. "BEAN-THROWING TIIIIME!" hollered Rin Ramen, broth sloshing, a box under each arm.

Aoni dove behind Tama, two paper horns poking out the top. "Oni horns!" cried Rin, winding up.

Aoni dove into the rice cooker. The cooker rattled and walked toward the back door.

"The RICE is escaping!" wailed Rin. Aoni burst out, trailing rice, into the spare bean box.

"See?" said Tama, delighted. "I TOLD you beans get nervous." The box scuttled off.

Rin chased. The box dodged. Rin slipped on spilled beans and SPLOSHED into a heap.

---

The back door swung wide. A huge spiked turtle-dragon in a kart filled the whole doorway.

"I heard somebody needs an ONI!" roared Bowser, red mane bristling, grinning ear to horn.

Rin flung a whole box. Beans bounced off Bowser's shell like popcorn. "HA! Tickles!" laughed Bowser.

Rin emptied the second box. Bowser caught beans in his mouth, both paws, one horn. "SECONDS!" roared Bowser.

Beans piled knee-deep. The scuttling box vanished. Bowser crunched a pawful happily.

"Best Setsubun EVER," said Bowser, and parked in the garden, grinning, a bean on each horn.

Rin stared into two empty boxes. "Every single bean," said Rin, "at the wrong oni."

---

The bean drift shifted. Out tumbled Aoni, mask crooked, beans on both horns. Rin froze mid-fling.

"New game," said Tama, stepping in front of Aoni. "Beans are for saying hello, not chasing anyone out."

"H-hello," said Aoni, waving. "I'm soft. My horns are paper. My shorts don't fit."

He tossed a bean up, wobbled in a circle, and caught it on his tummy. BOING.

Rin melted into a slurpy laugh. "A giggle," said Tama, "is the best broom for worries."

---

Tama unwrapped the polka-dot napkin: one more box. "I packed a little extra," said Tama.

"Out with worries, in with welcome!" shouted everyone, and beans flew up in happy showers.

"THROW HARDER!" bellowed Inosuke through the window, a bare-chested fighter in a grey boar-head mask.

Eyes-Closed Throw: disaster. Behind-the-Back: worse. Aoni won, a bean on each horn, humming a victory hum.

---

By lantern-time, Aoni was curled in the warmest corner under a napkin-blanket.

Rin poured warm tea. "Everything's better warm and bubbly," he hummed, steaming.

"Even cozier than I imagined," murmured Aoni. "Thank you for not throwing me out."

"We don't throw friends out," whispered Tama, tucking him snug. "We pack extra room. Always."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
Little one, if a worry creeps under your table tonight, offer it a giggle and watch it tiptoe away. Most scary things are small, soft, and hoping to be let in. Goodnight.
