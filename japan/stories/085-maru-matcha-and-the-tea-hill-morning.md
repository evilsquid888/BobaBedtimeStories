# Story 85: Maru Matcha and the Tea-Hill Morning

**Main Character:** Maru Matcha
**Guest Characters:** Pock Ramune, Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju, Kori Kakigori
**Setting:** Misty terraced green-tea hills at sunrise in Shizuoka
**Theme:** The earliest, quietest hour holds a gentle gift
**Japanese Spotlight:** **midori** (mee-doh-ree) — green
**Cameo:** Mash Burnedead (Mashle) — looms out of the fog carrying the whole morning tea harvest in one arm, mistakes Maru's swirl for a cream puff, answers "arms" when asked about magic, and parks the basket mountain across the only path; walk-on: Tails (Sonic)

---

Long before the sun cracked its first yawn, Maru Matcha was up. Of course she was.

The tea hill snored under blankets of fog. Maru tiptoed out, whisk tucked behind one ear.

Plip. A dewdrop landed splat on her nose. "Good morning to you too," said Maru.

Every leaf glowed deep dreamy **midori** (mee-doh-ree) — green. The air smelled like the first idea of morning.

Maru wanted her friends on the top terrace before sunrise. The early hour had a gift.

---

Pock Ramune zipped up the path and bonked straight into a tea bush. "Pop! Ow. I'm UP!"

"What time IS it?" wheezed Pock. "It's o'clock-in-the-dark. There isn't even a number for it."

Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju shuffled up, hugging herself. "It's COLD. Why isn't this at an onsen?"

"Because the onsen has steam," said Maru, "and the hill has something better. Climb."

---

Pock zoomed ahead, lost the path in fog, and zoomed back the wrong way. "The hill moved. Pop."

Yuki stepped on a dew-slick terrace, wobbled, and slid back down on her tummy. Wheee. Oof.

"Warm cheeks," muttered Yuki, face-first in wet tea leaves. "Warm heart. COLD everything else."

Maru hooked Yuki's paw with her whisk and tugged, slow and steady, up the slippery step.

---

From the fog loomed a mountain of tea baskets, carried in ONE arm by a blank-faced, muscle-bound student.

"Excuse me," said Mash Burnedead, perfectly calm. "Have you seen any cream puffs?"

His eyes landed on Maru's frothy swirl. He leaned in, mouth open. "That is my HEAD," said Maru.

"How did you LIFT all that?" fizzed Pock, bubbles everywhere. "Was it MAGIC?"

"Arms," said Mash, and set the basket mountain down, right across the only path up.

He sat on top, blank as a plate, chewing one tea leaf, still hoping for cream puffs.

---

Up the basket mountain they went, Yuki sliding, Pock bouncing, leaves raining down like confetti.

Higher. The fog thickened until Maru's swirl was the only thing anyone could see.

"Follow the swirl," said Maru calmly. "Whisk slow. Climb slow. Feel calm."

---

They flopped onto the top terrace, muddy and puffing, as the sky turned cold-tea gray.

Maru set four cups in the wet grass, neat as buttons. One extra, for whoever came.

"I can't sit," fizzed Pock. "I have too many bubbles. They're all going go-go-go."

"Then sit," said Maru, "and let the bubbles do the going for you."

Pock plopped down. A dewdrop slid down his glass, slow as honey, and he watched the whole way.

---

Then the first thread of sunlight slid over the far hills.

The fog between the rows glowed from the inside, like a lamp made of breath.

Every dewdrop caught a spark, until the hillside was strung with a million tiny lights.

The midori went from sleepy-dark to bright wet jewel-green, all at once, all over.

"This is the gift," said Maru, lifting her whisk. "It only comes to those awake for it."

"I'm not crying," said Yuki quickly. "It's dew. The dew got on my face."

---

"It's the COOLEST," sighed a voice. There stood Kori Kakigori in a tiny blanket, sunglasses up.

"I take it ALL back," said Kori. "Early is cool. Tell no one. I have a reputation."

Maru knelt and whisked. Swish, swish, swish. Green froth rose like little clouds. "Sip slow," said Maru.

Yuki sipped fast, got a green froth moustache, and never noticed for the rest of the story.

"The morning is so quiet," whispered Pock, "the quiet has a flavor. Pop."

---

The fog lifted, gentle as a blanket, and the hills rolled out below, green upon green.

A small orange fox whirred over the rows, two tails spinning like a propeller. "Morning!" called Tails.

Four friends huddled on top of the world. "Come back," said Maru, "whenever the green calls you."

---
