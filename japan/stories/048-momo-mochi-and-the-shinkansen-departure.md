# Story 48: Momo Mochi and the Shinkansen Departure

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi
**Guest Characters:** Tama Onigiri, Niko Nigiri, the Dango Three (Pinku, Shiro, Cha)
**Setting:** A spotless bullet-train platform as the shinkansen arrives
**Theme:** Saying goodbye to one place means hello to another
**Japanese Spotlight:** **shinkansen** (sheen-kahn-sen) — bullet train
**Cameo:** Motoko Kusanagi and a Tachikoma (Ghost in the Shell) — the Major stands perfectly still on the platform while her blue spider-tank drops from the roof, declares itself luggage, jams itself in the luggage door and bounces the runaway cart the wrong way; walk-on: Dragonite (Pokémon)

---

The platform was so clean you could eat breakfast right off the floor.

Tama Onigiri suggested exactly that, since she had packed three breakfasts, just in case.

"Just in case of what?" asked Niko Nigiri, straightening his nori belt.

"In case of the case," said Tama wisely, and handed everyone a rice ball.

Momo Mochi bounced so high she stuck to a pillar. "Squish together, stay together!"

Momo wanted one thing: everyone squished into one window seat when the sea appeared.

Today they rode the **shinkansen** (sheen-kahn-sen) — bullet train — west to the sea.

---

One small friend stood frozen stiff: Cha, the green dumpling of the Dango Three.

"What if it's so fast I fall off the world?" squeaked Cha.

"You can't fall off the world," said Pinku. "I checked. It's round and sticky, like us."

"You did NOT check," whispered Shiro.

Momo knelt to button-sized Cha. "First trips have wiggly tummies," she said. "The cure is the window-seat squish."

---

Far down the track a hum grew into a whoosh, then a glorious FWOOOOM.

The shinkansen slid in, long and white and pointy-nosed, like the world's politest dolphin.

"Everyone aboard, neatly!" announced Niko. "Car four, by the window."

Up they bustled. All except Cha, who hopped into Tama's spare lunch box, stick and all.

"We're in a BOX," came Pinku's voice, muffled and furious.

---

The lunch box sat on a wobbly luggage cart. The brake slipped. The cart rolled away.

"THE DANGO!" cried Momo, stretching one taffy arm after it. It came up short.

Niko sprinted neatly, tripped over his own cape, and slid along the floor on his belly.

Tama tossed rice balls under the wheels. The cart bumped, wobbled, and rolled faster.

---

THUNK-THUNK-THUNK-THUNK. A round blue spider-tank dropped from the station roof, right in the cart's path.

"Luggage!" chirped the Tachikoma, folding its legs. "I am luggage! Small luggage! Round-trip, please!"

Beside it stood the Major, purple hair, grey jacket, arms folded, still as a very cool lamppost.

The Tachikoma squeezed into the luggage door first, legs wiggling. The cart bonked off its blue bottom.

"Tachikoma," said the Major, moving only her mouth. "You are a tank. Out."

The Tachikoma backed out, sat down with a clank, and waved one leg sadly. The Major stayed still.

---

Off shot the cart the other way, straight at the passenger doors. Psssht. Closing.

Niko wedged his whole rice self into the gap. "Not neatly," he gasped. "Deliciously!"

Momo bounced off a pillar, off a bench, higher each time, and sailed clean over the cart.

She hooked the lunch box with both arms and landed inside the train. FLOOMP.

The lid popped open. Three dumplings blinked up. "I'm... ON the train," said Cha.

---

The platform slid backward and vanished. "Goodbye, platform!" cried Cha. "Hello, mountain! Hello, OTHER mountain!"

"He's saying hello to everything," said Momo, tucking the skewer into her lap. "Same wave."

Momo wrapped her pillowy self around the three like a steamed blanket. The window-seat squish.

---

WHOOMP. The train dove into a tunnel and the whole world went black.

"The mountain ate us!" squealed Cha. "Goodbye, everyone! It was nice knowing you!"

"Count the dark with me," said Momo, squishing gently. "**Ichi** (ee-chee) — one. **Ni** (nee) — two. **San** (sahn) — three."

On four, the train burst into gold, and the WHOLE OCEAN spread out, glittering.

Outside, a round orange Dragonite with a delivery bag flapped alongside the window, waving.

"The dark was the mountain saying goodbye," breathed Cha, "so the ocean could say hello."

"A goodbye," said Momo, cheeks glowing, "is just a hello standing backwards."

---

"Snacks!" announced Tama. "I packed a little extra, just in case."

Three dumplings leaned on one skewer, leaned on Momo, who leaned on the warm glass.

"Squish together," Momo whispered, "stay together." The train hummed them west.

---
