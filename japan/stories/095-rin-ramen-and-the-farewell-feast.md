# Story 95: Rin Ramen and the Farewell Feast

**Main Character:** Rin Ramen
**Guest Characters:** Maru Matcha, Momo Mochi, Dai Daruma, and the whole gang (Tama Onigiri, Niko Nigiri, Tako & Yaki, Kori Kakigori, Sora the Soft-Cream, Mero Melonpan, Dora the Dorayaki, Pock Ramune)
**Setting:** A big communal table set for a grand farewell dinner
**Theme:** A shared meal holds a whole friendship inside it
**Japanese Spotlight:** **itadakimasu** (ee-tah-dah-kee-MAHS) — let's eat (thanks for the food)
**Cameo:** Tanjiro, Nezuko, Zenitsu and Inosuke (Demon Slayer) — four travelers tumble out of the dark; Inosuke shovels a noodle mountain into his boar mask, Zenitsu's wail about everyone leaving shakes the wobbly table, Nezuko hums from her box, and Tanjiro sniffs the broth and bows to Rin like a master; walk-on: Usopp (One Piece)

---

The longest table in the world stood under the stars, lantern to lantern.

In the middle, on a stool, Rin Ramen waved a ladle like a tiny conductor.

"MORE STEAM!" called Rin. "This is a FAREWELL FEAST, not a Tuesday lunch!"

It was six tables pushed together, and one wobbly one that was "basically fine."

It tipped a soy dish into Dai Daruma's lap. "Fall down seven, bounce up eight!" said Dai.

Tomorrow everyone went home. Rin's plan: a feast so big the goodbye could never arrive.

---

His broth pot was taller than he was. He stirred it from a ladder.

"Six hours of cooking," said Maru Matcha, drifting over. "Whisk slow. Feel calm."

"I CAN'T feel calm!" slurp-laughed Rin. A noodle-curl bounced off his head into the broth.

"Even the calmest tea," said Maru, "was once boiling water."

Rin had no idea what that meant, but it sounded nice. He kept stirring.

---

Then down the lane came the whole gang: a bouncing, fizzing, wobbling wave of treat-friends.

Momo Mochi stretched her taffy arms down the whole table and hugged everybody at once.

"Squish together, stay together!" said Momo, peach cheeks glowing.

Rin made enough noodles to wrap around the moon and tuck it in.

Rin looked down the table at every friend from the journey. His steam went thick and quiet.

He lifted the ladle to serve the first bowl. And froze. The steam stopped.

"If I serve it," said Rin, wobbly, "we eat it. Dinner ends. Everybody goes home."

A tear sizzled on his cheek. He plopped another noodle mountain on the table instead.

---

Four travelers tumbled out of the dark: checkered haori, yellow haori, boar mask, and a wooden box.

"FOOD!" bellowed Inosuke, shoveling a whole noodle mountain into his boar mask. The mask stayed on. Somehow.

"Everybody's LEAVING tomorrow?" shrieked Zenitsu, wailing so hard the lanterns swung and the wobbly table shook.

"Mmm!" hummed Nezuko, peeking from the box on Tanjiro's back, bamboo muzzle and all.

Tanjiro sniffed once and bowed low. "Onions," said Tanjiro, "added at hour three. Masterful."

The four settled at the far end, Tanjiro still bowing, Inosuke still chewing, Zenitsu sniffling.

---

CREAK. The wobbly table folded like a tired knee.

Rice balls, dumplings and seventeen trays slid down the table like a toboggan.

"Bounce up EIGHT!" said Dai, hopping under the broken leg and holding it up with his head.

Momo stretched both arms wide and caught the trays, flump flump flump, like a net.

Then the great pot tipped. It slid, slow and enormous, down the table. Toward Rin.

---

Maru raised her whisk and tapped the pot once. It stopped, teetering over Rin's head.

Golden broth sloshed over the rim and dripped on his noodle-curls.

"Rin," said Maru calmly. "Serve it, or wear it."

Rin looked up at the wobbling feast he had tried to keep forever.

He lifted the ladle high and scooped the first bowl straight from the tipping pot.

The pot, lighter now, settled back with a sigh. Rin's steam returned in one happy plume.

"A feast isn't the end of a friendship," said Rin. "It IS the friendship. So share it!"

---

"Everybody! Paws together!" called Rin. Paws, fins, arms and stubby legs pressed together.

"**Itadakimasu** (ee-tah-dah-kee-MAHS) — let's eat, thanks for the food!" roared the whole table, and the stars leaned in.

And then: slurp.

Dai challenged Momo to a noodle-tower contest and lost, buried to the eyebrows.

"I once ate a noodle THIS long," said Usopp, a long-nosed traveler in a cap, arms wide.

Rin sat at the center, steaming gently, watching everyone eat the feast.

---

The last bowl emptied. The last steam curled toward the moon. The goodbye felt full.

Friends leaned against friends down the long table, yawning, warm to the brim.

"Everything's better warm and bubbly," said Rin softly. And it was.
