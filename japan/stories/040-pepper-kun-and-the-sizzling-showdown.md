# Story 40: Pepper-kun and the Sizzling Showdown

**Main Character:** Pepper-kun (Pepper Lunch sizzle)
**Guest Characters:** Mero Melonpan; Rin Ramen; Gyo, a tiny hungry dumpling
**Setting:** A sizzling Pepper Lunch counter in a buzzing food court
**Theme:** Showing off is fun, but sharing the plate is better
**Japanese Spotlight:** **suteeki** (soo-TEH-kee) — steak / sizzling beef
**Cameo:** The Mask Council: Zechs Merquise, Rau Le Creuset and Full Frontal (Gundam) — three masked judges take a table that appears from nowhere, raise plates, demand a LOUDER sizzle and argue over whose mask is the most dramatic until popcorn buries them; walk-on: Sanji (One Piece)

---

The night-market food court glowed with a hundred lanterns and smelled like everything delicious.

Pepper-kun, an iron hot-plate in a chef's bandana, struck a pose. "Prepare for the SIZZLE of the CENTURY!"

He flexed. Steam shot off his shoulders. Tsss-POP! A baby bun clapped so hard it fell over.

"Stir it, sizzle it," he sang, scattering corn like confetti, "and SHARE it! Well. Mostly the first two."

Pepper-kun wanted one thing: to be the loudest plate in the whole food court.

---

Two counters down bubbled the trouble: Rin Ramen, who was impossible to be grumpy at.

"Everything's better warm and bubbly!" sang Rin, his slurpy laugh booming across the hall.

Treat-folk flocked to him. Rin waved a friendly noodle at Pepper-kun. That was the annoying part.

Pepper-kun's butter swirl went flat. "Anybody can bubble. But sizzle? Sizzle is ART."

He raised his tiny spatula like a sword. "Tonight. A Sizzling Showdown. Loudest plate wins."

---

By the lanterns, a puffy green-gold bun snored. Mero Melonpan could nap through a typhoon.

"Who's all that **suteeki** (soo-TEH-kee) — sizzling beef — for?" yawned Mero, one eye open.

"For showing OFF," said Pepper-kun, practicing a deeper bow and bonking the counter. "Louder than fireworks."

"That's a lot of beef to be loud alone," said Mero, and dozed off mid-yawn.

---

Pepper-kun banged his spatula like a gong. "GATHER 'ROUND! The Showdown BEGINS!"

Dumplings hopped closer. Buns bounced in. Rin wobbled over, steaming politely.

He cranked the heat. "FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!" TSSSSSS! Beef flew into a spiral. He bowed.

---

Three uniforms swept in, red, white, red. Three masked judges sat at a table that simply appeared.

"The Mask Council will judge," declared Zechs, silver bird-mask gleaming, platinum hair down to the stool.

Rau Le Creuset raised a plate, white half-mask tilted. "Adequate," said Rau silkily.

Full Frontal raised his plate upside down. "Louder," said Frontal behind his dark visor. "Much louder."

Pepper-kun cranked the heat higher. "My mask is the most dramatic," said Zechs, striking a pose.

"Mine has seen more drama," purred Rau. Frontal polished his visor with a napkin.

At the table's end, a blond cook in a black suit sniffed the steam. "Smooth," said Sanji.

---

Pepper-kun heaped on ALL the beef. ALL the corn. ALL the butter. Sparks danced on his rim.

"THREE! TWO! ONE!" Butter whooshed up like a golden geyser. The corn began to pop.

POP. POP-POP. POPOPOPOPOP! Corn burst into popcorn and rained over the whole hall.

Lanterns swung. A dumpling wore three kernels as a hat. Rin caught six in his broth. "Delicious."

Three plates rose high and stayed there, popcorn piling on three masks. The Council held very still.

Pepper-kun shot into the air on his own steam and landed upside down on Mero.

Mero slept on. "Crackle on the outside," he murmured, and kept snoring.

---

The popcorn settled. The hall went quiet. One tummy grumbled, loud and lonesome.

"Loudest ever," said Gyo, a tiny front-row dumpling, kindly. "But we're hungry. Nobody's getting any."

Pepper-kun looked at his tall plate. "Oh," he said, steam puffing small. "I forgot the sharing part."

---

Pepper-kun wobbled upright, grabbed his spatula, and this time he served.

"Front row, first bite!" Plip! "You! And YOU! Corn for the corn-lovers!"

He flipped a beef strip behind his back. It landed on Gyo's plate. Gyo squealed.

He plopped a spiral into Rin's bowl. "Everything's better warm and bubbly," said Rin, "and SHARED."

"The sizzle's just the doorbell," said Pepper-kun, tipping his spatula. "The feast is the point."

---

"Everybody count!" called Pepper-kun, and a hundred voices chanted, "FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!"

Pepper-kun pulled his bandana down like a blanket. "Stir it, sizzle it, share it. Mostly the last."

By the lanterns, Mero smiled in his sleep, one popcorn kernel perched on his crust.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
