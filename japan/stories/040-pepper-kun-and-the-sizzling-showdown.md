# Story 40: Pepper-kun and the Sizzling Showdown

**Main Character:** Pepper-kun (Pepper Lunch sizzle)
**Guest Characters:** Mero Melonpan; Rin Ramen; Gyo, a tiny hungry dumpling
**Setting:** A sizzling Pepper Lunch counter in a buzzing food court
**Theme:** Showing off is fun, but sharing the plate is better
**Japanese Spotlight:** **suteeki** (soo-TEH-kee) — steak / sizzling beef

---

The night-market food court glowed with a hundred lanterns and smelled like everything delicious.

In the middle sat a tiny iron hot-plate in a chef's bandana, about to get LOUD.

"Crumbs and noodles!" announced Pepper-kun, striking a pose. "Prepare yourselves for the SIZZLE of the CENTURY!"

He flexed. Steam shot off his shoulders. Tsss-POP! A baby bun clapped so hard it fell over.

"Stir it, sizzle it," he sang, scattering corn like confetti, "and SHARE it! Well. Mostly the first two."

Because Pepper-kun wanted one thing: to be the loudest plate in the whole food court.

---

The trouble bubbled two counters down. It was Rin Ramen, who was impossible to be grumpy at.

"Everything's better warm and bubbly!" sang Rin, his slurpy laugh rolling across the hall like thunder.

Treat-folk flocked to him. Rin waved a friendly noodle at Pepper-kun. That was the annoying part.

Pepper-kun's butter swirl went flat. "Anybody can bubble. But sizzle? Sizzle is ART."

He raised his tiny spatula like a sword. "Tonight. A Sizzling Showdown. Loudest plate wins."

"Fine by me," said Rin cheerfully, which made it so much worse.

---

By the lanterns, a puffy green-gold bun snored. Mero Melonpan could nap through a typhoon.

"Who's all that **suteeki** (soo-TEH-kee) — sizzling beef — for?" yawned Mero, one eye open.

"For showing OFF," said Pepper-kun, practicing his deeper bow. "Louder than gongs. Louder than fireworks."

"That's a lot of beef to be loud all by yourself," said Mero, and dozed off mid-yawn.

---

Pepper-kun banged his spatula like a gong. "GATHER 'ROUND! The Sizzling Showdown BEGINS!"

Dumplings hopped closer. Buns bounced in. Rin wobbled over, steaming politely. A semicircle of hungry faces.

Pepper-kun cranked the heat. "FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!" TSSSSSS! Steam tickled the lanterns.

The treat-folk roared. He flung the beef into a perfect spiral. He winked. He bowed.

"Not loud enough," he muttered, glancing at Rin, and cranked the heat higher.

---

He heaped on ALL the beef. ALL the corn. ALL the butter. Sparks danced on his rim.

"THREE! TWO! ONE!" The butter whooshed up like a golden geyser. The corn began to pop.

POP. POP-POP. POPOPOPOPOP! Corn burst into popcorn and rained over the whole food court.

Lanterns swung. A dumpling wore three kernels as a hat. Rin caught six in his broth. "Delicious."

Pepper-kun shot into the air on his own steam, sizzling, and landed upside down on Mero.

Mero slept on. "Crackle on the outside," he murmured, and kept snoring.

---

When the popcorn settled, the hall went quiet. One tummy grumbled, loud and lonesome.

"It was the loudest ever," said Gyo, a tiny front-row dumpling, kindly. "But we're hungry. Nobody's getting any."

Pepper-kun looked down at his gloriously tall plate. His steam puffed small.

"Oh," said Pepper-kun. "I made all that noise about sharing, and forgot the sharing part."

Being the loudest plate felt lonely. Like clapping with only one spatula.

---

Pepper-kun wobbled upright, grabbed his spatula, and this time he served.

"Front row, first bite!" Plip! "You! And YOU! Corn for the corn-lovers!"

He flipped a beef strip behind his back. It landed on Gyo's plate. Gyo squealed.

He plopped a steaming spiral into Rin's bowl. Rin slurped and laughed his bubbliest laugh.

"Everything's better warm and bubbly," said Rin, "and SHARED." He tipped his bowl in salute.

"The sizzle's just the doorbell," said Pepper-kun, tipping his spatula back. "The feast is the point."

---

Soon the whole food court was passing plates and trading bites, louder than any sizzle.

"Everybody count!" called Pepper-kun, and a hundred happy voices chanted, "FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!"

Pepper-kun pulled his bandana down like a blanket. "Stir it, sizzle it, share it. Mostly the last part."

By the lanterns, Mero smiled in his sleep, one popcorn kernel perched on his crust.

---
