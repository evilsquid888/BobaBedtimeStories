# Story 35: Kuma Kuri and the Autumn Leaf Trail

**Main Character:** Kuma the Kuri (roasted chestnut / kuri kinton sweet)
**Guest Characters:** Maru Matcha
**Setting:** A mountain temple path blazing with red and gold autumn leaves
**Theme:** Change can be the most beautiful thing of all
**Japanese Spotlight:** **momiji** (moh-MEE-jee) — autumn maple leaves
**Cameo:** Unicorn Gundam (Gundam Unicorn) — stands among the maples like an odd white tree, splits its armor open with a KA-CHUNK and glows red to match the leaves, whirling every leaf on the path, then stays a tree-sized statue; walk-on: Shikamaru (Naruto)

---

Kuma the Kuri was hiding inside a teakettle, and planned to stay.

"Kuma," said a calm voice down the spout. "Kettles are for tea. You are a chestnut. Different jobs."

"It's still summer in here," echoed Kuma. "I want it to STAY. Also my toes are cold."

"Chestnuts don't have toes," said Maru Matcha.

"Then why are they cold?" demanded Kuma. Maru had no answer. Kuma had won before breakfast.

---

"Walk with me," said Maru. "If it's terrible, you may go straight back in."

Kuma climbed out, fuzzy cap first, and stopped so fast he nearly tipped over.

The whole mountain had turned to fire, the friendly marshmallow-toasting kind. Crimson, orange, buttery gold.

"**Momiji** (moh-MEE-jee) — autumn maple leaves," said Maru. "The mountain wears them only a little while."

"Oh," whispered Kuma. "They're showing off."

Then the breeze plucked his lucky leaf, last autumn's, flat and brown, right out of his scarf.

---

"MY LEAF!" Kuma hopped after it. But it was leaf-shaped, and the whole path was leaves.

"Seen my leaf?" he asked a mossy stone lantern. It said nothing. Kuma took that as a no.

He stuck his whole head in a puddle of gold leaves. Blub. Autumn soup. No leaf.

Then, between two maples, a tall smooth white trunk with one long horn on top. Odd tree.

---

It was a robot, all white and sleek, taller than the maples, standing very still.

"Excuse me, tall sir," said Kuma politely. "Have you seen a small brown leaf?"

KA-CHUNK. The robot's armor split along every seam and glowed red, red as the reddest maple.

Its horn opened into a V. Every leaf on the path leapt up in the whoosh.

Kuma hopped in the whirl of red, grabbing at leaf after leaf after leaf. None of them brown.

The Unicorn Gundam settled into its glow, red as autumn exactly, and stayed. A tree-sized statue.

---

Kuma stomped off in a huff and stepped on a leaf. Crunch. He froze. The best sound ever.

"Maru. MARU. Did you hear that?" He stomped into a drift. Crick-crack-CRUNCH.

"Small and roasty, ready and toasty!" he cheered. "The whole mountain is crunchy like ME!"

He took a running start, which for a chestnut is a determined waddle, and flopped backward. FWUMP.

Beside him lay a ninja with a spiky ponytail, watching clouds. "What a drag," yawned Shikamaru.

"Agreed," said Kuma happily, and flopped again.

---

Up where the steps grew steep stood the grandest maple on the mountain, every leaf scarlet.

"Three hundred autumns old," said Maru. "And it isn't tired of this yet."

Kuma looked up. And up. Snagged on the highest branch fluttered one small brown leaf. His.

He hopped. He hopped higher. He hopped onto Maru's bowl-head. Not even close.

"Please," said Kuma to the tree, very politely. "That's my leaf. It lives next to my heart."

---

A great gust swooped down the mountain and the old maple let go. Every leaf. A red river.

Kuma threw both arms up and the leaves swept over him, around him, right up to his cap.

The brown leaf whirled past, waved once, and sailed off over the valley with ten thousand friends.

"It gives all its momiji away," said Kuma, digging out. "Every year. And it never runs out."

"You can let go of something AND love it," said Maru. "Like crisp outsides and soft hearts."

Kuma bowed low to the tree. The reddest leaf of all landed on his nose. You're welcome.

---

"Summer didn't take anything," said Kuma, flat in his pile. "It handed everything to autumn."

At the bottom the old teakettle waited in the grass. Kuma patted it. "I'm done hiding."

He tucked the new reddest leaf into his scarf, right by his heart, and the mountain crackled softly.

---
