# Story 60: Sora Soft-Cream and the Sapporo Snow Festival

**Main Character:** Sora the Soft-Cream
**Guest Characters:** Momo Mochi, Kori Kakigori, Pock Ramune
**Setting:** The Sapporo Snow Festival, full of giant snow sculptures
**Theme:** Big dreams can be built one handful at a time
**Japanese Spotlight:** **yuki-matsuri** (yoo-kee mah-tsoo-ree) — snow festival
**Cameo:** Frieren, Fern, Stark and Aura the Guillotine (Frieren: Beyond Journey's End) — Frieren's useless spell makes the leaning tower steam like warm bread, Stark hides from a snow dragon, and Aura orders the snow to obey until her scales tip toward a snow rabbit; walk-on: Piplup (Pokémon)

---

Sora the Soft-Cream drifted through the white, cone crunching, humming. "Soft swirls, slow smiles."

WHUMP. A beach-ball snowball bowled past and knocked the curl off her head.

"Oh no," said Sora, very softly, because Sora said everything softly. "My curl."

It lay buried in the drift. Sora was now a swirl with a flat top.

Beyond the drift roared the **yuki-matsuri** (yoo-kee mah-tsoo-ree) — snow festival. Castles. Dragons. A giant cat.

---

Her friends stood around a knee-high heap shaped, honestly, like a sad potato.

"TALLER, darlings!" boomed Kori Kakigori, sunglasses on in winter. "The COOLEST tower ever!"

"Pop the marble!" fizzed Pock Ramune, zipping past. "Let the fun fizz out!"

Momo Mochi squished Sora into a hug. "You're here! Squish together, stay together!"

"What can I do?" asked Sora, one degree from melting. She wanted, very much, to build something tall.

Everyone went quiet. "Maybe," said Kori gently, "you could hold my sunglasses?"

---

Sora drifted to a quiet corner, scooped one little scoop, and patted it down.

Scoop. Pat. Scoop. Pat. The slowest snow-building anyone had ever seen.

Across the field, Kori piled snow SO fast his tower leaned like pudding.

Pock drilled fizzy tunnels all through the base. "Ventilation, pop!"

Momo stretched herself around the whole thing like a mochi belt. "I'll hold it!"

---

The tower groaned and leaned, one breath from falling.

Three travelers tramped out of the snow. Frieren, a small white-haired elf, raised her staff.

"I know a useful spell," said Frieren. The tower puffed bread-smelling steam and sagged like a warm loaf.

"That is not useful, Mistress Frieren," said Fern, purple-haired and deadpan.

Stark hid behind Fern with his huge axe. "The dragon," he whispered. The dragon was snow.

Then Aura the Guillotine swept in beside a giant floating golden scale. "Snow tower! OBEY!" said Aura.

The scale tipped toward a snow rabbit instead. Aura stomped into the drifts and sat sulking, back turned.

---

The tower, warm and wobbly, chose down. FLUMPH. One avalanche on Kori, Pock and Momo.

The heap tipped over the slope edge and rolled.

A giant snowball, three friends inside, bowling straight toward Sora's quiet corner.

"MELTDOWN!" came Kori's voice from inside. "A total MELTDOWN, and I would KNOW!"

Sora set down her scoop. The snowball hit her cone and stopped. Excellent doorstop, that cone.

She dug, one scoop at a time. Out came Pock. Out came Momo. Out came sunglasses.

---

Then Kori, upside down. "I'm ALIVE," he announced. "Slightly slushy. And I smell like TOAST."

Pock shook off the snow and stopped fizzing. "Hey," he said. "What's THAT?"

In the quiet corner, something had grown. A tower, smooth as soft-serve, taller than the lamppost.

It looked exactly like Sora. Just enormous. "You built that?" whispered Momo.

"I did one scoop," said Sora. "Then one more. It was nice, so I forgot to stop."

"It's the COOLEST," whispered Kori, lowering his sunglasses, and for once he wasn't joking.

---

"**Ganbatte** (gahn-baht-teh) — keep going!" cried Momo. "Sora-style. One scoop at a time!"

Kori scooped with enormous flair and only fell over twice.

Piplup, a small blue penguin, slid past on its belly delivering snow. Proud chirp.

Momo squished the gaps, because squishing was the thing Momo was best at.

Scoop by scoop the swirl climbed, and everyone patted a crown on top.

Pock dug Sora's curl out of the drift and plopped it back on her head. Floppy. Perfect.

---

That night, lanterns glowed inside every sculpture, and Sora's tower shone softest gold of all.

"The fastest builders built nothing," fizzed Pock sleepily. "The slowest built the whole thing. Pop."

Sora leaned against the giant swirl of herself and let the snow land on her nose.

"Soft swirls," she murmured, "slow smiles."
