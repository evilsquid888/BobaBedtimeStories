# Story 100: The Grand Rising-Sun Reunion

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi
**Guest Characters:** Niko Nigiri, Kori Kakigori, Maru Matcha, and the entire cast — every friend from the whole journey, including Kei the kei-truck and the Sushi-ya Express
**Setting:** A spectacular finale festival beneath Tokyo Tower and Skytree, with fireworks over all of Japan
**Theme:** Every good story circles back to friends and home
**Japanese Spotlight:** **oyasumi** (oh-YAH-soo-mee) — goodnight
**Cameo:** Ash Ketchum and Pikachu (Pokémon) — backs up the plaza steps gawking at the towers, bumps the firework rack, and Pikachu's sneeze-spark fires the first firework an hour early, so the whole sliding gang looks up and slides into the snack table; walk-on: Soot Sprites (My Neighbor Totoro)

---

The biggest, bounciest evening in the history of evenings began with a hug that got carried away.

Momo Mochi had hugged forty-one friends in a row, and her taffy arms had tangled the plaza.

"Squish together, stay together!" sang Momo. "Oops. Maru, is that your whisk in my elbow?"

"It is," said Maru Matcha calmly. "Whisk slow. Feel calm. Also, please un-squish my whisk."

Tonight was the Grand Rising-Sun Reunion. Momo wanted every friend under one sky for the fireworks.

---

Niko Nigiri stood very straight beside a stack of paper lanterns, salmon cape gleaming.

"Lanterns first. Then the feast. Then, precisely on time, the **hanabi** (hah-NAH-bee) — fireworks," said Niko. "Neatly!"

"Or deliciously!" cheered Momo, and stretched an arm across the plaza to squish him.

Glowing lanterns rose over the plaza until Tokyo looked like a jar of fireflies.

Then, through the lantern light, swept a towering mountain of strawberry shaved ice in sunglasses.

"Sweeties! Your COOLEST friend has ARRIVED!" cried Kori Kakigori, arms wide.

---

The plaza was warm. Very warm. "LITERAL meltdown!" gasped Kori. "I'm a puddle of my former fabulous self!"

"The hot-spring tent!" cried Momo. "Steam is cool-warm and never pours. Everybody, LIFT!"

Momo wrapped both arms around Kori and heaved. Kori was heavy. Also slippery.

Niko fanned him with his cape and marched ahead. "Make way! Fragile! Fabulous!" called Niko.

Kori dripped a slick pink trail. The whole chain of friends behind them hit it and slid.

WHOOSH went the gang across the plaza, a bowling ball of treat-friends headed for the snack table.

---

Up the steps, backwards, gawking at the towers, came a trainer in a red-and-white cap and blue jacket.

"Wow, Pikachu, LOOK at those towers!" shouted Ash Ketchum, and backed straight into the firework rack.

The small yellow mouse on his shoulder sneezed sparks. "Pika-CHU!" squeaked Pikachu. The first firework lit.

WHEEE-BOOM. One golden chrysanthemum burst over the plaza, a full hour early. Every face looked up.

Every sliding treat-friend looked up too, and slid, still gawking, toward the snack table.

"Sorry! That one's on us!" called Ash, and sat down on the steps with Pikachu, grinning sheepishly.

---

Momo flung an arm out to stop the slide. The arm lassoed the cracker pyramid instead.

The pyramid teetered. Tottered. Tipped. A glorious, crunchy, slow-motion avalanche.

Crackers splashed into the broth pot. Crackers bonked Niko's cap. One landed on a dozing bun's nose.

Then Momo laughed, a big bubbly laugh, and the whole plaza laughed with her.

Niko looked at the mess, then at the crackers in his cape. "Not neatly, then," said Niko. "Happily."

---

At the hot-spring tent, Maru whisked the steam into a snug cloud around Kori, cozy-warm and drip-proof.

"Ahhh," sighed Kori, sunglasses fogging. "Sweeties, I am still fabulous. Slightly smaller. But STILL fabulous."

A scurry of fuzzy black fluffballs with big white eyes crept in, each holding one tiny star candy.

The Soot Sprites pressed a star candy into every paw. Maru accepted hers with a solemn bow.

---

A hush fell over the plaza like a quilt pulled up to a chin.

"Momo," said Niko, voice wobbling. "Would you light the rest? Not neatly. Just happily."

Momo stretched her arm up, up, past Tokyo Tower, nearly to Skytree, and booped the hanabi awake.

Golden chrysanthemums. Fizzing blue spirals. And one enormous pink heart that looked exactly like Momo.

---

When the last spark faded, the whole plaza went soft and quiet, one breathing heap.

"Every good story," said Momo softly, "circles back to friends and home."

"Coolest ending ever," murmured Kori, mostly a happy puddle in sunglasses.

Maru raised her whisk like a wand toward the sleeping city. "**Oyasumi** (oh-YAH-soo-mee) — goodnight," said Maru.

"Oyasumi," murmured everyone, one by one, until the word itself seemed to glow.

And the towers dimmed, just a little, the way you turn down a lamp for someone you love.
