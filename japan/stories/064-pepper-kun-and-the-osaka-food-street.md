# Story 64: Pepper-kun and the Osaka Food Street

**Main Character:** Pepper-kun (Pepper Lunch sizzle)
**Guest Characters:** Pika the Kushikatsu (a dazzling new fried-skewer star); a sleepy street pigeon
**Setting:** Osaka's neon Dotonbori food street, the "nation's kitchen"
**Theme:** Someone else's shine doesn't dim yours
**Japanese Spotlight:** **kushikatsu** (koo-shee-kah-tsoo) — crispy golden fried skewers on sticks
**Cameo:** Jotaro and Star Platinum (JoJo's Bizarre Adventure) — strike a dramatic pose under the neon in front of Pepper-kun's counter, the whole street copies them, and the spirit snatches every stray corn kernel out of the air; walk-on: Vegeta (Dragon Ball)

---

At lantern-time, the sky over **Dotonbori** (doh-tohn-boh-ree) — Osaka's food street — turned plum purple.

Neon flickered awake, pink, orange, electric blue. The giant mechanical crab waved one creaky leg.

Pepper-kun, a round iron hot-plate piled with beef, corn and butter, hopped onto his counter.

He tightened his chef's bandana. "Five! Four! Three! Two! ONE. SIZZLE!" Tssss-POP!

Cheers. Steam. Happy tummies. "Best moment of the day," said Pepper-kun. "And all mine."

He fed the loud ones up front, and found the shy ones behind lantern posts.

---

Then, right between "three" and "two," a new sound rolled down the street. FWOOOOMP.

Every head turned. Pepper-kun finished his countdown for one pigeon, who was asleep.

Three stalls down stood Pika the **kushikatsu** (koo-shee-kah-tsoo) — crispy fried skewers — fanned like a peacock's tail.

"Good evening, Osaka!" sang Pika. "One stick! One dip! NO double-dipping, sweethearts!"

Her line stretched past the crab, over the bridge, and around a corner.

"Buttery beef spectacular? Anyone?" called Pepper-kun. The pigeon woke up. It wanted corn.

---

"Not jealous," muttered Pepper-kun, polishing his plate. "She has a fwoomp. I have a sizzle."

Next night: his biggest show ever. Countdown from TEN. The line wandered off at six.

He juggled three beef strips, caught two, and wore the third as a meaty hat.

He unveiled his brand-new corn-confetti cannon. It worked perfectly. Backwards.

A golden fountain of corn fired straight into his own bandana and stayed there, jingling.

His finale: a steam cloud so big he vanished inside it. "TA-DAAA!" said the fog.

---

The fog cleared. At his counter stood a tall stranger in a long dark coat and cap.

"An AUDIENCE!" cried Pepper-kun. "Three! Two—" The stranger raised one bent arm in a dramatic pose.

Behind him flickered a huge glowing purple spirit, striking the exact same pose. "Yare yare," said Jotaro.

Pika struck the pose. The pigeon struck the pose. The crab struck it with eight legs.

Star Platinum snatched every stray corn kernel from the air, faster than blinking, and stacked them.

Jotaro tugged his cap brim low and stood under the neon, statue-still, the spirit frozen behind him.

---

Behind a lantern post, Pepper-kun sulked. "Only so much shine on one street," he grumbled. "She's hogging it."

Sitting still, he watched the line. Loud ones got skewers. A teeny mochi got bumped out three times.

And Pika, dazzling Pika, couldn't see past her own lights. Panko flying. Sauce everywhere. "NEXT!"

---

A sneaky spark whispered: good. Then the mochi sighed, and Pepper-kun's legs moved first.

"Saw you waiting," said Pepper-kun, setting down beef, corn and one butter curl. "So dinner came to you."

"**Itadakimasu** (ee-tah-dah-kee-mahs) — thanks for this food," whispered the mochi, and lit up like a paper lantern.

"Third lantern post, shy chestnut, two sticks, extra sauce," Pepper-kun told Pika. "I deliver. You fry."

Pika blinked through the panko. "You're the famous one. Why help me?"

"Because I can see the back of your line," said Pepper-kun. "And you can't."

---

Fryer fwoomped, plate sizzled, and golden kushikatsu rode out hot on iron beside the beef.

"One dip ONLY!" hollered Pika, and the whole line hollered it back like a festival chant.

By the takoyaki stall, Vegeta folded his arms at the batter balls. "Octopus," he scowled. "In a BALL."

The shoving stopped. Nobody shoves once they know they'll be seen. The pigeon got corn.

---

Past midnight, Pika leaned down. "Embarrassing thing. I turned everything up to out-shine YOU."

Pepper-kun nearly dropped his spatula. "Out-shine ME? I built a corn cannon. I wore a BEEF HAT."

They laughed so hard the crab waved both legs at once. A Dotonbori first.

The neon blinked out one by one. "Another lamp doesn't dim yours," said Pepper-kun. "It brightens the street."

He pulled his bandana down over his eyes like a blanket. "Three. Two. One." Tsss. "Goodnight, Dotonbori."

---
