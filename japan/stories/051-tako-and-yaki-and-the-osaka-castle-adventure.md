# Story 51: Tako and Yaki and the Osaka Castle Adventure

**Main Character:** Tako & Yaki (takoyaki twins)
**Guest Characters:** Miss Yunomi, a tidy teacup tour guide; Chizu, an old folding map
**Setting:** The grand grounds of Osaka Castle with its golden details
**Theme:** Curiosity is twice as fun with a partner
**Japanese Spotlight:** **shiro** (shee-roh) — castle
**Cameo:** The Knight Sabers and Priss (Bubblegum Crisis) — four glossy hardsuits drop onto a turret roof in a perfect line and pose, shaking every blossom and the clue out of the plum tree onto the twins, Priss roars up late on her red motorcycle, and the tour guide decides they are the armor exhibit; walk-on: Panda (Jujutsu Kaisen)

---

Osaka Castle rose above the trees like a stack of green-roofed pancakes, each smaller than the last.

Through the stone gate bounced two golden batter-balls, joined on top by one wobbly bonito-flake wave.

"**Shiro** (shee-roh) — castle!" they cheered together, and high-fived so hard they bonked foreheads.

"Tako, LOOK," said Yaki, pointing up with his whole body, which tipped him over.

At the tippy-top of the tower, something flashed. Gold. Fishy. A tail flicked.

"A golden fish on a roof," said Tako. "We're going to see it up close."

Their guide, a tidy teacup named Miss Yunomi, waved a small plain flag. "We touch NOTHING golden."

---

That sounded exactly like a dare.

Past the **ishigaki** (ee-shee-gah-kee) — stone wall they went, every rock as big as an elephant.

"Good morning, rock elephant," said Yaki, bowing until his bonito flakes brushed the moss.

Miss Yunomi talked about a great lord. The twins drifted, like a curious smell toward a kitchen. Gone.

---

At the tower's foot leaned Chizu, an old creased paper map drawn in little pictures.

"Shachihoko," said Chizu, nodding at the roof. "Golden fish with tiger faces. Always two. East and west."

Tako looked at Yaki. Yaki looked at Tako. The same wonderful thought landed in both.

"Roll us once, we come as two!" they sang, and Chizu chuckled dust off his corners.

"Follow the picture-clues," said Chizu. "Lantern, plum tree, well. They lead to the best view."

---

A stone lantern was carved with a tiny striped tower. The twins frowned.

"That makes no sense," said Tako.

"It makes PERFECT sense," said Yaki. "You just have to look at it wobbly."

They tilted sideways until their sauce ran uphill. "Count the layers!" shouted Yaki.

They bounced backward to count. "One, two, eleventy, a bunch," said Tako. "Five!"

---

The second clue hung high in a plum tree. Tako bounced onto Yaki's head. A whisker short.

Then four sleek armored figures, blue, red, green and pink, dropped from the sky onto the turret roof.

The Knight Sabers landed in a line and posed. The turret rang like a gong. Every blossom fell.

Down came the wooden fish too, plop, onto two takoyaki buried to their eyes in pink.

VRRRM. A red motorcycle skidded up late, red-jacketed rider waving. "Missed the pose?" rasped Priss. The helmets nodded.

"The armor exhibit," sniffed Miss Yunomi, bustling past the petal pile. "Lovely. Touch NOTHING shiny."

The Knight Sabers held their pose. Priss leaned on her motorcycle. Two petal-dusted takoyaki popped out, sneezing.

---

The last clue sat on the rim of a well, its stones smooth as candy.

Tako leaned to look. Then more. Then entirely too much.

PLOP went Tako, straight into the dark, and the bonito wave snapped tight like a rope.

"YAKI!" came a small echo. "It's dark down here! And lonely! And DARK!"

Yaki dug in, puffed up, and heaved. The wave stretched. The wave held.

Up popped Tako, dripping, into a hug. "Curious alone is scary," he said. "Together, it's a game."

---

Up the long stone steps they puffed, as the afternoon melted into gold.

At the peak curled the two shachihoko, gleaming like they'd been dipped in sunshine.

"Which one's shiniest?" whispered Yaki. The east fish blazed. The west blazed back.

"Neither," said Tako. "Together they're twice as shiny. Like us."

Miss Yunomi puffed up the steps. "I lost two batter-balls," she wheezed. "A teacup's worst day."

"Ishigaki! Shachihoko! Rock elephants!" babbled Yaki, hopping. "We learned the WHOLE lesson. Twice as fast!"

---

The sun slipped lower and the whole shiro glowed rosy, like a castle made of honey.

A big panda in a school jacket sat down beside them. "Nice castle," rumbled the Panda.

The twins leaned together, shoulder to round little shoulder, bonito wave finally still.

"Best treasure?" yawned Tako.

"Knowing stuff," said Yaki. "And you." High-five. Of course.

---
