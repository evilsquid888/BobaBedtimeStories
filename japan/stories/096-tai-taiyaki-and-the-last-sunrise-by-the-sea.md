# Story 96: Tai Taiyaki and the Last Sunrise by the Sea

**Main Character:** Tai Taiyaki
**Guest Characters:** Kuma the Kuri, Maru Matcha, Dai Daruma; a fat scarf-stealing gull
**Setting:** A quiet seaside cliff at dawn near the journey's end
**Theme:** Endings are just sunrises in disguise
**Japanese Spotlight:** **hinode** (hee-NO-deh) — sunrise
**Cameo:** Sung Jinwoo and Igris with shadow soldiers (Solo Leveling) — steps out of the cliff's shadow, says "Arise," and a line of purple shadow soldiers guards the beach; the gull drops the scarf on Igris's plume and every soldier kneels and salutes it; walk-on: Piplup (Pokémon)

---

Waking sleepy treats before dawn is like folding fog into a neat square.

But Tai Taiyaki had a flashlight, a plan, and a fin that would not quit.

"PSST! Wake up! The BEST part of the adventure is about to happen!" he whispered.

"Tai," yawned Maru Matcha from under a blanket, "the moon is still wearing its pajamas."

"Swim toward the sweet stuff! Today it's the SKY, from the clifftop, all of us!" said Tai.

Dai Daruma bounced up, bonked the lantern, and bounced up again. "Fall down seven! Let's CLIMB!"

---

Dai bounced up three steps and tumbled down four. "Bounce up EIGHT!"

Kuma the Kuri came last, small and sturdy in his autumn-leaf scarf, minding the stragglers.

The sky over the sea went from black to the faintest gray. "Faster!" called Tai.

Then a fat gull swooped out of the dark and snatched Kuma's scarf clean off.

Kuma stood very still. Two tiny tears slid down his glossy cheeks.

"It's not the scarf," he whispered. "It's the LAST sunrise. Tomorrow we all go home."

"Endings feel like a campfire going out," said Kuma. "And I'm small. I'll be cold."

---

Tai's heart squeezed. Then his fin twitched. "Nobody steals a sunrise from MY friend!"

He launched off the path and swam through the air after the gull, waddling furiously.

Down they swooped to the gray beach, where a small blue penguin was sliding on its belly.

"Chirp!" said Piplup proudly, and slid straight between Tai's fins like a bar of soap.

Tai wobbled, flopped onto the sand, and spat out a mouthful of seaweed. The gull cackled.

---

Then the cliff's shadow stood up, and a calm stranger in a black coat stepped out of it.

"Arise," said Sung Jinwoo softly, and a line of soft purple shadow soldiers rose along the beach.

The gull flew over them. Every glowing head tilted up. Then every head tilted back down.

A tall shadow knight in red-black armor stood last. The gull dropped the scarf right onto his plume.

Igris knelt and saluted the scarf. Every shadow soldier knelt and saluted it too.

"That is not what I meant," murmured Jinwoo, and sat down on the sand to think about it.

The gull snatched the scarf off the plume, flapped up the cliff, and dropped it on a ledge.

---

Tai dove for the ledge, caught the scarf in his mouth, and landed. Stuck.

"FALL DOWN SEVEN!" hollered Dai from the top, and bounced straight off the edge to help.

"That's two," said Maru, peering over. "Nobody else jump."

Kuma looked down. Kuma looked at the crumbly rock. Kuma took a breath.

"Small and roasty," he said quietly. "Ready and toasty." And he climbed down, cap first.

He wedged his sturdy chestnut body into a crack, round and firm, like a step.

Tai hopped over him. Dai bounced over him. Kuma came up last, scarf snug again.

---

"The shy one saved the brave ones," said Maru. "That is how it usually goes."

"Watch the **hinode** (hee-NO-deh) — sunrise," panted Tai. "Tell me if it looks like an ending."

The gray sea went peach, then rose, then a bright laughing gold.

The very top of the sun lifted out of the sea like a bun from a steamer.

Far below on the sand, a shadow knight still held his salute, facing the light.

"I GET IT!" hollered Dai. "The day fell DOWN last night, and it bounced UP!"

---

"The sun never drowns," said Tai. "It swims around. Endings are just sunrises in disguise."

Kuma's roasted glow came back. They squished together in the hinode. Dai got squished too and went boing.

The gull landed beside Kuma and dropped a shiny shell. Possibly an apology.

"Swim toward the sweet stuff," Tai whispered to the sun. And the sun swam right along.

---
