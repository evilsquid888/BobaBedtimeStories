# Story 32: Momo Mochi and the Pink Petal Snow

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi (mochi rice cake)
**Guest Characters:** Mero Melonpan
**Setting:** A hilltop park during a swirling cherry-blossom snowfall
**Theme:** Catching a little joy is better than chasing all of it
**Japanese Spotlight:** **hanami** (hah-NAH-mee) — flower-viewing

---

On the very first morning of pink, Momo Mochi bounced out of bed and off the ceiling. BONK.

She squished her nose to the window. The whole hilltop had turned into a cloud of cherry blossoms.

It was **hanami** (hah-NAH-mee) — flower-viewing — day. Pink snow, falling and falling.

"I am going to catch every single one!" she squealed, cheeks going peach.

A petal landed on her dimple. She went cross-eyed looking at it and toppled over. Floomp.

---

Up the hill she bounced, puffing starch behind her like a tiny determined comet.

Under the biggest tree a crackly green-gold bun lay flat on his back, dozing. Mero Melonpan.

"Mero! Help me catch ALL the petals!" called Momo.

"Mm," said Mero, eyes closed. "Catching sounds like a lot. I'm busy." He was not busy.

Momo cupped both squishy paws. A petal floated down. She lunged. Splat. Face-first in the grass.

The petal landed three steps away, looking, Momo thought, rather smug about it.

---

She dashed left. Missed. Dashed right. Missed. Leapt high. The petal swooped sideways, giggling.

One landed on her head. "GOTCHA!" She grabbed her own head, missed, and bonked her dimple.

"Where did it GO?" The petal was already sitting on Mero's nose.

She stretched for a high one, up and up. Her arm wound twice around the tree trunk.

Fwip-fwip-fwip. She unwound herself and sat down with the world going pink and round and pink.

"Bigger," she decided. "I'll go to the SOURCE." And she climbed the tree.

---

Up the trunk, out along a branch, both arms stretched toward the thickest pink cloud of all.

The branch bent. Momo stretched longer. Longer. Petals everywhere, every one just out of reach.

"Momo," said Mero from below, dreamily, "you look like a very brave noodle."

The branch sprang. Momo sproinged off it, bounced twice, and landed flat on her back beside Mero.

She had chased nine hundred petals. She had caught exactly zero.

---

Her arms lay around her in tired loops. Her cheeks had gone from rosy to pooped.

"I just wanted to catch the magic," she huffed. "There's so MUCH of it."

Then, flat on her back, there was nothing left to do but look up.

Oh. The whole sky was one slow pink river, swirling, tumbling, in no hurry at all.

A petal landed on her cheek. She let it.

One landed in her open resting paw, the very paw that had caught nothing all day.

"Huh," said Momo softly. "When I stop chasing, they come to ME."

---

Beside her, Mero yawned and sat up, and petals poured off him like a pink avalanche.

He was covered. Head to crumbs. Hundreds of petals. Possibly a thousand.

"Mero," gasped Momo. "You caught more than ANYONE."

"Did I?" said Mero, blinking. "I just lay still. Crackle on the outside, dreams on the inside."

A petal landed on Momo's open mouth. "I fwas twying to catfh AW of it," she mumbled.

She blew it off, pfft, and it spiraled up, delighted.

"You can't hold a whole sky in two paws," said Momo. "You lie back and let some land."

---

They lay in a row all afternoon, soft and crackly, and let the hanami happen TO them.

They named the silliest petals. One looked exactly like a tiny pig. They argued about it happily.

When the sun went low and gold, a few petals sat in Momo's paw. Just a few.

And a few, in your open paw beside a friend, turned out to be exactly, perfectly enough.

"Squish together," said Momo, stretching her arms around Mero, "stay together."

Mero was already dozing again under a blanket of pink. "Mm," he said. "Stay."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
You don't have to grab every bit of joy with both paws. Lie back, like soft Momo on the spring grass, and let the good things drift down and land where they like. A few are plenty. Goodnight.
