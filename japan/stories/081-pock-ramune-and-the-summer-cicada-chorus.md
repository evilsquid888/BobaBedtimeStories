# Story 81: Pock Ramune and the Summer Cicada Chorus

**Main Character:** Pock Ramune (Ramune marble soda bottle)
**Guest Characters:** Zee, a tiny shy green cicada; Stripes, a stripy cicada who counts to three; Bibi, a baby cicada who blows raspberries; a hungry beetle
**Setting:** A green countryside hillside buzzing with summer cicadas
**Theme:** Every voice adds to the song
**Japanese Spotlight:** **natsu** (nah-tsoo) — summer
**Cameo:** Kakashi and Pakkun (Naruto) — poof in under the crooked tree mid-sing-off, score the cicada chorus seven out of ten (Pakkun gives it a four), and the outraged hill buzzes Pock's marble clean out; walk-on: Agumon (Digimon)

---

It was the fizziest morning of **natsu** (nah-tsoo) — summer, and Pock Ramune could not sit still.

He zipped up the green hillside, pop pop pop, like a firework that had lost its rocket.

He bonked off a tree trunk, bounced off a boulder, and landed upside down in the grass.

From every tree came a sound like a thousand tiny saws made of sunshine. Zzzeeee. Zzzeeee.

It was the song of the **semi** (seh-mee) — cicadas, wrapped around the hill like a buzzy blanket.

"I have decided," Pock announced to nobody, "to find the LOUDEST cicada of the whole summer. Pop!"

---

He pressed his cool glass ear against the first tree. "Helloooo? Are you the loudest? Sing!"

A cicada no bigger than a sesame seed peeked out. "Zee?" said Zee. "Zee-zee."

"Very gentle," said Pock, fizzling. "But I want the BOOMER." Zee's tiny zee shrank back behind her leaf.

Next tree: Stripes, who only sang on three. "One, two, three... zzz! Wait. Was that three?"

Next: Bibi, a baby who couldn't buzz yet, so she blew a raspberry straight into Pock's marble.

---

"A SING-OFF!" he fizzed, doing three backflips. "Loudest cicada wins the Champion Cup! POP!"

The Champion Cup was a polished acorn cap on a twig. A beetle was trying to eat it.

Every cicada on the hill came buzzing to the big crooked tree until the branches sagged.

Pock hopped onto a round stone and raised one arm. "Loudest buzz wins! One, two, POP!"

---

A hundred cicadas shoved their voices forward at once. ZRRRGGHZZZBRRRZZT.

POOF. White smoke billowed under the tree, and a tall silver-haired ninja leaned on the trunk, reading.

"Don't mind me," said Kakashi lazily, turning a page of his little orange book. "I'm judging."

Cicadas buzzed so hard his hair blew flat. "Seven out of ten," said Kakashi. "Points off for spit."

A small pug in a tiny vest sat down beside him. "Four," grunted Pakkun. "I've heard louder puddles."

Outraged, the hill buzzed hardest yet. Kakashi tucked Pakkun under one arm and hopped to the top branch.

There he perched, reading, one lazy wave held high, as the tree shook like a wet dog.

---

The buzz rattled Pock's marble right out. POP! He fizzed over the whole front row.

The Champion Cup toppled off its twig and bounced away down the slope. The beetle chased it.

The cicadas grumbled and huffed off, one by one, to sulk in their own corners.

Pock sat down. "I came to find the loudest voice," he whispered, "and I made every voice stop."

---

Then, from the lowest branch, something tiny. Zee. Zee-zee.

Zee was still singing. Not for a cup. Because it was summer, and that was her part.

Pock floated down beside her, slow as a settling bubble, and listened.

"You're not the loudest, sweetie," said Pock. "You're something better. You're a piece of the song."

---

He floated from grumpy corner to grumpy corner. "Loud isn't the prize," said Pock. "Come sing your part."

Stripes came back, still counting. Bibi came back. The beetle came back too, bumping the Champion Cup uphill.

---

Pock climbed his stone one last time. "Sing your own voice, soft or wobbly or stripy. Together."

The shy zee slipped in like a silver thread. Stripes lost count, and it didn't matter at all.

Bibi's pbbbt tumbled in after, and the whole tree giggle-buzzed.

A hundred small voices wove into the fullest summer sound, and the dewdrops sparkled back up.

Pock added his own pop, pop, pop. "Squish my marble," he sniffled, "that's the best sound in natsu."

---

In the tall grass, a small orange dinosaur with big claws listened, eyes closed. "Nice," said Agumon.

The beetle set the Champion Cup on the lowest branch, next to Zee, and nobody argued.

The whole hill hummed, warm and gold, and a fizzy bottle dozed among the cicadas.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
