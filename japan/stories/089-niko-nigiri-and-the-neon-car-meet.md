# Story 89: Niko Nigiri and the Neon Car Meet

**Main Character:** Niko Nigiri (nigiri sushi) & the Sushi-ya Express truck
**Guest Characters:** Kei the kei-truck; Maru Matcha; a lot full of wonderfully different cars
**Setting:** A neon-lit parking-lot car meet at night
**Theme:** Show your own style and you'll find your crew
**Japanese Spotlight:** **nakama** (nah-kah-mah) — crew / companions
**Cameo:** Dom and the Charger (Fast & Furious) — one rev shakes every decoration off the lot, Dom lives a quarter mile at a time, Kei lives at putt-putt, and the swamp-green sushi truck gets called family; walk-on: Kaneda's red bike (Akira)

---

Import Night. The whole parking lot glowed like spilled jewels, pink neon humming, blue neon fizzing.

The Sushi-ya Express stopped exactly between the lines. Ding.

"Not one wheel out of place," said Niko Nigiri, smoothing his cape. "Let's do this neatly... or deliciously!"

Around him: a van painted like a cat, a car floating on grape-soda light, a mirror-shiny speedster.

"Everyone's so fancy," said Niko. "We're a food truck with a curtain. We simply must FIT IN."

"PUTT-PUTT, here I AM!" Kei bounced in so hard her mud-flaps flapped.

---

The Express rang her bell uncertainly. "Very sure!" said Niko, which he always said before things got interesting.

He borrowed under-glow from the grape-soda car. The truck glowed swampy green, like a forgotten salad.

He slapped a giant chrome flame over the noren curtain, to look **hayai** (hah-yah-ee) — fast.

"But you don't go fast," said Kei. "You park very carefully and ring a bell."

Crooked stripes. A spoiler that fell off, got taped on, and fell off again, onto Kei.

---

Fuzzy dice big as pillows went on the mirror. Now the truck saw one square inch.

She crept forward one inch and bonked the jazz bus, which gave a surprised toot.

Then the speakers. Enormous ones. Niko flipped the switch.

BOOM. The pickled ginger leapt off its plate and did a terrified dance across the asphalt.

Three soft-boiled eggs wobbled off the counter and rolled away giggling.

---

Then the ground growled. A black muscle car with a tall silver blower prowled in, rumbling.

Out stepped Dom, broad and bald in a white tank top, and leaned on the Charger.

Dom revved the Charger once. The lot shook. Dice swung, speakers toppled, eggs bolted.

"I live my life a quarter mile at a time," said Dom, low and gravelly.

"I live mine at putt-putt," beeped Kei, bouncing up to the Charger's enormous tire.

Dom looked at the swamp-green sushi truck a long time. "Family," he said, and patted the hood.

The spoiler fell off again. Dom folded his arms, leaned on the Charger, and stayed that way.

---

Niko chased the eggs under the cat van, around the speedster, and came back with somebody's hubcap.

The Express looked like a sushi truck that lost a fight with a fireworks store.

"I copied EVERYONE at once," whispered Niko. "Now we look like a recycling bin in disguise."

---

A skateboard glided up, steered by a tiny bamboo whisk. Maru Matcha blinked at the disaster, slowly.

"Whisk slow," said Maru. "Feel calm."

"I can't feel calm!" said Niko, very un-Niko-ishly. "I don't know what we ARE anymore!"

"Why did the cat van come tonight?" asked Maru. "Because it loves cats. So it became a cat."

"They came to find their **nakama** (nah-kah-mah) — crew," said Maru. "You find a crew by showing, not hiding."

"But we're not flashy," said Niko. "We're a little sushi truck that does things neatly."

"Then be that," said Maru. "Beautifully."

---

Niko took it all off. Neatly. Under-glow returned with a bow. Wires coiled in perfect loops.

He wiped the truck white and indigo again, untangled the lanterns, and set the fish-flag free.

He folded down the counter and made sushi. Not flashy sushi. Neat sushi, each grain in place.

Ting-ting-ting. "Fresh sushi, anywhere the road goes!" sang the Sushi-ya Express.

---

The silver speedster rolled over first, shyly. Then the cat van, the sticker car, the jazz bus, humming.

"See? SEE?" beeped Kei, bouncing. "You didn't have to fit in. They came to YOU!"

"Nakama," said Niko softly. "We found our nakama."

---

The neon dimmed. One by one, every wonderfully different car flicked its lights twice: goodnight.

Even Kaneda's bike, a long glossy red motorcycle lying in its own skid, blinked its headlight twice.

Niko sat on the warm counter beside Maru and yawned the neatest yawn in the lot.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
You are not meant to be a copy of anyone. Glow your own soft color into the dark, and the right friends will find their way to it. Tuck in close. Breathe slow. Goodnight.
