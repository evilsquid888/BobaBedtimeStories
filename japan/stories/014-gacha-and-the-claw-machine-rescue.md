# Story 14: Gacha and the Claw-Machine Rescue

**Main Character:** Gacha the Gachapon
**Guest Characters:** None
**Setting:** A jingling Tokyo crane-game (UFO catcher) arcade
**Theme:** Teamwork lifts what one alone cannot
**Japanese Spotlight:** **issho ni** (ee-shoh nee) — together

---

Gacha the Gachapon rolled into the arcade like a marble shot from a slingshot, bouncing off a stack of tokens and landing — *plonk!* — right in a basket of prize tags.

"Surprise!" Gacha announced, popping open just enough to wink. "You never know where I'll turn up!"

The arcade was the jingliest place in all of Tokyo. Lights blinked pink and gold. Machines went *bloop-bloop-doodle-oop*. A claw the size of a soup ladle dangled over a mountain of plush toys, and somewhere a tiny voice was going, "Help? Hello? Anyone? Heeelp-ish?"

Gacha froze mid-bounce. A voice that small could only mean one thing: someone very small was in very big trouble.

"That," Gacha whispered, "is the most mysterious surprise of all. And I LOVE a mystery."

Gacha rolled toward the claw machine, leaving a trail of curious little clatters, and pressed right up against the glass to look inside.

There, wedged deep at the bottom of the prize pile — under a heap of plushies taller than a teapot — was a tiny, trembling plush bunny with one floppy ear and a stitched-on smile that was trying *very* hard to stay brave.

"I'm Mochi-Usagi," the little bunny squeaked. "And I have been stuck down here for THREE WHOLE SONGS. The claw keeps grabbing me and dropping me. *Whoosh — clutch — plop.* Every time! I am beginning to think the claw does not even LIKE me."

Gacha gasped, which for a capsule means rocking back and forth very fast. "The claw likes everybody. The claw just has the grip of a wet noodle. Don't you worry, little one. I'll get you out!"

And Gacha — being round, being smooth, and being roughly the size of a small grapefruit — immediately tried to climb up the outside of the glass. Gacha got about two inches up.

Then rolled back down.

*Plonk.*

"Hmm," said Gacha. "Detail I overlooked: I have no arms. Or legs. Or, technically, a plan."

Mochi-Usagi's floppy ear drooped. "Oh no. You're stuck too?"

"Not stuck," said Gacha, rolling upright with a proud little spin. "Just *thinking.* And here is what I think: one little capsule can't do this alone." Gacha's painted grin spread wide. "But you know what a capsule is FULL of? Surprises. And the best surprise of all is friends. **Issho ni** (ee-shoh nee) — together — that's the secret prize inside every hard thing. Wait right there, Mochi-Usagi!"

"I literally cannot go anywhere else," the bunny pointed out.

Gacha rolled off at top capsule-speed — which is a wobbly, giggly, bumpity sort of speed — straight out the arcade door to find the whole gang.

They were just down the street, sharing a paper cone of warm chestnuts.

"FRIENDS!" Gacha cried, skidding to a stop and spinning like a top. "Emergency! Adorable emergency! A teeny plush bunny is trapped at the bottom of a claw machine and the claw has the grip of overcooked udon and we must rescue them issho ni — TOGETHER!"

Everyone leapt up at once.

"A rescue?" boomed Rin Ramen, steam puffing happily off the top of the bowl. "Everything's better warm and bubbly — and a rescue is the bubbliest thing of all! Count me in!"

"A rescue should be done *neatly,*" said Niko Nigiri, standing very straight, salmon cape gleaming. "Or," Niko added with a little bow, "deliciously. Let's do this neatly... or deliciously!"

"Fall down seven, bounce up eight!" cheered Dai Daruma, wobbling so hard with excitement that he tipped clean over and rolled all the way to the arcade door before anyone could catch him.

Momo Mochi bounced up, peach cheeks glowing. "A trapped friend? Oh, that's a job for a SQUISH! Squish together, stay together!" Momo's taffy arms stretched out and gathered everyone into one big group huddle, including, by accident, a confused mailbox.

"Whisk slow," murmured Maru Matcha, twirling her tiny bamboo whisk. "Feel calm. We will think clearly, and then we will lift." She did not raise her voice, and somehow that made everyone listen harder.

So in they all tumbled — Gacha rolling, Rin steaming, Niko marching neatly, Momo bouncing, Dai wobbling, and Maru gliding calm as a pond.

The claw machine loomed before them, glowing and glittering and full of plushies. And down at the very bottom, one floppy ear waved.

"FRIENDS!" Mochi-Usagi squeaked, eyes shining. "Are you all here to get me out?"

"We are here," said Gacha grandly, "to give it a turn — surprise inside! Now. Who has a plan?"

Everyone started talking at once.

"We tip the machine!" said Rin.

"We do NOT tip the machine," said Niko, horrified. "We will do this with order and dignity. And quarters."

"We squish the glass with love!" said Momo, pressing both cheeks to the window and leaving two adorable smudges.

"We could," offered Dai, "yell encouraging things until the claw believes in itself."

Maru raised one half-moon eye. "Or," she said gently, "we play the game. The right way. But together — because no one of us can do it alone." She pointed her whisk at the claw controls, which were very high and very far apart. "The button is over here. The joystick is over there. The timer is fast. One friend cannot reach both and watch the bunny too."

Everyone went quiet, looking at the controls. Maru was right. The joystick to aim, the button to drop, and the little window to watch through — all three were too far apart for one small dessert to manage.

"Then we split the job," said Niko, eyes bright. "Like a perfect bento box. Everyone gets one neat part!"

And oh, what a beautiful plan it was.

Dai Daruma planted himself at the joystick. With his round crimson body, all he had to do was lean — *thunk* one way, *thunk* the other — to nudge the claw left and right. "I am BUILT for leaning!" he declared, wobbling proudly. "Leaning is my whole personality!"

Niko Nigiri stood guard at the big red button, standing tall and ready, salmon cape fluttering. "I shall press," Niko announced, "at precisely the correct moment. Not a heartbeat early. Not a heartbeat late."

Rin Ramen scooted to the glass to be the spotter. "I'll watch our little bunny and call out where to go!" Rin's broth swirled with excitement. "Left a noodle! Right a noodle! That's an official ramen measurement, by the way."

Momo Mochi pressed up beside the bunny's side of the glass, peach cheeks beaming. "And I'll be the cheer squad! Mochi-Usagi, you are doing AMAZING just by being brave!"

And Maru Matcha stood in the middle of them all, whisk lifted like a tiny conductor's baton. "I'll keep the rhythm," she said softly. "When we hurry, we drop things. When we move together, we lift them." She breathed in slow. "Whisk slow. Feel calm. Ready?"

"Ready!" the whole gang shouted.

Gacha rolled up onto a token and inserted it with a satisfying *clink-clink-WHIRR.* The machine sang a happy little tune, and the claw shivered to life high above.

"Surprise round, BEGIN!" Gacha cried.

"Left a noodle!" called Rin.

*Thunk,* leaned Dai.

"Right HALF a noodle!"

*Thunk-ish,* leaned Dai.

"Forward two corn-kernels!"

"That is not a real measurement!" laughed Niko.

"It is in MY bowl!"

Maru kept the rhythm — *whisk... whisk... whisk...* — slow and steady, and the whole team breathed along with it. The claw glided over the prize pile, smoooth as soft-serve, until it hovered right above one floppy gray ear.

"Steady..." whispered Maru.

"You can do it, claw! Believe in yourself!" cheered Dai, who could not help encouraging *everyone,* even machinery.

"NOW, Niko!" Rin shouted. "DROP THE CLAW!"

Niko pressed the button at precisely the correct moment — *not a heartbeat early, not a heartbeat late* — and the claw came down with a slow, dramatic *hummmm.*

The whole arcade seemed to hold its breath.

The claw closed around Mochi-Usagi... and lifted...

...and the noodly grip began to slip!

"It's letting go!" gasped Momo. "Everybody — cheer LOUDER! That's how love works on noodle-claws!"

So they did. Rin steamed and slurped a cheer. Dai wobbled and roared, "BOUNCE UP EIGHT!" Niko held the button with both hands and the dignity of a thousand sushi chefs. Momo squished against the glass so hard her cheeks went rosy as plums. And Gacha spun in delighted circles, popping open and shut, open and shut.

"Together!" they all shouted. "Issho ni!"

And it was the plan that did it — Niko's button-press, not a heartbeat early, not a heartbeat late, and every friend doing their one neat part. (Though between you and me, I like to imagine the claw heard the word *together* and decided it didn't want to be left out.)

The claw swung over the chute and — *plip!* — dropped Mochi-Usagi safely into the prize door.

The little plush bunny tumbled out into Momo's waiting taffy arms, both ears flopping with joy.

"YOU DID IT!" Mochi-Usagi squealed. "I'm FREE! And I wasn't even scary-stuck at the end, because you were all out there being so silly!"

"WE did it," Gacha corrected, beaming. "All of us. That's the surprise inside every capsule, every claw machine, every hard thing in the whole wide world." Gacha gave a happy little spin. "Alone, I couldn't even climb the glass. But issho ni? We can lift just about anything."

Momo squished the whole gang — and the bunny, and the still-confused mailbox — into one enormous group hug.

"Squish together," Momo sang, "stay together!"

And from somewhere deep in the warm, giggly middle of the pile, a tiny floppy-eared voice said, "Best. Claw machine. EVER."

By and by, the arcade lights dimmed one row at a time, and the machines blinked their last sleepy *bloop-bloops* like fireflies settling down for the night. Mochi-Usagi rode home tucked snug under Momo's taffy arm, both floppy ears already drooping with sleep. The friends wandered out into the quiet, lantern-lit street, yawning in a happy little chain — Rin first, then Dai, then everyone — their footsteps and rolls and wobbles growing slower and softer all the way home.

## Goodnight Blessing
Little dreamer, the biggest things in the world are never lifted by one pair of hands alone — they're lifted together, slow and steady and side by side. When something feels too heavy for you tonight, remember the noodle-claw, and the friends who cheered, and the word that made it hold on: issho ni, together. You are never truly stuck, not while there are kind hearts nearby. So snuggle down, let the day go soft, and know that someone, somewhere, is glad to lift the world with you tomorrow. Goodnight.
