# Story 74: Momo Mochi and the Warm Winter-Solstice Bath

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi
**Guest Characters:** Kori Kakigori, Maru Matcha, Mero Melonpan
**Setting:** A cozy bathhouse on the winter solstice with floating yuzu fruit
**Theme:** Small warm rituals carry you through the cold
**Japanese Spotlight:** **yuzu** (yoo-zoo) — yuzu citrus
**Cameo:** Pen Pen (Evangelion) — waddles into the brand-new yuzu bath with his own towel before anyone else, sinks to the eyes, spreads his flippers across the whole tub and turns down every trade while the yuzu bob around him like ducklings; walk-on: Kapibarasan (Kapibarasan)

---

On the coldest night of the year, Momo Mochi waddled up a snowy lane, arms full of trouble.

Lemony, bumpy trouble: eleven **yuzu** (yoo-zoo) — yuzu citrus, smelling like a hug wearing perfume.

One yuzu bonked down the steps. Then two. Then a whole parade of grumpy little suns.

"Squish me sideways!" puffed Momo, lunging with both taffy arms. Two came back stuck to her cheeks.

Tonight was **toji** (toh-jee) — the winter solstice, when the dark stays longest and cold pinches hardest.

Momo wanted a bath so full of yuzu you couldn't see the water. A tub of treasure.

---

Inside the bathhouse, Maru Matcha hugged her whisk like a teddy bear, swirl drooping.

Kori Kakigori's sunglasses had frosted over. "I can't even see my own COOLNESS, sweeties!"

Mero Melonpan was asleep standing up. "Crackle on the outside," he mumbled. "Dreams on the... zzz."

Momo burst in with a swirl of snow and starch. "I brought the winter solstice!"

"YUZU!" said Momo, and lined them up on the floor, where they bonked away in every direction.

The big stone tub sat cold and empty. Under it, the fire was out.

---

"A **yuzuyu** (yoo-zoo-yoo) — yuzu bath needs HOT water," said Momo. "Everybody warm something!"

Momo hugged the tub to warm it. The stone was so cold her arms stuck fast.

"I am hugging a rock," said Momo, flat against the tub. "The rock is winning."

Kori leaned over the embers and blew. Frost came out. The last spark went fft.

"I made it WORSE," wailed Kori. "Who let an ice mountain near a fire?"

Maru whisked the cold water into froth. It stayed freezing. "Feel CALM," said Maru, whisking frantically.

Then Momo spotted the woodpile. Mero was asleep on top, snoring crumbs.

---

Three friends heaved. Mero slid off the logs into the tub, snoring.

Momo stuffed the stove with wood and blew with all her mochi might. WHOOMP.

Hot water roared in, lifting Mero like a crackly little boat. "Five more minutes," said Mero.

Momo plopped the yuzu in, giving each a job. "You, left corner. You two, best friends."

The steam cleared. The tub glowed gold, like a treasure chest spilled open.

---

The back door banged open. In waddled a penguin with a red crest, backpack and towel.

"Kwaa," said Pen Pen, and hopped into the tub ahead of everyone, towel folded on his head.

He sank to his eyes. The yuzu bobbed around him like ducklings round a very serious duck.

Kori dipped one icy toe in. Pen Pen spread both flippers and took up the entire tub.

Momo held out a yuzu as a trade. Pen Pen took it and sank lower. Negotiations were over.

He stayed exactly there, sunk to the eyes, sipping his juice can through the steam.

"The bath has a penguin in it," said Maru, whisking calmly. "Winter is full of surprises."

---

Kori sank in at the far end and melted at the edges, the bath turning sunset-pink.

"Don't worry, sweeties," sighed Kori. "I am simply becoming a STRAWBERRY YUZU LIMITED EDITION."

Maru floated perfectly still, a yuzu on her swirl like a tiny golden moon.

Momo meant to dip one toe. The water was so cozy she slid right in. Floomp.

"Ahhh," said Momo. Then: "...Oh." Warm mochi spreads.

Momo went soft, then softer, then stretched across the whole bath like a blanket.

Everyone floated on top of her, wrapped in warm mochi, toasty from flipper to swirl.

"A small warm thing," said Momo, muffled under three yuzu, "carries you all the way to morning."

---

At the far end, a round beige capybara sighed and sank to its nose under a yuzu.

---

Later, Momo blooped back into a plump rice cake, and each friend took one yuzu home.

Momo waddled home through the snow with the last one. It smelled like morning all the way.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
The night is long, little one, but you are wrapped in warm things: soft blankets, soft breaths. Let a small cozy warmth carry you to morning. The dark is only the world tucking you in. Goodnight.
