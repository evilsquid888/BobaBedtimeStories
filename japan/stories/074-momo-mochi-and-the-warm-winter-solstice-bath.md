# Story 74: Momo Mochi and the Warm Winter-Solstice Bath

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi
**Guest Characters:** Kori Kakigori, Maru Matcha, Mero Melonpan
**Setting:** A cozy bathhouse on the winter solstice with floating yuzu fruit
**Theme:** Small warm rituals carry you through the cold
**Japanese Spotlight:** **yuzu** (yoo-zoo) — yuzu citrus

---

On the coldest night of the year, Momo Mochi waddled up a snowy lane, arms full of trouble.

Lemony, bumpy trouble: eleven plump **yuzu** (yoo-zoo) — yuzu citrus, which smell like a hug wearing perfume.

One yuzu escaped and bonked down the steps. Then two. Then a whole parade of grumpy little suns.

"Squish me sideways!" puffed Momo, lunging with both taffy arms. Two yuzu came back stuck to her cheeks.

Tonight was **toji** (toh-jee) — the winter solstice, when the dark stays longest and the cold pinches hardest.

Momo wanted a hot bath so full of yuzu you couldn't see the water. A tub of treasure.

---

Inside the glowing bathhouse, Maru Matcha hugged her whisk like a teddy bear, swirl drooping.

Kori Kakigori's sunglasses had frosted over completely. "I can't even see my own COOLNESS, sweeties!"

Mero Melonpan had fallen asleep standing up. "Crackle on the outside," he mumbled. "Dreams on the... zzz."

Momo burst through the door in a swirl of snow and starch. "I brought the winter solstice!"

"You brought... lemons?" said Kori, peering over the frost.

"YUZU," said Momo, and lined them up on the floor, where they rolled away in every direction.

The big stone tub sat cold and empty. Under it, the fire had gone out.

---

"A **yuzuyu** (yoo-zoo-yoo) — yuzu bath needs HOT water," said Momo. "Everybody warm something!"

Momo hugged the tub to warm it up. The stone was so cold her arms stuck fast.

"I am hugging a rock," said Momo, flat against the tub. "The rock is winning."

Kori leaned over the embers and blew. Frost came out. The last spark went fft.

"I made it WORSE," wailed Kori. "Why did anyone let an ice mountain near a fire?"

Maru whisked the cold water into a lovely froth. It stayed freezing. "Feel CALM," she said, whisking frantically.

Then Momo spotted the woodpile. Mero was asleep on top of it, snoring crumbs.

---

Three friends heaved. Mero slid off the logs and landed in the tub, still snoring.

"Five more minutes," said Mero, and slept on.

Momo stuffed the stove with wood and blew with all her mochi might. WHOOMP. Flames!

Hot water roared in, lifting Mero gently like a crackly little boat.

Momo plopped the yuzu in, giving each a job. "You, left corner. You two, best friends."

The steam cleared. The whole tub glowed gold, like a treasure chest spilled open.

And the smell! Bright and sweet, every sunny day winter had stolen, given back at once.

"It smells," sighed Mero, waking at last, "like the inside of a good nap."

---

Kori sank in and melted at the edges, syrup swirling until the bath turned sunset-pink.

"Don't worry, sweeties," sighed Kori. "I am simply becoming a STRAWBERRY YUZU LIMITED EDITION."

Maru floated perfectly still, a yuzu balanced on her swirl like a tiny golden moon.

Mero drifted in slow circles, asleep again, two yuzu following him like ducklings.

"Never wake him," whispered Momo. "Waking a Melonpan from a steam-nap is against the rules of winter."

---

Momo meant to dip one toe. The water was so cozy she slid all the way in. Floomp.

"Ahhh," said Momo. Then: "...Oh."

Warm mochi spreads. Momo went soft, then softer, then stretched across the whole bath like a blanket.

"MOMO!" gasped Maru. "You're everywhere!"

The friends floated on top of her, wrapped in warm mochi, and nobody shivered. Not even Kori.

"A small warm thing," said Momo, muffled under three yuzu, "carries you all the way to morning."

"Stay JUST like that," sighed Kori. "Warmest I've ever been in my frosty life."

---

Later, Momo blooped back into a plump round rice cake, and each friend took one yuzu home.

Momo waddled home through the snow with the eleventh, and it smelled like morning the whole way.

---
