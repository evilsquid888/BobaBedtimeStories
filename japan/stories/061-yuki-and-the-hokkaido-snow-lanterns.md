# Story 61: Yuki and the Hokkaido Snow Lanterns

**Main Character:** Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju
**Guest Characters:** Kinkan, a plump kumquat who keeps the hot-spring lodge; Chibi, the smallest ice lantern
**Setting:** A snowy Otaru canal lined with glowing ice lanterns
**Theme:** A little light shared chases away the cold
**Japanese Spotlight:** **rantan** (rahn-tahn) — lantern
**Cameo:** Calcifer (Howl's Moving Castle) — sulks in the canal's only lit ice lantern, refuses to share a single spark and blazes so hot he melts his own house into slush; walk-on: Wooper (Pokémon)

---

On the coldest night of winter, Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju burrowed deeper into her snowdrift.

"Mmmf," she said. "Five more minutes."

The cold pinched her ears and dropped a snowball down her neck.

"EEP!" Yuki shot up like a popped bun, snow fountaining off her head.

Below, Otaru lay hushed and silver by its frozen canal. Every lamp had blinked out.

"Cold without cozy," said Yuki, rubbing her cheeks. "That's against the rules. I'll fix it."

---

The wind practiced its spookiest wooooo across the black ice.

"You sound like a kettle that forgot the words," Yuki told it.

All along the canal stood hundreds of ice lanterns, like frosty teacups. All dark.

"Light us?" squeaked the smallest, Chibi. "We're **rantan** (rahn-tahn) — lanterns. Glowing is our whole job."

"I'd love to," said Yuki, patting her fluffy sides. "But I have no flame. I'm mostly bun."

---

She huffed warm breath on Chibi. Fog. A half-second glow. Dark again.

She piled snow around the lanterns to keep them snug. Darker still.

"Lovely thought," said Chibi politely, "but you've tucked me in for a very long nap."

Yuki dug Chibi out, blushing. Then, far down the canal, something flickered orange.

One lantern. Lit! Yuki hopped toward it so fast she skidded the last stretch on her bottom.

---

Inside sat a small living flame with a grumpy face. It flared tall. "WHAT," it crackled.

"I'm Calcifer," said the flame. "Only fire in a whole city of ice. A disgrace."

"Could you spare one spark?" asked Yuki sweetly. "For a few hundred friends?"

"SHARE?" Calcifer blazed so hot his ice lantern began to drip. "I'm barely a CANDLE!"

Drip became trickle. Trickle became puddle. The lantern slumped like warm butter.

"My house," hissed Calcifer, shrinking to a blue ember in the slush. "May all your bacon burn."

---

Up the hill Yuki puffed, toward the glowing **onsen** (ohn-sen) — hot spring — lodge. "I need KIND fire."

On the porch, by one candle, sat Kinkan, a plump kumquat in a knitted hat.

"I need fire," Yuki announced, then heard how that sounded. "For a GOOD reason!"

"One candle," yawned Kinkan. "Hundreds of lanterns. The cold's too big, little bun."

He lent it anyway, and tucked a stubby spare into her paw. "Mind the wind."

---

Yuki cupped the flame in both paws and tiptoed downhill like a bun carrying soup.

Wooooo went the wind. The flame bent flat. Yuki hunched over it like a snowball.

WOOOOO, bigger. Yuki dove behind a bridge post. The flame shivered, and held.

WOOOOOOO. The gust hit the bottom of the hill. Out went the candle.

Yuki stood in the dark holding a thread of smoke. "Oh. Oh, no."

Then she looked at Chibi. Hollow, round, thick ice walls. A cup. A tiny house.

---

Back up she hopped, borrowed the flame again, and set the candle INSIDE an ice lantern.

The ice walls hugged the flame. The wind huffed and puffed at them and gave up.

Down the hill it came, a tiny floating moon in a monkey's paws.

Yuki lit Chibi from it. Chibi bloomed buttery gold. "I'm GLOWING!" squeaked Chibi.

Then Yuki lit the stubby spare from Chibi. Chibi glowed exactly as bright as before.

"THAT'S the secret!" cried Yuki, bouncing. "Light makes MORE light! Take THAT, cold!"

---

Lantern to lantern, flame to flame, Yuki danced down the canal leaving a ribbon of gold.

A tiny blue Wooper popped through a fishing hole in the ice and flopped happily. "Woop!"

The whole canal glowed gold, doubled in the black ice, snow drifting like honey sparks.

Yuki flopped down in the middle, cheeks plum-pink. "One light did it," she yawned. "Then it shared."

Up the hill, Kinkan set his kettle whistling and let the brave bun nap among her rantan.

---
