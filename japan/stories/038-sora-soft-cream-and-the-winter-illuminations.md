# Story 38: Sora Soft-Cream and the Winter Illuminations

**Main Character:** Sora the Soft-Cream
**Guest Characters:** Yume, a lost baby snow-monkey-bun; Hana, Yume's big sister
**Setting:** A park glittering with millions of winter lights
**Theme:** Cold nights can hold the warmest glow
**Japanese Spotlight:** **kirei** (kee-RAY) — beautiful

---

On the coldest night of winter, the Light Park gate glittered like a dropped jewelry box.

There stood Sora the Soft-Cream, a tall pale-gold swirl, in the silliest, fluffiest scarf ever seen.

"Soft swirls, slow smiles," hummed Sora, breath puffing frost. "Tonight I shall not drip even one drip."

A snowflake landed on her curl and stayed there, sparkling, like a tiny crown.

"**Kirei** (kee-RAY) — beautiful," whispered Sora. All summer she hid from the sun. Winter was a soft-serve holiday.

But one worry sat in her tummy like an ice cube: are beautiful things beautiful all alone?

---

The first tunnel was a wave of blue lights frozen mid-crash, twinkling instead of splashing.

Sora tipped her whole swirl back to look up. "**Yuki** (YOO-kee) — snow, but made of stars."

Then a tiny something hopped past her cone, chasing a glowing moth. Round, fluffy, rosy-cheeked, and lost.

"I chased the glowy butterfly," squeaked the baby bun. "It was kirei! Now I can't find my family."

The little bun shivered so hard it nearly bounced off the path.

Sora, being ice cream, was the coldest hugger in the park. But Sora had a scarf.

She wound it round until only two rosy cheeks poked out. "Aahh," steamed the bun. "I'm Yume."

---

"A park is big," said Sora. "But lights are made for finding your way home. Carousel first."

The carousel of light turned slow and grand, golden horses rising and falling like a music box outdoors.

Yume stood on Sora's cone like a tiny lookout. "No family. But ooh, the wheel is kirei!"

The glowing moth fluttered past. Yume leapt after it onto a golden horse, which immediately went UP.

Sora waddled after the carousel on her cone-bottom, which is the slowest chase in history.

A string of blue bulbs snagged her curl and came along, so now Sora wore a glowing necklace.

Yume plopped back into Sora's swirl, still clapping. "My great-grand-bun claps at everything. Once at a radish."

---

Next came the Sea of Lights, blue and white bulbs rippling across the ground like a slow tide.

"My family loves this part," said Yume. "It looks like the **onsen** (OHN-sen) — hot spring — but sparkly."

A wave of light rolled under them, and Sora surfed it on her cone, Yume squealing on top.

Yume peered everywhere. No family. Little shoulders drooped inside the scarf.

"We're not lost," said Sora. "We're just not done looking. I packed patience. It never melts."

Then every light in the Sea of Lights flickered, buzzed, and went out. Dark.

---

The worry-ice came back bigger. What if a small cold creature depended on Sora, and she failed?

"I'm glad it's you, though," said Yume from inside the scarf.

Sora's whole swirl wobbled. "Pardon?"

"You're warm," said Yume. "Not outside. Inside. That's the warmest kind."

The ice cube in Sora's tummy turned into a candle. Her snagged necklace of bulbs glowed brighter.

"We don't need the path-lights," said Sora, standing straighter than soft-serve has ever stood. "We ARE lights."

---

Out of the dark rose a pine wrapped in a hundred thousand white lights, bright as moonbeams.

"Yumeee?" called a voice. "Yumeeee!"

"THAT'S MY BIG SISTER HANA!" squeaked Yume. "HANA! I'M HERE! WITH A SOFT-CREAM! AND A SCARF!"

Hana tumbled out from under the tree, and behind her bounced a whole pile of rosy snow-monkey-buns.

There were hugs. Oh, so many hugs, steaming and giggling in a squishy pile.

Yume toddled back holding up the scarf. "Keep it," said Sora. "Keep somebody warm with it next time."

---

"Stay for the finale," said Hana. "The biggest lights happen at the very end."

At the stroke of the hour every light in the park blazed up at once. Whoom.

"Kirei," whispered the whole family.

"Kirei," agreed Sora, glowing softest of all, with a small warm bun dozing against her cone.
