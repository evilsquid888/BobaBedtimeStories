# Story 38: Sora Soft-Cream and the Winter Illuminations

**Main Character:** Sora the Soft-Cream
**Guest Characters:** Yume, a lost baby snow-monkey-bun; Hana, Yume's big sister
**Setting:** A park glittering with millions of winter lights
**Theme:** Cold nights can hold the warmest glow
**Japanese Spotlight:** **kirei** (kee-RAY) — beautiful
**Cameo:** Vivy and Matsumoto (Vivy: Fluorite Eye's Song) — a blue-haired AI songstress rises on a stage of light and sings one note that makes every bulb pulse, sweeps Sora and Yume the wrong way on a wave of lights, strings up her nagging cube like a bauble, and pops the whole Sea of Lights dark; walk-on: Mimikyu (Pokémon)

---

On the coldest night of winter, the Light Park gate glittered like a dropped jewelry box.

There stood Sora the Soft-Cream, a tall pale-gold swirl in the silliest, fluffiest scarf ever.

"Soft swirls, slow smiles," hummed Sora, puffing frost. "Tonight I shall not drip one drip."

She struck a pose. Her cone skidded on the ice, and she posed again, sideways, against a lamppost.

"**Kirei** (kee-RAY) — beautiful," whispered Sora, righting herself. Winter was a soft-serve holiday.

One worry sat in her tummy like an ice cube: are beautiful things beautiful alone?

---

Sora tipped her swirl back under a tunnel of blue lights. "**Yuki** (YOO-kee) — snow, but made of stars."

Then a tiny something hopped past, chasing a glowing moth. Round, fluffy, rosy-cheeked, and lost.

"I chased the glowy butterfly," squeaked the baby bun. "It was kirei! Now I can't find my family."

Sora, the coldest hugger in the park, wound her scarf round it. "Aahh," steamed the bun. "I'm Yume."

---

"Lights are made for finding your way home," said Sora. "Carousel first."

The carousel of light turned slow and grand, golden horses rising and falling like a music box.

Yume stood on Sora's cone like a tiny lookout. "No family. But ooh, the horses are kirei!"

The moth fluttered past. Yume leapt after it onto a golden horse, which immediately went UP.

Sora waddled after the carousel on her cone-bottom, which is the slowest chase in history.

A string of blue bulbs snagged her curl and came along. Sora now wore a glowing necklace.

Yume plopped back into Sora's swirl, still clapping. "My great-grand-bun claps at everything. Once at a radish."

---

Next, the Sea of Lights: blue and white bulbs rippling across the ground like a slow tide.

A ripple of light passed under them, and Sora surfed it on her cone, Yume squealing.

"We're not lost," said Sora to the drooping scarf. "Not done looking. I packed patience. It never melts."

---

Then a stage of light rose from the Sea, and on it stood Vivy, a blue-haired songstress.

Vivy sang one note, clear as ice. Every bulb in the park pulsed with it.

The Sea of Lights swelled into a real wave and swept Sora and Yume the wrong way.

A white cube unfolded into a teddy bear. "Vivy! The mission! Not a CONCERT!" nagged Matsumoto.

Vivy sang higher. Lights wrapped Matsumoto head to toe and hung him up, a blinking bauble.

One highest note. POP. Every bulb went dark. "Encore?" said Vivy, bowing, Matsumoto blinking like a tired star.

---

The worry-ice came back bigger. A small cold creature depended on Sora, in the dark.

"I'm glad it's you, though," said Yume from inside the scarf. Sora's swirl wobbled.

"You're warm," said Yume. "Not outside. Inside. That's the warmest kind."

The ice cube in Sora's tummy turned into a candle. Her snagged necklace of bulbs glowed brighter.

"Who needs path-lights," said Sora, standing straighter than soft-serve has ever stood. "We ARE lights."

---

Out of the dark rose a pine wrapped in a hundred thousand white lights. "Yumeee?" called a voice.

"THAT'S MY BIG SISTER!" squeaked Yume. "HANA! I'M HERE! WITH A SOFT-CREAM! AND A SCARF!"

Hana tumbled out with a whole pile of rosy snow-monkey-buns. Hugs. So many steaming, giggling hugs.

One hugger was lumpy, draped in cloth, with a drawn-on smile. "Squeak," said the Mimikyu.

Yume counted cousins and got one extra. "You can stay," she decided. The Mimikyu wiggled with joy.

Yume held up the scarf. "Keep it," said Sora. "Keep somebody warm with it next time."

---

"Stay for the finale," said Hana. "The biggest lights come last."

At the stroke of the hour, every light in the park blazed at once. Whoom.

"Kirei," whispered Sora, glowing softest of all, with a small warm bun dozing against her cone.
