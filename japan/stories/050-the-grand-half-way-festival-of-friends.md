# Story 50: The Grand Half-Way Festival of Friends

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi (mochi rice cake / daifuku)
**Guest Characters:** The whole cast — Niko Nigiri, Tama Onigiri, Rin Ramen, Tako & Yaki, Tai Taiyaki, Maru Matcha, Dai Daruma, Mero Melonpan, the Dango Three (Pinku, Shiro, Cha), Kori Kakigori, Pock Ramune, Dora the Dorayaki, Neko-Pan, Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju, Coro the Korokke, Pepper-kun, Gacha the Gachapon, Sora the Soft-Cream, Kei the kei-truck, the Sushi-ya Express
**Setting:** A giant summer matsuri where the whole cast reunites halfway through their journey across Japan
**Theme:** Friendship is the best kind of homecoming
**Japanese Spotlight:** **tomodachi** (toh-moh-dah-chee) — friend
**Cameo:** Mario and Bowser in a Mario Kart race (Mario Kart) — a red kart and a giant kart tear up the stall lane with Momo stuck to a bumper, a winged blue shell finds Bowser and tips him into the cotton candy; walk-on: Wobbuffet (Pokémon)

---

Momo Mochi could not stop bouncing. Off the grass, off a lantern pole, off a drum. BOING.

Below the hill, a thousand lanterns glowed over the biggest **matsuri** (mah-tsoo-ree) — festival — ever.

"Fifty stories!" sang Momo. "Halfway across Japan! And tonight, EVERYONE is coming!"

And there came the whole gang, a river of treat-friends pouring onto the field.

Momo wanted one thing: to ring the Half-Way Bell and watch every lantern float up.

"Squish together," she announced, arms stretching wide enough to hug a field, "stay together!"

---

In the middle stood the **yagura** (yah-goo-rah) — festival tower — tall and wooden.

Kori Kakigori leaned against it, melting one dramatic degree at a time. "Sweetie, the view is COOL."

Momo bounced up the steps. On top, the hook hung empty. Just a sad swinging rope.

"The Half-Way Bell is GONE," said Momo. Her bounce went flat as a pancake.

"No bell, no fireworks, no lanterns," gasped Kori. "Saddest festival in HISTORY. Magnificent, but TRAGIC."

"We don't panic at a party," said Momo. "**Tomodachi** (toh-moh-dah-chee) — friends — find things together. Search!"

---

First, the goldfish pool. Momo leaned over, bounced in, SPLOOSH, and came up wearing three goldfish. No bell.

Next, the snow-cone stand. "I have searched my SOUL," declared Kori, "and also the snow-cone stand."

"You searched the snow-cone stand," said Momo, "because it has your picture on it."

---

Then the candy-apple cart, where Momo got stuck. Mochi, it turns out, is very sticky.

She pulled one arm free. The cart tipped. Twenty candy apples rolled across the grass.

Momo bounced after them, catching apples on arms, head and cheeks, till she looked like a candy-apple tree.

By the tower, Mero Melonpan dozed standing up, crumbs sparkling.

"Mmf," he mumbled, paw waving at the stalls. "The bell went to find its own party. Ding."

---

Past the sizzling stalls they went, following a faint, shy ding. Ding. Ding.

VROOOM. A red go-kart tore up the lane, a red-capped moustached racer aboard. Momo's cheek caught its bumper.

"Wahoo!" whooped Mario, and off went Momo behind him, a mochi streamer flapping.

A kart the size of a stall roared behind, driven by a huge spiky-shelled turtle-dragon. "GWA-HA-HA!" laughed Bowser.

A winged blue shell whistled overhead, hunting. By the stalls, a tall blue Wobbuffet saluted it. "Wobba!"

BOOF. The shell found Bowser. Confetti everywhere. His giant kart tipped nose-first into the cotton-candy stall.

"Every time," growled Bowser, delighted, from the cotton candy. Up the lane, Mario posed on his seat.

---

Momo unpeeled from the bumper, dizzy. Then, from the grass: ding. A glossy capsule rocked guiltily.

Gacha the Gachapon popped open with a sheepish click. Inside, among his mochi prizes, sat the bell.

"It was SHINING," said Gacha, words tumbling. "One turn. It fell IN. It wouldn't come OUT. I hid."

Momo squished Gacha softly. "You kept it safe," she said, "right where the best surprises live."

---

Back at the tower, the last light slipped behind the hills. The hook waited, high.

"Climb ME, sweetie," said Kori, straightening to his full frosty height. "I'm basically a mountain."

Momo climbed. Kori was slippery with syrup. Momo slid down, floomp, onto dozing Mero.

Try two: Gacha bounced up, popped open mid-air, and rained mochi prizes on everyone.

Try three: Momo stretched BOTH taffy arms from Kori's peak, longer than ever.

Gacha opened one last time. Momo plucked the bell and hooked it. Click.

She gave the rope one great joyful PULL. DONNNNG.

---

Fireworks bloomed gold and pink. A thousand lanterns lifted off the field into the night.

Below, the whole gang squished together, a field of glowing treat-friends, looking up.

"Home isn't the end of the road," said Momo. "Home is who you're standing with."

"Surprise inside," whispered Gacha, happy at last. "The surprise was us."

And under the lanterns, under the fireworks, they squished together. And stayed together.
