# Story 44: Mero Melonpan and the Sleepy Bakery Dream

**Main Character:** Mero Melonpan
**Guest Characters:** Niko Nigiri, Momo Mochi, Maru Matcha
**Setting:** A warm-smelling neighborhood bakery at closing time
**Theme:** Rest helps your imagination rise like dough
**Japanese Spotlight:** **nemui** (neh-moo-ee) — sleepy
**Cameo:** Ainz Ooal Gown (Overlord) — inside the dream, a very polite skeleton king in a vast purple robe floats down to announce he now rules the bakery kingdom, discovers nobody is the least bit scared, sinks politely into the dough and asks if he may also have a nap; walk-on: Kon the plush (Bleach)

---

At closing time, the bakery on Cinnamon Corner did what it did best: it smelled delicious.

On the warm window shelf, Mero Melonpan yawned. "Crackle on the outside," he mumbled, "dreams on the inside."

"Mero!" squeaked Niko Nigiri, standing very straight on the counter. "No dozing! We count the muffins!"

Too late. A snore puffed out, poof, and a sparkly crumb floated up like a sleepy firefly.

"Look at his FACE," cooed Momo Mochi, bouncing on her pillowy tummy. "Let him dream a teeny bit?"

"No time for teeny dreams," said Niko, tapping his nori belt. Then he saw the shimmer.

---

Golden crumbs lifted off Mero's crust, twirling into a glowing doorway of crackly bread.

"He's dreaming so warmly the dream is leaking out," said Maru Matcha calmly, whisk raised. "Follow him."

---

The ground was springy and warm as fresh bread. Puffy crust-hills rolled away under an amber sky.

"Welcome," yawned Mero, drifting over on two crumbs, "to the Kingdom of **Nemui** (neh-moo-ee) — sleepy."

Momo hugged the nearest hill. It hugged her back. "SQUISH! Oh, it squishes!"

A bread-cloud bonked Niko softly. He giggled, then straightened his cape, alarmed. "So much to organize here."

---

At the center the Great Oven glowed dim, and the hills around it drooped flat as forgotten dough.

"Leave it to me," said Niko. "I read a pamphlet once about ovens."

He hopped onto a flat hill and bounced to fluff it. It went flatter. He sank cape-deep.

---

"Squish together, stay together!" cried Momo, stretching her arms like taffy to pull him out.

The dough held on. Momo stretched, and stretched, and SPROINGED, and landed stuck beside him.

"Faster!" puffed Niko, wriggling. "Rest faster! Stoke faster!" The hill sagged with every wriggle.

Maru hooked a bread-cloud with her whisk and towed it toward the Oven. It yawned and deflated.

The whole kingdom sank, slow as syrup, and the faster they rushed, the flatter it got.

---

The amber sky split in a purple swirl, and down floated a tall skeleton in a vast robe.

"Ahem. Tremble, please!" declared Ainz Ooal Gown, raising his seven-serpent staff. "I rule this bakery now!"

Momo stretched over and hugged his ribs. "You're so BONY. Like a hug with extra crunch!"

"Do you have a pamphlet?" asked Niko from the dough. "Rulers should have a pamphlet."

The dough swallowed his robe to the hips. "Everyone seems very calm," said Ainz. "Is that allowed?"

"Then may I nap too?" asked Ainz, and sank into the dough until only his staff showed.

---

Mero curled against the cool Oven. "The bony fellow had it right. Just be cozy. That's the job."

Niko lay back, stiff as a chopstick. "I am resting. Very efficiently. Should I rest faster?"

"That's like baking bread by shouting at it," giggled Mero.

"I have never shouted at bread," said Niko with dignity. "Well. Perhaps once. The bread won."

Momo stopped bouncing. Almost. The counting stopped. Nothing but soft breathing and bread-smell.

"You can't push a dream to rise," said Maru, whisking slowly. "Dreams puff up when they rest."

---

A glow flickered inside the Great Oven. The flat hills puffed and crackled into green-gold crosshatches, tick-tick-tick.

The hill beneath them rose like a loaf, lifting them, laughing, into the amber sky.

"It's working!" whispered Momo. "We're not even DOING anything!"

Below, a serpent staff poked from a rising hill, and a very grand snore drifted up.

---

Back on the shelf, the bakery was dim and snug. Mero blinked awake.

Momo was squished against his side. Maru whisked slow circles. Niko lay flat, smiling at nothing.

On the bench below, a scruffy stuffed lion snored like a tiny lawnmower. "Five more minutes," mumbled Kon.

"The muffins," said Niko softly, "can wait until morning. Even I feel a little nemui."

"Crackle on the outside," murmured Mero, chin sinking into his crust, "dreams on the inside. Always."

---
