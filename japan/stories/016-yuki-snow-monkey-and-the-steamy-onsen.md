# Story 16: Yuki Snow-Monkey and the Steamy Onsen

**Main Character:** Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju
**Guest Characters:** None
**Setting:** A snowy mountain hot spring where real snow monkeys bathe
**Theme:** Rest is not lazy — it makes your heart stronger
**Japanese Spotlight:** **onsen** (ohn-sen) — hot spring

---

It is a known and slightly embarrassing fact that Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju could fall asleep absolutely anywhere. In a teacup. On a windowsill. Once, memorably, standing straight up in the middle of a sentence about cabbage.

So on the snowiest morning of the whole winter, when Yuki — a fluffy white steamed bun shaped like a baby snow monkey, with two rosy onsen-warmed cheeks and the sleepiest smile on the mountain — announced that today they would all hike to the famous **onsen** (ohn-sen) — hot spring — her friends fully expected her to doze off before they reached the door.

"A rest day!" cheered Maru Matcha, twirling her tiny bamboo whisk. "Whisk slow. Feel calm."

"A REST day?" gasped Pock Ramune, fizzing in nervous loops. "But — pop! — resting is what you do when you've run out of fun! There's no fun LEFT in resting!"

Yuki wiggled her warm little ears. "Oh, Pock," she yawned cozily. "Just you wait."

---

The path up the mountain was tall with snow, the kind that squeaks under your feet and sparkles like spilled sugar. Yuki led the way, leaving small round footprints that looked like buttons. Behind her bounced Pock Ramune, Maru Matcha, Mero Melonpan (who was already half asleep on his feet), and Kuma the Kuri, snug in his autumn-leaf scarf.

"Are we there yet?" asked Pock. "Pop! Are we there yet? Are we — pop! — there yet?"

"You've asked eleven times," said Maru calmly. "A river that rushes never sees the moon in its own water."

Pock blinked. "...Is that a no?"

"That's a no," said Maru.

The trouble with Pock Ramune was that he had never, not once in his whole fizzy life, sat still. He fizzed when he was happy. He fizzed when he was nervous. He fizzed in his sleep, which made him very difficult to share a tent with. The very idea of *resting* made the marble in his neck rattle with worry — *what if I miss something? What if the fun happens without me?*

So as they climbed, Pock fizzed faster and faster, until his bubbles streamed out behind him like a little blue comet's tail.

"Pock," said Yuki gently, "you're going to fizz yourself flat before we even arrive."

"I'm FINE!" Pock fizzed. "I'm fine I'm fine I'm — pop! — fine!"

And that was the exact moment a very small, very round snow monkey poked its head out from behind a snowdrift and went *"hoo?"*

---

Now, here is the thing about real snow monkeys. They are the coziest creatures on the entire mountain. They have round pink faces and thick silver coats and they spend their whole winter doing the wisest thing anyone can do in the cold: they soak in the onsen, blinking slowly, letting the steam curl their fur, thinking about absolutely nothing at all.

This little one — the friends would come to call him Pon — was a baby. He had snow on his nose. He looked at the dessert friends, then waddled right up to Yuki and patted her fluffy cheek as if to say, *you are also a snow monkey, but a bun.*

"He thinks you're family!" laughed Kuma softly.

"We sort of are," said Yuki, beaming. "Warm cheeks, warm heart, warm friends."

Pon took Yuki's stretchy little hand and tugged. And there, just over the last rise, breathing great clouds of silver steam into the cold blue air, was the onsen.

Everyone gasped — even Mero, who woke up specifically to gasp and then nearly fell asleep again mid-gasp.

It was a wide stone pool, steaming hot, ringed with snow-frosted rocks. All around the edges sat snow monkeys, dozens of them, up to their chins in the warm water, eyes half-closed, looking like the most relaxed creatures ever invented. One had a tiny pile of snow balanced on its head and did not seem to mind at all.

"Oh," whispered Mero Melonpan. "Crackle on the outside, dreams on the inside. This is the dreamiest place I have ever almost-napped in."

---

They peeled off their scarves and slipped into the warm water — all except Pock, who hovered anxiously at the edge.

"Pop! What do we DO?" he fizzed. "What's the GAME? What are the RULES? Is it a race? Is somebody keeping score?"

"There's no game," said Yuki, sinking down to her rosy cheeks with a blissful little *mmmmf*. "You just... soak."

"You just *soak*," Pock repeated, horrified. "For how LONG?"

"As long as your heart needs," said Maru, her matcha-cream swirl wobbling happily in the steam. "Even a kettle must rest, or it boils itself dry."

Pock dipped one toe in. The warm water touched him and — *pop!* — a single bubble drifted up and burst. He flinched. "See! I'm already fizzing! I can't stop! Resting just makes me worry about all the resting I'm not doing properly!"

The baby snow monkey, Pon, paddled over and looked at Pock very seriously. Then he did the only thing snow monkeys really know how to do. He blinked. Slow. Cozy. *Hoooo.*

And he kept blinking at Pock, slow and steady, as if to say: *like this. Try it like this.*

---

"Tell you what," said Yuki, stretching her warm taffy arms across the water. "We're going to teach you the cozy art of a good warm rest. It's the most important thing we snow-monkey-buns know. Ready? Step one: get in."

Pock got in. The water was so warm and so kind that he let out a tiny, surprised "...oh."

"Step two," said Maru, "breathe out the bubbles you've been holding."

Pock breathed out. A whole long ribbon of nervous fizz left him in a stream of bubbles that floated up and popped like a hundred tiny good-mornings. The snow monkeys watched them rise and made delighted *hoo hoo* sounds, batting at the bubbles with their little hands.

"Step three," said Kuma, glowing roasty-warm in the steam, "look up."

Pock looked up. Far above, the sky was the soft pale blue of a winter afternoon, and the steam rose and rose and turned the falling snowflakes gold where the sun caught them.

And Pock Ramune — who had never once in his life sat still — went quiet.

His marble stopped rattling. His bubbles slowed to a lazy little *fizz... fizz... fizz.* The warmth soaked all the way into his frosty glass, into the place where all his worrying lived, and melted it like sugar in tea.

"Oh," he said, very small. "*Oh.* This is... I didn't know resting felt like... this."

"Mm-hm," yawned Mero, who had now achieved full nap and was floating peacefully like a fluffy buoy. "Best feeling there is."

---

They soaked as the afternoon turned slowly gold. Yuki told sleepy stories. Maru shared little proverbs that made everyone smile. Pon the baby monkey climbed onto Yuki's head and balanced a snowball there, and Yuki let him, because that is what family does.

And the strangest, loveliest thing happened to Pock.

The more he rested, the *more* he felt — not less. He noticed the way the steam smelled faintly of stone and snow. He noticed Kuma's quiet brave smile. He noticed that resting wasn't the *end* of the fun, like he'd always feared. It was the thing that *filled him back up* so there could be more fun later.

"I always thought," Pock said slowly, watching a snowflake melt on the warm water, "that if I stopped fizzing, I'd stop being *me.* That resting was just... lazy. Doing nothing."

Yuki shook her head, scattering Pon's snowball with a soft *pomf.* "Resting isn't lazy, Pock. It's how your heart gets strong again. A spring soaks all winter so it can leap all spring." She winked one warm eye. "Even bubbles need a quiet bottle to live in between the pops."

Pock thought about this. Then he gave one happy, gentle, contented *pop!* — not a nervous one this time. A full one. A rested one.

"You know what?" he fizzed softly. "I think that was the best pop I've ever popped."

---

By the time the first stars came out — sharp and silver over the steaming pool — the friends were warm all the way through to their fillings. The snow monkeys were dozing in cozy heaps. Pon had fallen asleep curled on Yuki's tummy like a little gray cloud, and she rocked him gently in the water, humming.

"Same time next snow?" murmured Mero.

"Same time next snow," everyone agreed.

And Pock Ramune — fizzy, zippy, can't-sit-still Pock Ramune — was the very last one who wanted to leave.

"Five more minutes," he sighed happily, sinking back into the steam. "Pop. Make it ten."

Yuki smiled her sleepiest smile and pulled her friends close. Outside the warm water the whole mountain glittered, cold and clean and beautiful. But here, in the onsen, in the rising silver steam, everyone's heart was warm and full and quiet and brave.

"Squish together," murmured Yuki, even though that was Momo's line, because tonight it just felt exactly right.

And they did.

## Goodnight Blessing

May your blankets be as warm as a mountain onsen, little one, and your worries float up and pop away like gentle bubbles in the steam. May you remember that resting is not lazy — it is how your heart grows strong and brave again, soft and slow as snow. Snuggle down now, warm cheeks and warm heart, and let the quiet hold you the way the warm water holds the sleepy snow monkeys. You did enough today. You are enough tonight. Close your eyes, breathe out the bubbles, and drift. Goodnight.
