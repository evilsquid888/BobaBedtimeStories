# Story 8: Momo Mochi and the Kotatsu Club

**Main Character:** Momo Mochi
**Guest Characters:** None
**Setting:** A snug Tokyo apartment with a warm kotatsu table on a snowy winter night
**Theme:** The coziest adventures happen close to home
**Japanese Spotlight:** **Kotatsu** (koh-tah-tsoo) — heated blanket-table

---

The snow came down so softly over Tokyo that it looked like someone was gently shaking powdered sugar over the whole city. And inside a snug little apartment on the fourth floor, Momo Mochi pressed her squishy snow-white face to the frosty window and gasped the biggest gasp a rice cake has ever gasped.

"Oh! Oh! It's freezing-cold-perfect-marvelous!" she squealed, her rosy peach cheeks glowing. Her arms stretched out like warm taffy and wrapped around her own pillowy middle for a self-hug, because Momo Mochi believed firmly that no good idea should be hugged alone.

And she had just had a VERY good idea.

In the middle of her tiny living room sat a low wooden table draped in a thick quilted blanket, with a soft glow humming underneath. A **kotatsu** (koh-tah-tsoo) — a heated blanket-table. The warmest, snuggliest, most magical piece of furniture ever invented.

"Tonight," Momo whispered to the snow, "I am starting a club."

---

She fired off little folded paper invitations, one to every friend she had, which was every friend in the whole world. *Come to the Kotatsu Club! Bring your softest self. Squish together, stay together!*

Within the hour, the buzzer went *bzzzt* so many times it nearly fainted.

In tumbled Rin Ramen, steaming and slurpy, golden broth swirling with excitement. In bounced Tako and Yaki, the giggly takoyaki twins, who immediately argued about who got to sit down first and then sat down at exactly the same time anyway. In drifted Mero Melonpan, already half-asleep, his crackly golden crust sparkling with crumbs. Maru Matcha glided in last, calm as a still pond, twirling her tiny bamboo whisk.

"Whisk slow," Maru murmured, surveying the cozy chaos. "Feel calm."

"FEEL TOASTY!" boomed Rin, who was already burrowing under the blanket. "Everything's better warm and bubbly!"

Soon the kotatsu was packed wall to wall with friends, blanket pulled up to their chins, ten pairs of legs toasting in the golden warmth below. Momo Mochi sat in the very middle, beaming so wide her hidden sweet filling nearly peeked out through her dimple.

"Welcome," she announced grandly, "to the very first meeting of the Kotatsu Club! The rules are simple. Rule one: be cozy. Rule two: there is no rule two. Rule three—"

"You said there was no rule two!" laughed Tako.

"—rule three," Momo continued, unbothered, "we share snacks."

A great cheer went up, and the snacks went round. Toasty mochi (which Momo politely did not find weird at all), warm roasted chestnuts, little teacups of matcha that Maru whisked into a soft green froth. Outside the snow tumbled and twirled. Inside, everyone glowed.

It was, Momo thought, the coziest moment of her entire pillowy life.

Which is, of course, exactly the moment the warmth went out.

---

The golden hum beneath the blanket gave a tiny *hmmm*... and then a sad little *...mmf*... and then nothing at all.

The kotatsu went cold.

"Oh no," whispered Mero, suddenly very awake. "The dream went out."

For one terrible second, everybody held their breath. Outside, the wind made a low *woooo* against the glass, and the apartment felt a whole lot bigger and a whole lot chillier than it had a moment ago.

"It's okay! It's okay!" Momo said brightly, even though her tummy was doing a worried little wobble. "I'll just— I'll fix it! I'm sure there's a— a button, or a switch, or a—"

She crawled under the blanket to investigate. She found the little heater box. She poked it. She patted it. She gave it a gentle motivational squish. She even said "please" very politely. Nothing. It had simply, sleepily, decided to take the night off.

"It's broken," Tako reported solemnly.

"VERY broken," agreed Yaki.

Momo crawled back out with a tiny crumb of starch on her nose and a great big lump in her throat. She had promised everyone the coziest night ever. She had made invitations. She had made *rules*. And now her wonderful Kotatsu Club had a kotatsu that didn't kotatsu at all.

"I'm sorry, everyone," she said softly, her peach cheeks drooping. "I wanted tonight to be so warm. But maybe... maybe you should all go home where it's heated, before you get cold."

The room went quiet. The snow whispered against the window.

And then Rin Ramen, who felt all his feelings at full volume, sniffled a great big steamy sniffle and said: "Go HOME? On the cozy-club's very first night? Absolutely not. I refuse on principle."

"But the heat—" Momo started.

"Forget the heat box," said Maru Matcha, and she said it so calmly that everyone leaned in to listen. She set down her whisk. "Momo. May I tell you a small cozy thought?"

Momo nodded.

"The warmest thing in this room," Maru said, "was never the box."

---

Maru shuffled closer to Momo and pressed her jade-green shoulder against Momo's pillowy side. Then Mero Melonpan, sleepy and sweet, flopped against Maru's other side with a soft *poff*. Then Rin scooched in, broth and all, and the takoyaki twins rolled together into the pile like two warm golden marbles.

"What are you all doing?" Momo asked.

"We're being the heater," said Tako.

"We're SNUGGLING," said Yaki.

And the funny thing — the marvelous, magical thing — was that it worked. As everyone pressed close under the big quilted blanket, all their little warmths added up. Rin's broth-steam. Mero's fresh-baked toastiness. The chestnut-roasted glow. Even Maru's cup of matcha, cradled in the middle, breathed up little curls of warm green steam.

The blanket trapped it all in a soft, snug cloud. And slowly, gently, the kotatsu got warm again — not from the box at all, but from everybody simply being together.

"It's... it's working!" Momo gasped. "We're heating it ourselves!"

"Of course we are," said Maru with a tiny half-moon smile. "A kotatsu is just a blanket and a promise to sit close. The rest is friends."

Momo's eyes went shiny and wobbly, but this time it was the happy kind of wobbly. Her stretchy taffy-arms shot out and gathered every single friend into one enormous, soft, squishy group hug, blanket and all.

"SQUISH TOGETHER," she cried, "STAY TOGETHER!"

"Can't— breathe—" wheezed Tako, delighted.

"Best— club— ever—" squeaked Yaki, even more delighted.

---

After that, the Kotatsu Club was the merriest it had ever been. With no machine to do the warming, they all had to do it themselves, which meant staying close, which meant nobody could wander off, which meant the night turned into one long pile of giggling, cozy, snuggly togetherness.

They told stories under the blanket where it was dim and golden. Mero shared a dream he'd been having about a marshmallow that ran for mayor. Rin told a dramatic tale about the bravest noodle he'd ever known, and got so emotional he steamed up the entire window. Tako and Yaki invented a game called *Guess Whose Foot That Is*, which caused exactly the amount of trouble you'd expect.

And Maru Matcha taught them all her favorite thing, which was to go very still and just *feel* — feel the snow falling far away, feel the blanket like a hug, feel all your friends breathing slow and warm beside you.

"Whisk slow," she murmured. "Feel calm."

Momo Mochi lay in the very center of the pile, blanket up to her rosy cheeks, and felt her whole pillowy heart go soft as fresh mochi. She had thought she needed a fancy glowing machine to make the perfect cozy night. She had thought the magic came from the heat.

But the magic, she realized, had been sitting right beside her the whole time. The magic was simply everyone she loved, close enough to squish.

"You know," she whispered sleepily, "I think this is even better than when it was working."

"Mmhmm," agreed Mero, who was already three-quarters asleep. "Crackle on the outside, dreams on the inside."

Outside, the snow kept tumbling over the great glittering city of Tokyo, dusting the rooftops in soft sugar. But on the fourth floor of one snug little apartment, a whole heap of dessert friends lay tangled together under one warm blanket, snug as a basket of buns fresh from the oven.

The broken heater never did get fixed that night. Nobody minded. Nobody even remembered it was broken.

Because the coziest adventures, Momo Mochi learned, don't need anywhere fancy to happen. Sometimes they happen on a cold night, in a small warm room, under one shared blanket, right at home — with everyone you love squished close.

"Same time next snow?" Momo whispered.

"Same time next snow," everyone whispered back.

And the Kotatsu Club drifted, one cozy friend at a time, gently off to sleep.

## Goodnight Blessing
May your blanket be soft and your toes be toasty, little one, and may you always have someone snuggled close enough to share the warmth. When the wind whispers cold against your window, remember that the coziest magic in the world isn't a machine or a faraway place — it's the people you love, gathered near. Snuggle down, breathe slow like Maru, and let the snow tuck the whole world in. You are warm, you are safe, you are loved exactly where you are. Goodnight.
