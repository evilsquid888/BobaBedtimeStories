# Story 93: Coro Korokke and the Neighborhood Clean-Up Day

**Main Character:** Coro the Korokke
**Guest Characters:** Kori Kakigori, Pock Ramune, Kei the kei-truck; a lane full of treat-folk neighbors
**Setting:** A neighborhood street on community clean-up morning
**Theme:** A clean, kind town is everyone's job
**Japanese Spotlight:** **kirei ni suru** (kee-RAY nee soo-roo) — to make clean / tidy
**Cameo:** Levi (Attack on Titan) — drops off a rooftop with a mop and a cravat over his nose, declares the lane filthy, polishes one cobblestone to a mirror shine and then the whole lane, so the runaway leaf boulder skates downhill twice as fast; walk-on: Waddle Dee (Kirby)

---

Coro the Korokke liked to doze until breakfast smelled like something. Not today.

He popped awake with a pfft of nervous steam.

He pressed his potato face to the window. Oh dear. Oh crumbs.

A paper cup sat on the lucky-cat statue's raised paw like a teeny hat.

The wind had tipped the leaf baskets and stuffed three napkins into a hedge.

"Somebody should **kirei ni suru** (kee-RAY nee soo-roo) — make it clean," whispered Coro. "Somebody big."

He looked at his broom. He looked at his tiny arms. He went out anyway.

---

Swish. Swish. Brooms are gossips. A window slid open above him.

"WHO is being BUSY at this UNCIVILIZED hour?" boomed Kori Kakigori, sunglasses already on.

"Just me," squeaked Coro. "Sorry. The lucky cat is wearing a hat."

"A fashion emergency AND a tidiness emergency," gasped Kori, milk swirl wobbling. "I'm IN."

He whooshed down the drainpipe and landed in a slushy splat. "I work fast, or I puddle."

"Is it a party?" popped Pock Ramune, zipping past in bubbles. "I LOVE parties!"

"It's a clean-up," said Coro.

"Even better!" Pock zoomed up a lamppost and popped a napkin loose with one bubble.

---

"What's MY job?" beeped Kei the kei-truck, putt-putting around the corner, flatbed hopeful.

"Hauler!" said Coro, steam settling to a simmer. "Pock, high places. Kori, the sidewalk."

Kori swept so fast the sidewalk frosted. Leaves rolled up before his broom.

The pile became a lump. The lump became a ball. The ball became a BOULDER.

"Darling," said Kori, admiring it, "I am the fastest cleaner in the WORLD." The boulder wobbled.

---

The street, you see, was ever so slightly a hill. Off it rumbled, gobbling every leaf pile.

"MY BOULDER!" wailed Kori, and whooshed after it, melting a glittery trail.

Pock zipped ahead and fired bubbles at it. Pop! Pop! They bounced right off.

Kori planted himself in front, arms wide. The ball rolled him flat as a strawberry pancake.

"I'm fine," said Kori from the pavement. "Still cool. Flatter. But cool."

Kei raced alongside, beeping. The ball swerved off her bumper and bounded toward the lucky cat.

---

A short soldier dropped from a rooftop, white cravat over his nose, mop raised like a sword.

"Filthy," said Levi, and mopped one cobblestone until it shone like a mirror.

The boulder hit the shiny stone, skidded, and shot downhill twice as fast.

He mopped the whole lane shiny in six swipes. The boulder skated like a hockey puck.

"Dust," said Levi, flicking a leaf off his sleeve with deepest disgust.

He stood on the lamppost top, cravat up, mop at attention, glaring at the leaves.

Coro stood frozen in the shining road, tiny broom up. His steam whistled like a kettle.

---

Then Coro did a thing he had never done. "EVERYBODY!" shouted Coro. "Brooms OUT!"

Windows flew open down the lane. A pomelo auntie. A tofu uncle. Tiny dumplings.

Treat-folk poured out with brooms, mops and one very long spoon.

They lined up across the street, bristles forward, like a very bristly fence.

The boulder hit the brooms, squashed, bounced, and rolled back UPHILL.

Straight up Kei's ramp, into her flatbed. FLUMP.

"Oh," said Coro softly. "It was never a job for somebody big. It's everybody's job."

---

The sun came up over a street that sparkled. The hedge looked freshly combed.

Pock popped the paper cup off the lucky cat's paw. The cat looked far more dignified.

"Still the fastest, sweeties," said Kori, peeling off the pavement and reshaping his peak.

A small orange Waddle Dee toddled past carrying a bin twice its size, wobbling proudly.

Everyone flopped down in the clean street and shared rice balls and tea. The lucky cat waved.

"Crispy outside," said Coro from the middle, steam puffing happily, "soft heart inside."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
