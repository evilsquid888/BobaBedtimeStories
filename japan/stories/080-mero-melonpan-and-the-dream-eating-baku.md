# Story 80: Mero Melonpan and the Dream-Eating Baku

**Main Character:** Mero Melonpan
**Guest Characters:** Fuku, a little round dumpling; Buku, a teapot-sized dream-eating baku
**Setting:** A cozy bedroom at night visited by a gentle dream-eating baku
**Theme:** A good friend can chase away a bad dream
**Japanese Spotlight:** **yume** (yoo-meh) — dream
**Cameo:** Jigglypuff (Pokémon) — bounces onto the dream bridge, sings Mero to sleep in the middle of the lantern chase, puffs up when Fuku keeps running, and doodles a moustache on him; walk-on: Drowzee (Pokémon)

---

If you ever want to find Mero Melonpan, follow the snore. Hooo... pah.

Tonight he dozed on a moonlit windowsill, dreaming of a giant marshmallow. He bit it. Pillow.

"Crackle on the outside," he murmured, "fluff on the inside." Then he heard a sniffle.

Across the room, a dumpling named Fuku slept with her eyebrows scrunched tight.

Mero floomped off the sill and waddled over, leaving crumbs like a snail made of pastry.

"No," whimpered Fuku, fast asleep. "Don't take the lantern. Not the lantern."

She was stuck in a bad **yume** (yoo-meh) — dream. Mero wanted those eyebrows unscrunched before morning.

---

Mero shuffled to the window. "Baku-baku-baaa-kuuu," he hummed. "Come gobble the grumpy bits, please."

The curtains breathed inward. In floated the **baku** (bah-koo) — dream-eater, teapot-sized, plush and cloud-grey.

"Mero!" whispered Buku the baku, bobbing in on invisible wings. "Is it snack time?"

---

Buku sniffed the pillow. "Worry-flavored," he said. "I only nibble the worried bit, like a bad raisin."

"Oh, I love that," said Mero. "I do that with raisins."

"Everyone does," said Buku solemnly. Then his ears drooped. "But this one's knotted."

"Someone soft has to go inside and sit beside her," said Buku. "Someone... bready?"

Mero swallowed hard and puffed up his crust. "Easy," said Buku, patting the pillow. "You take a nap."

---

Mero snuggled in, closed his eyes, and did what he did best. Whoosh. Down he tumbled.

The dream was grey and rainy: a lonely path, and a wind tugging at everything.

Far ahead, Fuku clutched a paper lantern. The wind yanked it loose and away it whirled.

"Mero!" cried dream-Fuku. "If it goes out I'll be all alone in the dark!"

"After it!" said Mero, and set off at top speed, which for Mero is a brisk waddle.

---

He bounced over a puddle. In dreams, bounces go up and keep going. Mero floated away.

"This is the opposite of a nap," said Mero, upside down in the air.

---

Fuku hauled him down by one foot. They pelted onto a wobbly bridge after the lantern.

BOING. A round pink balloon creature dropped onto the bridge, marker raised like a microphone.

"Jiiiig-gly-puuuff," sang Jigglypuff, swaying. "Jiiig-gly-puuuff." The lullaby dripped over the bridge like honey.

Mero, napping champion of Japan, dropped flat on the bridge in two seconds. Hooo... pah.

Fuku pelted on after the lantern, too busy to listen. Jigglypuff puffed bigger, BIGGER, a pink sulk.

It uncapped the marker and drew a curly moustache on sleeping Mero's crust. Squeak, squeak.

Then it drifted up and hung in the rain clouds, a sulky pink balloon, cheeks puffed.

---

Fuku ran back and shook him. "Mero! MERO! Why do you have a moustache?"

They scrambled up a hill of squishy pillows. The lantern blinked, and went out. Dark.

So Mero stopped running. He sat, leaned against her, and smiled his widest moustached smile.

His crackly crust began to glow, the warm gold of a kitchen at breakfast.

"Even if the lantern goes out," said Mero, "you wouldn't be alone. I'm right here. And I glow."

---

Fuku squished up close. The storm shrank to a breeze. The knot went loose.

Up in the bedroom, Buku's trunk wiggled. "There it is. Soft as warm mochi."

Snrrf, snrrf. He nibbled out the cold wind and the lonely path. The sweet stayed.

His tummy went round as a balloon. A tiny milk-scented burp. "Excuse me."

Fuku's eyebrows smoothed out at last, and her little paws opened like two flowers.

---

Over the windowsill peeked a yellow-and-brown tapir creature, sniffing. "All eaten," whispered Buku. "Sorry."

"Teamwork," said Buku at the curtain. Poof. Warm milk smell, and the gentlest eater was gone.

Fuku, fast asleep, reached over and squished Mero close, like a stuffed animal.

Hooo... pah. Two friends on one bed, one small moustache, and all the dreams were good.

## Goodnight Blessing
May your pillow be soft and your dreams sweeter still. If a worried thought tiptoes in, a kind friend can nibble it gently away. You are never alone in the dark. Goodnight.
