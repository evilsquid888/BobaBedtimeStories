# Japan — Character Guide

New & notable characters in the Japan bedtime-story collection.

### Momo Mochi — mochi rice cake (daifuku)
- **Catchphrase:** "Squish together, stay together!"
- **Appearance:** A soft, pillowy snow-white rice cake dusted with potato starch, plump and squishable, with rosy peach cheeks (momo means peach) and arms that stretch like warm taffy for hugs. A faint dimple where the sweet filling hides.
- **Voice:** Warm, bouncy, endlessly affectionate; uses gentle exclamations and loves a group squish. The cozy heart of the cast.

### Niko Nigiri — nigiri sushi (rice topped with salmon)
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's do this neatly... or deliciously!"
- **Appearance:** A tidy oval pillow of pearly white rice wearing a glossy coral-orange salmon cape, bound by a neat little nori belt. Stands very straight and proud, with bright attentive eyes.
- **Voice:** Polite, organized, a planner who loves doing things 'the right way' but learns to roll with surprises. Slightly formal, very kind.

### Tama Onigiri — onigiri rice ball
- **Catchphrase:** "I packed a little extra, just in case!"
- **Appearance:** A plump triangle of warm white rice with a single crisp black nori band like a sash, a tiny pickled-plum blush on one cheek. Comfy and round-shouldered, like a little backpack of comfort.
- **Voice:** Reliable, snacky, the friend who always packs extra for everyone. Gentle humor, a bit motherly-cozy in a kid-sized way.

### Rin Ramen — bowl of tonkotsu ramen
- **Catchphrase:** "Everything's better warm and bubbly!"
- **Appearance:** A steaming ceramic bowl on stubby legs, golden broth swirling, with noodle-curl 'hair', a soft-boiled egg cheek, a corn freckle, and a nori name-tag. Always faintly steaming when excited.
- **Voice:** Hearty, big-feelings, slurpy laugh; warm and a touch dramatic about flavor. The 'let's all eat and feel better' friend.

### Tako & Yaki — takoyaki (two octopus-batter balls)
- **Catchphrase:** "Roll us once — we come as two!"
- **Appearance:** A giggly pair of round golden batter-balls connected by a wobbly bonito-flake wave on top, drizzled with sauce zig-zags. Bounce in unison and finish each other's sentences.
- **Voice:** Twin-energy, fast and playful, talk over each other and high-five constantly. Mischief that's always sweet.

### Tai Taiyaki — taiyaki fish-shaped cake
- **Catchphrase:** "Swim toward the sweet stuff!"
- **Appearance:** A cheerful golden fish-shaped waffle-cake with a crispy fin-tail and a warm red-bean heart you can see when he smiles wide. Swims through air with a little waddle.
- **Voice:** Adventurous, splashy optimism, loves the sea and 'swimming' even on dry land. Brave and bubbly.

### Maru Matcha — matcha (whisked green tea) with a matcha-cream swirl head
- **Catchphrase:** "Whisk slow. Feel calm."
- **Appearance:** A small jade-green bowl-headed friend with a frothy matcha-cream swirl on top like soft-serve hair, calm half-moon eyes, and a tiny bamboo whisk she carries like a wand.
- **Voice:** Calm, thoughtful, gently funny in a deadpan way; the steady one who helps everyone breathe. Speaks in cozy little proverbs.

### Dai Daruma — daruma (sweet-bean-paste daruma manju)
- **Catchphrase:** "Fall down seven, bounce up eight!"
- **Appearance:** A round, roly-poly crimson dome-shaped bun with big determined eyebrows and one painted eye (the other waiting for a wish). Wobbles but always rights himself.
- **Voice:** Stubbornly encouraging coach-voice, full of pep and 'never give up' energy; lovably bossy about courage.

### Mero Melonpan — melon pan (crackly sweet bread)
- **Catchphrase:** "Crackle on the outside, dreams on the inside."
- **Appearance:** A puffy domed bun with a crackly golden-green cross-hatched crust, fluffy and sweet, with sleepy happy eyes and crumbs that sparkle. A bit of a dreamer who naps anywhere.
- **Voice:** Soft, sleepy, sweet; speaks like he's half-dreaming and finds wonder in tiny things. Cozy and slow.

### The Dango Three (Pinku, Shiro, Cha) — hanami dango (pink, white, green skewered dumplings)
- **Catchphrase:** "Three on a stick, stuck together for good!"
- **Appearance:** Three little round dumplings on one stick — Pinku (pink, bold), Shiro (white, shy), Cha (green/matcha, clever) — who travel everywhere together and pivot like a tiny carousel.
- **Voice:** A bickering-but-loving trio; Pinku leads, Shiro worries, Cha solves. Together they finish a thought in three pieces.

### Kori Kakigori — kakigori shaved ice (strawberry syrup)
- **Catchphrase:** "Stay cool, sweeties — but not TOO cool!"
- **Appearance:** A towering fluffy mountain of shaved ice drizzled with ruby strawberry syrup and a condensed-milk swirl, sunglasses perched on top, perpetually one degree from melting and loving it.
- **Voice:** Flamboyant, dramatic summer-diva energy; everything is the COOLEST or a total meltdown. Big-hearted under the drama.

### Pock Ramune — Ramune marble soda bottle
- **Catchphrase:** "Pop the marble — let the fun fizz out!"
- **Appearance:** A frosty pale-blue glass bottle with the famous marble in the neck, fizzy bubbles for freckles, and a 'pop!' grin. Zips around leaving a trail of happy bubbles.
- **Voice:** Zippy, giggly, can't sit still; punctuates sentences with a 'pop!' Excitable and friendly.

### Dora the Dorayaki — dorayaki (red-bean pancake sandwich)
- **Catchphrase:** "A good story is the best filling."
- **Appearance:** Two fluffy honey-brown pancakes hugging a sweet red-bean smile, soft and round, with a comfy beret. Loves stories, gadgets, and snacks (a gentle nod to a certain robot cat's favorite treat).
- **Voice:** Cozy storyteller, curious about everything, collects little facts and shares them warmly. Gentle wonder.

### Neko-Pan (Maneki Melon Cream) — maneki-neko-shaped melon cream bun
- **Catchphrase:** "Wave for luck, share the luck!"
- **Appearance:** A cream-filled bun shaped like a beckoning lucky cat, one paw raised, gold-foil collar bell, and a sweet custard heart. Brings little bits of luck wherever it waves.
- **Voice:** Cheerful, lucky-go-happy, waves hello to everyone; believes everyone deserves a lucky day.

### Yuki the Snow Monkey-Manju — snow-monkey-shaped steamed manju bun
- **Catchphrase:** "Warm cheeks, warm heart, warm friends."
- **Appearance:** A fluffy white steamed bun shaped like a baby snow monkey with rosy onsen-warmed cheeks, who adores hot springs and curling up in steam.
- **Voice:** Snuggly, sleepy-warm, loves a soak and a nap; a tiny bit cheeky like a real monkey. Cozy mischief.

### Kuma the Kuri (Chestnut) — roasted chestnut / kuri kinton sweet
- **Catchphrase:** "Small and roasty, ready and toasty."
- **Appearance:** A glossy mahogany chestnut with a fuzzy little cap, autumn-leaf scarf, and a cozy roasted-warm glow. Round and sturdy like a tiny brave acorn-knight.
- **Voice:** Soft-spoken, brave in quiet ways, loves autumn and cozy fires. The shy hero who steps up when needed.

### Coro the Korokke — korokke (potato croquette)
- **Catchphrase:** "Crispy outside, soft heart inside."
- **Appearance:** A crispy golden-breaded oval, fluffy mashed-potato fluff inside, a little steam puff when nervous. Soft, humble, and warm.
- **Voice:** Humble, kind, easily flustered but big-hearted; the everyday hero who just wants everyone fed and happy.

### Pepper-kun (Pepper Lunch sizzle) — Pepper Lunch sizzling beef-and-rice on a hot iron plate
- **Catchphrase:** "Stir it, sizzle it, share it!"
- **Appearance:** A little round black iron hot-plate friend piled with sizzling beef strips, golden corn, butter-melt swirl and a ring of rice, sizzling happily with tiny sparks of steam. Wears a chef's bandana.
- **Voice:** Energetic griddle-master, loves a dramatic SIZZLE, counts down the cook like a showman. Warm and a little theatrical.

### Gacha the Gachapon — gachapon capsule-toy capsule (a treat-capsule full of tiny mochi prizes)
- **Catchphrase:** "Give it a turn — surprise inside!"
- **Appearance:** A glossy two-tone capsule with a surprise-filled grin, rolls like a marble, pops open to reveal tiny mochi treasures. Always full of mystery.
- **Voice:** Suspenseful-giddy, loves surprises and 'you never know what you'll get!', a gentle gambler of joy.

### Sora the Soft-Cream (Hokkaido Soft Serve) — Hokkaido milk soft-serve ice cream
- **Catchphrase:** "Soft swirls, slow smiles."
- **Appearance:** A tall swirl of creamy pale-gold soft-serve in a crisp cone, gentle sleepy eyes, a little drip-curl. Pure dairy comfort, calm as a snowfield.
- **Voice:** Gentle, dreamy, a little melty-soft; the calm Hokkaido friend who loves wide skies and slow days.

### Kei the kei-truck — (vehicle friend) a tiny cheerful Japanese kei-truck the food characters drive
- **Catchphrase:** "Putt-putt, let's GO!"
- **Appearance:** A teeny, round, butter-yellow Japanese mini-truck with big headlight eyes, a stubby flatbed, and tidy little mud-flaps. Hums happily, bounces on its springs, and wears a knitted seat-cover like a sweater.
- **Voice:** Eager, putt-putt cheerful, loyal little engine that could. Beeps when excited.

### The Sushi-ya Express — a sushi food truck (mobile sushi counter)
- **Catchphrase:** "Fresh sushi, anywhere the road goes!"
- **Appearance:** A gleaming white-and-indigo food truck with a noren curtain, a tiny rooftop fish-flag, glowing paper-lantern headlights, and a fold-down counter that beams warm light. Smells of vinegared rice and sea breeze.
- **Voice:** Warm, welcoming, proud host on wheels. Rings a little bell to call friends over.
