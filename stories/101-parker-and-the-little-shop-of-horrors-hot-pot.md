# Story 101: Parker and the Little Shop of Horrors Hot Pot

**Main Character:** Parker the Shiba Inu
**Guest Characters:** Boba, Mochi, Taro, Bīng, Mango, Caǎo, Aiyu, Feng, Hóng Dòu, Rosie, Tori, Cat the penguin, Marcus, Kelly the Soup Dumpling (new), Audrey the Plant (new), Guō Guō the Hot Pot (new)
**Setting:** A brand-new shop at the darkest end of the night market, at midnight, in the rain
**Theme:** Friends who stand in front of you are worth standing up for, and there is always room at the table.
**Mandarin Spotlight:** 火鍋 (huǒguō) = hot pot
**Format:** A rap musical. Read it out loud over a beat: *boom-boom-CLAP, boom-boom-CLAP.*
**Look:** Dark, rich, hand-painted late-80s / early-90s anime, the kind you'd find on a rented videotape.

---

## Intro (spoken, no beat yet)

Midnight. Rain. The neon signs of the **夜市** (yèshì) = night market buzzed pink and green in the puddles, like a cartoon somebody taped off the TV in 1989.

And at the very end of the lane, where the lamps gave up, a new shop had appeared overnight. Not *opened*. *Grown.* Vines for a door. A sign in dripping purple paint:

**LITTLE SHOP OF HORRORS — FREE SAMPLES\***

Fifteen friends stood out front, dripping.

"Free samples," said Boba. "Let's bounce!"

Kelly — a plump little **小籠包** (xiǎolóngbāo) = soup dumpling, Marcus's best friend, eighteen neat pleats on top, round glasses forever fogged by her own steam, reads the back of every cereal box — wiped her lenses and leaned in to the tiny print under the star.

"'*You are the samples,*'" she read. "Huh. Marcus, I think we should —"

The vines shut behind them. And Cat, the little blue penguin in the yellow scarf, who cannot help herself, dropped a beat.

## Track 1: "I Ordered a Hot Dog"

*(boom-boom-CLAP)*

In the back of the shop, in a cracked clay pot,
Sat a plant as big as a parking lot —
Green lips, purple spots, a tongue like a slide,
And a voice like a tuba with a bullfrog inside.

**AUDREY:**
"The name is Audrey. Yeah, I'm a dude.
Been sittin' in the dark and I'm in a MOOD.
I'm sick of the drizzle, I'm bored of the fog —
Tonight, little snacks, I want a HOT DOG."

Then he threw back his enormous head, and the whole shop rattled:

"**FEED ME!**"

Every head turned, slowly, to the Shiba Inu.

Parker stood there in his plain navy vest, brass whistle on its cord, curly tail perfectly still.

"I should mention," said Parker, low and unhurried, "that I am a lukewarm dog at best."

**THE HOOK (everybody, even the plant):**

> ***"FEED ME!"*** *(that's Audrey — shout it with him)*
> **CHOMP-chomp, GULP! Another one down!**
> **It's the Little Shop of Horrors in the night-market town!**
> **Count 'em on your fingers, count 'em on your toes —**
> **One more bite, and down the hatch it goes!**

---

## Track 2: "One by One"

**Boba** bounced first: "Let's bounce! Not today!
You want my friend Parker? Go through me, okay?"
One vine. One flick. One glossy little POP.
"Mmm. Chewy." *(Fourteen left. Don't stop.)*

**Mochi** stretched wide like a soft white wall:
"Stick together, team! He can't eat us all!"
Audrey slurped her up like a noodle off a spoon —
"Sticky. Five stars. I'll be flossin' till June." *(Thirteen.)*

**Taro** pushed up his glasses: "Now, young sprout, be wise.
Good things take time to st—" SURPRISE.
A burp. A purple puff. A very polite sigh.
"Tastes like a library. Dusty. Dry." *(Twelve.)*

**Bīng** struck a pose in his shades: "MORE IS MORE!
Eat ME, I'm the STAR!" — and he slid across the floor.
GULP. Then a shiver. Then Audrey's eyes went wide:
"BRAIN FREEZE! BRAIN FREEZE! ...Worth it," he sighed. *(Eleven.)*

> ***"FEED ME!"*** *(that's Audrey — shout it with him)*
> **CHOMP-chomp, GULP! Another one down!**
> **It's the Little Shop of Horrors in the night-market town!**

**Mango** in his sweatband ran laps around the pot:
"Catch me if you can — I'm in season, you're not!"
He could. He did. One vine like a lasso thrown.
"Juicy," said the plant, and spat the sweatband out, alone. *(Ten.)*

**Caǎo** didn't run. Pulled his beanie down low.
"Chill. I've got this." (He did not, though.)
Went down so calm he didn't even scream.
From deep inside: "Honestly? Roomy. Like a dream." *(Nine.)*

**Aiyu** stepped up, shaking, see-through and small:
"You can see right through me, so I can't lie at all:
I'm scared." But she stood there, the bravest of the bunch,
And the plant, almost gently, had jelly for lunch. *(Eight.)*

**Feng** smoothed his wrapper. "Sir. I was born
To be wrapped beautifully. Do not leave it torn."
Audrey unwrapped him neatly and folded up the foil.
"Classy," he rumbled. "Buttery. Royal." *(Seven.)*

Grandma **Hóng Dòu** shook her ladle: "Shame on you, dear!
Everyone needs a little sweetness. Come here!"
She bonked him on the lip. He swallowed her — *glug.*
From inside, muffled: "This plant needs a HUG." *(Six.)*

> ***"FEED ME!"***
> **CHOMP-chomp, GULP! Another one down...**
> *(Quieter this time. There are only six voices left to sing it, and one of them is a dog who does not sing.)*

## Bridge (spoken — turn the beat down low)

Six left. Tori, Marcus, Kelly, Cat, Rosie, and one dog.

The rain drummed on the roof. The neon outside flickered purple, then red. Thick shadows crawled up the walls, the way they do in the good old cartoons right before something happens.

"Okay," whispered Kelly. "Now it's a little bit scary."

"A little bit," Marcus agreed. "That's a step too."

---

## Track 3: "Down to the Dog"

**Tori** clipped her phone to her sleeve and stood tall,
Put her little sister right behind her by the wall.
"You've got this, little sip." One wink. One grin.
One vine. One gulp. And the big sis went in. *(Five.)*

**Marcus** zipped his hoodie, compass pin bright:
"I've left a hundred parties. Leaving this one tonight."
He made it to the door. The door was a vine.
"...That's a step too," he sighed. "Going down. Fine." *(Four.)*

**Kelly** wiped her glasses, steam curling free:
"I read the fine print, so this one's on me.
Clause nine, you big cabbage: CAUTION — SOUP INSIDE —"
GULP. "HOT! HOT! HOT!" *Ptoo* — and the glasses flew wide. *(Three.)*

Parker caught the glasses in the air and tucked them, very carefully, into his vest pocket.

**Cat** the little penguin spun once on her head,
Yellow scarf flying. "Kung fu style," she said,
And bowed, very politely. Audrey bowed back.
Then GULP. "Ooh. Feathers. A tickly snack." *(Two.)*

**Rosie's** pink straw drooped. Smell the rose — count four.
Blow the candle — count four. Then she walked across the floor.
"One step at a time. One sip at a time.
He's my friend," said Rosie —

And she didn't get to finish the rhyme.

*(One.)*

---

## Track 4: "No More Friends" (slow — no beat at all)

Silence.

No hook. No clap. Just rain, and one small dog in a big purple shadow.

Audrey leaned down until his green lips filled the whole frame. "*Feed me,*" he whispered. "*My hot dog.*"

Parker didn't run. Parker didn't bite.
He set four paws square in the last of the light.
"They all stood up for me. Every single one.
Right beside them is where a general goes. So I'm not done."

But he had no plan. Parker had forgotten his lines in forty plays, and this was the forty-first. Every **朋友** (péngyou) = friend he had was gone. So he did what you do when you have nothing left at all.

He looked up.

He blew one long note on the brass whistle. Then he gave one big bark — the kind he saves for when it counts.

"*Help.*"

And somewhere, in a very old book, a line was already waiting for him:

> *"And call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me."*
> — Psalm 50:15 (KJV)

---

## Track 5: "A Table in the Presence of Mine Enemies"

The roof peeled open like a tin of pineapple cakes.

The storm clouds split. A column of gold-white light came down through the rain, and every drop inside it hung still, shining. A choir started up — huge, far away, humming. Then a synthesizer. (It was that kind of cartoon.)

Audrey squinted. "Who turned on the big light?"

Down the beam, slow and steaming, riding a little ring of blue flame like rocket boots, came a **火鍋** (huǒguō) = hot pot.

A great round copper hot pot, split down the middle like a yin-yang — one side bubbling spicy red, one side gentle and golden. Steam poured off his shoulders like a cape. In one hand, a ladle. In the other, chopsticks as long as spears.

> *"Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over."*
> — Psalm 23:5 (KJV)

*(boom-boom-CLAP — the beat comes back, twice as big)*

**GUŌ GUŌ:**
"I'm Guō Guō the Hot Pot, sent from above!
Half of me is spicy, and half of me is love!
The Spirit heard a bark from a dog in a jam,
So He set a table, Audrey — and here I am.

You gobbled up the sweetness, you gobbled up the tea,
You gobbled up the dumpling with the warranty.
You wanted a hot dog? Here's what we'll do:
The table's getting set... and the vegetable is YOU."

"Parker," said Guō Guō. "Count me in, General."

Parker blew the whistle. "**加油** (jiāyóu) = go for it!"

And here is the important part, so lean in close: Audrey was a *plant*. Chopping up a plant isn't scary. It's called *cooking.*

So Parker blew his whistle on every beat, and on every beat the great chopsticks came down.

**CHOP!** went a vine — out popped Rosie, straw held high!
**CHOP!** — there's Kelly. ("Glasses?" "Vest pocket." "Good guy.")
**CHOP!** — Cat the penguin, moonwalking, scarf a-sweep!
**CHOP!** — Marcus and Tori in a tangle, in a heap!
**CHOP!** — Grandma Hóng Dòu, who'd been tucking them to sleep!
**CHOP-CHOP!** — Feng (re-wrapping), Aiyu (glowing), Caǎo ("Is it noon?"),
Mango, Bīng and Taro, Mochi stuck to a spoon —
And last, with a bounce and a glossy little POP:
**Boba!** "LET'S BOUNCE!" ...Fifteen. Full stop.

## Track 6: "Room in the Pot"

The rain quit. The purple sign fizzled out. Guō Guō settled down in the middle of the lane, right where the scary shop used to be, and a long table unrolled around him with fifteen bowls on it.

In went Audrey: crisp green ribbons, swirling in the broth.

"Oh," said a deep voice from the spicy side. "Oh, that's *nice.* Is this a jacuzzi?"

Because here's what nobody had ever told Audrey, growing all alone in the dark: in this world, being delicious is the happiest thing a vegetable can be.

"I spent my whole life trying to *eat* the party," he bubbled, "when I could've *BEEN* the party. Somebody dip me in sesame sauce. I'm ready."

Kelly put her glasses on, leaned over the pot, and they fogged up solid white. "I can't see a single thing," she said. "Ten out of ten."

"Best party I haven't left," said Marcus.

Cat spun on her head at the end of the table, well away from the hot broth, because she is a careful penguin.

"What's the next step?" asked Rosie.

"Dip, blow, eat," said Tori. "You've got this, little sip."

Parker got the first bowl. That is also what a general is for.

"**好吃** (hǎochī) = delicious," he said, and his curly tail wagged exactly once.

**THE LAST HOOK (softly now, getting sleepy):**

> **Dip-dip, slurp — pass it around!**
> **It's the Little Shop of HOT POT in the night-market town!**
> **Count 'em on your fingers, count 'em on your toes —**
> **Everybody's here, and the good broth flows.**

When the last bowl was empty, Guō Guō rose back up the beam of light, steam trailing behind him like a scarf. "Same table," he called down. "Any time you bark."

And on the windowsill, in a chipped teacup, one tiny green sprout with one tiny purple spot opened one tiny mouth.

"*Feed me?*" it squeaked. "*...Please?*"

Parker slid over a single fish ball.

"Small snacks only," said the general.

"Deal," said the sprout.

## Goodnight Blessing
Little one, some nights feel like the last verse of the song, when the beat has stopped and you are the only one left in the room. You are not. Fifteen friends would stand in front of you, and when even they run out, you can still look up and call, and help is already on its way down through the rain. So count your friends on your fingers, count them on your toes, and let the warm steam carry you off to sleep. There is a place set for you at the table. Goodnight.
