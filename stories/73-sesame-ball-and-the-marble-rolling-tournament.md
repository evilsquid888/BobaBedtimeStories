# Story 73: Sesame Ball & the Great Rolling Tournament

**Main Character:** Sesame Ball (芝麻球)
**Guest Characters:** Passion Fruit
**Setting:** A downhill cobblestone alley turned racetrack in Jiufen
**Theme:** Roll bravely, but never roll over a friend.
**Mandarin Spotlight:** 比賽 (bǐsài) = competition

---

High in the misty mountain town of Jiufen, where the alleys twist like noodles and the red lanterns bob like sleepy fireflies, there lived the bounciest, boldest, roundest little hero you ever did meet — Sesame Ball.

He was a golden sphere the size of a tennis ball, coated tip to toe in tiny toasted sesame seeds that glittered like a thousand freckles. Inside, he was sweet and squishy with red bean paste, but on the outside he was crunch, courage, and one very large grin.

"TODAY," Sesame Ball announced to absolutely no one, balancing on top of a slippery stone step, "is the day I roll into legend!"

Then he slipped, bounced off a lantern, ricocheted off a teapot, and landed face-first in a bucket of mountain dew.

"...After breakfast," he added, wiping his eyes. "Legends need breakfast."

A passing crowd of tourists giggled. Sesame Ball took a bow. He always took a bow.

---

You see, today was the day of the famous Jiufen **比賽** (bǐsài) = competition — the Great Rolling Tournament, the wildest, twistiest race in all of Taiwan. Round snacks from across the island would tumble down the steepest cobblestone alley in town, bouncing past tea houses and souvenir stalls, all the way to the harbor view at the bottom. First one to the finish line won the Golden Lantern trophy and, more importantly, eternal bragging rights.

Sesame Ball had wanted this his whole life. Well — his whole three weeks of life, but for a sesame ball that is basically forever.

At the starting line, the racers gathered, all of them perfectly round and ready to roll. There was Tangyuan, jolly and white, wobbling in place. There was a coconut all the way from the south coast. And there, polishing his wrinkly purple shell with a tiny cloth, was Passion Fruit.

"Sesame Ball, my crunchy little friend!" cried Passion Fruit, his cheeky orange eyes sparkling. "Ready to lose to a fruit? I dare you to even *try* to beat me."

"Passion Fruit, you dare me to do everything," laughed Sesame Ball. "Yesterday you dared me to sneeze."

"And you did! Magnificently! Sesame seeds *everywhere*." Passion Fruit wiped a fake tear. "It was beautiful, darling."

Passion Fruit was a wrinkly purple sphere with bright orange, seedy insides and the heart of a born prankster. He loved a good dare, a good giggle, and — most of all — a good **比賽**. The two had been rivals and best friends since the day they met, which made every race twice as fun and twice as loud.

"Just don't cry when I win," said Sesame Ball, puffing out his sesame chest.

"Just don't crack when you lose," said Passion Fruit, winking.

High above the starting line, perched on a red lantern, stood the race official — a tiny round mochi clerk named Diàn Diàn, glowing softly in his little convenience-store vest.

"Racers, beep-boop, take your places!" he chimed. "Remember the one golden rule of the tournament — roll bravely, but never roll over a friend! Beep! On your marks... get set..."

Every round snack tucked in tight.

"GO!"

---

Down they tumbled!

Sesame Ball burst off the line like a cannonball, bouncing down the slick stone steps with a *bah-doing, bah-doing, bah-DOING!* The crowd roared. Lanterns swayed. A startled cat leapt onto an awning and decided this was a great day to nap somewhere else.

"WHEEEE!" cried Sesame Ball, his sesame seeds catching the lantern-light like sparks. "Out of my way, world, here comes GREATNESS!"

He zoomed past Tangyuan, who was singing a song about family and rolling much too slowly to finish it. ("We are all... one big... rolling family!" Tangyuan warbled, three whole steps behind everyone else and entirely unbothered about it.) He whizzed past the coconut, who was, frankly, too heavy and kept getting stuck in cracks. ("Bro," sighed the coconut, wedged sideways for the fourth time. "Not again. This town has too many *holes*, dude.")

He bounced past a tea house where the customers leaned out their windows waving little flags. He spun around a corner so fast that a basket of mooncakes nearly toppled, and a very dignified pineapple cake spectator clutched his golden chest in alarm. "Reckless youth!" the pineapple cake huffed — but he was smiling, and he waved his tiny flag too.

Beside him, neck and neck, rolled Passion Fruit, laughing his cheeky laugh as the wind whistled through his wrinkly shell.

"Catch me if you can, crunchy!" Passion Fruit called.

"I'm gonna catch you AND your shadow!" Sesame Ball shouted back, and the two of them blurred down the alley in a glorious purple-and-gold race, bouncing off the cobblestones, dodging the legs of giggling tourists, sailing under a noodle cart and over a sleeping dog who didn't even twitch.

The finish line, far below at the harbor overlook, glittered with the Golden Lantern trophy. Sesame Ball could practically taste victory. It tasted, he imagined, like extra red bean paste.

He pulled ahead. Just a little. Just enough.

"Almost... there..." he panted.

And then — disaster.

Passion Fruit, pushing too hard, hit a slick mossy stone at a bad angle. His wrinkly shell skidded sideways. He spun out of control, bounced off the corner of a tea-house step, and went flying — *whump!* — straight into a deep, dark gap between two cobblestones, where he wedged tight and stuck fast.

"Oof! Ow! Help!" Passion Fruit's little voice echoed up from the crack. "I'm stuck! And it's dark down here! And there's a snail looking at me funny!"

Sesame Ball heard it. He was three bounces from the finish line. Three bounces from the Golden Lantern. Three bounces from legend.

He could see the trophy glowing, golden and round and absolutely meant for him.

He could hear the crowd chanting his name — "SE-SA-ME! SE-SA-ME!" — the sweetest sound a little sphere ever heard.

And underneath all of it, small and far away, he could hear his very best friend, stuck and scared in a cold dark crack, all alone. Passion Fruit, who had dared him to sneeze. Passion Fruit, who laughed at his terrible jokes. Passion Fruit, who once stayed up all night just to watch the lanterns with him because Sesame Ball couldn't sleep.

Sesame Ball did not even have to think.

He slammed on his brakes — *skreeeech!* — spun a full circle, and rolled *back up the hill*, which for a sesame ball is roughly as hard as pushing a mountain uphill while wearing socks.

"Hold on, Passion Fruit! I'm coming!"

"What are you DOING?" cried Passion Fruit. "You were about to WIN! Go, you marvelous meatball, GO!"

"I'm not a meatball, I'm a *dessert*," huffed Sesame Ball, straining back up the slope, "and I'm not leaving you in a hole with a rude snail!"

---

He reached the crack. Passion Fruit was wedged in deep, his orange insides peeking out, his cheeky grin gone wobbly.

"I can't reach the edges," Passion Fruit said quietly. "I'm too round. I just... slip."

Sesame Ball thought hard. Then he grinned his enormous grin.

"Round, you say? Well. Lucky for you, I am the roundest, bounciest, most *seedy* sphere in all of Jiufen — and my seeds are crunchy enough to grip!" He wedged himself against the upper edge of the crack. "Grab onto my seeds and climb!"

Passion Fruit reached up. His little hands found the rough, toasty sesame coating — and held. Sesame Ball pushed off with everything he had, anchoring against the stone, his red bean middle squishing with the strain.

"On three!" he grunted. "One... two... THREE — *DUNK ME, I'M READY!*"

"That's Youtiao's line!" laughed Passion Fruit.

"It felt right!"

With one mighty heave, Sesame Ball yanked his friend free. The two of them popped out of the crack and rolled in a tangled, giggling, purple-and-gold heap onto the cobblestones, dizzy and breathless and entirely covered in moss.

"You're free!" cheered Sesame Ball.

"You're filthy!" cheered Passion Fruit, and they both burst out laughing so hard they wobbled.

It was only then that they remembered the race.

They looked down the hill. Far below, Tangyuan was just now crossing the finish line, very slowly, still singing his family song, looking extremely surprised to have won anything at all.

"We lost," said Sesame Ball.

"We super lost," agreed Passion Fruit.

And then — something wonderful happened.

The crowd, who had seen the whole thing — every brave, uphill, moss-covered second of it — did not groan. They did not boo. They *cheered*. They cheered louder than they had cheered for the winner. They cheered so loud the lanterns shook and the misty mountain echoed it back twice. Even the rude snail, way down in the crack, gave a tiny slow clap with its tiny slow self.

"He turned around!" someone shouted. "He gave up the trophy to save his friend!"

"Now THAT'S a champion!" cried another.

Tiny Diàn Diàn floated down on a lantern, beeping with joy. "Beep-boop! The Golden Lantern goes to the fastest roller — but the GOLDEN HEART award," he announced, producing a shiny medal shaped like two snacks holding hands, "goes to the bravest one. Sesame Ball, this is for you!"

The crowd went wild. Sesame Ball blushed so hard his sesame seeds turned pink at the edges.

"I didn't do it for a medal," he mumbled, turning even pinker. "I just... I couldn't leave him down there. That's all."

"That," said Passion Fruit warmly, draping a wrinkly arm around him, "is exactly why you deserve it, darling." He sniffled, just a little, his orange insides shimmering. "And for the record — that is the bravest, silliest, *crunchiest* thing anyone has ever done for me."

"You'd have done it for me," said Sesame Ball.

"Obviously," said Passion Fruit, wiping his eye. "But I'd have complained the entire time."

---

That night, the two friends sat on the harbor steps, sharing a warm cup of mountain tea and watching the lanterns glow gold against the dark blue sea.

"You really gave up winning," said Passion Fruit softly. "For me."

"Of course I did," said Sesame Ball. "Trophies are nice. But you can't share a trophy's terrible jokes. You can't dare a trophy to sneeze." He bumped his friend gently. "Roll bravely. But never roll over a friend. That's the whole point of having a race worth winning."

Passion Fruit was quiet for a moment. Then, very seriously, he said: "I dare you... to be my best friend forever."

"That's the easiest dare you've ever given me," said Sesame Ball.

And under the soft glow of a thousand lanterns, the boldest little sphere in Jiufen and his cheeky purple pal leaned together, warm and round and entirely, perfectly happy — winners of the only **比賽** that ever truly mattered.

## Goodnight Blessing
May you roll through your days bold and bright, brave as a sesame ball on the steepest hill. And when someone you love stumbles into a hard place, may you always be brave enough to turn around. Remember, little one — finishing first is fun, but finishing *together* is forever. The lanterns are dimming now, the mountain is soft and quiet, and your bed is the coziest finish line of all. Snuggle in, close your eyes, and let your dreams roll gently downhill into morning. Goodnight.
