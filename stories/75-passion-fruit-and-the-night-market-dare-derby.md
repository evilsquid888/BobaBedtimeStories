# Story 75: Passion Fruit & the Sour-Dare Derby

**Main Character:** Passion Fruit (百香果)
**Guest Characters:** Chòu Chòu (Stinky Tofu)
**Setting:** A carnival dare-booth corner of a lively night market
**Theme:** The boldest dare is being kind when no one dares you to.
**Mandarin Spotlight:** 挑戰 (tiǎozhàn) = challenge

---

If you ever wander to the far corner of the Lantern Lane night market — past the squid stalls, past the goldfish-scooping pool, past the grandma selling balloons shaped like dumplings — you will find a wobbly little booth painted with crooked stars. And bouncing on top of it, grinning like a freshly cracked grin, sits Passion Fruit.

He is a wrinkly purple ball, all crinkled and crumpled like a tiny old wizard's coin purse, and inside him sloshes a sea of golden orange seeds so tangy they could make a statue sneeze. He had a sign. The sign said, in dribbly paint:

**THE SOUR-DARE DERBY.**
**Are you BOLD enough? 挑戰 (tiǎozhàn) = challenge!**
**One pucker, you lose. No pucker, you WIN!**

"Step right up, darlings!" he hollered, which was a thing he'd borrowed from Lychee and never given back. "Today's the night! Tonight we crown the Pucker-Proof Champion of the whole market! Who dares?"

A small crowd was already squealing.

---

The rules were simple and slightly evil. Passion Fruit would dare you to do something so sour, so tangy, so face-scrunchingly intense that your cheeks would fold up like an accordion — and if you puckered, you were out. If you kept a calm, smooth face, you advanced.

He was very, *very* good at this. Mostly because being sour was his entire personality.

"First up — Mango!" he announced.

Mango bounced forward, sweatband snug, already stretching. "I'm an *athlete*, Passion Fruit. I do not pucker. Puckering is for the weak."

"Bold words." Passion Fruit squirted a single drop of himself onto a tiny spoon. "I dare you to lick the Spoon of Doom."

Mango licked it.

For one whole second, Mango was a hero. Then his whole orange face caved inward like a deflating beach ball, his eyes crossed, and he made a noise like a balloon letting go of a tree. *"GLEEEORP."*

"PUCKER!" the crowd roared with delight.

"I was *stretching* my face on purpose," Mango wheezed, staggering off to find some water. "That was a warm-up pucker. It doesn't count."

"It absolutely counts," said Passion Fruit, ringing a little bell. *Ding!* "Next challenger!"

One by one, they came, and one by one, they puckered. Coconut Jelly tried to play it cool — "I got this, bro, totally chill, this is nothing, broooo—" — and then folded up so hard his sunglasses popped clean off his face. *Ding!* Egg Waffle gave a long carnival-barker speech about how no sourness could shake a true showman, took the dare mid-sentence, and finished the sentence as a single high-pitched squeak. *Ding!* Even Boba gave it a go, bounced once, tasted the Spoon of Doom, and went so wrinkly for a moment that he looked like Passion Fruit's cousin. *Ding! Ding!*

Pineapple Cake stepped up next, very formal, bowing first. "I shall accept your challenge with dignity and grace," he announced. He took a dignified, graceful lick. Then his golden rectangle face folded so neatly down the middle that for a moment he looked like a tiny tangy envelope. *Ding!* "I withdraw with dignity," he mumbled through his folds, "and grace."

Mochi tried to be clever and hugged the spoon instead of licking it. "You can't pucker if you don't taste it!" he declared, very proud — but a single droplet of Passion Fruit sneaked onto his pillowy cheek anyway, and he wobbled, scrunched, and stretched into the longest, sourest, stickiest face anyone had ever seen, like taffy in a windstorm. *Ding!* It took three snacks to un-stretch him.

"More challengers? MORE!" cried Passion Fruit, gleeful, ringing his bell so fast it nearly fell off the booth. The crowd was laughing so hard that nobody noticed the small shadow gathering at the edge of the lanterns.

"Nobody," Passion Fruit announced, twirling on top of his booth, "is brave enough to out-sour ME. That, my darlings, is because I *am* the dare. I *am* the derby. I am the most daring fruit in all of—"

"Um."

The crowd went quiet.

There, at the very edge of the lantern light, half-hidden behind a pillar, stood a small golden cube of fried tofu wearing a heart-shaped cabbage cape. Around him drifted little curls of friendly purple sparkle-cloud — his famous **臭 (chòu) = stinky** aroma, which announced him the way trumpets announce a king, whether he liked it or not.

It was Chòu Chòu.

---

"Oh," said Lychee, fanning herself, "it's the *smelly* one."

A few snacks giggled. A few snacks took one tiny polite step backward. Chòu Chòu's big puppy eyes got even bigger, and his clothespin — which he kept clipped to his cape as a joke — drooped just a little.

Passion Fruit, who never met a moment he couldn't make louder, leaned down with a wicked twinkle. "Welllll, well, well. Little Chòu Chòu wants to play the Sour-Dare Derby? Bold! Verrry bold! But sour and stinky aren't the same thing, mister. You sure you can handle a *real* 挑戰?"

"I—" Chòu Chòu's voice was barely a whisper. "I don't want to do a sour dare."

"Then why are you HERE?" Passion Fruit threw up his crinkly arms. "This is the DARE corner! You come here to be DARED!"

Chòu Chòu shuffled his crispy little feet. "I came because... it's the only corner where nobody stands too close. So nobody has to smell me. I thought maybe I could just... watch the games from far away." He gave a small, brave shrug. "Bold on the outside, gold on the inside, I guess. Mostly I just stand far away."

And here the whole market got very, very quiet. Even the goldfish in the scooping pool stopped blowing bubbles. Even the dumpling-balloons seemed to hold their breath.

Because, you see, deep down — past his loud sign and his evil little spoon and his borrowed *darlings* — Passion Fruit had a heart. A wrinkly, tangy, surprisingly soft heart, the kind that sloshed around inside him whenever he wasn't being loud enough to ignore it. And right now that heart did a strange thing it had never done during a dare derby before.

It *ached*.

He thought about all the snacks he'd dared that night. Every single one of them had a crowd cheering them on. Every single one had friends close enough to laugh with. And here was Chòu Chòu, who had walked all the way to the edge of the market just so that nobody would have to stand near him. That wasn't a dare. That was the opposite of a dare. That was a snack so used to being a punchline that he'd quietly agreed to disappear.

Passion Fruit did not like that. He did not like that one tiny tangy bit.

---

Passion Fruit stopped twirling.

"Wait," he said. "You stand *far away*... on purpose? So nobody smells you?"

Chòu Chòu nodded. "It's easier. People scrunch up their faces when I get close. Just like..." He looked at the Spoon of Doom. "Just like your derby. Except they're scrunching at *me*."

Oof. That landed right in Passion Fruit's gooey middle.

Now, Passion Fruit had dared snacks to do all sorts of ridiculous things that night. Lick the Spoon of Doom. Eat a whole salted plum without blinking. Stare directly at a lemon. But suddenly, looking at that little cube of tofu trying to make himself small in the shadows, he realized he'd been daring everybody to do the *easy* kind of bold. The loud kind. The show-off kind. The kind he was best at.

And he had never, not once, dared anybody to do the hard kind.

So Passion Fruit hopped down off his booth — which he NEVER did mid-derby — walked right up to the purple sparkle-clouds, and did not scrunch his face even a little.

"Chòu Chòu," he said. "I've got a new 挑戰 for you. The biggest one yet. The Final Dare of the entire Sour-Dare Derby."

Chòu Chòu gulped. "Is it the spoon?"

"No spoon." Passion Fruit grinned, but it was a different grin now — a warm one. "I dare you to come into the middle of the market. Right under the brightest lantern. And just... *be you*. Cape, sparkle-clouds, clothespin, all of it. Loud and proud and exactly as you are. No hiding in any corner."

The crowd gasped. That was, frankly, the scariest dare anyone had heard all night.

Chòu Chòu's whole body wobbled. "But — they'll *smell* me. Everyone will scrunch."

"Maybe," said Passion Fruit honestly. "But here's a secret, kiddo — and don't tell the others." He lowered his voice. "I make everybody scrunch their faces too. Every night. And they keep coming back. Because once they're close enough, they find out the inside's worth it." He bonked Chòu Chòu gently with a wrinkly fist. "Bold on the outside, gold on the inside. You said it yourself. So go show 'em the gold."

---

Chòu Chòu took one shaky step.

Then another.

Then, with his heart-shaped cape fluttering and his purple sparkle-clouds swirling like little friendly fireworks, he walked all the way into the middle of Lantern Lane and stood under the very brightest lantern, where everyone could see him and — yes — smell him completely.

He took a deep breath. And in a voice that started tiny and got bigger and bigger, he said:

"Hello! I'm Chòu Chòu! I'm a little stinky! And I'm *crispy and warm and golden inside* and I am DONE hiding in corners!"

For a second, nobody moved.

Then Mango (still slightly puckered) shouted, "That's the bravest thing I've seen all NIGHT, and I licked the Spoon of Doom!"

And the whole market erupted. Snacks crowded in — actually *toward* him, sparkle-clouds and all. Boba bounced over and got close enough to find out that Chòu Chòu smelled mighty but hugged even mightier. Lychee, fanning herself, admitted, "All right, *darling*, I'll allow it — you do have a certain charm." Even Coconut Jelly came back for his sunglasses and stayed for the friend.

Passion Fruit climbed onto his booth one last time, rang his little bell as loud as it would ring, and bellowed:

"LADIES AND GENTLE-SNACKS! Tonight's Sour-Dare Derby has a CHAMPION! And it's not for keeping a smooth face — pfft, ANY coward can keep a smooth face." He winked at the reader, the way he sometimes did when he thought nobody was looking. "Tonight's champion did the boldest 挑戰 of all. The one nobody dared him to do, until he dared himself. The dare of just *being kind to himself*, out loud, in the open. Give it up for the Pucker-Proof, Cloud-Sparkly, Heart-Caped CHAMPION — CHÒU CHÒU!"

And the funny thing? Not one single snack scrunched their face.

They were all too busy cheering.

---

After that, the dare booth changed its sign. It still says **挑戰 (tiǎozhàn) = challenge** in dribbly paint, but underneath, in slightly neater letters, Chòu Chòu added a line:

*The bravest dare is being kind — especially to yourself — when nobody dared you to.*

Passion Fruit pretended to grumble about it ruining his cool sour image. But every night, when he thinks the market isn't watching, he reads that line again and grins his soft, wrinkly grin. And he always saves the very best lantern-spot, the brightest one, for a little golden cube with a heart-shaped cape and clouds like friendly purple fireworks.

Because some dares you win by puckering least.

And some you win by being most *you*.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
Little one, snuggle down now, soft as a folded cape. Remember that the boldest thing you'll ever do isn't the loud thing or the show-off thing — it's being gentle and true, especially to your own dear heart, even when nobody dares you to. You are golden on the inside, sparkles and all, and the people who matter will always come close enough to find out. Let the lanterns dim, let the market hush, let your breathing go slow and warm. Tomorrow there will be new dares, but tonight there is only rest. Goodnight.
