# Story 21: Sunrise on Haleakala

**Main Character:** Dole Whip Dani
**Guest Characters:** Kai the Shave Ice, Manapua Mano
**Setting:** The chilly summit of Haleakala volcano on Maui before dawn
**Theme:** The best things are worth waking up early for.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** la (LAH) = sun / day

---

It was so early in the morning that the morning hadn't even started yet.

The stars were still out, the roosters were still snoring, and the moon looked like it was thinking about staying in bed. And there, bouncing up a winding mountain road in a rattly little tour bus, was a swirl of golden pineapple soft-serve so excited she was practically melting on purpose.

"Wake UP, wake UP, wake UP!" sang Dole Whip Dani, her glossy curl wobbling like a happy spring. "Today is the day we see the most magical thing in the whole entire world!"

Behind her, two very sleepy friends groaned in the dark.

"Dani," mumbled Kai the Shave Ice, his paper-umbrella hat tipped sideways over one eye, "it is so early the *birds* haven't woken up. And birds wake up for a *living*."

"Five more minutes," wheezed a soft pillowy voice from the back seat. It was Manapua Mano, the steamed pork bun, curled into a warm doughy ball with little wisps of steam puffing out of him like a sleepy teakettle. "Just five. Or maybe five hundred."

---

Now, you may be wondering where three desserts were going at such a ridiculous hour. Let me tell you, dear reader, because it is a very good story and I have been saving it just for tonight.

They were climbing **Haleakala** (hah-lay-AH-kah-lah), the great sleeping volcano on the island of Maui. It is so tall that clouds gather *below* it, like a fluffy white blanket tucked around its shoulders. And every single morning, right at the very top, something happens that people travel across whole oceans to see.

"The **la** (LAH) = the sun," Dani had explained the night before, eyes sparkling like sugar in sunlight. "It rises right here, out of the sea of clouds. Haleakala even *means* something. It means *House of the Sun!* Can you believe it? The sun has a HOUSE! And we get to be there when it comes home!"

She had been so bubbly about it that her friends had said yes before they remembered that "sunrise" meant "waking up while it is still completely dark and also freezing."

Because here is the thing nobody tells you about the top of a tropical island: it gets *cold* up high. Cold enough to make a shave ice shiver.

The whole way up, Dani had handed out supplies from a little woven bag she'd packed the night before. "Hot cocoa for everyone! Mittens! Tiny blankets! I even brought snacks, because you can't watch a sunrise on an empty tummy, that's just *science*."

"Snacks?" Manapua perked up instantly, both eyes popping open. "What kind of snacks? Where? Show me the snacks. I am awake now. I am SO awake. Snacks!"

"See?" Dani laughed. "I knew the magic word."

Kai pulled a tiny blanket over his head like a hood and grumbled good-naturedly. "The only magic word *I* want to hear is 'go back to the warm beach.'"

The bus huffed and puffed up the last curve and parked. The doors hissed open. A blast of icy mountain air rushed in.

"Whoo!" Dani gasped, hopping out. "It's chilly! It's chilly up here! I love it, I'm a little melty, this is GREAT for me!"

Kai stepped out and froze — literally. "Oh no," he whispered, holding very still. "Dani. Dani, I can feel my syrups getting firmer. My lilikoi stripe just went *crunchy*."

"That's the spirit!" Dani chirped, completely missing the point.

Manapua rolled out last and immediately bounced three times to stay warm. "It's f-f-freezing! My steam is turning into tiny clouds! Look — I'm making weather! I'm a whole weather system!" He bounced again. "Somebody warn the news!"

---

They shuffled up a stony path in the dark, the wind whistling soft and lonely around the giant mountain. It was quiet up here. The kind of quiet that makes you whisper even when you don't have to.

The ground beneath their feet was strange and wonderful — red and rust-orange and grey, all swoops and cinder cones, like the surface of a faraway planet. Manapua kept stopping to gawk.

"It looks like the *moon*," he whispered. "Or like a giant scooped out a thousand bowls of haupia and forgot to fill them back up. Hey — do you think the moon has snacks?"

"Manapua," Kai said gently, "everything you see, you ask if it has snacks."

"Because everything *should*," Manapua said, with the deep conviction of a steamed bun who has never been wrong about this.

They passed a little cluster of silvery, spiky plants glowing faint in the starlight, curled up tight like sleeping silver hedgehogs. Dani gasped and clapped her hands.

"Those are **silverswords**! They only grow here — *nowhere else in the whole world* — way up high where it's cold. They wait years and years, all quiet and patient, and then one day they bloom into the most beautiful flowers you ever saw." She wiggled with delight. "They're like us! They know the best things take a little waiting!"

"They also know how to dress warm," Kai muttered, eyeing their fuzzy silver leaves with envy. "Smart plants."

"Maybe this was a bad idea," Kai murmured, his teeth — well, ice doesn't have teeth, but his syrup stripes were definitely chattering. "I'm a *beach* dessert, Dani. I belong by warm sand. Not on top of a frozen rock in the dark waiting for a sun that might not even show up."

"He's got a point," Manapua said, hugging his own pillowy sides. "What if the la is sleepy too? What if it overslept? We came all this way and the sun just... rolls over and snores? I'd snore. I'm a great snorer."

For just a moment, even Dani's curl drooped a tiny bit.

Then she did what Dole Whip Dani always does. She turned a problem into a plan.

"Okay, team!" she said, spinning around with a swirl. "If we're cold, we cuddle! Kai, you stand in the middle — you're the coolest, so you go where the warm guys can hug you. Manapua, you're a steamed bun, you're basically a tiny warm oven, so you snuggle his left. I'm sunny and bright, so I'll take his right. We make a friend-sandwich!"

"A friend-sandwich," Kai repeated slowly.

"The coziest sandwich in Hawaii!" Dani beamed.

And so they did. Manapua scooted close, puffing warm cinnamon-y steam against Kai's frosty side. Dani pressed in on the other, glowing golden and toasty-warm like a little scoop of sunshine. And in the middle, Kai stopped shivering.

"Hey," he said, surprised. "Hey, that's... actually really nice."

"That's **'ohana** (oh-HAH-nah) = family," Dani said softly. "When one of us is cold, we *all* get warm together. Now — everybody look east."

"Which way is east?" Manapua asked, swiveling in a slow circle. "Is east the snack way? I'm hoping east is the snack way."

Dani giggled and gently turned him by his little swirl-knot until he faced the right direction. "East is *that* way, silly bun. East is the way the la comes home. Watch the edge of the clouds, right where they touch the sky. Don't blink. Or — okay, you can blink. Blinking is allowed. But not too much!"

So they settled in, three little desserts cuddled into the warmest friend-sandwich on the whole island, and they waited together in the hush of the dark.

---

So they looked. Three little desserts in a warm huddle on the roof of the world, staring out at a sky that was still dark and full of stars.

And they waited.

The wind hushed. The stars began to blink out, one by one, shy and quiet. And way, way out past the edge of the mountain, where the great soft blanket of clouds met the sky, the darkness started to thin — like someone was very slowly turning up a dimmer switch on the whole universe.

"Ooooh," whispered Manapua. "It's doing something."

The bottom of the sky turned the gentlest pink. Then peach. Then a warm dreamy orange, the exact color, Dani realized with a happy gasp, of *her own swirly self*.

"That's MY color!" she squealed. "The sunrise is wearing my outfit!"

The clouds below them caught the light and turned to rolling fields of rose-gold cotton candy. Kai's eyes went huge. "The *colors*," he breathed, because if there is one thing Kai loves, it is colors. "Strawberry. Lilikoi gold. A blue so soft it's like coconut milk. Dani — I make rainbows for a *living* and I have never, ever seen one this big."

And then — there.

A single sliver of fierce, glowing gold peeked over the sea of clouds. The first edge of the **la**.

It rose slowly, the way warm things do — *low and slow*, as a certain roly-poly pig back home liked to say — climbing up out of the clouds like it was coming home through its own front door. Warmth poured across the summit. Real, honest, golden warmth, washing over three cold little friends until they glowed.

All around them, the whole world changed at once. The strange red moon-ground lit up like glowing embers. The silversword plants caught the light and shimmered like they were made of spun sugar. Even the little puffs of steam rising off Manapua turned to gold and drifted up to join the dawn. It was as if someone had reached out and gently switched the entire mountain from "night" to "morning," and the morning was the most beautiful one ever made.

A soft cheer rose from somewhere down the slope — other early-risers, people and creatures who'd climbed up in the cold just like they had, all of them gasping and laughing and pointing at the same impossible gold. Dani felt her heart swell three sizes. *Everyone here woke up early,* she thought. *Everyone here decided the magic was worth the cold. And it was. Oh, it was.*

Manapua stopped shivering and let out a long, blissful steam-sigh. "Ohhh. Oh, that's the good stuff. That's warm-from-the-inside warm."

Kai didn't say anything at all. He just stared, sparkling all over with melting frost that looked exactly like happy tears, his rainbow stripes lit up like stained glass.

And Dole Whip Dani — sun-loving, sun-colored Dani — tipped her glossy curl back, closed her sparkly eyes, and let the sunshine soak right into her.

"Welcome home, la," she whispered. "This is your house. And today, we got to be your guests."

---

For a long, golden moment, nobody spoke. They just sat in the warmth at the top of the House of the Sun, watching the world below light up gold and pink and gentle blue.

Finally Kai cleared his throat. "Dani. I am so sorry I complained. I have decided this is the single most beautiful thing I have ever seen, and I would wake up at this ridiculous hour a hundred more times to see it again."

"Me too," sighed Manapua. "Even though I'm still mostly clouds." He puffed a tiny steam-puff and it floated off golden into the dawn. "Look. I made a sunrise cloud. I'm part of it now."

Dani laughed her warm, bubbly laugh and pulled both friends close. "See? *This* is why you get up early. The best things in the whole wide world — they don't come to you while you're cozy in bed. You gotta climb up cold and wait in the dark and shiver a little. And then..." She gestured at the blazing golden sky. "Then the la comes home, and it was worth every single minute."

She grinned her sunniest grin.

"Stay cool and swirl on, you guys. The House of the Sun says aloha."

And down they went, three warm friends, sticky and sleepy and so, so happy, with a whole bright new day waiting just for them.

## Goodnight Blessing
Little one, the best and brightest things are worth waking up for — but right now, the only thing worth waking up for is a good, deep, cozy sleep. Let the warm gold of the sunrise tuck itself behind your eyes. Snuggle in close like a friend-sandwich, where everyone is warm together. The la has gone home to its house behind the clouds, and so should you. Sleep soft, sleep sweet, dear heart. Goodnight.
