# Story 21: Sunrise on Haleakala

**Main Character:** Dole Whip Dani
**Guest Characters:** Kai the Shave Ice, Manapua Mano
**Setting:** The chilly summit of Haleakala volcano on Maui before dawn
**Theme:** The best things are worth waking up early for.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **la** (LAH) = sun / day

---

It was so early that the morning hadn't even started yet. The stars were still out.

Up the mountain road rattled a little bus, and inside it bounced a swirl of golden soft-serve.

"Wake UP, wake UP!" sang Dole Whip Dani. "Today we see the most magical thing in the world!"

"Dani," groaned Kai the Shave Ice. "The birds aren't up yet, and birds wake up for a LIVING."

"Five more minutes," wheezed Manapua Mano from the back, steaming like a sleepy teakettle. "Or five hundred."

"Haleakala means House of the Sun," said Dani. "We'll watch the **la** (LAH) = sun come home!"

Dani jingled her woven supply bag. "Cocoa, mittens, tiny blankets, and SNACKS."

Manapua's eyes popped open. "Snacks? Where? I am awake. I am SO awake."

---

The bus huffed up the last curve. The doors hissed open. Icy mountain air rushed in.

"It's chilly! I'm a little melty, this is GREAT for me!" said Dani, hopping out.

Kai stepped out and froze. "Dani. My syrups are getting firmer. My lilikoi stripe just went crunchy."

Manapua bounced three times to stay warm, puffing steam clouds. "I'm making weather! Somebody warn the news!"

It was **anuanu** (ah-noo-AH-noo) = cold up here, colder than a shave ice's deepest shiver.

---

They shuffled up a stony path. The ground was red and rust and grey, like a faraway planet.

"Like a giant scooped out a thousand bowls of haupia," whispered Manapua. "Does the moon have snacks?"

Then a gust snatched Dani's woven bag right off her arm and bounced it over the crater rim.

"THE COCOA!" cried Dani, and hopped after it, swirl first.

The bag tumbled down the cinder slope. Dani hopped. Manapua bounced. Kai slid, stiff as a sled.

The bag bounced off a rock and went further. Dani grabbed air. Manapua grabbed Dani.

Kai, frozen solid, slid right past both of them. "Can't... stop... brah."

---

At the bottom, the bag hung snagged on a silvery spiky plant curled up like a hedgehog.

Kai bumped gently to a stop against the same plant. "Nice catch, plant."

"A silversword!" gasped Dani. "They grow nowhere else in the world. They wait YEARS to bloom."

Dani looked up. Far above, the rim was turning from black to the faintest grey.

"The la is coming!" said Dani. "We have to be at the top when it comes home!"

---

Dani made a plan, because Dani always does. "Friend-sandwich! Kai in the middle! Everybody push!"

Manapua pressed his warm steamy side to Kai's left. Dani glowed golden on his right.

Kai's syrups softened. "Hey. Hey, that's actually really nice."

Up the cinder slope they went, three desserts in a huddle, the sky paling above them.

Manapua's steam puffed behind them like a tiny train. "Choo choo! Snack train! Choo!"

The rim lightened to pink. Then peach. They scrambled over the top with one hop to spare.

---

Below them, a whole sea of clouds glowed rose-gold like cotton candy.

"Strawberry. Lilikoi gold. Coconut-milk blue," breathed Kai. "I make rainbows for a living. Never THIS big."

A sliver of fierce gold peeked over the clouds. The la came home through its own front door.

Warmth poured across the summit. The red ground glowed like embers. The silverswords shimmered like spun sugar.

"That's MY color!" squealed Dani. "The sunrise is wearing my outfit!"

Manapua's steam puffs turned gold and floated up to join the dawn. "I'm part of the sunrise now."

---

"I'm sorry I complained," said Kai. "I would wake up this early a hundred more times."

"The best things don't come to you cozy in bed," said Dani. "You climb up cold and wait."

Dani sipped the rescued cocoa, swirl tipped back, soaking in the sun. "Welcome home, la."

Down the mountain path went three warm friends, sticky and happy, a whole bright day waiting.

---
