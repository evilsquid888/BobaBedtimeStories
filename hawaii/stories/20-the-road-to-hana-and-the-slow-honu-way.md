# Story 20: The Road to Hana and the Slow Honu Way

**Main Character:** Honu Haupia
**Guest Characters:** Mac the Macadamia, Lilikoi Lani
**Setting:** The winding, waterfall-lined Road to Hana on Maui
**Theme:** Slowing down lets you see the wonders you'd miss.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **hana** (HAH-nah) = work / craft (also the town Hana)

---

The trouble began, as trouble often does, with a wiggly purple fruit who could not sit still.

"WE'RE GOING TO HANA TODAY!" hollered Lilikoi Lani, bouncing on the dashboard until her seeds rattled.

Behind the wheel, moving exactly as fast as a drifting cloud, sat Honu Haupia.

Mac the Macadamia peeked from his glossy shell in the back seat. "Is the road bumpy?"

"Six hundred curves," said Honu slowly. "Fifty-four little bridges. This road does not like to be zoomed."

"Everything likes to be zoomed!" said Lani. "I'll ZOOM us there before lunch! VROOM!"

---

The road curled around the island like a noodle that got lost and kept going.

Honu steered through the first curve. Then the second. Mac's shell went clack, clack against the door.

Lani hopped onto the gas pedal. "Faster!" The car leapt forward with a surprised honk.

Honu eased her flipper down. The car stopped before a chubby crab crossing the road sideways.

Lani flew off the dashboard and landed in a roadside fern, upside down.

"We could've been THREE curves ahead!" wailed Lani from the fern.

---

Then she saw it. A waterfall no bigger than a kitchen tap, wearing a tiny rainbow.

"Small," whispered Mac, popping out. "But mighty."

"Pfft. I've seen bigger," said Lani. She stared anyway for a whole minute, which is forever in lilikoi.

Honu hummed an old turtle tune with no hurry in it. "Slow flippers, far journeys."

"Slow flippers, NUMB BOTTOM," said Lani.

---

Around the next bend the car wheezed, hiccuped, and went putt... putt... putt.

Honu eased it under a giant mango tree. Steam curled from the hood like a sigh.

"The engine is tired," said Honu. "It was pushed too fast. It needs one hour of rest."

"AN HOUR?" Lani's seeds rattled. "An hour is a THOUSAND YEARS in lilikoi time!"

She announced she would walk to Hana herself. Four steps later she remembered her legs were tiny.

A mango dropped off the tree and bonked her on the head. Plop.

Meanwhile Mac had wobbled down a mossy path. "Everybody... you HAVE to see this."

---

Below lay a secret pool, clear and blue, fed by a waterfall three turtles tall.

Bamboo clacked overhead. Sunlight came down in golden ropes. Silver fish stitched the water with sparkles.

"You cannot find this at fifty miles an hour," said Honu. "This place belongs to the slow."

Lani opened her mouth to say something cheeky. Nothing came out.

She climbed onto a warm rock, dipped her toes in the **wai** (WYE) = fresh water, and went aaaaahhhh.

"Sour first," said Honu gently. "Sweet always. Isn't that your saying, little one?"

---

Mac popped his shell open and floated in it like a tiny boat, paddling in happy circles.

Lani stood on the rock directing him like a very bossy lighthouse. "Left! No, MY left!"

Honu floated on her back, shell jiggling. "This road was built by many arms, stone by stone."

"That is **hana** (HAH-nah) = work, craft," said Honu. "You cannot rush a thing made with love."

"So the slow part isn't the boring part," said Lani, floating. "The slow part is the GOOD part."

"Now you are thinking like a honu," said Honu.

---

When the hour was up, the engine started first try. Putt-putt-PUTT. It had enjoyed the rest too.

They drove on slowly. A waterfall like a wedding veil. A mossy bridge like a green dragon.

"STOP! Rainbow TREES!" shouted Lani. "Most stylish forest I have ever met!"

---

At last the little car hummed into quiet Hana as the sky turned guava-pink.

"We made it," whispered Mac.

"We made it SLOW," said Lani, snuggling into Honu's wobbly shell. "Slow is my favorite speed."

"Now you are a honu," said Honu, and the tired little engine ticked softly as it cooled.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
Slow your flippers, little one, the way the honu does. There is no race to sleep and no prize for getting there first. The best things are the ones you pause to see. Drift slow, drift safe, drift far. Goodnight.
