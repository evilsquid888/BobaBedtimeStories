# Story 3: Malia the Malasada and the Warm Morning Batch

**Main Character:** Malia the Malasada
**Guest Characters:** Niu the Coconut; Manapua Mano; Pua the Pineapple; a line of hungry treat-folk
**Setting:** A bustling Portuguese bakery in Honolulu at dawn
**Theme:** Sharing what you have makes more, not less.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **mahalo** (mah-HAH-loh) = thank you
**Cameo:** Totoro (Studio Ghibli) — presses his whole face to the bakery window wanting sugar, and one sneeze blows every crystal off Malia; walk-on: Hamtaro (Hamtaro)

---

The very first thing Malia the Malasada did that morning was sneeze.

Seven sugar crystals shot off her cheeks like tiny shooting stars. "Excuse me!" she giggled.

She was the last malasada of the batch, plump, golden, rolled extra-thick in sparkly sugar.

The bakery glowed pink with dawn. The fryer hummed.

Malia hugged her own sparkly self. "This sugar is MINE. Every crystal. Not one grain for anybody!"

A fly wobbled past. Malia clutched tighter. "Mine," she squeaked.

---

Outside the steamy window, a line of hungry treat-folk waited on the back step.

First came Niu the Coconut, tapping the glass with his three little face-dots.

"Good morning, Malia! Spare a crystal or two?"

"If I give some away, I'll have LESS," said Malia. "And less is sad. So, no."

"My motto: soft outside, full of sugar I'm not sharing," she added.

Niu blinked his dots. "That motto," he said gently, "needs work."

---

Next bounced Manapua Mano, so hard the whole windowsill rattled.

"Sugarsugarsugar! Malia! The teensiest sprinkle?" He squashed his face to the glass.

Malia's tummy flipped. "I can't. I worked SO hard to be this sparkly."

"You fell out of a fryer," Manapua pointed out.

"It was very tiring," sniffed Malia.

Behind him swept Pua the Pineapple, crown held high. "I, Pua, do not usually ASK."

"Nope!" sang Malia, and hopped a half-inch farther down the tray, very pleased with herself.

---

Then the sun hit the window, and the bakery got HOT.

A crystal slid off Malia's shoulder and melted into a sad little smear. "My SUGAR!"

She hopped under the ceiling fan to cool off. WHOOSH. Twenty crystals blew away like confetti.

She dove into the flour bin and came out white as a snowball. "I'm a SNOWBALL," she wailed.

The tighter she squeezed, the faster it melted.

---

The whole window went grey and furry. Two round eyes and an enormous toothy grin filled the glass.

Totoro had smelled sugar. He squashed his big grey nose flat on the glass and huffed hopefully.

"Not for forest spirits EITHER!" squeaked Malia, hugging her last sparkles.

Totoro's whiskers twitched. His nose wrinkled. His huge chest swelled up and up and up.

AH-AH-CHOOOO! The window rattled, and every crystal on Malia whooshed off in a glittering blizzard.

Totoro blinked, pleased, and sat down in the lane with a leaf on his head, grinning.

---

Malia blinked through the settling sparkle. One crystal left, right on the tip of her nose.

"I kept ALL of it," she sniffled, "and I have less anyway. WHY?"

"Held tight, sugar melts," called Niu through the glass. "Shared around..." He didn't know the ending.

Malia spotted sugar heaped on the top basket by the open window. She climbed.

Basket by basket, up she went. The tower leaned. It leaned more. "Uh-oh," said Malia.

CRASH. Out the window she shot in a glittering shower, splat into the line.

---

Manapua caught a faceful. "MALIA! You're the SWEETEST!"

Niu caught crystals in his woven hat and sang out **mahalo** (mah-HAH-loh) = thank you. "Mahalo, mahalo!"

Every mahalo made Malia warmer. Glow-warm, all the way to her custard heart.

She looked at the crystal on her nose. Then she tipped sideways. "Take it ALL! Share it warm!"

Manapua hugged her so hard his steam fogged her all over. Brand-new crystals sparkled up, fresh.

Malia gasped. "I have MORE sugar than before!"

"That's how it finishes," said Niu. "Shared around, it comes back."

---

The sugar tray on the back step rustled, and up popped Hamtaro, a tiny orange-and-white hamster. "Heke?"

"Fresh, warm, and never alone," giggled Malia, and offered him a crystal, then everyone a bite.

"Mahalo," whispered Malia. The sweetest word on the whole island.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
May your arms stay open and your heart stay warm. The sweetest things grow sweeter when you pass them around, and mahalo is a word worth saying often. Snuggle down now, dusted in sugar, warm and never alone. Goodnight.
