# Story 9: Lilikoi Lani and the Sour Surprise

**Main Character:** Lilikoi Lani
**Guest Characters:** Mac the Macadamia, Pua the Pineapple, Niu the Coconut, a grumpy gecko, a hundred ladybugs
**Setting:** A backyard fruit garden in Manoa Valley, Oahu
**Theme:** A good joke lifts people up, never puts them down.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **akamai** (ah-kah-MY) = smart / clever

---

Deep in misty Manoa Valley, rain fell soft as a kitten's sneeze and rainbows came in threes.

In a backyard fruit garden lived the cheekiest fruit on Oahu: Lilikoi Lani, small, purple and wrinkly.

"Sour first," she whispered, sneaking up on a dozing snail, "sweet ALWAYS!" Pop! A tangy mist. Boo!

"Today," she told a passing ladybug, "is the greatest prank day in the history of pranks!"

"Lani," murmured Mac the Macadamia from his shell, "I do not think the gecko wants your flower hat."

---

Prank one. Lani tipped a dewdrop off a leaf, plip, onto Pua the Pineapple's royal crown.

"My CROWN! It's wilting! It's..." Pua patted it. Just water. "LANI!"

"Crown up, sweetheart!" sang Lani, zooming off. Even Pua fluffed her leaves back up, smiling.

Prank two. "Niu! There's a centipede on your hat!" cried Lani.

Niu the Coconut didn't blink one face-dot. "A wise coconut checks before he panics. Be cleverer."

Cleverer. The word stuck to Lani like syrup. She'd be the most **akamai** (ah-kah-MY) = smart fruit ever.

She spotted Mac dozing in the sun, shell open a crack, the way he did when feeling safe.

---

Lani crept up, took a huge breath, and shouted, "A HUNGRY GIANT IS COMING TO EAT THE GARDEN!"

Mac shrieked. His shell slammed shut so hard he bounced, boing, boing, boing, under a fern.

"Got you good!" laughed Lani. "Sour first, sweet always! Come out, Mac. Mac?" The shell stayed shut.

A tiny voice came from inside. "I really thought we were in trouble. I'm too small to help."

"And you knew that," said Mac. "And you made it your joke."

Lani's bright seeds went very still. For the first time in her zesty life, nothing felt funny.

---

"I made a real mess," mumbled Lani to Niu, her scent trail gone droopy and dull.

"Clever AT friends, or FOR them?" said Niu. "Akamai lifts somebody up."

"A good joke is Manoa rain, falling on everybody. A mean one's a puddle you slip in."

"When you dripped on my crown, I laughed," said Pua. "Your joke said I know you."

Lani's seeds glowed. "I need the most akamai prank ever. Your crown, Pua. Niu's hat. A hundred ladybugs."

---

Pua lent her crown for structural support, but only after three compliments.

Lani lined up a hundred ladybugs in rows, which is like teaching raindrops to march.

The ladybugs wandered. Then the grumpy gecko sneezed and the rows scattered like sprinkles.

Lani bribed the gecko with a guava, then squirted one sweet tangy mist over the vines.

Every ladybug followed the scent and settled into place, row by row, wing by wing.

---

As the Manoa mist turned gold, then pink, then violet, Lani crept to the fern.

"Mac, I'm sorry. That wasn't funny, that was just sour," said Lani. "Open your shell and look up."

Creeeak. One shy eye peeked out. Mac looked up.

---

Across the whole garden, a hundred ladybugs held perfectly still in the shape of one ENORMOUS macadamia.

Inside its shell they made a heart. Pua held one corner with her crown, Niu the other.

"Here's the joke," said Lani. "You spent all day thinking you were small. THAT's the silly part."

"You warm this whole garden just by being in it. That's not small. That's mighty."

Mac laughed. The deep-down belly laugh he saved for her very best jokes.

"Small but mighty," hiccuped Mac, "shell to heart!"

"Now THAT," mumbled Niu, who never admitted anything, "was akamai."

---

That night, under the lilikoi vine, Lani and Mac sat shell-to-seed watching stars wink on.

"I'll still prank you," Lani warned sleepily.

"I know," said Mac. "Just lift me up with it?"

"Always," she promised. And the gentlest rain in Hawaii pattered down, soft as a kitten's sneeze.

---
