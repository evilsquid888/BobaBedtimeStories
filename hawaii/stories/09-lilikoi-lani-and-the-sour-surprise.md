# Story 9: Lilikoi Lani and the Sour Surprise

**Main Character:** Lilikoi Lani
**Guest Characters:** Mac the Macadamia, Pua the Pineapple, Niu the Coconut, a grumpy gecko, a hundred ladybugs
**Setting:** A backyard fruit garden in Manoa Valley, Oahu
**Theme:** A good joke lifts people up, never puts them down.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **akamai** (ah-kah-MY) = smart / clever
**Cameo:** Team Rocket: Jessie, James and Meowth (Pokémon) — drop in by cat-head balloon to out-prank Lani, bite the ripest lilikoi on the vine and blast off over the garden wall; walk-on: Psyduck (Pokémon)

---

Deep in misty Manoa Valley, rain fell soft as a kitten's sneeze and rainbows came in threes.

In a backyard garden lived the cheekiest fruit on Oahu: Lilikoi Lani, small, purple and wrinkly.

"Sour first," she whispered, sneaking up on a dozing snail, "sweet ALWAYS!" Pop! A tangy mist. Boo!

"Today," Lani told a passing ladybug, "is the greatest prank day in prank history!"

"Lani," murmured Mac the Macadamia from his shell, "the gecko does not want your flower hat."

---

Prank one. Lani tipped a dewdrop off a leaf, plip, onto Pua the Pineapple's crown.

"My CROWN! It's wilting! It's..." Pua patted it. Just water. "LANI!"

"Crown up, sweetheart!" sang Lani, zooming off. Pua fluffed her leaves up, smiling anyway.

Prank two. "Niu! A centipede on your hat!" cried Lani.

Niu the Coconut stayed still as a stone, face-dots unimpressed. "Wise coconuts check first. Be cleverer."

Cleverer. The word stuck to Lani like syrup. She'd be the most **akamai** (ah-kah-MY) = smart fruit ever.

---

Then she spotted Mac dozing in the sun, shell open a crack, feeling safe.

Lani crept up, took a huge breath, and shouted, "A HUNGRY GIANT IS COMING TO EAT US!"

Mac shrieked. His shell slammed shut so hard he bounced, boing, boing, boing, under a fern.

"Got you good!" laughed Lani. "Come out, Mac. Mac?" The shell stayed shut.

From inside, a tiny voice: "I really thought we were in trouble. I'm too small to help."

"And you knew that," said Mac. "And you made it your joke."

Lani's bright seeds went very still. For once in her zesty life, nothing felt funny.

---

"I made a real mess," mumbled Lani to Niu, scent trail gone droopy.

"Clever AT friends, or FOR them?" said Niu. "Akamai lifts somebody up."

"When you dripped on my crown, I laughed," said Pua. "Your joke said I know you."

Lani's seeds glowed. "I need the most akamai prank ever. A hundred ladybugs. Everybody, help."

---

Lani lined up a hundred ladybugs in rows, like teaching raindrops to march.

The gecko sneezed. Rows scattered. Lani tried bigger: one sweet mist, and they drifted back into place.

---

Then a giant balloon shaped like a grinning cat's head bumped down over the garden wall.

Out hopped magenta-haired Jessie, lavender-haired James and Meowth, posing. "Prepare for trouble!" sang Jessie.

"And make it double!" sang James, tossing a rose. "We hear there's a prankster. We're BETTER."

"Dat's right!" said Meowth, plucking the ripest lilikoi off the vine. "Nobody out-pranks Team Rocket!" Chomp.

SOUR. Three faces puckered small as raisins. The balloon popped, and every ladybug scattered.

Up they shot, over the wall, a twinkle in the sky. "Blasting off agaaain!" wailed Jessie.

---

Lani blinked at the bare vines, then squirted her sweetest mist ever. Wing by wing, they drifted back.

A yellow duck waddled in, licked a dropped sour seed, and grabbed its head. "Psy..." said Psyduck.

---

As the Manoa mist turned gold, then pink, then violet, Lani crept to the fern.

"Mac, I'm sorry. That wasn't funny, that was just sour," said Lani. "Open up and look."

Creeeak. One shy eye peeked out. Mac looked up.

---

Across the garden, a hundred ladybugs held perfectly still in the shape of one ENORMOUS macadamia.

Inside its shell they made a heart. Pua steadied one corner, Niu the other.

"Here's the joke," said Lani. "You spent all day thinking you were small. THAT's the silly part."

Mac laughed his deep-down belly laugh, saved for her best jokes. "Small but mighty, shell to heart!"

"Now THAT," mumbled Niu, who never admitted anything, "was akamai."

---

That night, under the lilikoi vine, Lani and Mac sat shell-to-seed watching stars wink on.

"I'll still prank you," Lani warned sleepily.

"I know," said Mac. "Just lift me up with it?"

"Always," she promised. And the gentlest rain in Hawaii pattered down, soft as a kitten's sneeze.

## Goodnight Blessing
Be akamai with your kindness, sweet always. Make somebody laugh and feel bigger, never smaller. Tuck your worries into a cozy shell. You are not too small. You never were. Goodnight.
