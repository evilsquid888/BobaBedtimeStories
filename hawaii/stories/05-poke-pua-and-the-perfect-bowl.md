# Story 5: Poke Pua and the Perfect Bowl

**Main Character:** Poke Pua
**Guest Characters:** Honu Haupia; Mr. Wiggleworth, a sea bass judge; Gus the gull
**Setting:** A fish market and poke counter near Honolulu harbor
**Theme:** Done with love beats perfect every time.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **ono** (OH-noh) = delicious
**Cameo:** Maomao (The Apothecary Diaries) — crawls out from under the judges' table to test the perfect bowl for poison, one cube at a time, until half of it is gone; walk-on: Sanji (One Piece)

---

The morning the harbor woke, Poke Pua was already lining up her sesame seeds.

Not pouring. Not sprinkling. Lining them up. One. By. One.

"A little left, sweet star," she whispered, nudging a seed with her kelp hair. "Perfect."

A seed rolled off the rim. Pua gasped like it had fallen off a cliff, and dove.

Today was the Harbor Tasting Contest. "I will build the most flawless bowl ever," said Pua. "And WIN!"

---

The fish market was a salty, slappy-floppy place. Fish-folk hollered prices.

"Same size," Pua muttered at the tuna cubes. "No wobblers. You. Out." The smallest sniffled and rolled away.

---

On the warm dock dozed Honu Haupia, a wobbly coconut-pudding sea turtle, shell jiggling.

"Little wave," said Honu slowly, "you have arranged that cube a long time. Does it taste right?"

Pua paused. "Taste? No TIME to taste. I've been making it LOOK like it tastes good."

Honu smiled slowly. "Slow flippers, far journeys," she said, and dozed off mid-smile.

Pua frowned at her bowl. Was it **ono** (OH-noh) = delicious? "Of course. Look how pretty."

---

Then a breeze off the water blew one sesame seed crooked.

Pua fixed it. A gull named Gus swooped past. His wing-wind blew seven seeds crooked.

Pua fixed them all, then built a wall of lime wedges around the bowl.

Gus landed on the wall and ate a lime wedge. "GUS," said Pua.

So Pua lifted the bowl onto her head and tiptoed toward the tent, Gus circling hopefully.

---

Inside the candy-striped tent, the judge, Mr. Wiggleworth, was a round sea bass in tiny glasses.

"Order!" he barked, though nobody was disorderly. "No bribing the judge! That means YOU, gull."

Pua set down her masterpiece. Ahi spiraled like a sunset. Edamame formed a constellation. Sesame stood like soldiers.

Mr. Wiggleworth lifted his spoon. He leaned toward the flawless bowl...

---

The tablecloth flipped up. Out crawled Maomao, dark-haired, in a plain apothecary's robe.

"Stop," said Maomao flatly, pushing the spoon aside. "Unknown dish. Could be poison. I must test it."

She ate one ahi cube. Her eyes sparkled like fireworks. "Suspicious," muttered Maomao, and ate another.

Cube by cube, the sunset spiral vanished. The constellation lost three stars. The soldiers deserted.

"Dangerously delicious," declared Maomao, chewing. "Tragically, zero poison." Her shoulders drooped with disappointment.

She sat cross-legged under the table, hugging a bundle of herbs, and sulked happily.

Pua stared at her half-eaten masterpiece. "She TESTED it," squeaked Pua. "With her WHOLE FACE."

---

Pua snatched her tweezers and rebuilt at top speed. Cube, cube, seed, seed, FASTER.

Gus swooped low for a closer look. His wingtip clipped the tent pole.

The pole bonked the table. The table hopped. And Pua's half-perfect bowl went FWOOMP.

Tuna flew. Seaweed sailed. Sesame rained down like the world's tiniest, most disorganized snowstorm.

Everything landed in one splashy heap of lime and soy. "It's RUINED!" cried Pua.

Mr. Wiggleworth, who had skipped lunch, scooped a spoonful. He chewed. His eyebrows climbed off his forehead.

"This," said Mr. Wiggleworth slowly, "is the most ono thing I have ever tasted."

---

"The lime is LAUGHING!" he cried, taking another bite. "The soy is hugging the tuna!"

Pua took one bite of her poke. Her eyes went huge. "Oh. OH." Like the whole noisy sea.

"I tried all morning to be perfect," laughed Pua. "The best thing was getting jumbled up."

Pua tossed her tweezers into the sea. "New rule. Tasting first, lining up never. Who wants poke?"

Sanji, blond hair over one eye, kissed the air at the heap. "Now THAT'S a bowl," he purred.

---

At sunset, Pua and Honu shared the day's last bowl on the warm dock. Messy. Perfect. Both.

"Fresh from the sea, that's me," sighed Pua. "And a little messy from the heart."

"The best ones always are," said Honu.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
You don't have to be perfect to be wonderful, just you, made with love. The messy moments are the most delicious. Let your sesame seeds land where they land. Snuggle down and let the tide rock you slow. Goodnight.
