# Story 32: The Dolphins of the Bay

**Main Character:** Kai the Shave Ice
**Guest Characters:** Dole Whip Dani; Poke Pua; a pod of spinner dolphins with one belly-flopping baby; a judgmental crab
**Setting:** A calm, sparkling dolphin bay at sunrise on the Big Island
**Theme:** Joy is best when it's shared and given space.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **nai'a** (nye-AH) = dolphin

---

Sunrise on the Big Island. The bay lay still as a dozing cat, peachy-pink and gold.

Kai the Shave Ice tilted so far his umbrella hat nearly slid off.

"The water looks like blueberry shave ice nobody's eaten yet," whispered Kai.

Dole Whip Dani yawned beside him, her golden curl still squished flat from the pillow.

"Kai, the sun's not even all the way up," said Dani. "What's the sparkly emergency?"

Poke Pua arranged her sesame sprinkles just so, and waited.

Kai pointed one drippy mitten. "The **nai'a** (nye-AH) = dolphin play here. Today, I JOIN the pod."

Right on cue, the bay broke open in a silver splash.

---

A dolphin leaped straight up, twirled three times like a wet happy top, and slipped back under.

Kai screamed. Quietly. A polite mountain of ice gets lots of practice at that.

"It TWIRLED! In the AIR!" whisper-shrieked Kai, sloshing lilikoi syrup. "Like a cinnamon roll with FINS!"

More rose. Two, five, a whole shimmering pod, grey backs curving up like smooth stones.

A baby dolphin, no bigger than a loaf of bread, flopped sideways. SPLAT.

"Belly-flop," gasped Dani, melting a little at the edges. "Best move ever. I'm learning it."

"They rest here all morning," said Pua softly. "Half nap, half keep watch. Then one just... boings."

---

Kai tiptoed into the shallows. Flomp. The nearest dolphin drifted politely away.

"Closer," said Kai. "Dani! Cannonball formation!"

"CANNONBALL!" hollered Dani.

FLOMP, FLOMP, FLOMP. Two treats charged the bay, hooting, syrup and soft-serve flying.

Kai hopped like a dolphin and belly-flopped. His umbrella hat floated off alone.

"Uh," said Pua, from the sand.

The nai'a bunched into a tight knot and slid toward the far side of the bay.

Kai stood knee-deep in cold water, hat gone. "They left," he said, small. "Did I scare them?"

---

Pua waded out and wrapped a cool seaweed arm around him.

"This bay is their resting place," said Pua. "Rush them, and they put their joy away small."

A sad drip slid down Kai's side. This time it wasn't syrup.

"I didn't want it small," said Kai. "I wanted MORE. I wanted to be IN it."

"Me too," said Dani, dimmed to a soft glow. "I forgot it wasn't mine to grab."

"Kind knuckleheads," said Pua. "The best kind. New plan: sit VERY still, VERY far away."

"I am EXCELLENT at sitting still," said Kai.

"You are vibrating right now," said Dani.

---

Far up the beach they sat, **mālie** (MAH-lee-eh) = calm, three little shapes giving the bay back.

Kai wiggled. Dani hummed. Pua rearranged her freckles four times. A crab walked past and judged them.

"How long do we wait?" whispered Kai, after eleven whole seconds.

"As long as it takes," whispered Pua. "No, that is not a number."

They played one-word-per-dolphin. "Graceful," breathed Pua.

"Bouncy," breathed Dani.

Kai scrunched his fluffy face. "Cold. In a GOOD way!" Too many words; he sat out a turn.

---

Slowly, like syrup spreading, the bay relaxed. One grey back curved up. Then another.

The baby peeked out. The knot loosened. And a dolphin shot up and twirled four whole times.

Kai clapped his soft mittens together, very gently. "Bravo," he whispered.

"From here I see the WHOLE pod," said Kai. "Sometimes the best seat's a little farther back."

"Squeeze joy and it shrinks," said Pua. "Give it room and it grows big enough for everybody."

The littlest dolphin did one more belly-flop. SPLAT. Just to make them laugh. It worked.

---

The sun climbed. Three friends leaned together on the sand, full of a quiet, shared, easy happy.

"Chill out and rainbow on," Kai whispered to the bay. "You beautiful, twirling nai'a."

Kai's umbrella hat drifted back to shore all by itself, and the bay sparkled like it heard him.

---
