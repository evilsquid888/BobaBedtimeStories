# Story 41: Pua the Pineapple and the Field of Gold

**Main Character:** Pua the Pineapple
**Guest Characters:** Niu the Coconut, Dole Whip Dani, Keoni, a small lopsided field pineapple
**Setting:** A golden pineapple field at sunset on the Oahu plateau
**Theme:** We grow best when we grow together.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **lokahi** (loh-KAH-hee) = unity / harmony

---

Pua the Pineapple marched up the red-dirt road with her leaf-crown so high it poked a passing cloud.

The cloud, being polite, scooted over.

"Out of my sunbeam, please," Pua announced to nobody. "Royalty coming through!"

She was home for the harvest, with one wish: to be the tallest crown in the field.

Then she crested the hill and forgot to be regal. The whole field GLOWED.

Thousands of pineapples in tidy rows, crowns dipped in sunset honey. "Oh," whispered Pua. "Oh, my."

Niu the Coconut bounced up, thunk-thunk-thunk, hat tipped back. "Welcome home, your highness. Long road?"

"A queen takes no shortcuts," said Pua. "Also I got lost twice. But mostly the queen thing."

---

"Swirl on up here!" A golden curl of soft-serve came bouncing through the rows, melty at the edges.

"Pua! PUA!" cheered Dole Whip Dani. "It's the **mahalo** (mah-HAH-loh) = thank you harvest! The whole field bows together!"

"Then I shall be seen from the SEA," said Pua, crown fluffing. "The tallest. Above it all."

"Go say hello to the field first," said Niu. "Meet the neighbors."

---

Off she glided between the rows. The first pineapple was small and lopsided, crown leaning left.

"I'm the sweetest pineapple here," Pua declared. "Obviously."

"Probably!" agreed Keoni. "It's the **aina** (EYE-nah) = land. Best red dirt on the plateau. You're standing in it."

Pua looked down. The same dirt. The exact same dirt. "Hmph," said Pua.

"Lovely crown, by the way," Keoni added. "Same clouds rained on it as mine. We drink together!"

"We do NOT drink together," said Pua. "I drink alone. Regally."

---

The sun touched the sea. Dani waved everyone to the little rise. "Crowns up! Bow time!"

Pua hopped onto a rock. Taller, but not seen-from-the-sea taller. Not good enough.

She hopped onto Niu. "I'm a stool now," said Niu. "Great. Tough shell, flat head."

"Still not tall enough!" Pua wobbled on top. "Keoni! Underneath, please! Quickly! Royally!"

Keoni squeezed under Niu. A pineapple-coconut-pineapple tower, crown on top, swaying in the breeze.

"Stay cool and swirl on," said Dani nervously. "But maybe... don't swirl."

Then the whole field bowed toward the sun, and the tower bowed the other way.

It leaned. It teetered. "Crown UUUUP!" cried Pua, and over the edge they went.

---

Pua tumbled down the rise, crown over heels, leaves flapping, straight toward the rows.

And a hundred crowns rose to meet her, soft and leafy, and caught her like a net.

Crown to crown they passed her, gently, bouncing, all the way back up to the rise.

Niu lay in a heap with Keoni. "Stool, reporting," said Niu. "Slightly squashed."

Pua sat up, dizzy, leaves full of dirt. "You... caught me. All of you."

"Takes a whole field," said Keoni, crown leaning. "A pineapple alone in a pot never gets sweet."

"I thought I was sweet because I was special," said Pua quietly. "Because of my crown."

"Sweet is a team sport, sister," said Dani. "Same sun, same rain, same dirt."

---

Pua stood up. Not on anything. Just among them, crown level with every other crown.

"**Lokahi** (loh-KAH-hee) = unity / harmony," she said. "All the parts together. Even the royal ones."

And the whole field bowed to the last sliver of sun, Pua bowing with them.

As they rose, a thousand fireflies rose too, a second field of gold floating over the first.

"Crown up, sweetheart," Pua whispered to the glowing field. "We did this together."

---

Three friends sat on the rise under drifting fireflies. The polite cloud dropped a few happy raindrops.

"I came home to be the tallest," yawned Pua. "Turns out I'd rather be held."

"Still royal, though," said Niu. "Royally squashed me."

---
