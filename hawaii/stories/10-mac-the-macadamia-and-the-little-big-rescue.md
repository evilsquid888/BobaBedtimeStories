# Story 10: Mac the Macadamia and the Little Big Rescue

**Main Character:** Mac the Macadamia
**Guest Characters:** Momi the Mango, Mac's best friend; Pua the Pineapple; Pepe the big Papaya
**Setting:** A macadamia farm and rocky cliff trail above Waikiki
**Theme:** The smallest one can make the biggest difference.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **wikiwiki** (wee-kee-wee-kee) = quickly / fast
**Cameo:** Edward Elric (Fullmetal Alchemist) — hears "small" from the bushes, explodes, claps and raises a stone staircase straight up into a cloud, then sulks on the top step; walk-on: Waddle Dee (Kirby)

---

High above Waikiki, the trade winds smelled of roasted nuts and sunshine.

There lived the smallest snack on the farm: Mac, a nut the size of a thumbtip.

"Mac, you're TINY," boomed Pepe the big papaya, and bounced clean over him. WHUMP.

"Mac, I nearly sat on you!" gasped Pua the Pineapple, leaping sideways, crown wobbling.

Clik-clak. Mac snapped his shell shut like a helmet. "Small but mighty, shell to heart," he whispered. Mostly.

---

Today the whole farm buzzed about the **pā'ina** (PAH-ee-nah) = feast on the cliff lookout.

Mac peeked out through one crack. He wanted one thing: to be invited. Just once.

"Hop in my basket pocket, little buddy!" sang Momi the mango, golden as sunrise.

"We go **wikiwiki** (wee-kee-wee-kee) = quickly," said Momi. "Sunset waits for nobody!"

Mac glowed so warm his shell steamed. Clik. Open, just a peek. "Really?"

---

The trail climbed a green bluff above the sparkling sea. Picnic time, on a wide flat rock.

"A view worthy of my crown," declared Pua, leaning nowhere, because queens do not lean.

Mac sat on a smooth stone and felt, for once, exactly the right size.

"I can see a sea turtle from here!" called Momi, leaning out a teensy bit.

CRUMBLE. The edge gave way. Momi yelped, slid, and dropped out of sight.

---

She landed on a ledge no wider than a chopstick, high above the blue drop.

"I'm okay!" called Momi, wobbly as haupia. "I'll just live here now. Forever."

"I'll save her!" cried Pua, leaning over. Pebbles skittered. Her crown snagged a bush.

Pepe bounced to the edge. The edge said CRACK. Pepe bounced away very quickly.

The only way down was a twisty crack, thin as a straw. "Too SMALL for anybody!" wailed Pua.

---

"WHO'S SMALL?!" A short teen in a red coat, blond braid flying, burst from the bushes.

Edward Elric stomped to the edge, metal arm shaking. "I heard somebody say SMALL!"

"I'll show you small!" snapped Edward, clapping his palms and slapping the ground. Blue sparks crackled.

KRRRK. A stone staircase rose out of the cliff. Up. Straight up into a cloud, toward nothing.

The rumble shook the picnic rock. The haupia jiggled off the edge. Pua lunged. Pua missed.

Edward marched up to the top step and sat, arms folded, in the cloud. "I'm COMPACT," he huffed.

---

"I can do it," said a very small voice. Everyone turned. There stood Mac.

"Small," said Mac, and for once he said it like a plan. "Small, smooth, and round."

Clik-clak. Helmet shut. Mac hopped to the crack, took one breath, and let go.

Down he went, PING-ping-DOINK, through the twisty tunnel, screaming and laughing at the same time.

POP. He shot out the bottom and landed, clik-clak, beside Momi.

"Told you," he puffed, shell popping open. "Small but mighty."

---

A tough vine of **pōhuehue** (POH-hoo-eh-hoo-eh) = beach morning-glory dangled just past Momi's reach.

Mac's reach was just right. He gripped it in his shell-jaws and zipped up, wikiwiki, like a bead.

"Loop it round the big rock!" he hollered from the top. "Everybody! WIKIWIKI!"

Pepe looped. Pua cinched the knot with her crown-tip. Tight as a birthday bow.

"PULL!" Mac stood at the edge, tiny and loud. "Almost there, Momi!"

WHUMP. Momi tumbled onto the grass, golden, whole, and giggling.

She wrapped Mac in the squishiest mango hug in Waikiki history.

---

The sky melted pink, then peach, then mango-orange over the sea.

"He IS small," laughed Momi, squeezing him. "That's why he fit right where it mattered."

A small round orange creature with stubby feet waddled up, carrying a macadamia twice its size.

Waddle Dee set it down beside Mac and bowed. Next to it, Mac looked enormous.

Nobody called him teeny now. "Small but mighty," said Mac, loud for everyone, "shell to heart."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
Sleep now, snug as a nut in its shell. Being small is never being less. Sometimes the tiniest heart fits exactly where the biggest love is needed. You are mighty. You are just right. Goodnight.
