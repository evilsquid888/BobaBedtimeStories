# Story 7: The Whole Gang Meets Honu Haupia

**Main Character:** Honu Haupia
**Guest Characters:** Manapua Mano, Lilikoi Lani, Musubi Manu, and the whole gang (Kai the Shave Ice, Niu the Coconut, Mac the Macadamia) as a crowd of little treat-friends
**Setting:** A quiet tide pool and reef just off Waikiki at sunset
**Theme:** Elders carry the stories that guide us home.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **honu** (HOH-noo) = sea turtle
**Cameo:** Lapras (Pokémon) — surfaces in the tide pool, trades the slowest greeting in history with Honu, then bows and sloshes the whole pool out to sea; walk-on: Wooper (Pokémon)

---

On Waikiki beach, the sun melting orange behind Diamond Head, somebody lost a sandal.

"It was on my foot!" wailed Manapua Mano, hopping in circles, leaving steamy bun-prints.

"Snacks cannot adventure with one sandal," said Manapua. "It's a rule. Probably."

"It's in the tide pool, you steamy goofball!" said Lilikoi Lani, zipping off like a purple comet.

She peered in and squeaked so high two crabs covered their ears.

Something wobbly. Something white. Something dozing with the gentlest snore on the island.

---

The whole gang skidded up. Musubi Manu lined everyone up. "Formation, **'ohana** (oh-HAH-nah) = family. Quiet flippers."

A turtle made of **haupia** (how-PEE-ah) = coconut pudding, her shell jiggling in snowy squares.

"Aloha, little ones," she said slowly. "I am Honu Haupia. A **honu** (HOH-noo) = sea turtle."

She lifted one slow flipper. Balanced on top sat Manapua's runaway sandal.

"MY SANDAL!" Manapua bounced nearly over the reef. "She's my favorite turtle ever!"

"I am," said Honu, with a twinkle, "the only turtle you have ever met."

---

But Honu was stuck. The tide had slipped away and left her channel bumpy with stones.

"My flippers are slow," said Honu. "I will wait. The ocean remembers where it left me."

"High tide is hours away!" said Musubi. "Everybody PUSH!"

---

The whole gang leaned on Honu's wobbly shell, heaving with every arm and flipper.

Honu jiggled exactly nowhere. She giggled, and the giggle made waves that washed everyone back.

"Stop pushing," chuckled Honu. "You are tickling my dessert."

"Plan B!" said Musubi, snapping his backpack open. "Bucket brigade! We fill the channel!"

Shells of seawater flew down the line, mostly onto Manapua. The thirsty sand drank every drop.

"Plan C," said Manapua, soggy. "I'm the softest thing here. I'll be a bridge."

He flopped across the stones. Honu wobbled onto him. Manapua squished flat as a pancake.

"Mmmf," said the pancake. "Plan C has a flaw."

---

The tide pool bulged. Up rose Lapras: long blue neck, knobby grey shell, enormous kind eyes.

Honu turned her head toward Lapras. Slowly. Sooo slowly. "Aaa...looo...haaa," said Honu.

Lapras lowered its great head. Slower still. It sang one long, low, musical note.

"They're GREETING," squeaked Lilikoi, bouncing like popcorn. "We'll be here till NEXT sunset!"

Lapras bowed to Honu. The bow sloshed every drop of the pool straight out to sea.

Lapras sang one last soft note and sank until only two kind eyes showed.

"Even the puddle left," said Musubi, wringing out his nori. "There is no Plan D."

---

Then Honu laughed, warm and deep as the sea.

"Oh, my dears," said Honu. "You keep pushing ME. You should listen to HER."

She tipped her head seaward. "Sit. Be still. Old turtles know a secret hurrying snacks forget."

So they sat in a row on the warm stones, Manapua hugging his sandal, pushing nothing.

---

Honu told how the islands were born from fire and patience, wave by slow wave.

"Hurrying snacks shout over the sea's whispers," said Honu. "Still ones hear the whole ocean."

While she spoke, a whisper of water slipped over the stones at the channel's mouth.

Then more. Then a sighing rush, as the patient ocean came back for what it left.

A tiny blue Wooper rode the ripple in, flopped onto Honu's shell, and grinned. "Woop!"

"It's EARLY!" gasped Lilikoi. "Did you CALL it?"

"I told it a story," said Honu. "The sea loves a story. So do little ones."

---

Under the first stars, the gang waded into the warm shallows beside her slow flippers.

Manapua told a long sandal story. Everyone agreed it was the best ever. The only one.

At the reef's edge Honu paused. "Slow flippers, far journeys," she said, sinking like a white moon.

"We have an elder now," whispered Musubi, wrapping his nori tighter. "The adventures are just beginning."
