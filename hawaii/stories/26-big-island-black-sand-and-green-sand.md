# Story 26: Big Island, Black Sand and Green Sand

**Main Character:** Kai the Shave Ice
**Guest Characters:** Honu Haupia, Lilikoi Lani
**Setting:** The black-sand and green-sand beaches of the Big Island
**Theme:** Different doesn't mean less — it means special.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **one** (OH-neh) = sand

---

Kai the Shave Ice hopped off the canoe onto the Big Island, looked down, and yelped.

"WHOA. The **one** (OH-neh) = sand is BLACK! Somebody spilled the night all over the daytime!"

He poked it with a drippy toe. Black. He scooped some. Black, black, sparkly black.

"The land made that one, little wave," said Honu Haupia, gliding up the beach like she was underwater.

"Warm lava meets cool sea and shatters into shiny black grains," said Honu. "Black one is baby one."

"Hi, baby beach!" Kai waved at the ground. The sand twinkled back, warm as a cozy heartbeat.

---

A purple blur zipped across the dark sand, kicking up sparkly black puffs.

"BLACK BEACH!" squealed Lilikoi Lani. "I dare anybody to find a MORE dramatic beach. Impossible. I win."

"I stowed away in Honu's shell wrinkle," said Lani proudly. "Also, rumor: this island has a GREEN beach."

Kai laughed so hard a strawberry stripe wiggled down his side. "Green sand? Silliest thing I ever heard."

"It's true," rumbled Honu. "Papakolea. Far down the island, past the cliffs, where the wind walks."

"Green like GREEN green?" said Kai. "I have to see it. Chill out and rainbow ON!"

---

Black sand gave way to red dirt, red dirt to golden grass, and the wind got bolder.

WHOOSH. The wind snatched Kai's paper umbrella clean off his head.

"MY HAT!" Kai lunged. The hat skipped away across the grass like a startled crab.

Lani zipped after it, got picked up by a gust, and sailed fifteen feet sideways. "WHEEEEE!"

"Does the wind always grab everybody's hats?" panted Kai.

"Only the fashionable ones," called Lani, drifting past backward.

The hat tumbled toward the cliff edge. Honu raised one slow flipper. The hat landed on it. Plop.

---

They climbed down into a great green bowl of cliffs, like a giant scoop had bitten the land.

Kai froze. Far below, kissed by foamy waves, the beach was GREEN. Sparkling, olive-and-emerald, jewel-dusted GREEN.

"It's..." Kai's voice came out very small for such a tall dome of ice. "It's beautiful."

"Olivine crystals," said Honu. "Born in the volcano, tougher than black sand. This beach is made of survivors."

"So the green is the part TOO TOUGH to leave?" said Lani, balancing one grain on a seed.

---

Kai looked at the green. Then back up the path toward the black. Then down at his stripes.

He plopped down and let the green sand pour over his drippy toes, no rainbow in sight, grinning.

"I thought rainbow was the only way to be pretty," said Kai. "The island does EVERY color."

"Different doesn't mean less," Kai declared. "It means SPECIAL!"

"FINALLY," said Lani, flopping into the sand. "I'm purple AND orange. I'm basically a walking lesson."

---

They buried Lani up to her wrinkly chin until only her orange-seed grin showed.

"I'm a GEM now!" she hollered, popping out. "Bow before me!" Kai nearly tipped out of his cone.

Honu drew slow flipper-turtles in the sand. The waves wiped them away. She drew them again, perfectly happy.

"The sea isn't taking my pictures," said Honu. "It's asking me to draw more."

Then the sun sank, and the sky went lilikoi-gold, strawberry-pink and blue-coconut blue.

"Honu! The SKY!" gasped Kai. "It's wearing MY stripes!"

"The sky has always been a shave ice," said Honu. "It waits for sunset to show its syrups."

---

That night, back on the warm black sand, Lani tucked herself into a wrinkle of Honu's shell.

Stars came out by the thousands, and the black sand sparkled back until beach and sky were one.

"Goodnight, black sand," murmured Kai, melting a little. "Goodnight, every color I haven't met yet."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
The world makes beauty in every color: black as starlight, green as the deep, and bright as your own true heart. You are not less for being different; you are special because of it. Snuggle down soft and safe. Goodnight.
