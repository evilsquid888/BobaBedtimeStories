# Story 37: How the Island Was Born

**Main Character:** Pele's Glow (Lava the gentle ember)
**Guest Characters:** Poi Popo, Mac the Macadamia
**Setting:** A cooling lava field stretching to the sea on the Big Island
**Theme:** New beginnings can rise from anything.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **aina** (EYE-nah) = land

---

On the Big Island, under a soft night sky, a brand-new field of black rock lay cooling.

Across it hopped a teacup-sized ember, pip-pip-pip, from one warm stone to the next.

"Whee! This one's still toasty!" chirped Pele's Glow, and bonked straight into a pebble.

He bounced off in a puff of giggling sparks. "Fourth time tonight! That pebble loves me."

Behind him, Mac the Macadamia peeked from his glossy shell. "So empty. Rock. More rock. Oop, more rock."

"I'd just like one green thing," sighed Mac. "One leaf. Then I'd believe this is a place."

"Empty?" Pele's Glow flared bright gold. "Keiki, this is the beginning of everything!"

---

Along came Poi Popo, gliding like a lavender cloud with nowhere particular to be, lei swaying.

"Popo, tell him!" said Pele's Glow. "This whole **aina** (EYE-nah) = land used to be ME."

"Rivers of glow poured down from the mountain," said Pele's Glow, "and then they got sleepy."

"When I get sleepy, I become an island," he added. "When Popo gets sleepy, she becomes a nap."

---

A breeze fluttered across the field, carrying a speck, a wisp, a teeny floating thread.

"A seed!" cried Pele's Glow. "The bravest traveler in the world! Mac, catch it. Gently!"

Mac cupped his little arms. The seed drifted down, down... and the breeze snatched it sideways.

"My green thing!" squeaked Mac, and he hopped after it, shell clacking.

The seed skipped over a warm ridge. Mac hopped up the ridge, tripped on THE pebble, and tumbled.

---

Pele's Glow puffed a warm breath to blow the seed back. He puffed a bit too hard.

The seed shot over a cracked old hill, straight toward a slow lavender cloud.

Poi Popo caught it in her lei. "Got it, keiki! Slow and steady wins the..."

Her nose twitched. "Ah... ah... AH-CHOO!" The seed went sailing toward the sea.

---

At the edge of the field, the rock ended, and the sea began, and OH.

Lava poured into the water, orange rivers hissing into silver steam, building new ground.

"That's my family," said Pele's Glow softly. "New land, new light, every night."

---

The seed tumbled through the steam, spinning toward the waves. Mac stood at the very edge.

Mac took one breath. Then two. Then he popped ALL the way out of his shell.

He leaped, flipped his shell open like a cup, and plip! The seed landed inside.

He landed on a brand-new rock, still warm as toast. "Small but mighty. Shell to heart."

"That rock is one minute old!" cheered Pele's Glow. "You're its first guest! Say something nice."

Mac looked down, bashful. "Um. Hello, land. You're very flat. And solid. Good job."

---

Back on the field, Pele's Glow breathed the softest warmth onto a little crack full of dust.

"Right there," he said. "The new aina already made a cozy pocket for it."

Mac hesitated. "What if it doesn't grow? I'm so small and the field is so big."

"Before this rock, fire. Before fire, a mountain. Before the mountain, the sea," said Poi Popo.

"Everything beautiful started as something that looked like nothing at all," she said. "Even you."

Mac tucked the seed in and patted a pinch of dust over it. "Sleep tight, little seed."

"Now the hardest ingredient," said Pele's Glow, glowing so proud the crack lit gold. "Patience."

---

"Years?" squeaked Mac. "I won't even get to see it!"

"Nobody who builds an island sees it finished," said Pele's Glow. "Add your piece. Trust tomorrow."

The three friends settled on the newest land in the world and watched the stars wheel slowly.

Mac yawned, shell wide open, hiding from nothing at all. "I think I'm part of the story now."

"Oh, keiki," whispered Pele's Glow, the softest gold. "You always were."

---
