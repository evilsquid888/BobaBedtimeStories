# Story 43: Dole Whip Dani and the Melting Day

**Main Character:** Dole Whip Dani
**Guest Characters:** Kai the Shave Ice, Niu the Coconut, Moki, a cheeky myna bird
**Setting:** The hottest sunny afternoon of the year in Waikiki
**Theme:** Asking for help is brave, not weak.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **lā** (LAH) = sun / day

---

The hottest **lā** (LAH) = sun / day of the whole year arrived in Waikiki at exactly lunchtime.

It arrived like a golden bully who'd forgotten his manners. The sand hissed.

A seagull tried to land, squawked, and flapped straight back up to hover like a feathery helicopter.

On the sunniest corner stood Dole Whip Dani, golden swirl gleaming, sparkly eyes sparkling a little TOO much.

"Aloha, beautiful lā!" she sang. "Sunniest treat in Waikiki, reporting! Stay cool and swirl on!"

"That sunny bench," said Dani, pointing. "Mine. ALL afternoon. Right in the middle of the lā."

A golden drip slid down her side. Plip. Dani pretended not to see it.

---

Niu the Coconut bounced past, sloshing pleasantly, and stopped so fast his hat slid over one eye.

"Dani," said Niu. "You're dripping."

"I'm GLOWING!" said Dani. Her glossy curl drooped sideways like a question mark.

"That's not a glow," said Niu. "That's a puddle starting. Come sit in the shade."

"Shade is for treats who can't handle a little lā!" said Dani. "I'm fine! I'm GREAT! I'm..."

SPLORP. Her whole left side slid off and landed on the sidewalk with a sad little splat.

"...I meant to do that," said Dani. "It's a new dance move. The Sunshine Shimmy."

"Dani. Sweetheart," said Niu. "The Sunshine Shimmy is just melting with extra steps."

---

She scooped her side back on, plopped it, and set off for the bench at top wobble.

Shade! A beach umbrella! She ducked under. A gust lifted it away down the beach like a kite.

Shade! A surfboard on a rack! She leaned under. The board slid off and surfed away.

At the shave-ice stand, a myna bird hopped close, sniffed her, and announced, "Ooh! Smoothie!"

"I'm not a smoothie," huffed Dani, wobbling on. "I'm a MASTERPIECE."

"You're a puddle with eyebrows," chirped Moki, and flew off before she could argue.

Behind her, a golden snail-trail. Her peak was a pancake. Her eyes slid toward her chin.

---

A cool shadow fell over her. "Whoa, sister," said Kai the Shave Ice. "You're leaking onto the avenue."

"Kaiii," slurred Dani, sagging toward him. "So cold and lovely. Why are there three of you?"

"There's one of me," said Kai gently. "Chill out and rainbow on. Lean on me. I got you."

"I do NOT need help," said Dani, peeling herself upright. "I am a strong, independent soft-serve and I..."

A golden glob slid off into the gutter. Niu bounced up, puffing, following her trail.

Dani felt small, and scared, and so hot. A tiny voice squeaked: "Kai? Niu? I need help. Please."

---

They MOVED. "Niu, the hat!" called Kai, catching it and holding it over Dani like a parasol.

"I'm the wheels!" said Niu, hopping close so she could lean on his cool hard shell.

"And I'm the AC," said Kai, pressing his frosty side to her back. "Banyan tree. Let's go."

Hop-bounce-shuffle. Halfway there, a rainbow stripe ran down Kai's side. Then two. Then three.

"Uh," said Kai. "Rainbow's running. Niu? I need help too. Quite a lot, actually."

"Everybody lean on the coconut!" cried Niu, and they bundled the last twenty hops like one wobbly sandwich.

---

Under the banyan, the world went cool and green, like stepping inside a giant leafy refrigerator.

Niu dribbled cold coconut water over Dani's swirl. Kai fanned her with his paper umbrella.

Slowly, her peak rose. Her curl curled. Kai's stripes firmed up. "I can FEEL my swirl!" said Dani.

"I thought asking for help meant I wasn't strong," said Dani, cheeks going rosy.

"Anyone can pretend they're fine," said Kai. "Only the strong ones say please."

"I've still got water left," said Niu, sloshing. "Who wants a sip?"

"Stay cool," sighed Dani, leaning on Niu's shell as the lā softened, "and swirl on. Together."

---

## Goodnight Blessing
Little dreamer, the lā can shine bright, but you don't have to hold up the whole sky. Asking for help is letting your ohana be strong with you. Snuggle into your blankets and let someone else hold the hat. Goodnight.
