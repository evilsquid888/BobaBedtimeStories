# Story 6: Niu the Coconut and the Tough Shell

**Main Character:** Niu the Coconut
**Guest Characters:** None
**Setting:** A swaying coconut palm grove above Waikiki
**Theme:** It's okay to let people see your soft, sweet inside.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **keiki** (KAY-kee) = child / little one

---

High above Waikiki, where the palm trees lean over like nosy aunties trying to peek at the ocean, lived the toughest little coconut in the whole grove. His name was Niu, and he was very proud of his shell.

"Knock on it," he told everybody. "Go on. Knock!"

So they knocked. The geckos knocked. The mynah birds knocked with their beaks. Even the wind tried, whooshing right up the trunk to give him a good rattle.

*Thock. Thock. Thock.*

"Hear that?" Niu said, beaming with all three of his little face-dots. "Solid as a stone! Tough shell, sweet heart — but you'll only ever get the tough shell part. The sweet heart stays mine."

He adjusted his tiny woven hat, which he wore at a very dignified angle, and gave his straw-sprout a proud little wiggle.

Nobody in the grove had ever heard Niu slosh. And Niu liked it exactly that way.

---

The thing about Niu was this: he was full of the coolest, sweetest, most refreshing water on the whole hill. Cold like a mountain stream. Sweet like a secret. But not one single soul had ever tasted a drop of it, because Niu had decided, a long time ago, that opening up was for soft coconuts.

"Once you crack," he liked to say, tucking his sprout down low, "everybody knows your insides. And then where's your mystery?"

The other grove keiki thought this was a little silly. Lani the Loco Moco — well, no, Lani wasn't here, but you know the type — anybody who knew Niu thought it was silly. But Niu didn't mind silly. Silly bounced right off his shell, *thock, thock,* and rolled away down the hill.

One especially warm afternoon, the sun was being a real show-off. It blazed and baked and turned the grove into a giant warm oven. The trade winds went on their lunch break. Even the ocean below looked too lazy to make proper waves.

That was when Niu heard the crying.

It was a tiny sound. A wobbly, hiccupy, melty little sound coming from the grass at the bottom of his palm tree. Niu peeked down with all three dots.

There, in a puddle of her own making, sat the smallest, sun-droopiest little snack he had ever seen.

She was golden-yellow and swirly, with a curl on top that had gone all sad and floppy, like a slide melting in the heat. A tiny pineapple-leaf tuft drooped over her huge, sparkly — and very teary — eyes. She was sitting in a little cup-body, and the edges of her were *definitely* melting.

"Oh no," whispered the little snack. "Oh no, oh no. I'm a puddle. I'm becoming a puddle."

"You're Dole Whip," Niu said, because he was the kind of coconut who liked to know things and announce them. "Pineapple soft-serve. You're a long way up the hill, keiki."

"I know!" she wailed, and a swirl of her drooped another sad inch. "I'm Dani! I rolled out of my cup at the beach stand and I bounced and I bounced and I — *hic* — I bounced all the way up here and now it's so HOT and I'm so MELTY and I just need something cold, something cool, just one little cold thing, or I'm going to turn into pineapple soup!"

Niu's tough shell went very still.

Because Niu knew — he absolutely knew — what one little cold thing she needed.

He had it. Inside him. Sloshing quietly where nobody had ever heard it. The coldest, sweetest water on the whole hill.

All he had to do was crack open and let her see it.

---

Niu the Coconut had never, ever cracked open for anybody.

His sprout went stiff. His face-dots went wide. A whole storm of thoughts whooshed around inside him like trade winds finally coming back from lunch.

*If I crack open, everyone will know what's inside me. They'll see my soft part. They'll see I'm not all shell. And then* — and here Niu's thoughts got very dramatic — *then I'll just be... a regular coconut.*

"Please," whispered Dani. Her sparkly eyes had gone dim, like a sunset deciding whether to set. "I don't want to be soup. Soup is a fine thing to be on PURPOSE. But I want to be a Dole Whip a little longer."

And that did it.

Niu looked at this brave, melty, far-from-home little keiki, who had bounced up a whole hill and was trying so hard not to cry more puddle, and something in his tough shell went *soft.*

"Stand back, keiki," Niu said. His voice came out wobblier than he meant it to. "And — don't laugh at the sound. It's a very dignified sound."

Then Niu the Coconut, for the first time in his entire life, let go of his pride. He leaned off his branch. He took a deep breath through his straw-sprout. And he let himself *fall.*

Down, down, down he tumbled, *whump-bump-bump,* bouncing off three branches and a sleeping gecko (who said "Eh!" but went right back to sleep), and landed right in the soft grass beside Dani with a tremendous —

*CRACK.*

His shell split open at the top. And out of him came a sound that the whole grove had waited his whole life to hear.

*Sloooosh.*

Cold, sweet coconut water bubbled up to the very top of him, glittering in the mean hot sun like a little spring.

"Drink," Niu said, going pink behind his dots. "Quick, before I'm embarrassed about it."

---

Dani didn't need telling twice. She leaned her melty self right over the crack and *sipped.*

You should have seen her.

The cold sweet water went into her like aloha goes into a hug. Her droopy curl perked straight up — *boing!* Her sad swirls firmed up, swirl by swirl. Her huge eyes went from dim sunset back to bright, sparkly noon. Her pineapple-leaf tuft stood up tall and waved like a little flag.

"Ohhh," she breathed. "Oh, that's the GOOD cold. That's the secret cold!" She bounced — actually bounced, *sproing!* — back up to her full swirly height. "Niu! You taste like — like the inside of the whole island being kind to me!"

"Well," said Niu, who did not know where to put all this attention. "Yes. I suppose I do."

"You said your sweet heart stays yours!" Dani laughed, doing a happy little spin in her cup. "You big silly coconut! Your sweet heart is the best thing about you!"

And here is the part Niu did not expect.

He had thought that cracking open would make him feel small. Like a secret let out. Like a regular old coconut.

Instead, he felt — *bigger.* Lighter. Warmer, somehow, even though all his cool water had gone out. He had spent his whole life being a hard shell with a hidden heart, and it turned out the heart felt much, much better when it got to do its job.

"Stay cool and swirl on!" cheered Dani, which was apparently her catchphrase, and she said it so happily that two passing mynah birds clapped their wings.

"Tough shell," Niu said slowly, trying out his own catchphrase like it was a brand new pair of slippers, "...sweet heart. And it turns out — the sweet heart's the part worth sharing."

---

By evening, the whole grove had heard the story. Not the *thock-thock* anymore — they'd heard the *slooosh.* And one by one, the geckos and the mynah birds and even the grumpy old wind came by, not to knock on Niu's shell, but to sit beside him in the cooling grass and watch the sun melt gold into the sea over Waikiki.

Dani stayed close, all firmed-up and happy, leaning her cool swirl against Niu's cracked shell like he was the comfiest porch on the hill.

"You know," she whispered, as the first stars blinked on, "you didn't lose your mystery."

"No?" said Niu.

"Nope. Now everybody knows the BEST mystery of all." She grinned a melty grin. "That the toughest little coconut up here is the sweetest one too."

Niu the Coconut looked out at his swaying palm grove, at his ocean, at his brave new far-from-home friend, and at his own open shell — which didn't feel broken at all. It felt *shared.*

And for the first time, he didn't tuck his sprout down low. He let it stand up tall, right out in the open, where everyone could see.

## Goodnight Blessing
Little keiki, you have a tough shell some days, and that is just fine — shells keep us safe and snug. But never forget the sweet, cool, wonderful water tucked inside you, ready to help someone on a too-hot day. Sharing your heart doesn't empty you out; it fills the whole world up. So rest easy, brave one, soft inside and strong outside, knowing you are exactly enough. Slosh gently. Dream sweetly. Goodnight.
