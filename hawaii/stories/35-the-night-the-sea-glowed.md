# Story 35: The Night the Sea Glowed

**Main Character:** Lilikoi Lani
**Guest Characters:** Kai the Shave Ice; Dole Whip Dani; Kimo, a sand crab with a drawn-on mustache; a hermit crab who has seen a lot
**Setting:** A dark beach at night where the waves sparkle with bioluminescence
**Theme:** The dark can hold the most beautiful surprises.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **pō** (POH) = night / darkness

---

A moonless **pō** (POH) = night on a quiet beach. The sea lay black and still, barely whispering.

Lilikoi Lani had pranked everything that could be pranked.

She had drawn a teeny mustache on Kimo the dozing crab, who saw his reflection and loved it.

She had jumped out at a hermit crab. "BOO!"

"Boo to you too," said the hermit crab, who had seen a lot.

Lilikoi flopped down and groaned the biggest groan a passion fruit can groan.

"It's so DARK," she said. "No colors. No tricks. The pō is officially the most boring time."

---

Kai the Shave Ice flopped down beside her, his syrup stripes just visible under the stars.

"The pō's not boring, brah," said Kai. "It's quieter. You listen for the fun."

"You can't HEAR a prank, Kai," said Lilikoi, flicking sand at him.

Dole Whip Dani came swirling down the beach. "You can hear my tummy! It says, why midnight?"

"Boredom," announced Dani, "is a fun-shaped hole waiting for FUN!" She squinted into the dark.

"Okay," said Dani. "The dark is a LITTLE dark."

"Fine," said Lilikoi, hopping up, sparkle dangerous. "If the night won't be fun, I'll MAKE it."

---

She zipped toward the black water, tangy scent trail streaming. "The wet-surprise! Nobody expects the wet-surprise!"

"We literally always expect the wet-surprise," sighed Kai.

Lilikoi tripped over Kimo, somersaulted, and landed at the edge of the dark sea.

She wound back one little arm and SMACKED the water with all her zesty might.

---

The sea lit up. Blue sparkles burst into the dark and pattered back into waves that glowed too.

Lilikoi froze. Her mischievous sparkle went very, very wide. "...Whoa," she breathed.

"It GLOWS!" squealed Dani, swirling so fast her curl nearly unswirled. "Do it AGAIN!"

"Sea-sparks, brah," whispered Kai. "Itty-bitty ocean creatures. When the water moves, they light up. Millions."

"They only shine in the deepest pō," said Kai. "No moon at all. Daytime, you'd never know."

---

"GLOW TAG!" shouted Lilikoi, and splashed Dani with a wave of blue light.

Lilikoi drew a wobbly turtle in the water with her arm. The sea held it three whole seconds.

Dani drew a lopsided pineapple. Kai drew a very slow wave. "It's a surfboard," said Kai. "Trust me."

Lilikoi ambushed Kai from behind a rock. SPLOOSH. He dripped glowing blue from spoon to cone.

---

Then Lilikoi's eyes went wide with a NEW idea. A kind one. "Dani, float right HERE. Hold still."

Lilikoi zipped in big loops around her friend, slapping and swirling the water as fast as she could.

A glowing blue ring spread out around Dani, round and bright, a crown made of starlight.

"A CROWN!" squealed Dani. "Pua would be SO jealous. Don't tell her. Actually DO. No, DON'T."

"Crown up, sweetheart," giggled Lilikoi, in her best dramatic-pineapple voice.

---

Kai floated on his back, rainbow stripes mixing with electric blue, a sunrise lost in the night.

"Chill out and rainbow on," said Kai. "The pō's got its own kine rainbow. You just gotta wait."

Lilikoi flopped back into the shallows, and the whole sea bloomed blue around her, hugging her in light.

Sparkles caught in every wrinkle, till the grumpy prankster looked like a fruit made of **hōkū** (HOH-koo) = stars.

"The dark doesn't hide the pretty stuff," said Lilikoi softly. "The dark is what SHOWS it."

---

They stayed till the sky softened and the blue faded into gentle grey.

"Same time tomorrow night?" asked Dani, shaking sparkles off her curl.

"Only if there's no moon," said Kai. "Darkest pō. That's the secret."

Lilikoi left one last glowing arm-print on the water. "Sour first," she yawned. "Sweet always."

"Also, Kai," said Lilikoi, "there's a sea-sparkle in your umbrella hat. Three seconds."

"...Aw, Lani," said Kai, and the glowing tide laughed along.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
Little one, when your room goes dark, remember the glowing sea. The pō is a soft blanket full of quiet wonders for those who wait and look closely. Let the dark hold you, like the sea holds the light. Goodnight.
