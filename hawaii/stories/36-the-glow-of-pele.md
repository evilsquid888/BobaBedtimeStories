# Story 36: The Glow of Pele

**Main Character:** Pele's Glow (Lava the gentle ember)
**Guest Characters:** Honu Haupia; Dole Whip Dani
**Setting:** The warm, glowing edges of Kilauea volcano on the Big Island
**Theme:** Even powerful things can be gentle and welcoming.
**Hawaiian Spotlight:** **pele** (PEH-leh) = volcano (and the goddess of fire)

---

A starry night, and a dark trail winding up a warm Big Island mountain.

Up it bounced Dole Whip Dani, a little melty lantern, dripping onto her own toes.

"Whoopsie-swirl!" she giggled, scooping a golden drip back onto her peak. "I am COMPLETELY in control."

She was not completely in control of her drips.

Behind her glided Honu Haupia, her shell shimmering in the starlight like a tray of tiny moons.

"Tonight, far journeys go UP," hummed Honu. "Up the warm side of the **pele** (PEH-leh) = volcano."

"A VOLCANO?" gasped Dani. "Is it gonna ROAR? Are we gonna be toasted pineapple?"

"We are going to make a friend," said Honu.

---

Higher up, the air grew cozy-warm, like a kitchen with something delicious baking.

Dani peeked over a ledge. Far below, something shimmered orange-gold, a sleepy sunset living underground.

"That," whispered Dani, "is the most beautiful melty thing ever, and I am MADE of melty things."

"Aloha, little travelers," crackled a tiny warm voice. "You came all this way just to say hello?"

Dani shrieked and backflipped directly into Honu's shell.

Up from a glowing crack floated a little ember, teacup-small, twinkling like a wandered-down star.

"I'm Pele's Glow," it said. "The smallest piece of the mountain's fire. I never burn a friend."

---

One sparkly eye peeked out of the shell. One drippy soft-serve arm stretched toward the ember.

Hug warm. The kind that wraps round your middle.

"Oh," breathed Dani. "You're a blanket made of sunset."

"Nicest thing anyone's said all week," said the Glow. "Mostly everyone runs away yelling."

"From YOU?" said Dani, arms on cup-hips. "You're the size of my breakfast! Who's scared of breakfast?"

---

The Glow led them to fresh black rock, so new it ticked and pinged like a cookie sheet.

"This was liquid fire yesterday," said the Glow. "Today it's land. You're the first to stand here."

Dani patted the rock softly. "Hello, baby island. You're gonna grow up to have shave ice stands."

---

The Glow rose and brightened, and its light spilled across the whole dark mountainside.

Every cove, cliff and beach below had once flowed warm and orange out of this mountain.

"Drop by drop, the **ʻāina** (AH-ee-nah) = land grows," said the Glow. "New land, new light, every night."

"I learned the biggest thing ever," whispered Dani, "from a piece of fire the size of a CUPCAKE."

"I prefer 'majestic,'" said the Glow.

"Majestic cupcake," Dani agreed solemnly.

---

Then: plip, plip, plip. All that warmth and bouncing had done Dani in.

"Oh no oh swirl!" cried Dani, soft-serve sliding off her peak. "I'm gonna be Dole SOUP Dani!"

She scooped. It dripped. She fanned herself with her leaf tuft. It dripped faster.

Honu fanned with both flippers. Her own shell wobbled softer. "I am also a dessert," she said.

Dani slid downhill on her own drips, a slow golden toboggan, toward the warm glowing crack.

Honu stretched one slow flipper and caught her by the cup. Just.

Then Pele's Glow did the cleverest thing. It dimmed itself down, soft as a nightlight.

It floated to a cooled ledge where a breeze came up off the dark sea. "Here. My shade-side."

---

Dani wobbled over. The sea breeze hit her, cool as a kiss. Her curl firmed right back up.

"A FIRE saved me from melting," gasped Dani. "Silliest, wonderfulest thing ever. Stay cool and swirl on!"

"Powerful doesn't mean unkind," said the Glow. "The strongest thing on the island is also the warmest welcome."

---

They stayed till the sky softened pink, tucked on warm new rock, the Glow flickering low between them.

"Lonely? Never," said the Glow. "Every beach your **ʻohana** (oh-HAH-nah) = family naps on says hello from me."

Honu hummed an old ocean song. Dani dozed, perfectly cool. The little fire kept on building.

---

## Goodnight Blessing
