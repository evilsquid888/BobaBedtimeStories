# Hawaii — Character Guide

New & notable characters in the Hawaii bedtime-story collection.

### Dole Whip Dani — Dole Whip (pineapple soft-serve)
- **Catchphrase:** "Stay cool and swirl on!"
- **Appearance:** A swirly golden-yellow soft-serve peak with a glossy curl on top, set in a little cup-body. Sun-bright, slightly melty at the edges on hot days, with a tiny pineapple-leaf tuft for hair and huge sparkly eyes.
- **Voice:** Sunny, bubbly, fast and warm. Turns every problem into a fun plan. Lots of joyful exclamations.

### Kai the Shave Ice — Rainbow shave ice (with lilikoi, blue coconut, and strawberry syrup)
- **Catchphrase:** "Chill out and rainbow on!"
- **Appearance:** A tall fluffy dome of snow-white ice drenched in rainbow stripes of syrup, sitting in a paper cone. A drippy, sparkly mountain with a paper-umbrella hat and a tiny spoon tucked behind one ear. Glistens like fresh snow on Mauna Kea.
- **Voice:** Mellow, cool, surfer-calm with sudden bursts of excitement. Loves describing colors and flavors.

### Malia the Malasada — Malasada (Portuguese sugar doughnut)
- **Catchphrase:** "Fresh, warm, and never alone!"
- **Appearance:** A plump, golden-brown puffy doughnut rolled in sparkly sugar crystals that fall off when she giggles. Warm and squishy, with a lilikoi-custard heart that peeks out when she smiles. Rosy sugar-dusted cheeks.
- **Voice:** Warm, giggly, generous, a little messy. Always offering bites and hugs. Bakery-grandma sweetness in a young, peppy voice.

### Musubi Manu — Spam musubi (rice + spam wrapped in nori)
- **Catchphrase:** "Wrapped up and ready!"
- **Appearance:** A neat rice-block body with a pink spam blanket on top and a crisp black nori seatbelt around the middle. Square, sturdy, and tidy, with a surfer's tan line where the nori wraps. Carries a tiny bento backpack.
- **Voice:** Loyal, dependable, brave, a bit of a planner. Practical jokes hidden under a tidy exterior. The trusty best friend.

### Poke Pua — Poke bowl (ahi tuna over rice with seaweed and sesame)
- **Catchphrase:** "Fresh from the sea, that's me!"
- **Appearance:** A wide rice-bowl body brimming with glistening ruby ahi cubes, green seaweed ribbons, edamame freckles, and a sprinkle of sesame stars. Fresh and ocean-cool, with kelp-strand hair and a confident grin.
- **Voice:** Fresh, confident, a little artsy — arranges everything 'just so.' Quick with a clever idea. Ocean-proud.

### Kalua the Pig — Kalua roast pig (luau pit-roasted pork)
- **Catchphrase:** "Low and slow, that's how love grows!"
- **Appearance:** A big, soft, smoky-golden roly-poly pig made of tender pulled pork, wrapped in ti leaves like a cozy blanket. Gentle giant with sleepy eyes, a curl of fragrant smoke rising from his back, and the warmest belly-laugh on the island.
- **Voice:** Big, slow, jolly, deeply kind. Speaks in cozy rumbles. The heart of every luau. Loves family above all.

### Honu Haupia — Haupia (coconut pudding) shaped like a sea turtle
- **Catchphrase:** "Slow flippers, far journeys."
- **Appearance:** A wobbly, snow-white coconut-pudding sea turtle with a softly jiggling shell patterned like haupia squares. Gentle ancient eyes, slow flippers, and a faint coconut shimmer. Glides like she's always underwater.
- **Voice:** Calm, wise, ancient and gentle. Speaks slowly with ocean metaphors. The elder guide of the group.

### Niu the Coconut — Coconut (whole drinking coconut)
- **Catchphrase:** "Tough shell, sweet heart!"
- **Appearance:** A round brown shaggy coconut with a green drinking-straw sprout on top and three little face-dots like a real coconut. Hard shell, soft sweet heart, always sloshing with cool water inside. Wears a tiny woven hat.
- **Voice:** Steady, grounded, a bit of a know-it-all who means well. Tough shell, soft center. Brave when it counts.

### Pua the Pineapple — Pineapple
- **Catchphrase:** "Crown up, sweetheart!"
- **Appearance:** A golden spiky pineapple with a proud green leaf-crown she treats like a royal headdress, diamond-pattern skin, and a sweet juicy sparkle. Stands tall and regal, sometimes a little vain about her crown.
- **Voice:** Proud, regal, generous once you know her, secretly insecure under the crown. Learns kindness over the arc. Dramatic flair.

### Lilikoi Lani — Lilikoi (passion fruit)
- **Catchphrase:** "Sour first, sweet always!"
- **Appearance:** A small round purple-wrinkly fruit who cracks open to a burst of bright tangy-orange seeds. Mischievous sparkle, zippy and zesty, leaves a tangy-sweet scent trail. Tiny and quick.
- **Voice:** Cheeky, zesty, surprise-loving prankster with a sweet streak. Fast-talking, dares everyone, kind underneath.

### Mac the Macadamia — Macadamia nut
- **Catchphrase:** "Small but mighty, shell to heart!"
- **Appearance:** A small, smooth, round cream-colored nut in a glossy shell he can pop open and shut like a helmet. Tiny but tough, a little shy, surprisingly strong. Often half-hidden in his shell.
- **Voice:** Quiet, shy, thoughtful, brave in a pinch. Short sentences. The underdog who proves small things matter.

### Poi Popo — Poi (taro paste) — an elder
- **Catchphrase:** "From the land, with aloha."
- **Appearance:** A soft, smooth, lavender-grey mound of pounded taro, gentle and old as the islands. Wears a flower lei and moves like a slow purple cloud. Smells of earth and aloha. Grandparent of the group.
- **Voice:** Ancient, loving, wise grandparent. Tells old island stories, speaks of aloha aina (love of the land). Endlessly patient.

### Manapua Mano — Manapua (steamed pork bun)
- **Catchphrase:** "Soft on the outside, full of love inside!"
- **Appearance:** A round, soft, pillowy white steamed bun with a rosy steam-blush, a sweet pork heart inside, and a little swirl-knot on top. Pillowy and warm, leaves wisps of steam, loves to bounce.
- **Voice:** Bouncy, hungry, big-hearted comic-relief friend. Always thinking about the next snack. Loyal and silly.

### Lani the Loco Moco — Loco moco (rice, burger patty, egg, gravy)
- **Catchphrase:** "Pile it high, sleepy guy!"
- **Appearance:** A hearty rice mountain crowned with a sunny fried egg like a golden hat, a burger-patty belt, and shiny gravy rivers running down. Big, sturdy, comforting, with a runny-yolk wink.
- **Voice:** Hearty, comforting, a bit sleepy and lazy in the best way. The cozy one who reminds everyone to rest and eat. Diner-friendly warmth.

### Pele's Glow (Lava the gentle ember) — Not food — a warm, friendly glowing lava ember spirit (volcano character, gentle)
- **Catchphrase:** "New land, new light, every night."
- **Appearance:** A small, soft, glowing orange-gold ember-being with a warm flickery body like a tiny friendly campfire, cooling to cozy black-and-red at the edges. Never burns friends — radiates warmth like a hug. Twinkles like sunset.
- **Voice:** Warm, ancient, gentle, awe-inspiring but never scary. Speaks of the land being born and growing. Cozy bedtime-fire warmth.
